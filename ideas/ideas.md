# Ideas

These are a collection of ideas to beat up on and try out.

## Niches
Should have a per-ticket value of $3,000 or more. These should be businesses whose close is heavily tied to a webpage lookup and a phone call.

- ac duct lining
- Stump grinding
- Pavement sealing / commercial pavement sealing / stripe painting
- popcorn ceiling
- custom leather sports car seats
- Epoxy flooring (garage/residential) — exploded via social media
- Turf installation (artificial grass) — water conservation push
- EV charger installation — new construction code requirements
- Metal building/barndominium construction — huge in Texas
- Spray foam insulation (still growing in smaller markets)
- Polished concrete floors — residential trend
- Exterior coating systems (like Rhino Shield)
- Solar screen/shade installation — Texas heat driven
- Fence coating/staining services — distinct from fence building
- Patio cover/pergola installation — outdoor living boom
- Radiant barrier installation — attic heat reduction, big in TX

## Locations
Central location should have a population of 80,000 or more. Targeting sub locations to give their own linked subpages is a valid strategy.

## Ruled out
- Dock building / seawall construction / pile driving: Too rural, not enough search volume
- **Emerging construction services × Abilene TX** (Nov 2026 DataForSEO pull, 71 keywords, $0.18): All 10 trending categories tested — epoxy flooring, artificial turf, spray foam insulation, metal building/barndo, polished concrete, patio cover/pergola, EV charger, smart home, radiant barrier, exterior coating. Every Abilene-specific keyword returned null volume. Generic/near-me variants showed at most 10/mo (Google Ads floor) with zero CPC. Spray foam was most consistent (6 of 12 months) but still only 10/mo vs. 11-score in Houston. Abilene DMA (~170K) too small for specialty construction niches. Signal 1 = 0, Signal 2 = 0 → walk away on all.
- **Patio cover/pergola × Albuquerque NM** (Nov 2026, full rr-validate, $0.69 total): Volume 210/mo but CPC only $4.02 — decent traffic, no commercial intent. Maps returned just 1 business (no renters). Extreme seasonality (peaks May, drops to 10% of peak Dec–Jan). Score: 6/16 → walk away. Killer: $4 CPC + 1 renter + seasonal cliff.
- **Epoxy flooring × Tucson AZ** (Nov 2026, full rr-validate, $0.69 total): CPC strong at $31–67 but volume only 50/mo. Map pack dominated by American Epoxy Arizona (183 reviews, 4.8★). Top 5 organic all strong local sites. Year-round demand and 20+ renters are positives, but rent estimate ~$174/mo on thin volume. Score: 7/16 → walk away. Killer: 50/mo volume can't generate enough leads despite high CPC.
- **Land clearing / bush hogging × 8 markets** (Nov 2026 DataForSEO pull, 80 keywords, $0.72): Tested across Abilene, Odessa, Midland, El Paso, Tulsa, Tucson, OKC, Albuquerque. Bush hogging returned null/zero everywhere — not a search-driven service in these regions. Land clearing showed faint signal: best was OKC "near me" at 30/mo (no CPC), Abilene had $35.66 CPC but only 10/mo. No market reached 200/mo (Signal 1 = 0 everywhere). Not viable for rank-and-rent at any tested population level.
