### Why Remodel Your Kitchen?

The kitchen is the room that gets used hardest and judged first. It is where families cook, eat, do homework, and gather when guests come over. When the layout does not work, the cabinets are dated, or the countertop stains every time someone sets a coffee mug down, the kitchen drags the whole house down with it.

A well-planned remodel addresses function and appearance at the same time. Opening a wall to create a pass-through to the living area changes how the house feels. Replacing dark laminate counters with quartz — the most popular countertop material in the Phoenix metro — makes the room brighter and eliminates the sealing routine that granite requires. Adding a kitchen island gives the cook a workspace and the family a place to sit without being in the way.

Kitchen remodels also carry strong resale value. Arizona's housing market rewards updated kitchens because buyers walk through the kitchen first and form an opinion before they check the bedrooms.

### Types of Kitchen Remodels

Not every kitchen needs a full gut. The scope depends on what is driving the project:

- **Minor update** — New hardware, a fresh backsplash, updated light fixtures, and a coat of paint on the cabinets. The layout stays the same, the cabinets stay in place, and the job takes one to two weeks.
- **Mid-range remodel** — New countertops, refaced or replaced cabinets, updated appliances, and possibly new flooring. No walls move. This is the most common scope in Goodyear and typically runs three to five weeks.
- **Full renovation** — Layout changes, new cabinets, new countertops, new appliances, plumbing and electrical rerouting, and often new flooring throughout. Walls may come down or go up. Expect four to eight weeks and a kitchen that is completely out of service during demolition and rough-in.

Call or submit a request through the site. Tell us about the kitchen: its approximate size, what you want to change, and whether you have a rough budget in mind. A local contractor follows up to schedule a free in-home visit. No pressure, no commitment.

### Step 2: Design Consultation

The contractor inspects the kitchen, measures the space, and discusses your goals. If the scope involves plumbing or electrical changes, they will outline the permit process — in Goodyear, any remodel altering plumbing, gas, or electrical systems requires a permit from the Development Services Department. The contractor handles that filing.

You will choose materials together: cabinet style and finish, countertop material (granite, quartz, butcher block, or solid surface), hardware, backsplash, and appliances. Most contractors bring samples or can direct you to local slab yards and showrooms in the Phoenix metro area.

### Step 3: Construction and Completion

Work begins with demolition of the old surfaces and rough-in of any new plumbing or electrical. Cabinets go in next, followed by countertop templating and fabrication (typically a one- to two-week turnaround for stone), backsplash, flooring, and appliance installation. The contractor schedules required inspections with the city and walks you through the finished kitchen before closing out the project.

Timeline depends on scope. A minor update can be done in a week or two. A mid-range remodel takes three to five weeks. A full renovation with layout changes runs four to eight weeks. The [timeline guide](/guides/how-long-does-a-kitchen-remodel-take/) walks through each phase.
