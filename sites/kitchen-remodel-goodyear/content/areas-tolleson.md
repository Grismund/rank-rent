## Tolleson Kitchen Remodel Permits

Kitchen remodels involving plumbing, electrical, or gas changes require a permit from the Tolleson Building Department. Surface-only work — countertops, cabinet doors, backsplash — typically does not require a permit.

Tolleson adopted the 2024 International Building Code, 2024 International Residential Code, 2024 International Plumbing Code, and 2023 National Electrical Code through Ordinance No. 620 N.S., effective July 1, 2025. All permitted work must comply with these current code editions. Three sets of construction plans must be submitted, and permit fees are based on the cost of construction.

The City of Tolleson requires permits for all remodeling and improvements to property. Work started without a permit can result in extra charges and fines levied against the property owner. Your contractor handles the permit application, plan submission, and inspection scheduling to avoid those complications.

Kitchen remodel inspections typically include rough plumbing, rough electrical, and final inspections before the project is signed off.

## What Tolleson Homeowners Are Choosing

Tolleson's remodel scope reflects the city's budget orientation and practical-minded homeowners:

**Granite countertops** are more common in Tolleson than in neighboring cities.
