## How to Use This Tool

Choose three inputs:

1. **Kitchen size** — galley or small, standard, or large/open layout. This determines the base square footage and cabinet count.
2. **Project scope** — minor update (cosmetic changes only), mid-range remodel (new cabinets and countertops), or full renovation (layout changes, new plumbing and electrical).
3. **Finish level** — standard, upgraded, or premium. This adjusts material costs for cabinets, countertops, and fixtures.

The calculator returns a low-to-high range. The low end assumes standard materials and minimal prep work. The high end assumes premium materials, additional complexity, or older infrastructure that needs updating.

## Where Our Estimates Come From

The ranges in this calculator are based on job economics data from the kitchen remodeling market, adjusted for the Goodyear area. Source data includes average project values from contractor pricing in the Phoenix metro, material costs from local suppliers, and labor rates typical of the West Valley.

A few notes on what the estimate includes and excludes:

- **Included:** demolition, materials, labor, standard fixtures, and cleanup
- **Not included:** permit fees, appliance costs (these vary too widely to generalize), structural engineering (if walls are moving), and design fees
- **Disclaimer:** these are estimates, not quotes. Costs vary by contractor and site conditions. The condition of your existing plumbing, electrical, and slab affects the final number.

## Common Questions About Remodel Costs

### How accurate is this calculator?

The calculator provides a range based on typical Goodyear-area pricing. Actual costs depend on your specific kitchen, materials, and the contractor you select. Treat the result as a starting point for budgeting, not a binding price.

### Does the estimate include permits?

Permit fees are not included. In Goodyear, permits are required for work involving plumbing, gas, or electrical changes. Fees are based on the cost of construction and are typically a small percentage of the overall project cost.

### What if my kitchen is an unusual size?

The calculator covers standard kitchen sizes. For unusually large, small, or irregularly shaped kitchens, a free in-home estimate from a local contractor will be more precise.

### Do I need a full remodel or just an update?

If the layout works and the cabinets are structurally sound, a minor update or [cabinet refacing](/cabinet-refacing/) may be sufficient. If the layout is the core problem, a [full kitchen remodel](/full-kitchen-remodel/) is the better investment. The [refacing vs. replacement guide](/guides/cabinet-refacing-vs-replacement/) helps you evaluate the decision.

### Why do costs vary so much?

Cabinet quality, countertop material, layout changes, appliance upgrades, and the condition of existing plumbing and electrical all shift the price. Two kitchens the same size can receive very different quotes based on these factors.
