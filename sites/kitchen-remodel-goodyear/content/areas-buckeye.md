## Buckeye Kitchen Remodel Permits

Kitchen remodels that involve plumbing, electrical, or gas changes require a permit from the City of Buckeye Development Services department. Surface-only work — countertops, cabinet doors, backsplash — with no changes to plumbing or electrical circuits typically does not require a permit.

Buckeye adopted the 2024 International Residential Code, 2024 International Plumbing Code, 2024 International Mechanical Code, and 2023 National Electrical Code effective January 1, 2025. All permit applications submitted after that date must comply with the 2024 code editions. Arizona has no statewide residential building code — each city adopts its own — so Buckeye's requirements may differ from neighboring Goodyear or Litchfield Park.

Adding circuits, moving outlets, or upgrading the electrical panel requires a separate electrical permit. Full gut kitchen renovations must bring all wiring and plumbing up to the current 2024 code. Kitchen remodel inspections typically include rough plumbing, rough electrical, and final inspections.

Your contractor handles all permit filings and inspection scheduling.
