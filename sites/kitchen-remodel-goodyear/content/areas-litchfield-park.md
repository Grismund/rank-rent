A [full kitchen remodel](/full-kitchen-remodel/) is typically the right approach for these properties because the systems behind the walls need attention alongside the visible surfaces.

## Litchfield Park Kitchen Remodel Permits

Kitchen remodels involving plumbing, electrical, or gas changes require a permit from the Litchfield Park Building Department. Applications are submitted to Building Safety at 214 W Wigwam Blvd, Litchfield Park, AZ 85340. Residential remodel permits typically take five to ten business days to process.

An important distinction: Litchfield Park currently enforces the 2018 International Residential Code, 2018 International Plumbing Code, and 2017 National Electrical Code. This is one code cycle behind neighboring cities like Goodyear, Avondale, and Buckeye, which have adopted the 2024 codes. Contractors working in multiple West Valley cities should verify which code edition applies before starting work.

Electrical permits are required for adding circuits, moving outlets, or upgrading panels. Full gut kitchen renovations must update all wiring and plumbing to Litchfield Park's current code edition. For properties in unincorporated areas near Litchfield Park, permits are handled by the Maricopa County Building Department.
