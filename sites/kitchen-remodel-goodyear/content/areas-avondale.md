## Avondale Kitchen Remodel Permits

Kitchen remodels in Avondale that involve plumbing, electrical, or gas changes require a permit from the City of Avondale Development Services Department. Surface-only work — countertops, cabinet doors, backsplash — does not require a permit.

The City of Avondale adopted the 2024 International Residential Code, 2024 International Plumbing Code, and 2023 National Electrical Code effective January 1, 2026. All permitted kitchen remodel work must comply with these current code editions. Arizona has no statewide residential building code — each city adopts its own — so Avondale's requirements may differ from neighboring Goodyear or Tolleson.

Adding circuits, moving outlets, upgrading the electrical panel, or running new wiring requires a separate electrical permit. Full gut kitchen renovations must update all wiring and plumbing to the current code edition. Kitchen remodel inspections typically include rough plumbing, rough electrical, and a final inspection before the project is signed off.

Your contractor handles the permit application, inspection scheduling, and code compliance.
