A **full kitchen remodel in Goodyear** is the most comprehensive option: layout changes, new cabinets, new countertops, updated appliances, and rerouted plumbing and electrical. It is also the most disruptive. The kitchen goes out of service for four to eight weeks, every trade is involved, and the result is a room that works and looks entirely different from what was there before.

Goodyear Kitchen Remodeling connects homeowners with local contractors who manage the entire process. You work directly with the contractor handling your project — no middlemen, no franchise dispatch.

## About Full Kitchen Remodel

A full remodel means everything is on the table. Walls can come down to open the kitchen to the living area. Plumbing can move to accommodate a new island or sink location. Electrical circuits can be added for modern appliances, under-cabinet lighting, or a dedicated outlet at the island.

This scope makes sense when the kitchen's problems go beyond surface wear. If the layout forces the cook to cross the room between the stove and the sink, if the cabinets are warped or falling apart, or if the electrical panel cannot handle today's appliance loads, a cosmetic refresh will not solve the underlying issues.

Many Goodyear homes were built in the mid-2000s with open floor plans and builder-grade finishes. The layout may work fine, but the cabinets, countertops, and fixtures are nearing 20 years of use. A full remodel replaces all of it in a single project rather than spreading smaller upgrades over several years.

## How Full Kitchen Remodel Works

### Planning and Material Selection

The contractor visits the kitchen, takes measurements, and reviews your goals. If you want to move walls, relocate the sink, or add an island, the contractor evaluates what structural, plumbing, and electrical work that requires.

Materials get selected during this phase: cabinet species and finish, countertop material, backsplash tile, flooring, hardware, and appliances. The contractor can recommend local slab yards for granite and quartz — several suppliers in the Phoenix metro serve the West Valley, including yards in Surprise and central Phoenix.

If the scope involves plumbing, gas, or electrical changes, the contractor files for a permit through the City of Goodyear Development Services Department. Goodyear adopted the 2024 International technical codes in March 2026, so all new work must meet current standards.

### Demolition and Installation

Work begins with removing the old cabinets, countertops, flooring, and fixtures. If walls are moving, the structural changes happen next, followed by rough plumbing and rough electrical. The city inspector visits to sign off on the rough work before anything gets covered up.

Cabinets install first once the rough-in is approved. Countertops are templated after the cabinets are set — a fabricator visits to laser-measure the exact dimensions, then cuts and polishes the stone at their shop. Expect a one- to two-week turnaround between templating and installation for granite or quartz.

Backsplash, flooring, appliance hookup, and trim follow. The contractor schedules the final inspection with the city to close out the permit.

### Final Walkthrough

The contractor walks you through the finished kitchen. You check cabinet alignment, countertop seams, appliance operation, plumbing fixtures, and electrical outlets. Any punch-list items get addressed before the project is complete.

## Popular Options for Full Kitchen Remodel

Goodyear homeowners making the full investment tend to prioritize these choices:

- **Quartz countertops** — the most popular material in the Phoenix metro. Nonporous, requires no sealing, and resists the mineral deposits that Goodyear's very hard water (18-23 GPG) leaves on other surfaces.
- **Shaker-style cabinets** — clean lines that work with both contemporary and transitional designs. Available in painted or stained finishes.
- **Kitchen island with seating** — the visual anchor of most Goodyear kitchen remodels, often with a waterfall countertop edge and pendant lighting.
- **Open floor plan conversion** — removing the wall between kitchen and living room is one of the most requested layout changes in homes built with separated rooms.

## How Much Does Full Kitchen Remodel Cost?

A full kitchen remodel in Goodyear typically falls between $12,000 and $35,000. The low end covers a standard-size kitchen with mid-grade cabinets and countertops. The high end includes premium materials, layout changes, and upgraded appliances.
