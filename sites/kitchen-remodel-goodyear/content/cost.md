The short answer on **kitchen remodel cost in Goodyear**: most projects fall between $5,000 and $35,000 depending on scope. A minor cosmetic update starts around $5,000. A mid-range remodel with new cabinets and countertops runs $12,000 to $20,000. A full renovation with layout changes, premium materials, and new appliances can reach $35,000 or more.

These figures reflect what Goodyear-area contractors are quoting in the current market. Your specific kitchen, material choices, and the contractor you select will shift the number.

*Last updated: November 2026. Costs vary by contractor and site conditions.*

## Typical Kitchen Remodel Costs

### Minor Kitchen Update

Budget **$5,000 to $12,000**. The layout stays the same. Cabinets stay in place. This scope covers cosmetic changes: painting or refacing cabinet doors, replacing hardware, installing a new backsplash, upgrading light fixtures, and swapping out the faucet. No plumbing or electrical is rerouted.

A minor update makes sense when the kitchen functions well but looks dated. Many Goodyear homes built in the mid-2000s have perfectly functional kitchens with finishes that feel twenty years old — because they are. New shaker doors in white paint, modern pulls, and a subway tile backsplash change the entire character of the room for well under $10,000.

Timeline: one to two weeks.

### Mid-Range Kitchen Remodel

Budget **$12,000 to $20,000**. This is the most common scope in Goodyear. It typically includes new or refaced cabinets, new countertops (quartz or granite), a new backsplash, updated flooring, and replacement appliances. The layout does not change, but most visible surfaces are new.

The mid-range budget is where material choices have the biggest impact. Quartz countertops at $80 to $200 per square foot and granite at $70 to $150 per square foot both fit within this range depending on the grade. Cabinet refacing at $4,000 to $8,000 saves money compared to full replacement at $8,000 to $20,000, leaving more of the budget for the countertop and appliances.

Timeline: three to five weeks.

### High-End Kitchen Remodel

Budget **$20,000 to $35,000**. Everything changes. Layout modifications may include removing walls to open the kitchen to the living area, adding or relocating an island, and moving plumbing and electrical to new positions. Cabinets are custom or semi-custom. Countertops are premium quartz, quartzite, or natural stone. Appliances are upgraded.

At this scope, the City of Goodyear will require permits for the plumbing, electrical, and potentially structural work. Permit fees are based on the cost of construction. The contractor handles the application and scheduling of inspections.

Timeline: four to eight weeks.

## What Affects Kitchen Remodel Pricing?

Five factors account for most of the price variation between similar-size kitchens:

**Cabinets.** The single largest line item in most kitchen remodels. Stock cabinets run $100 to $300 per linear foot. Semi-custom runs $200 to $650. Custom exceeds $650. Cabinet refacing ($4,000 to $8,000 for a full kitchen) offers a lower-cost alternative when the boxes are in good condition.

**Countertops.** Material and total square footage drive this cost. A 40-square-foot quartz countertop at $120 per foot installed comes to $4,800. The same footprint in entry-level granite at $70 per foot is $2,800.

**Layout changes.** Moving the sink, relocating plumbing, or opening a wall adds structural, plumbing, and electrical work. Each of those is a separate scope with its own cost — plumbing relocation alone can add $1,000 to $3,000.

**Appliances.** A basic appliance package (range, refrigerator, dishwasher, microwave) runs $2,000 to $5,000. Premium brands push that to $8,000 or more.

**Permits and inspections.** Required in Goodyear for any work involving plumbing, gas, or electrical changes. Fees are based on the cost of construction and are typically a small share of the overall budget.

## Where Your Kitchen Remodel Budget Goes

On a mid-range Goodyear kitchen remodel, the budget typically breaks down along these lines:

- **Cabinets and hardware:** 30 to 35 percent
- **Countertops and backsplash:** 15 to 20 percent
- **Labor (installation, plumbing, electrical):** 20 to 25 percent
- **Appliances:** 10 to 15 percent
- **Flooring:** 5 to 10 percent
- **Permits, design, and miscellaneous:** 5 to 10 percent

The exact split depends on your priorities. Homeowners who choose cabinet refacing instead of replacement shift budget toward countertops and appliances. Those who keep existing appliances can put more into premium stone and custom features.
