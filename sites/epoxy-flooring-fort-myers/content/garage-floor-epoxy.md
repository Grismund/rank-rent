## What Is Garage Floor Epoxy?

Two components: a resin and a hardener. Mix them on the slab and a chemical reaction turns the liquid into a rigid, glossy shell locked onto the concrete. It does not sit on top the way paint does, and it does not soak in the way a stain would. It becomes the surface.

The gap between a professional installation and a hardware-store kit is thickness. A retail product goes on at 2 or 3 mils. A professional system builds to 10 to 20 mils in layers — primer, base coat, topcoat — and that extra material is where the impact resistance and longevity come from. One lasts a couple of years. The other lasts a couple of decades.

## Why Choose Garage Floor Epoxy

What it does for a Fort Myers garage in practical terms:

- **Spills stay on the surface.** Oil, gas, brake fluid, fertilizer — nothing penetrates. Wipe it up whenever you get around to it.
- **It absorbs daily wear.** Tires, rolling tool chests, dropped wrenches. The floor takes it without complaint.
- **Cleaning takes minutes.** Sweep and the occasional mop. No scrubbing embedded stains out of raw concrete.
- **The space gets brighter.** Gloss reflects overhead lights back into the room, a noticeable difference in a garage that usually runs dim.
- **Ground moisture slows down.** Fort Myers sits on sandy soil over a shallow water table. Bare slabs let vapor through year-round; a primed coating resists it.

Standard epoxy can yellow under direct sunlight and may show hot-tire marks if you park a truck that just came off I-75 in July. Both issues have solutions: a polyaspartic or UV-stable topcoat prevents yellowing, and a quality clear resists tire pickup. ## Garage Floor Epoxy Installation Steps

The coating is the visible part. The preparation underneath it determines whether the floor lasts two years or twenty.

### Concrete Preparation

Everything comes off the slab first. The contractor walks the floor looking for cracks, spalling, dark patches that signal moisture, and remnants of old sealers or paint. In Fort Myers, moisture inspection matters more than in most of the country — sandy soil and a shallow water table mean slabs can test wet even when the surface looks bone dry. An ASTM F2170 relative humidity probe test confirms whether the concrete reads below the 75 percent threshold manufacturers require before coating.

Cracks get filled with a semi-rigid filler. The crew diamond-grinds or shot-blasts the entire surface, stripping the top skin of the concrete to expose the pores the primer needs.

### Epoxy Application

Primer soaks into the opened concrete and bridges to the base coat. The base coat carries the color and most of the thickness.

If you want flake or chip broadcast — most Fort Myers homeowners do — the crew throws it into the wet base by hand. Flake adds texture, hides minor slab imperfections, and provides traction when the floor is wet from rain tracked in off the driveway.

### Curing and Final Coat

A clear topcoat locks the decorative layer in place and provides the abrasion and chemical resistance. Then stay off it. Standard epoxy needs 24 to 72 hours before foot traffic and five to seven days before you park. Southwest Florida humidity can stretch those windows, so your contractor will give you dates tied to the forecast rather than round numbers.
