## Why Bonita Springs Homeowners Choose Epoxy Flooring

### Climate and Concrete Conditions

Bonita Springs sits on the same sandy substrate over marine limestone that defines all of Lee County, with a water table that stays shallow year-round. Concrete slabs wick moisture upward through their pores continuously. The Southwest Florida building code sets a threshold of 3 pounds of moisture per 1,000 square feet in 24 hours for floor coatings, and ASTM F2170 probe readings must remain below 75 percent before epoxy is applied.

What sets Bonita Springs apart from inland communities is its coastal exposure. Properties near the Imperial River, Estero Bay, and the Gulf of Mexico face constant salt-laden air. Oceanfront and canal-side slabs in Lee County deteriorate 40 to 60 percent faster than inland ones when left unprotected. A sealed epoxy surface acts as a barrier against that salt intrusion from above while a vapor-blocking primer addresses moisture from below.

Annual humidity ranges from 65 to 80 percent, with summer peaks above 85 percent. Coastal conditions can reduce a standard garage floor coating's effective lifespan by up to 40 percent without proper materials and installation techniques. Professional installers compensate with extended curing windows and climate-specific products — polyaspartic topcoats that set rapidly regardless of humidity, and moisture-mitigating primers rated for high-vapor-transmission slabs.

Older Bonita Springs homes built before 2000 may lack modern vapor barriers under their slabs, which makes pre-coating moisture testing even more critical. On these older foundations, a mitigating primer is often necessary before the base coat.

The snowbird population is significant here. Seasonal residents in Bonita Springs' retirement and 55-plus communities lock up for months at a time and need floors that require zero maintenance during their absence. A properly installed epoxy floor stays clean and dry in a closed garage regardless of how long it sits.

Hurricane preparedness drives a practical demand as well. Shutters, generators, fuel cans, plywood, and emergency equipment all need a home from June through November, and a sealed floor means no permanent staining when those items leak or leave residue.

## What to Know Before Coating in Bonita Springs

**Permits:** The City of Bonita Springs administers its own building permits through the Community Development Department. Most residential epoxy coating over existing concrete does not require a permit. Commercial installations involving structural modifications, floor drains, or fire-rating changes may. Contractors must hold a State of Florida certification or a Lee County Certificate of Competency — Bonita Springs uses Lee County's regional contractor registration system.

**HOA requirements:** Bonita Bay, Pelican Landing, and The Colony all have architectural review boards. Interior garage work is generally exempt from review, but check with your association before scheduling. Some covenants regulate visible modifications, and a high-gloss metallic floor that catches sunlight through an open door could be considered one.

**Moisture testing:** Bonita Springs' coastal location, high humidity, and older housing stock make pre-coating moisture testing mandatory. An ASTM F2170 probe test is the standard. Older slabs without modern vapor barriers often require a moisture-mitigating primer as an additional system component.
