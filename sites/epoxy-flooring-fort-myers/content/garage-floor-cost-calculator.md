This **garage floor cost calculator for Fort Myers** gives you a price range for an epoxy floor before you talk to anyone. Pick your garage size, the finish you have in mind, and the condition your slab is in. The estimate appears immediately.

## How to Use This Tool

Three inputs:

1. **Garage size.** One-car is roughly 250 square feet, two-car 400 to 500, three-car 600 to 750. That sets the footage.
2. **Finish type.** Solid color, flake, metallic, or polyaspartic. Each one carries different material and labor costs.
3. **Slab condition.** New concrete, minor cracks, or an old coating that needs to be removed. More preparation means a higher number.

From those three answers you get a low and a high. The range covers material, standard concrete preparation, and the labor to install.

## Where Our Estimates Come From

The numbers behind this calculator reflect what Fort Myers contractors are quoting today. Solid-color and flake systems install for roughly $3 to $7 per square foot. Metallic runs $5 to $12. Polyaspartic adds about 20 to 30 percent to a comparable epoxy system.

A typical two-car garage lands between $1,600 and $3,500, consistent with what local installers quote over the phone. Those figures assume standard prep: diamond grinding, minor crack filling, and a moisture test.

What the calculator cannot account for is your specific slab. A floor with real damage, active moisture vapor, or a stubborn old coating will come in above the range it shows. Treat the result as a budget number, not a bid. The [epoxy flooring cost guide](/cost/) explains each pricing factor in more detail.

## Common Questions About Epoxy Costs

### How accurate is this epoxy cost calculator?

Accurate enough to budget with, not accurate enough to sign on. It uses typical Fort Myers pricing and assumes a slab in reasonable condition. The contractor's on-site estimate will account for your concrete's actual state and any extra preparation it requires.

### Does the estimate include concrete prep?

It covers the basics: diamond grinding, minor crack filling, and a moisture vapor test. If the slab has serious damage, an active moisture problem, or an old coating requiring chemical stripping, those are additional costs that can push the contractor's real price above what the tool shows.

### What is the difference between solid, flake, and metallic finishes?

Solid color is one tone and the least expensive option. Flake (also called chip or broadcast) adds colored vinyl pieces for texture, visual depth, and better traction when the floor is wet. Metallic uses pigments that swirl during curing, producing a unique pattern on every floor — the premium choice at $5 to $12 per square foot. All three are applied over the same prepared concrete.

### How long does an epoxy floor installation take?

One to two days for most garages. Then standard epoxy needs 24 to 72 hours before you walk on it and five to seven days before you park. Polyaspartic is faster — often ready for full use within 24 hours. Your contractor will give you specific dates based on the product and the week's weather conditions.
