## Why Estero Homeowners Choose Epoxy Flooring

### Climate and Concrete Conditions

Estero shares the subtropical climate of Southwest Florida: average annual temperature of 74 degrees, average rainfall of 58 inches, and humidity that ranges from 65 to 80 percent year-round with summer peaks that can reach 90 percent during the wet season. Summers are long, hot, and oppressive with persistent moisture.

The village sits on sandy soil over marine limestone, the same substrate as the rest of Lee County, with a water table close to the surface. Slabs wick moisture upward through concrete pores constantly. Southwest Florida building code requires concrete moisture to be no more than 3 pounds per 1,000 square feet in 24 hours for floor coatings, and ASTM F2170 probe readings must be below 75 percent before epoxy goes down. 85 percent of epoxy failures in Florida trace back to moisture beneath the slab.

One important distinction for Estero: many homes here are in newer developments built after 2000. Newer slabs generally include better vapor barriers under the concrete than older construction, which reduces (but does not eliminate) moisture risk. However, Estero's proximity to Estero Bay and coastal waterways means the ambient humidity is higher than in fully inland communities like Lehigh Acres, and that ambient moisture affects curing behavior.

### Popular Applications in Estero

Estero's upscale communities feature two- and three-car garages, often 500 to 750 square feet. Homeowners use them for vehicle parking, golf cart storage, home workshops, and home gyms. The garage in an Estero home is as much a living space as a storage area, and a raw concrete floor does not meet the standard these homeowners expect.

Seasonal residents — snowbirds — are particularly common in Estero's 55-plus communities like The Brooks and Grandezza. They leave for months at a time and want a garage floor that stays clean and dry without any maintenance while the house is vacant. Epoxy delivers exactly that.

Hurricane season adds a practical layer. When shutters, generators, fuel cans, and emergency equipment all need dedicated garage space from June through November, a sealed floor simplifies cleanup and prevents fuel or chemical stains from becoming permanent.

## What to Know Before Coating in Estero

**Permits:** The Village of Estero, incorporated in 2014, administers its own building permits through the Community Development Department. Most residential epoxy coating over existing concrete does not require a permit. Commercial installations involving structural modifications, floor drains, or fire-rating changes may. Contractors must hold a State of Florida certification or a Lee County Certificate of Competency — Estero uses Lee County's regional contractor registration system.

**HOA requirements:** Estero is dominated by HOA-governed communities. Miromar Lakes, The Brooks, WildBlue, and Grandezza all have architectural review processes. Interior garage floor work rarely triggers review, but communities with active covenant enforcement may require notice or approval for modifications visible from the outside, such as a high-gloss metallic floor visible through an open garage door. Check before scheduling.

**Moisture testing:** Despite newer construction and generally better vapor barriers, Estero's coastal humidity and proximity to Estero Bay mean slab moisture testing is non-negotiable. An ASTM F2170 probe test is the standard, and readings must come back below 75 percent before any coating is applied.
