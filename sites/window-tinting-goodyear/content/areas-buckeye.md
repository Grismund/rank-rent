## Window Tint Regulations in Arizona

Arizona law requires front driver and passenger windows to allow at least 33 percent VLT. Back side windows and the rear windshield have no VLT minimum as long as the vehicle has dual side mirrors. Reflectivity is capped at 35 percent. These rules apply to all vehicle types.

Arizona has no vehicle safety inspection requirement and no formal tint inspection after installation. Enforcement occurs during traffic stops. The full details are in the [Arizona tint laws guide](/guides/state-window-tint-laws-explained/).
