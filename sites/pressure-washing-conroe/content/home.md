## Professional Pressure Washing Services

Pressure washing uses water delivered at high pressure — typically 2,000 to 4,000 PSI for residential work — to strip dirt, algae, mold, mildew, and stains from hard surfaces. Soft washing uses lower pressure combined with cleaning solutions to treat surfaces that high pressure would damage, like roofs and painted siding. Both methods are standard across the Conroe area, and a qualified contractor selects the approach based on what the surface can handle.

### Why Hire a Pressure Washing Service?

The equipment matters, but technique matters more. Too much pressure on vinyl siding drives water behind the panels. Too little on a concrete driveway leaves the algae roots intact, and the stains return in weeks. Contractors calibrate pressure, nozzle angle, and chemical concentration for each surface type. They also manage wastewater properly — under the Clean Water Act and Texas TPDES program, wash water cannot flow into storm drains.

Homes under heavy tree cover or with north-facing walls may need exterior cleaning twice a year. Properties near Lake Conroe or along shaded greenbelts in areas like Grand Central Park often need service every six to nine months. A professional assessment identifies what actually needs cleaning and how often, so you are not paying for work that does not need doing yet.

## Pressure Washing Services We Offer

Conroe Pressure Washing connects you with contractors who specialize in four categories of exterior cleaning:

**[House Washing](/house-washing/)** covers siding, brick, stucco, and trim from ground to roofline. Most Conroe homes benefit from a full house wash every 12 to 24 months — sooner if the home sits under dense tree canopy. Expect to pay $0.15 to $0.40 per square foot depending on the siding material and the extent of biological growth.

**[Driveway Pressure Washing](/driveway-pressure-washing/)** targets concrete and paver driveways that collect tire marks, oil drips, red clay staining, and the dark algae streaks that East Texas humidity produces. Surface cleaning with a rotary attachment leaves even results without the striping that a wand alone can create. Typical cost: $100 to $200 for a standard two-car driveway.

**[Deck and Patio Cleaning](/deck-and-patio-cleaning/)** requires careful pressure selection. Wood decks call for lower PSI to avoid fiber damage; composite and stone handle more. Conroe's humidity means decks may need cleaning every 6 to 12 months — the most frequent cycle of any residential surface. Cost runs $0.25 to $0.50 per square foot.

**[Roof Soft Washing](/roof-soft-washing/)** uses low-pressure chemical application to kill algae and lichen without damaging shingles. High-pressure water on a roof voids most manufacturer warranties. Soft wash is the only method contractors in our network use on roofing. Plan on every 24 to 36 months, at $0.20 to $0.50 per square foot.
