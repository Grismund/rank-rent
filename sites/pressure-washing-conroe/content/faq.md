## Pressure Washing Questions Answered

### How often should I pressure wash my house in Conroe?

Most Conroe homes benefit from a full exterior cleaning every 12 to 24 months. That is the general guidance, but the real answer depends on your property. Homes under heavy tree canopy, near Lake Conroe, or with north-facing walls that stay shaded may need service every 6 to 12 months. East Texas humidity averages 73 percent year-round, and that sustained moisture level feeds algae and mildew growth faster than in drier climates. If you see green or black discoloration on the siding, it is time — waiting only allows the growth to spread and become harder to remove. The [how often to pressure wash guide](/guides/how-often-should-you-pressure-wash-your-house/) goes deeper on timing by surface type.

### What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water — typically 2,000 to 4,000 PSI — to remove dirt, algae, and stains from hard surfaces like concrete driveways and sidewalks. Soft washing uses low pressure (under 1,000 PSI) combined with a cleaning solution that kills biological growth chemically before rinsing it away. Soft washing is the standard method for roofs, siding, painted surfaces, and wood, where high pressure would cause damage. Most residential jobs in Conroe use both methods on the same property — pressure for the driveway and sidewalks, soft wash for the house and roof. The [soft washing vs. pressure washing guide](/guides/soft-washing-vs-pressure-washing/) covers the full comparison.

### Can pressure washing damage my siding?

It can if the wrong technique is used. That is exactly why soft washing exists. High pressure drives water behind vinyl siding, tears wood fibers, and can crack older stucco. A qualified contractor evaluates the siding material on each elevation of the house and selects the appropriate pressure and nozzle. Vinyl, Hardie board, and painted wood all call for soft wash — low pressure with a cleaning solution. Bare brick and stone can handle higher pressure. The risk comes from someone who uses the same high-pressure setting on everything. The contractors in our network assess the surface first and adjust accordingly.

### Is pressure washing safe for my roof?

Standard pressure washing is not safe for roofs. High pressure strips granules from asphalt shingles, cracks tile, and voids most manufacturer warranties. Soft washing is the only appropriate method — it uses low-pressure application of a cleaning solution that kills algae, moss, and lichen chemically, followed by a gentle rinse. Every contractor in our network uses soft wash exclusively on roofing. The [roof soft washing page](/roof-soft-washing/) covers the full process.

### Do I need to be home during the service?

You do not need to be home for the entire service, but most contractors prefer to walk the property with you before starting. This ensures both parties agree on the scope, any areas of concern are noted, and access to water and power is arranged. After that initial walkthrough, the work proceeds whether you are present or not. Payment terms vary by contractor.

### What about water restrictions in Montgomery County?

Conroe and surrounding Montgomery County communities have seasonal and drought-related irrigation restrictions. From May through October, Conroe limits landscape irrigation to specific days and times. Montgomery County MUDs enforce their own drought contingency plans with tiered restrictions. The good news for pressure washing: it is typically categorized as a non-irrigation activity and is permitted even during Stage 2 restrictions. Hand watering with a nozzle-equipped hose is allowed any time and any day. During severe drought conditions (Stage 3), operators should confirm with the local MUD.

### How long does a pressure washing job take?

A single-surface job — one driveway or one side of a house — typically takes 30 to 90 minutes. A full house wash runs two to four hours depending on the home's size and the severity of biological growth. A complete property package covering driveway, house, deck, and fence can take a full day. These are working times; the contractor may also need setup and wastewater management time on each end. Weather can extend timelines — pressure washing during active rain is not effective, so contractors reschedule around storms.
