## About House Washing

House washing is the process of cleaning a home's exterior surfaces — siding, brick, stucco, Hardie board, trim, soffits, and fascia — using either soft washing or pressure washing depending on the material. Soft washing combines low-pressure water (typically under 1,000 PSI) with a biodegradable cleaning solution that kills algae, mold, and mildew at the root. Standard pressure washing uses higher PSI for durable surfaces like brick and unpainted concrete.

Most Conroe homes benefit from a full house wash every 12 to 24 months. Homes under heavy tree cover, near Lake Conroe, or with north-facing walls that stay shaded throughout the day may need service twice a year. The green and black discoloration that homeowners notice on siding is not just dirt — it is living biological growth that spreads if left untreated.

## Advantages of House Washing

**Curb appeal is the obvious one.** A washed house looks maintained. The difference between a cleaned exterior and one that has gone two years without service is visible from the street.

**Preventing damage is the practical one.** Algae and mildew do not just sit on the surface. On wood siding they feed on the material itself. On vinyl they create conditions that trap moisture against the wall. On brick they work into mortar joints over time. Regular washing stops that cycle before repair costs enter the picture.

**Health considerations matter in this climate.** Mold spores on exterior walls can migrate indoors through windows, doors, and HVAC intakes. Southeast Texas already ranks as one of the highest mold-risk regions in the continental United States. Keeping exterior surfaces clean reduces the organic load feeding that exposure.

**Property value protection.** In Conroe's growing market — the city has seen steady population gains and new subdivisions in Grand Central Park and surrounding areas — exterior condition is part of what buyers evaluate. A house that has been regularly maintained sells that story at a glance.

## The House Washing Process

### Surface Assessment

The contractor walks the property, identifies the siding material on each elevation, and notes areas of heavy biological growth, staining, or damage. Vinyl, Hardie board, and painted wood all call for soft wash. Bare brick and stone can handle higher pressure. The assessment determines which approach goes where.

### Pre-Treatment

A cleaning solution is applied to the exterior surfaces before any rinsing begins. The solution typically contains sodium hypochlorite or a similar agent at a concentration calibrated for the surface type. It sits for 5 to 15 minutes, long enough to kill algae and mildew at the root rather than just displacing them. This step is what separates professional house washing from spraying water at the wall.

### Washing and Rinse

Working from the top down, the contractor rinses the entire exterior at the appropriate pressure for each material. Windows, doors, and landscaping are protected during the process. The result is immediate and visible — the difference between a treated section and an untreated one is obvious within minutes.

Wastewater is managed according to Clean Water Act and TPDES requirements. The runoff from a house wash contains cleaning chemicals and biological material that cannot enter storm drains. Contractors in our network capture and direct that water appropriately.
