This **pressure washing cost calculator Conroe** homeowners use gives you a price range before you talk to anyone. Select the surfaces you need cleaned, enter the approximate size, and the estimate appears immediately. Use it to budget or to compare against a contractor's quote.

## How This Calculator Works

Three inputs:

1. **Surface type.** Driveway, house exterior, deck or patio, roof, or fence. Each one carries a different per-unit cost based on the cleaning method and equipment required.
2. **Size.** Square footage for flat surfaces (driveways, siding, decks, roofs) or linear footage for fences. Enter your best estimate — it does not need to be exact.
3. **Condition.** Light maintenance cleaning, moderate buildup, or heavy biological growth requiring pre-treatment. More severe conditions push toward the higher end of the range.

From those three answers you get a low and a high. The range covers standard cleaning solution, equipment, labor, and proper wastewater management.

## How We Calculate These Ranges

The numbers behind this calculator reflect what Conroe-area contractors are quoting today. Per-square-foot rates by surface type:

- **Driveway:** $0.10 to $0.25 per square foot
- **House exterior:** $0.15 to $0.40 per square foot
- **Deck or patio:** $0.25 to $0.50 per square foot
- **Roof (soft wash):** $0.20 to $0.50 per square foot
- **Fence:** $1 to $3 per linear foot

A typical residential driveway comes in at $100 to $200. A full house wash runs $225 to $600. These figures are consistent with what local contractors quote over the phone for standard jobs in the Conroe and Montgomery County area.

What the calculator cannot account for is your specific property. A driveway with deep oil staining, a roof with heavy moss, or siding with years of untreated mildew buildup will come in above the range the tool shows. Treat the result as a budget number, not a bid. The [pressure washing cost guide](/cost/) explains each pricing factor in more detail.

## Cost Estimator FAQ

### How accurate is this pressure washing cost calculator?

Accurate enough to budget with, not accurate enough to sign on. It uses typical Conroe-area pricing and assumes surfaces in reasonable condition. The contractor's on-site estimate accounts for your property's actual condition and any extra preparation required — oil pre-treatment, heavy biological growth, access challenges, or multi-story height.

### Does the estimate include cleaning solution?

Yes. The ranges include the cleaning solution, equipment, labor, and standard wastewater management. What is not included: oil stain pre-treatment, post-wash sealing, and any repairs to the surfaces being cleaned. Those are additional services quoted separately.

### What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing costs?

Soft washing (used on roofs, siding, and painted surfaces) tends to cost slightly more per square foot than pressure washing (used on concrete and stone) because the cleaning solution is a larger cost component. Roof soft washing runs $0.20 to $0.50 per square foot, while driveway pressure washing runs $0.10 to $0.25. The [soft washing vs. pressure washing guide](/guides/soft-washing-vs-pressure-washing/) explains when each method is used.

### How often should I budget for pressure washing?

In the Conroe area, plan on driveway cleaning every 12 to 18 months, a full house wash every 12 to 24 months, deck cleaning every 6 to 12 months, and roof soft washing every 24 to 36 months. East Texas humidity shortens these cycles compared to drier regions. The [frequency guide](/guides/how-often-should-you-pressure-wash-your-house/) covers recommended timelines by surface.

### Do bundle packages cost less?

Generally, yes. When a contractor handles multiple surfaces in a single visit, the mobilization and setup cost is paid once rather than per trip. Homeowners who bundle a driveway wash with a house wash and a deck cleaning typically pay less than scheduling each service on separate days. Ask your contractor about package pricing when you request a quote.
