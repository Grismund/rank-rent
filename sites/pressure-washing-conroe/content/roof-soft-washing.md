The dark streaks running down asphalt shingle roofs across Montgomery County are not dirt or water stains. ## About Roof Soft Washing

Soft washing is the only appropriate method for cleaning a roof. It uses low-pressure water — typically under 100 PSI at the nozzle, comparable to a garden hose — combined with a cleaning solution that kills algae, moss, lichen, and mold on contact. High-pressure washing on a roof strips granules from asphalt shingles, cracks tile, and voids most manufacturer warranties. Every contractor in our network uses soft wash exclusively on roofing.

Conroe roofs develop algae and organic growth faster than many areas because of the sustained humidity and the organic debris falling from the tree canopy. Homes under heavy cover or along shaded greenbelts near Lake Conroe may show visible black streaking within 12 to 18 months of a previous cleaning. The recommended cycle is every 24 to 36 months, adjusted based on the property's exposure.

## Advantages of Roof Soft Washing

**Extends roof life.** Gloeocapsa magma does not just discolor shingles — it feeds on the limestone calcium carbonate in the granules, gradually degrading the shingle's protective layer. Left untreated for years, this biological activity accelerates granule loss and shortens the roof's functional lifespan. Regular soft washing removes the organisms before they cause meaningful damage.

**Energy efficiency.** Dark algae streaks absorb more heat than clean shingle surfaces. On a roof that faces south or west, that added heat absorption translates to higher attic temperatures and increased cooling costs during Conroe's long, hot summers — when August highs average 93 degrees and the air conditioning runs from April through October.

**Prevents secondary damage.** Moss and lichen growing between shingles can lift edges, allowing wind-driven rain to penetrate the underlayment. In an area that receives 47 inches of rain per year and faces tropical weather threats from June through November, keeping the roof sealed is not optional.

**Curb appeal and property value.** A roof covered in black streaks looks aged regardless of how new the shingles are. Cleaning restores the original color and signals to buyers and neighbors that the home is properly maintained.

## The Roof Soft Washing Process

### Surface Assessment

The contractor inspects the roof from the ground and, where safe access allows, from the roof surface. They identify the roofing material, note areas of heavy biological growth, check for damaged or missing shingles, and evaluate the gutter and flashing condition. Any pre-existing damage is documented before work begins.

### Pre-Treatment

The cleaning solution — typically a sodium hypochlorite blend calibrated for the roofing material — is applied across the entire roof surface using a low-pressure spray system. The solution dwells for 15 to 20 minutes, killing algae, moss, and mold at the cellular level. This chemical action is what distinguishes soft washing from simply rinsing the roof.

Landscaping below the roofline is rinsed before and during the application to protect plants from chemical runoff. Gutters and downspouts are checked to ensure the runoff is captured rather than flowing across hardscape or into garden beds.

### Washing and Rinse

After the dwell period, the roof is rinsed at low pressure from ridge to eave, following the lap direction of the shingles to avoid driving water underneath them. The dead biological material washes off with the rinse. Some residual staining may lighten over the following two to four weeks as rain continues to flush the treated surface.
