What does a **tub to shower conversion cost Conroe** homeowners pay? Most projects fall between $3,000 and $8,000, but that range covers a wide variety of finishes and configurations. This guide breaks down where the money goes, compares conversion costs to other bathroom projects, and explains what pushes a Conroe conversion toward the high or low end of the range.

*Last updated: November 2026. Based on Conroe-area contractor pricing. Costs vary by contractor and site conditions.*

## Overview

A tub-to-shower conversion removes the bathtub and installs a walk-in shower in the same footprint. The project involves plumbing changes (the tub drain and shower drain are in different positions), waterproofing, tile or panel installation, and a glass enclosure.

The cost range breaks down into three tiers:

- **Basic conversion ($3,000 to $4,500):** Standard ceramic or porcelain tile, a prefabricated shower pan, a standard curb, a framed glass door, and builder-grade fixtures. The plumbing modification is included. This tier gets the job done practically.
- **Mid-range conversion ($4,500 to $6,500):** Upgraded porcelain tile, a mortar-bed shower pan with a linear drain, a semi-frameless glass enclosure, name-brand showerhead and valve, a built-in niche, and a bench. This is what most Conroe homeowners choose.
- **Premium conversion ($6,500 to $8,000+):** Large-format or natural stone tile, a curbless zero-threshold design, a frameless glass panel or door, a rain showerhead with handheld, multiple niches, and a full-width tile bench. The curbless design requires precise slope engineering and adds to labor costs.

The plumbing modification is a fixed component in every tier, typically $500 to $1,500 of the total. The rest of the variation comes from tile, glass, and fixture choices.

## How They Compare

How does a tub-to-shower conversion stack up against other bathroom projects in Conroe?

**Tub-to-shower conversion vs. bathtub replacement:** Replacing the tub with a new tub of the same type runs $1,500 to $5,000 and does not require the plumbing rerouting that a conversion does. A conversion costs more ($3,000 to $8,000) because the drain moves and the walls get fully retiled. The trade-off is that you gain a shower designed for daily use.

**Tub-to-shower conversion vs. shower remodel:** A shower remodel ($2,000 to $7,000) updates an existing shower without changing the fixture type. A conversion is a more involved project because you are changing the fixture type entirely — removing a tub and creating a shower where one did not exist. The plumbing change is what drives the extra cost.

**Tub-to-shower conversion vs. full bathroom remodel:** A full remodel ($10,000 to $15,000+) includes the conversion as one component alongside new tile throughout, vanity replacement, toilet, lighting, and potentially layout changes. If the conversion is the only project, it stands alone at the lower cost. If you are remodeling the rest of the bathroom too, the conversion folds into the larger scope.

## Practical Takeaways

**What pushes costs down:** Keeping the curb (no curbless), choosing a prefabricated shower pan instead of a custom mortar bed, using stock porcelain tile, and selecting a framed or semi-frameless glass enclosure. A conversion in a standard 60-inch alcove with these choices typically falls in the $3,000 to $4,500 range.

**What pushes costs up:** Curbless design (requires precise floor slope work), large-format or natural stone tile, frameless glass, a linear drain instead of a standard center drain, and adding a built-in bench. Each of these is a reasonable upgrade, but they add up.

**Conroe-specific considerations:** Homes in the Conroe area are predominantly slab-on-grade construction. Relocating the drain means cutting through the concrete slab, rerouting the pipe, and patching — which costs more than drain work in homes with accessible crawlspaces. Conroe's hard water (8 grains per gallon on municipal supply, higher on well water) also means contractors recommend low-porosity tile and fixtures that resist mineral buildup, which may steer you toward porcelain over natural stone.

**Permit note:** Any tub-to-shower conversion in Conroe requires a building permit because the plumbing is modified. The contractor handles the permit application, rough plumbing inspection, shower pan flood test, and final inspection. Permit fees are typically a small fraction of the total cost.
