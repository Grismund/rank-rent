Montgomery County water tests at 8 grains per gallon on the municipal supply and significantly higher on well water. That mineral content leaves deposits on faucets, showerheads, and glass enclosures. Contractors working in this area specify fixtures and finishes that tolerate hard water from the start.

## Bathroom Remodel Services for Your Home

### Why Remodel Your Bathroom?

Three reasons drive most bathroom remodels in Conroe. First, function: a 20-year-old builder-grade bathroom with a fiberglass tub surround and a pedestal sink was fine in 2006, but it does not hold up to a family of four using it twice a day. Second, water damage: the combination of humidity, hard water, and Southeast Texas storms means grout, caulk, and subfloor materials fail faster here than in drier climates. Third, accessibility: aging in place is a real concern, and a curbless shower or grab-bar installation makes a bathroom usable for decades longer.

### Types of Bathroom Remodels

Bathroom remodeling covers a wide range of scope and cost:

- **Budget refresh** ($3,000 to $6,000): New vanity, faucet, toilet, and light fixtures. Existing layout stays the same.
- **Mid-range remodel** ($6,000 to $10,000): New tile, updated shower or tub, new vanity, and cosmetic upgrades throughout.
- **Full remodel** ($10,000 to $15,000+): Gut the room to the studs and rebuild with new plumbing, electrical, tile, fixtures, and layout changes.

## Remodeling Options Available in Conroe

Conroe Bath Remodeling connects you with contractors who handle five categories of bathroom work:

**[Full Bathroom Remodel](/full-bathroom-remodel/)** is the complete rebuild. Everything comes out, the layout changes if needed, and the room gets rebuilt with new plumbing, tile, fixtures, and finishes. This is the job for a master bath that needs to be rethought from the floor up.

**[Shower Remodel](/shower-remodel/)** covers shower replacement, retiling, glass enclosure upgrades, and converting an old fiberglass insert into a tiled walk-in. Shower work is the most common single project in a bathroom renovation.

**[Bathtub Replacement](/bathtub-replacement/)** means swapping an old tub for a new soaking tub, freestanding model, or walk-in tub. If the existing tub is cracked, stained beyond repair, or simply outdated, replacement is more practical than refinishing.

**[Tub-to-Shower Conversion](/tub-to-shower-conversion/)** removes the bathtub entirely and replaces it with a walk-in shower. It is the most common accessibility upgrade and one of the most requested projects in Conroe.

**[Small Bathroom Remodel](/small-bathroom-remodel/)** covers guest baths, half baths, and powder rooms where the goal is a meaningful update on a controlled budget.
