## What Is Full Bathroom Remodel?

A full remodel guts the existing bathroom completely. Walls come down to framing (or further, if water damage has reached the studs). The old tub, shower, toilet, vanity, tile, flooring, and light fixtures are all removed. Plumbing and electrical are updated, rerouted, or replaced as needed. The room is then waterproofed, tiled, finished, and inspected.

The scope typically includes: demolition, plumbing rough-in, electrical rough-in, moisture barrier and waterproofing membrane, backer board, tile (floor and walls), new tub or shower installation, vanity, toilet, faucets, lighting, ventilation fan, paint, and trim. In Conroe, any project that relocates plumbing or modifies electrical circuits requires a building permit from the City of Conroe building inspection office.

## The Full Bathroom Remodel Process

### Planning and Design

The contractor visits the bathroom, assesses the existing plumbing and electrical, checks for water damage behind walls and under flooring, and discusses your goals. Decisions include layout, tile material and pattern, fixture brands, vanity style, lighting, and whether to keep the tub or convert to a walk-in shower. The contractor then produces a scope of work, timeline, and price.

If the project requires a permit — and most full remodels in Conroe do — the contractor submits plans to the City of Conroe building department and obtains the permit before any demolition begins.

### Demolition and Rough-In

Demo takes one to two days for a standard bathroom. The crew removes all fixtures, tile, drywall, and flooring down to the framing. They inspect for water damage, mold, and subfloor integrity. In a humid climate like Conroe's, finding moisture damage behind a tub surround is common, not unusual.

Plumbing rough-in follows: new supply lines, drain relocations, valve placements. Electrical rough-in updates wiring, adds GFCI outlets (required by code in all bathrooms), and installs the ventilation fan. A rough inspection is required before closing the walls.

### Finishing and Inspection

Waterproofing membrane goes on the walls and shower pan area. Backer board, then tile. Fixtures, vanity, toilet, mirrors, and trim follow. The final inspection covers plumbing connections, electrical, ventilation, and waterproofing.

A full remodel in Conroe typically takes three to six weeks from demo to completion, depending on the scope, material lead times, and inspection scheduling.

## Choices for Your Full Bathroom Remodel

The finish level determines both the look and the cost:

- **Tile:** Ceramic is the budget option at $1 to $5 per square foot for material. Porcelain runs $3 to $10. Natural stone (marble, travertine) starts at $5 and goes higher. In a humid climate, porcelain is the practical choice — lower porosity means less moisture absorption.
- **Vanity:** Stock vanities from home improvement stores start around $200. Semi-custom runs $500 to $1,500. Custom-built or furniture-style vanities go higher. Double vanities are the standard request for master baths.
- **Shower:** Fiberglass inserts are the least expensive option. Tiled walk-in showers with glass enclosures are the most popular upgrade. Frameless glass adds cost but opens the room visually.
- **Tub:** Standard alcove tubs start around $200 for the fixture. Freestanding soaking tubs range from $500 to $3,000+. Walk-in tubs for accessibility run $2,000 to $5,000 for the fixture alone.
- **Fixtures:** Faucets, showerheads, and hardware. Budget sets start around $100 for the room. Mid-range runs $300 to $800. High-end fixtures from brands like Kohler and Delta go higher.

## Full Bathroom Remodel Cost in Conroe

A full bathroom remodel in the Conroe area typically falls between **$10,000 and $15,000** for a standard master bathroom. The final number depends on bathroom size, the extent of plumbing and electrical changes, tile and fixture selections, and whether the layout changes.
