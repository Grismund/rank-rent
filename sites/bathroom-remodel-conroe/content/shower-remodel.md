After 15 to 20 years, the signs are hard to miss: cracked grout, discolored caulk, water stains on adjacent walls, or a fiberglass pan that flexes underfoot.

## What Is Shower Remodel?

A shower remodel replaces or upgrades the shower enclosure, walls, floor, fixtures, and sometimes the plumbing behind it. The scope ranges from cosmetic (new tile over sound substrate, new fixtures) to structural (tear out everything to the framing, repair water damage, re-waterproof, and rebuild).

Common shower remodel projects in the Conroe area include:

- Replacing a prefabricated fiberglass insert with a custom-tiled walk-in shower
- Retiling an existing shower with updated porcelain or ceramic tile
- Adding a frameless or semi-frameless glass enclosure
- Installing a curbless (zero-threshold) shower for accessibility
- Upgrading the showerhead, valve, and controls to a thermostatic or multi-head system
- Rebuilding a leaking shower from the studs out

## The Shower Remodel Process

### Planning and Design

The contractor inspects the existing shower, checks for signs of water damage behind the walls, and discusses your goals. Key decisions include tile material and layout, shower size and threshold (curbed or curbless), glass enclosure type, showerhead configuration, and niche placement. If plumbing changes are involved — moving the drain, adding a second showerhead, changing valve locations — a permit is required in Conroe.

### Demolition and Rough-In

Demo removes the old tile, backer board, shower pan, and fixtures. The crew inspects the framing for water damage and rot. In Conroe's humidity, moisture that penetrated past failed grout or caulk can damage studs and subfloor over time. Any damaged framing gets replaced before the rebuild begins.

Plumbing rough-in repositions supply lines and valves as needed. If the drain location changes, the contractor cuts and re-plumbs it. A rough plumbing inspection is required before closing the walls.

### Finishing and Inspection

The waterproofing membrane goes on first — shower pan liner, wall membrane, and sealed seams. This is the most critical step. Backer board follows, then tile installation. The showerhead, valve trim, glass enclosure, and accessories are installed last. A shower pan flood test may be required during inspection to verify the waterproofing holds.

Most shower remodels in Conroe take one to three weeks depending on scope, tile complexity, and whether the glass enclosure is custom-ordered.

## Choices for Your Shower Remodel

- **Tile:** Large-format porcelain tile is the most popular choice for shower walls — fewer grout lines means less maintenance in hard water. Subway tile remains common. Mosaic tile works for accent bands and niches. Natural stone requires sealing and more upkeep.
- **Glass enclosure:** Frameless glass ($800 to $2,000+ installed) gives a clean, open look. Semi-frameless is a mid-range option. Framed enclosures are the most affordable. A simple glass panel with no door works for curbless designs.
- **Showerhead:** Single fixed head, handheld, rain head, or multi-head systems. Thermostatic valves hold temperature steady when someone runs water elsewhere in the house.
- **Niche:** A recessed shelf built into the shower wall for soap and shampoo. Easier to clean than a hanging caddy and built to last the life of the shower.
- **Bench:** A built-in tile bench adds function and an accessibility element.

## Shower Remodel Cost in Conroe

Shower remodels in the Conroe area typically range from **$2,000 to $7,000**. A simple retile of an existing shower with stock fixtures falls at the low end. A full tear-out and rebuild with custom tile, frameless glass, and upgraded plumbing lands at the high end.

The main cost drivers are tile material and quantity, glass enclosure type, the extent of plumbing changes, and whether water damage repair is needed behind the walls. Drain relocation on a slab-on-grade foundation adds significant cost.
