## Local Considerations

**Permits.** Re-sanding or sealing existing pavers generally does not require a building permit in unincorporated Lee County. Lehigh Acres uses Lee County's permitting system. Paver driveways, retaining walls over 4 feet, and projects affecting drainage or the right-of-way do require a permit. Work within Lee County right-of-way — such as driveway aprons connecting to public roads — requires a separate right-of-way permit.

**HOA requirements.** Many Lehigh Acres subdivisions have deed restrictions governing paver materials, colors, and patterns. HOA architectural review board approval may be required before making hardscape changes. Confirm with your HOA before scheduling installation or replacement work.

**Environmental regulations.** Lee County follows NPDES stormwater regulations for hardscape projects. The county enforces fertilizer and chemical use blackout periods from June 1 through September 30 near waterways. Sealant runoff near canals or retention ponds may trigger additional compliance requirements. Water-based, low-VOC sealers are preferred for applications near water features.

**Contractor licensing.** Florida Statute 489.103 exempts paver sealing from state contractor licensing when the project does not exceed $2,500 and no building permit is required. For larger projects, a state-licensed contractor is required.
