## Paver Sealing Questions Answered

### How often should pavers be sealed in Cape Coral?

Every 2 to 3 years for most properties. Waterfront homes along Cape Coral's 400-plus miles of canals may need resealing closer to every 2 years due to higher salt exposure. Inland properties with less direct salt contact can often go 3 years between applications. The sealer type matters too — natural/matte finishes last 3 to 5 years, wet-look 2 to 4 years, and high-gloss 2 to 3 years. The [resealing frequency guide](/guides/how-often-should-pavers-be-sealed/) covers timing in detail.

### What is the best sealer for Cape Coral pavers?

There is no single best sealer — the right choice depends on the paver material, the look you want, and the surface's exposure. For most Cape Coral driveways, a water-based wet-look sealer offers a good balance of color enhancement, protection, and durability. Travertine pool decks need a breathable, penetrating sealer that allows moisture to escape the stone. High-salt waterfront properties benefit from sealers specifically formulated for marine environments. Your contractor should recommend a product based on your specific conditions.

### How long does paver sealer take to dry?

Foot traffic is typically safe after 24 to 48 hours. Vehicles should stay off for 48 to 72 hours. Full cure takes about a week. Cape Coral's humidity and temperature affect those timelines — a sealer applied during the dry season (November through May) will cure faster and more reliably than one applied in the humid summer months. Your contractor will give specific timing based on the product and forecast.

### Can you seal pavers yourself?

You can purchase sealer at most home improvement stores and apply it yourself. Whether the result holds up is another question. Professional paver sealing involves more than rolling on product — the surface must be pressure-washed, old sealer stripped if present, joints cleaned and re-sanded with polymeric sand, and sealer applied in thin, even coats at the right temperature and humidity. Skipping the prep work or applying too much sealer causes hazing, peeling, and white blotching that is expensive to fix. Most contractors see a steady stream of DIY correction work.

### Does paver sealing prevent weeds?

It significantly reduces weed growth. Joint-stabilizing sealers harden the polymeric sand between pavers, leaving no loose material for seeds to root into. Some weeds can still find their way through cracks or edges, but sealed surfaces see dramatically less growth than unsealed ones. For Cape Coral properties where tropical climate drives aggressive weed and ant colonization of paver joints, sealing is the most effective preventive measure.

### What is the difference between wet-look and natural sealer?

Natural (matte) sealer protects without changing the paver's appearance — no added color or sheen. Wet-look sealer enhances the color as if the surface is damp, adding depth and richness. Both protect against UV, salt, and moisture. Wet-look is the more popular choice in Cape Coral because it revives faded color and gives a finished appearance. Natural is preferred when homeowners want invisible protection. The [wet-look vs. natural guide](/guides/wet-look-vs-natural-paver-sealer/) compares them side by side.

### Do pavers need to be cleaned before sealing?

Yes, always. Sealer bonds to the paver surface, and anything between the sealer and the paver — dirt, mold, algae, efflorescence, old sealer residue — prevents proper adhesion. Professional cleaning with a commercial pressure washer is a required step before any sealing job. The [combined cleaning and sealing](/paver-cleaning-and-sealing/) service handles both in one visit.

### How much does paver sealing cost in Cape Coral?

Most residential jobs fall between $400 and $1,200. A small patio or walkway runs $200 to $500. A standard driveway is $500 to $900. A full property with driveway, patio, and pool deck runs $800 to $1,400. Surface condition, sealer type, and paver material all affect the price.
