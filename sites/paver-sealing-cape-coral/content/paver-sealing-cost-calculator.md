This **paver sealing cost calculator Cape Coral** homeowners use gives you a price range for your sealing project before you talk to anyone. Pick your surface size, the sealer finish you prefer, and the condition your pavers are in. The estimate appears immediately.

## How This Calculator Works

Three inputs:

1. **Surface area.** Small patio or walkway is roughly 100 to 200 square feet. A standard driveway is 400 to 600 square feet. Full property (driveway, patio, and pool deck combined) runs 800 to 1,200 square feet.
2. **Sealer finish.** Natural/matte, wet-look, or high-gloss. Each carries different material costs that affect the total.
3. **Paver condition.** Clean and previously sealed, moderate buildup needing standard cleaning, or heavy staining, algae, or old sealer needing extra preparation. More prep work means a higher number.

From those three answers you get a low and a high. The range covers cleaning, joint sand replenishment, and two coats of sealer.

## How We Calculate These Ranges

The numbers behind this calculator reflect what Cape Coral contractors are quoting today. Natural/matte sealers install for roughly $1 to $2 per square foot. Wet-look runs $1.50 to $3. High-gloss ranges from $2 to $4. These per-foot rates include standard cleaning and joint sand work.

A typical driveway lands between $500 and $900, consistent with what local contractors quote. Full property jobs run $800 to $1,400. Those figures assume standard conditions: moderate cleaning needs, joints that need re-sanding, and two coats of sealer.

What the calculator cannot account for is your specific surface. Pavers with deep mold growth, significant efflorescence, failed old sealer, or settled sections that need [repair](/paver-repair/) will come in above the range it shows. Treat the result as a budget number, not a bid. The [paver sealing cost guide](/cost/) explains each pricing factor in detail.

## Cost Estimator FAQ

### How accurate is this paver sealing cost calculator?

Accurate enough to budget with, not accurate enough to sign on. It uses typical Cape Coral pricing and assumes pavers in reasonable condition. The contractor's on-site estimate will account for the actual state of your surface and any extra preparation needed.

### Does the estimate include cleaning?

Yes. Paver sealing requires a clean surface for proper adhesion. The calculator assumes standard pressure washing and joint cleaning as part of the project. If the pavers need unusually heavy cleaning — thick algae, deep mold, or old sealer removal — the actual cost will be higher. The [combined cleaning and sealing](/paver-cleaning-and-sealing/) page covers what that work involves.

### What is the difference between natural, wet-look, and high-gloss sealers?

Natural (matte) preserves the paver's original look without adding color or sheen — the least expensive option at $1 to $2 per square foot. Wet-look enhances color as if the surface is damp, the most popular choice in Cape Coral at $1.50 to $3 per square foot. High-gloss adds a reflective shine and makes colors pop — the premium option at $2 to $4, best suited for pool decks and patios rather than driveways. The [wet-look vs. natural guide](/guides/wet-look-vs-natural-paver-sealer/) compares the two most common finishes.

### How often will I need to reseal?

Most Cape Coral properties reseal every 2 to 3 years. The finish type affects timing: natural/matte lasts 3 to 5 years, wet-look 2 to 4 years, high-gloss 2 to 3 years. Waterfront properties with heavy salt exposure tend toward the shorter end. The [resealing frequency guide](/guides/how-often-should-pavers-be-sealed/) covers the details.
