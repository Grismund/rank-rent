## Understanding Paver Cleaning

Professional paver cleaning goes beyond what a garden hose can do. Contractors use commercial-grade pressure washers calibrated to the paver material — higher pressure for concrete pavers, lower for softer travertine and natural stone — combined with surface cleaners that deliver even coverage without leaving stripe marks. For stubborn stains like rust, oil, or deep mold, targeted cleaning solutions are applied before the pressure wash.

The goal is a surface that looks close to the day it was installed: original color visible, joints clear of organic growth, and no residue that would interfere with a fresh coat of sealer.

## Benefits of Paver Cleaning

**Restores original appearance.** Cape Coral's UV index reaches "Extreme" levels from April through October, and salt deposits from Gulf breezes dull paver color over time. Pressure washing removes the surface layer of grime and reveals the color underneath.

**Removes biological growth.** Mold, algae, and moss thrive in Cape Coral's humidity, especially on shaded pool decks and north-facing walkways. Beyond looking bad, biological growth creates slippery surfaces that are a safety concern around pool areas.

**Prevents long-term damage.** Organic matter trapped in joints holds moisture against the paver, accelerating erosion and spalling. Regular cleaning interrupts that cycle before damage becomes permanent.

**Prepares for sealing.** Sealer will not bond properly to a dirty or algae-covered surface. Professional cleaning is the required first step before any [paver sealing](/paver-sealing/) or [combined cleaning and sealing](/paver-cleaning-and-sealing/) project.

**Addresses efflorescence.** White mineral deposits that form when moisture carries salts to the paver surface are common in Cape Coral due to the high water table and sandy limestone substrate. Pressure washing combined with an efflorescence cleaner removes the deposits and gives the pavers a clean slate.

## How Paver Cleaning Is Done

### Surface Preparation

The crew inspects the paver surface and identifies problem areas — heavy mold, oil stains, rust, loose pavers, or significant efflorescence. Furniture, planters, and vehicles are moved off the surface. Adjacent landscaping and walls are protected from overspray.

### Pressure Washing

A commercial surface cleaner is used for the main wash. This tool maintains consistent distance from the pavers and distributes pressure evenly, avoiding the etching marks that a hand-held wand can leave on softer materials like travertine.

For concrete pavers, pressure typically runs 2,500 to 3,000 PSI. Travertine and natural stone get a gentler treatment at 1,200 to 1,800 PSI to avoid surface damage. Oil stains, rust, and deep mold may need a pre-treatment with a degreaser or specialized cleaner before the pressure wash.

The runoff is managed to avoid sending cleaning chemicals into storm drains or the canal system. Lee County's NPDES stormwater regulations prohibit illicit discharge into the municipal storm sewer system, and responsible contractors plan for containment.

### Post-Cleaning Assessment

Once the surface dries, the contractor assesses whether additional treatment is needed — a second pass on stubborn areas, or paver [repair](/paver-repair/) before proceeding to sealing. If the cleaning is a standalone service, the contractor may recommend a sealing timeline based on the paver condition they observed.
