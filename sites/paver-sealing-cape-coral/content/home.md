## Professional Paver Sealing Services

Paver sealing applies a protective coating over the surface and into the joints of interlocking brick, concrete, or natural stone pavers. The sealer bonds to the material, locking in color, stabilizing joint sand, and creating a barrier against moisture, salt, UV radiation, and biological growth. It does not change what the pavers are — it protects what they already look like.

The contractors in our network handle the entire project, from the first on-site inspection through the final seal coat. They bring their own pressure washing equipment, joint sand, sealant, and schedule. You are not calling a national franchise that sends whoever is available that week.

### Why Seal Your Pavers?

Cape Coral's combination of salt air, tropical rain, and intense UV creates conditions that degrade unsealed pavers faster than virtually any other region in the continental United States. Here is what sealing addresses:

- **Color fading.** UV index readings of 10 to 12 from March through October break down the pigments that give pavers their color. Sealer blocks UV penetration and preserves the original appearance.
- **Efflorescence.** White powdery deposits form when salt and minerals are carried to the surface by moisture wicking up through the sandy substrate. Sealer reduces moisture penetration and limits the cycle.
- **Joint sand erosion.** Heavy summer rains wash sand out of paver joints, allowing shifting, weed growth, and ant colonies. A joint-stabilizing sealer locks the sand in place.
- **Weed and moss growth.** Open joints and porous surfaces collect organic matter. Sealed joints leave nothing for weeds to root into.
- **Salt damage.** Airborne salt from the Gulf settles on surfaces, absorbs humidity, and forms a brine that corrodes paver material from the inside out. A sealed surface prevents that penetration.

Most Cape Coral properties need sealing every 2 to 3 years. Waterfront properties with higher salt exposure benefit from the shorter end of that cycle.

### Types of Pavers We Service

Contractors in our network seal all common paver materials found in Lee County:

- **Concrete pavers** — the most popular choice for Cape Coral driveways, available in a wide range of colors and patterns
- **Travertine** — the top choice for pool decks because it stays cool underfoot and offers natural slip resistance
- **Clay brick** — traditional driveways and walkways that can last 50 to 100 years with proper sealing and maintenance
- **Shellstone and natural stone** — used in coastal properties for their organic appearance

Each material responds differently to sealer chemistry. A qualified contractor will recommend the right product for the paver type and the exposure conditions on your property.

Once you choose a contractor and agree on a price, they schedule the work around Cape Coral's weather. The crew pressure-washes the entire surface, removes weeds and debris from joints, re-sands with polymeric sand, and applies the sealer. Most residential jobs take a day of active work. Then you wait 24 to 48 hours for the sealer to cure before foot traffic, and 48 to 72 hours before vehicles. Your contractor will give you specific timing based on the product used and the week's conditions.
