## Window Tint Regulations in Texas

Texas Transportation Code Section 547.613 requires front side windows to allow at least 25 percent VLT (visible light transmission). Rear and back side windows have no minimum VLT restriction when the vehicle has an outside mirror on each side. The windshield allows tint of 25 percent VLT or more above the manufacturer's AS-1 line (top 5 inches). Red, blue, and amber tint colors are prohibited. A compliance sticker must be placed between the film and glass on the driver's side window. Medical exemptions are available under Section 547.613.
