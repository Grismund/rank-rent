## How to Use This Tool

Select your answers from three drop-downs:

1. **Vehicle size.** Compact or sedan, midsize SUV or crossover, or full-size truck or SUV. Glass area drives the base price, and vehicle size is the simplest proxy for it.
2. **Film grade.** Economy dyed, carbon infrared, or ceramic. Material cost and performance scale with the grade.
3. **Existing tint removal.** Toggle this if your windows already have aftermarket film that needs stripping. Factory-tinted glass does not count — that is built into the glass itself and stays in place.

The calculator returns a low-to-high range covering material and labor for a standard Conroe-area installation.

## Where Our Estimates Come From

Pricing is built from what Montgomery County tint shops are charging in the current market. The underlying rates:

- Economy dyed film runs $50 to $150 for a full vehicle, with sedans at the low end and trucks at the high end.
- Carbon infrared film runs $150 to $250. This is the most popular grade for daily-driven pickups and SUVs along the I-45 corridor — heat rejection is meaningful without the premium cost.
- Ceramic film runs $200 to $400 on most vehicles and $300 to $600 on full-size trucks and SUVs with extended glass.

The calculator assumes a vehicle in normal condition. What it cannot predict: unusual glass shapes (like the rear slider on certain truck models), aftermarket modifications, or existing film that is especially difficult to remove. For those situations, the installer's on-site assessment replaces the estimate.

## Common Questions About Tinting Costs

### How close is this calculator to a real quote?

Close enough to plan your budget. The ranges track what local shops are charging, so you will not be blindsided by the actual number. But a calculator cannot inspect your glass, assess old film adhesion, or factor in vehicle-specific quirks. The installer quote is the final number.

### What happens if I already have tint on the car?

Select "yes" for existing tint removal. Stripping old film adds $100 to $200 to the job depending on how many windows are covered and how well the old adhesive releases. Film that has baked under East Texas sun for several years tends to leave more residue, increasing the removal time. Factory privacy glass on the rear — the dark-tinted glass that comes from the manufacturer — does not need removal and should not be selected.

### Which film grade should I pick for Conroe weather?

Conroe gets roughly 2,889 hours of sunshine annually, with a UV index peaking at Extreme levels in late spring. Humidity sits between 73 and 77 percent year-round. In that environment, ceramic provides the strongest combination of heat rejection, UV blocking, and durability. Carbon is the practical middle choice for drivers who want real heat reduction at a lower price point. Dyed film works for budget-conscious owners who plan to sell or trade within a few years. The [film comparison guide](/guides/ceramic-vs-carbon-vs-dyed-tint/) has the full breakdown.

### How long until the tint is fully cured?

Most films cure in three to five days. During that period you may see small water pockets or slight haziness between the film and glass — both are normal and disappear as the moisture evaporates through the film. Conroe's humidity slows that evaporation compared to arid climates, so allow the full cure window before judging clarity. Keep windows up during curing.
