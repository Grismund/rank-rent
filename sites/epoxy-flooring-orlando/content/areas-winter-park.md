### Climate and Concrete Conditions

Winter Park shares Central Florida's humid subtropical climate, but the numbers make the challenge concrete-specific. Average annual humidity runs 72 percent — well above the 40 to 60 percent range that epoxy requires for proper chemical bonding during application. September is the most humid month at around 70 percent average relative humidity, and during summer mornings, readings frequently exceed 80 percent.

Approximately 90 percent of single-family homes built in Florida since 1970 sit on slab-on-grade foundations, and Winter Park is no exception. The water table beneath Orange County commonly sits just 2 to 4 feet below the surface. After heavy rain, sub-slab relative humidity can approach saturation, driving moisture vapor upward through the concrete. Sandy soils in the area do not retain moisture evenly, which leads to differential settlement and hairline cracks in garage slabs over time.

That combination of persistent humidity, a shallow water table, and aging concrete means that bare slabs in Winter Park absorb moisture from below and from the air simultaneously. An epoxy coating with a moisture-mitigating primer addresses both vectors.

## Local Considerations

**Permits:** Cosmetic epoxy coating over an existing concrete slab generally does not require a building permit in Orange County or the City of Winter Park. Florida HB 735, effective July 1, 2025, eliminated local occupational licensing for flooring trades, so municipalities can no longer require a separate local license for this type of work. Under Florida Statute 489.103, any construction contract exceeding $2,500 or requiring a building permit must still be performed by a state-licensed contractor. A local business tax receipt is required to operate within Winter Park.

**Humidity and scheduling:** With an annual average humidity of 72 percent, Winter Park sits outside the ideal epoxy application window for most of the year. At 70 percent relative humidity, epoxy tensile strength drops by roughly 25 percent per ASTM D638 testing. When humidity exceeds 85 percent, standard epoxies develop amine blush — a waxy film that compromises adhesion and appearance. November through January provides the most stable installation conditions in Central Florida, with humidity levels consistently below 70 percent. Portable dehumidifiers can extend that window for garage spaces.

**VOC regulations:** The EPA sets a federal VOC limit for floor coatings at 400 grams per liter under 40 CFR Part 59 Subpart D, which applies throughout Florida. Florida DEP requires surface coating operations to use coatings containing 5.0 percent or less VOC by volume, or limit total high-VOC coating use to 6.0 gallons per day averaged monthly.
