## Local Considerations

**Permits:** Osceola County does not require building permits for minor finish work such as cosmetic epoxy coatings over existing concrete slabs. Florida HB 735, effective July 1, 2025, eliminated local occupational licensing for flooring trades across the state. Contractors must hold the appropriate Florida license through DBPR for work exceeding $2,500 or requiring a building permit, and must register that license with Osceola County.
