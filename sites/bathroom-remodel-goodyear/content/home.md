### Why Remodel Your Bathroom?

Hard water is the number-one driver of bathroom wear across the West Valley. At 13 to 22 GPG, Goodyear-area water is roughly three times the national average. That mineral content coats fixtures, clouds glass, and corrodes internal valve components over time. Homes built in the early 2000s are now showing the cumulative damage.

Beyond hard water, Arizona's dry desert air and 300-plus sunny days per year create temperature swings that stress grout and caulk joints. The combination of mineral deposits and thermal cycling leads to cracked grout lines, failed caulk seals, and mold-prone gaps that go unnoticed until water damage appears behind the wall.

A remodel also adds real value to your home. Bathroom upgrades consistently rank among the top renovation projects for return on investment, particularly in a housing market growing as fast as the West Valley.

### Types of Bathroom Remodels

Bathroom remodels fall into three general tiers based on scope and budget:

- **Budget refresh** ($3,000 to $6,000) — New fixtures, fresh paint, updated lighting, and hardware replacement. The layout stays the same, and no walls come down.
- **Mid-range remodel** ($6,000 to $10,000) — New tile, vanity, countertop, and fixtures with possible plumbing updates. Keeps the existing footprint but refreshes every visible surface.
- **Full gut-and-rebuild** ($10,000 to $15,000) — Tear everything out down to the studs. New plumbing, new electrical, new layout if desired. This is the option when the bathroom needs a complete transformation.
