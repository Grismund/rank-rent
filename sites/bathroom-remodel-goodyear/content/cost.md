## Average Bathroom Remodel Prices

Bathroom remodel pricing in the Goodyear area falls into three broad tiers based on how much of the room gets touched.

### Budget Bathroom Remodel

**$3,000 to $6,000** — This covers a fixture and finish swap. The layout stays the same, the walls stay up, and the plumbing stays in place. Typical work includes new tile flooring, a replacement vanity with countertop, updated faucet and showerhead, fresh paint, and new lighting and hardware.

A budget remodel is ideal for guest bathrooms, hall baths, and powder rooms where the underlying structure remains solid but the surfaces have deteriorated from years of hard-water exposure. See our [small bathroom remodel](/small-bathroom-remodel/) page for details on this scope of work.

### Mid-Range Bathroom Remodel

**$6,000 to $10,000** — A layout-preserving remodel that replaces every visible surface and may include some plumbing updates. The existing footprint does not change, but the tile, vanity, countertop, toilet, fixtures, and shower or tub surround all get replaced. The contractor may update supply lines or valve assemblies while the walls are open.

This is the most common tier for Goodyear-area master bathrooms where homeowners want a noticeable upgrade without the complexity of reconfiguring the room.

### High-End Bathroom Remodel

**$10,000 to $15,000** — A full tear-out and rebuild. Everything comes out down to the studs. New plumbing, new electrical, new waterproofing, and potentially a new layout. This tier is appropriate when the bathroom has structural problems, outdated wiring, or a configuration that no longer serves the household.

## Factors That Influence Bathroom Remodel Cost

Several variables push your project toward the lower or upper end of its tier:

- **Tile selection** — Ceramic tile runs $2 to $8 per square foot for materials. Porcelain costs $3 to $12. Natural stone can reach $20 or more. Tile is typically the largest material line item in a bathroom remodel.
- **Vanity and countertop** — A stock vanity with a laminate top costs a few hundred dollars. A custom vanity with a quartz countertop can exceed $2,000.
- **Plumbing changes** — Keeping drains and supply lines in place saves money. Moving a drain, adding a new fixture location, or upgrading pipes adds $500 to $2,000 depending on complexity.
- **Permit and inspection fees** — Ranging from $100 to $500 in West Valley municipalities. Required any time the work involves plumbing, electrical, or structural changes.
- **Contractor licensing** — Arizona law requires ROC-licensed contractors for projects over $1,000 in labor and materials. Working with licensed contractors ensures code compliance and protects your warranty.

Hard water is an indirect cost factor specific to the Goodyear area. Homeowners who do not address their water quality during the remodel will see mineral scale damage return on new fixtures. Some contractors recommend pairing a remodel with a whole-house water softener to protect the investment.

## Bathroom Remodel Cost by Category

Here is how a typical mid-range bathroom remodel budget breaks down in the Goodyear area:

- **Labor** — 40% to 50% of the total project cost. Covers demolition, rough plumbing, electrical, tile installation, fixture mounting, and finishing.
- **Tile and surfaces** — 20% to 25%. Floor tile, wall tile, shower surround, and countertop materials.
- **Fixtures and hardware** — 10% to 15%. Faucet, showerhead, toilet, lighting, mirror, towel bars, and cabinet hardware.
- **Vanity and storage** — 10% to 15%. Vanity cabinet, countertop, and any built-in storage like medicine cabinets or shower niches.
- **Permits and overhead** — 5% to 10%. Building permit fees, dumpster rental, material delivery, and cleanup.
