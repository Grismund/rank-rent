### Climate and Concrete Conditions

The limestone's high permeability means groundwater moves freely upward through capillary action into every slab poured on grade. Many older Hialeah homes from the 1950s through 1970s building boom were built over vapor barriers that have since aged and degraded, allowing even more moisture to wick through the concrete.

Florida slabs release 30 percent more moisture vapor during summer months, and the standard epoxy application maximum is 3 pounds per 1,000 square feet per 24 hours. Hialeah slabs regularly exceed that number. The high water table washes fine material out from under the concrete over time, contributing to settlement cracks that compound the moisture problem by creating direct pathways for vapor.

**Permits:** Residential interior floor coating is permit-exempt in Miami-Dade for single-family homes.
