## Epoxy Flooring Questions Answered

**How long does epoxy flooring last in Miami?**

A professionally installed epoxy system lasts 10 to 20 years in a residential garage under normal use. Polyaspartic coatings fall in the 15- to 20-year range. DIY paint kits from hardware stores typically fail within 2 to 5 years, especially in South Florida where moisture vapor pressure through the slab accelerates delamination. Lifespan depends on proper surface preparation, moisture mitigation, and the coating system selected. The [durability guide](/guides/how-long-does-an-epoxy-floor-last/) covers this in detail.

**Does epoxy flooring work with Miami's high humidity?**

It does when installed correctly. Miami averages 73 percent humidity year-round, which exceeds the ideal 40 to 60 percent range for epoxy application. At 70 percent relative humidity, epoxy tensile strength drops by 25 percent. Professional installers compensate by scheduling applications during the dry season (October through April), using dehumidification equipment, and applying moisture-mitigating primers on the slab. The chemistry performs well once cured — the humidity challenge is during installation, not after.

**Is moisture testing required before coating a garage floor?**

Miami-Dade County requires ASTM F2170 in-situ relative humidity testing for every industrial floor coating installation. For residential garages, it is not legally mandated but is strongly recommended by experienced contractors because the Biscayne Aquifer sits close beneath every slab in this area. Skipping the test risks applying epoxy over concrete that is too wet, which causes blistering and peeling — often within the first rainy season. Any contractor who does not mention moisture testing on a Miami slab should raise a concern.

**What is the difference between epoxy and polyaspartic coatings?**

Both are two-part catalyzed resin systems applied over prepared concrete. Epoxy is less expensive and has a longer pot life, giving the installer more working time. Polyaspartic costs 20 to 30 percent more but cures in hours instead of days, resists UV yellowing from sunlight, and returns the floor to vehicle traffic within about 24 hours. Many Miami contractors install a hybrid system — epoxy base for adhesion, polyaspartic clear for speed and UV stability. The [comparison guide](/guides/epoxy-vs-polyaspartic-garage-floors/) breaks down the trade-offs.

**How long before I can park on a new epoxy floor?**

Standard epoxy systems require 48 to 72 hours for foot traffic and five to seven days before parking a vehicle. Polyaspartic topcoats cut those timelines to roughly 12 hours for foot traffic and 24 hours for vehicle use. Miami's heat accelerates the chemical reaction but high humidity slows solvent evaporation, so the installer sets the return-to-service schedule based on the specific product and that week's weather conditions.

**Do I need a permit for epoxy flooring in Miami-Dade?**

Residential interior floor coating on single-family homes and duplexes is permit-exempt in Miami-Dade County. All commercial properties and condominium units require a flooring permit, including a Flooring Permit Affidavit, floor plans, and soundproofing documentation. Contractors must carry a Florida DBPR Certified license or a Miami-Dade Certificate of Competency.

**Can epoxy be applied over old paint or a previous coating?**

Not without removing the old material first. Epoxy bonds to concrete, not to other coatings. Diamond grinding or shot-blasting strips the existing surface and exposes fresh concrete for the new system to bond into. Applying epoxy over paint or sealant leads to delamination because the bond is only as strong as the weakest layer underneath.

**How do I maintain an epoxy floor?**

Sweep regularly and mop with a mild soap and water solution once a month. Avoid harsh acid-based cleaners that can dull the topcoat over time. For garage floors, rinse away salt residue tracked in from coastal roads — the coating resists salt corrosion, but accumulated deposits can become visible if left for extended periods. That is the full maintenance routine.

**Is epoxy flooring slippery when wet?**

A smooth, high-gloss epoxy surface can be slick when wet. Flake broadcast and quartz aggregate finishes add texture that significantly improves wet traction. For garages, workshops, and commercial spaces where water exposure is regular, contractors recommend a textured finish. Anti-slip aggregate can also be added to the clear topcoat on any system.
