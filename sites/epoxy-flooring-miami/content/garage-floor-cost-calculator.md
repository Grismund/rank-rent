## How We Calculate These Ranges

The price ranges in this tool are built from two data points: the per-square-foot installed cost by coating type and the typical square footage for each garage configuration in the Miami metro.

Standard epoxy systems (solid color, flake, quartz) run $3 to $7 per square foot installed. Metallic systems run $5 to $12. Polyaspartic full systems fall between $4 and $10 per square foot.

Miami pricing skews slightly higher than national averages because moisture mitigation is factored into nearly every quote. The Biscayne Aquifer sits close beneath every slab in this market, and a vapor-mitigating primer — typically $1 to $2 per square foot — is included in the professional estimate rather than treated as an unexpected add-on.

Garage sizes used in the calculator:

- **One-car:** approximately 250 square feet
- **Two-car:** approximately 450 square feet
- **Three-car or oversized:** approximately 700 square feet

The ranges do not include major structural repair, full slab replacement, or removal of thick industrial coatings — those are priced separately after inspection.

## Cost Estimator FAQ

**Why does the range seem wide?**

A floor with minimal cracking, no previous coating, and normal moisture readings sits near the low end. A floor with heavy cracking, an old coating that needs removal, or elevated moisture readings that require a mitigation primer lands near the high end. Finish selection also matters — metallic costs roughly double what solid color costs per square foot.

**Does this include moisture mitigation?**

The ranges assume a standard moisture-mitigating primer is included, which is standard practice in Miami. If your slab shows unusually high vapor transmission (above 10 pounds per 1,000 square feet per 24 hours), a heavier mitigation system may be needed at additional cost.

**Is polyaspartic more expensive than epoxy?**

Typically 20 to 30 percent more. On a two-car garage that means roughly $400 to $800 above the epoxy price. The trade-off is a faster return to service (about 24 hours versus five to seven days) and better UV resistance.
