## What Drives Epoxy Demand in Miami Beach

### Climate and Concrete Conditions

Miami Beach's water table can sit as shallow as 12 inches below grade during the wet season. That proximity drives moisture vapor through every slab on the island at rates averaging 4 to 8 pounds per 1,000 square feet per 24 hours — well above the 3-pound limit where standard epoxy adhesion begins to fail. Approximately 60 percent of premature flooring failures in Florida trace to humidity-related problems during installation.

Humidity on the island averages 71 percent year-round, peaking at 78 percent in September. At 70 percent relative humidity, epoxy tensile strength drops by 25 percent. This is why experienced installers on Miami Beach use climate-controlled tents maintaining 45 to 55 percent humidity during application, and schedule work during the October-through-April dry season whenever possible.

Salt compounds the moisture problem. The island is surrounded by water — Atlantic Ocean to the east, Biscayne Bay to the west. Salt-laden air does not just sit on the surface of concrete; chloride ions penetrate it and attack the embedded rebar. The resulting rust expands to two to six times the volume of the original steel, cracking slabs from within. A sealed epoxy surface blocks that chloride intrusion path.

## Local Considerations

**Permits:** Residential interior floor coating on single-family homes is permit-exempt in Miami-Dade. All commercial properties and condominiums require a flooring permit through the Miami Beach Building Department. Contractors must hold a Florida DBPR Certified license or a Miami-Dade Certificate of Competency.

**Hurricane zone:** Miami-Dade County falls within the High Velocity Hurricane Zone with 175 mph design wind speeds. Building products must carry a Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance. This applies to epoxy systems used in permitted commercial and condo installations.

**Optimal timing:** The best installation window on Miami Beach is October through April, when humidity drops to 67 to 74 percent and temperatures remain mild. Summer installations require dehumidification equipment and careful scheduling around afternoon rain cycles.
