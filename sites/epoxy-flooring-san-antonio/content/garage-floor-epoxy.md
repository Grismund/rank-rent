## About Garage Floor Epoxy

Garage floor epoxy is a two-part resin system that chemically bonds to prepared concrete. Unlike paint, which sits on the surface and chips under pressure, epoxy penetrates the pores of the slab and cures into a rigid, high-gloss finish. The result is a floor that resists tire marks, chemical spills, hot-tire pickup and abrasion far longer than any topical coating.

San Antonio garages face specific demands. Houston Black clay beneath the foundation moves the slab seasonally, so crack repair must happen before anything goes down. Summer slab temperatures climb past 100 degrees by mid-morning, which affects how quickly the resin kicks off. A contractor who understands Bexar County concrete adjusts their product selection and scheduling to work with these conditions rather than against them.

## Advantages of Garage Floor Epoxy

A coated garage floor does several things bare concrete cannot. It blocks moisture from wicking through the slab during heavy spring rains when the clay beneath the house swells and forces water upward. It resists staining from motor oil, brake fluid and lawn-chemical runoff. And it cleans with a mop and water instead of a pressure washer.

For the military families stationed near Randolph, Lackland or Fort Sam Houston, an epoxy floor simplifies the PCS cycle. Garages that serve as staging areas for household goods stay cleaner and suffer less damage between moves. When it is time to sell, a finished garage floor signals a well-maintained property to buyers walking through.

## The Garage Floor Epoxy Process

### Concrete Preparation

Every job starts with slab inspection. The contractor checks for cracks, spalling, previous coatings, moisture stains and signs of slab movement. ASTM F1869 calcium chloride tests and ASTM F2170 relative humidity probes measure moisture vapor transmission through the concrete. If readings exceed safe levels, a moisture mitigation primer goes down first.

The slab is then diamond-ground or shot-blasted to remove the top layer and open the pore structure of the concrete. This mechanical profiling is what creates the bond. Acid etching alone does not cut it on San Antonio slabs, which often carry years of silicone sealers, tire dressing and embedded grime.

### Epoxy Application

After prep, the crew fills cracks with flexible repair material and applies a primer coat. The base coat of epoxy goes down next, usually at 8 to 12 mils wet thickness. If you chose a flake finish, vinyl color chips are broadcast into the wet epoxy at this stage. For a solid color, the base coat is the finished look beneath the clear seal.

### Curing and Final Coat

A clear topcoat seals everything in. Standard epoxy topcoats need 24 to 72 hours before foot traffic and roughly a week before you drive on them. Polyaspartic topcoats cut that timeline to as little as four hours, a meaningful difference when San Antonio humidity can climb past 70 percent and slow traditional cure times even further.
