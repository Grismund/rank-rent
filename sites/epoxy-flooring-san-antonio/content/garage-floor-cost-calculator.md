Use this garage floor cost calculator San Antonio homeowners trust to get a quick pricing estimate for epoxy coating. Select your garage size and preferred finish type, and the tool generates an installed cost range based on current Bexar County contractor pricing. No personal information required.

## How to Use This Tool

Pick the option that matches your garage from the stall-count options above: single-stall (roughly 250 square feet), double-stall (roughly 450 square feet) or triple-stall (roughly 700 square feet). Then choose a finish, solid color, vinyl flake blend, metallic epoxy or polyaspartic topcoat system. The tool produces a low-to-high range that reflects what San Antonio contractors typically charge for that combination.

If your garage does not match one of the preset sizes, use the per-square-foot rates from the [cost guide](/cost/) to calculate manually. Multiply your floor area by the low and high per-square-foot rate for your chosen finish to get a range.

## Where Our Estimates Come From

The numbers behind this calculator are drawn from the niche brief for the San Antonio epoxy flooring market. Installed pricing data reflects per-square-foot rates collected from contractor quotes and industry benchmarks across the Bexar County metro. We update the ranges periodically to track market movement.

These are not binding quotes. Actual pricing depends on your slab's condition, whether moisture mitigation is needed, the number of coats in the system and the specific products your contractor uses. A slab with heavy cracking or a previous coating that must be stripped will cost more to prepare than a clean, newer slab in a subdivision like Stone Oak or [Converse](/areas/converse/).
