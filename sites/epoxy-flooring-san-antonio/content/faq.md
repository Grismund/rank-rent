## Common Epoxy Flooring Questions

### How long does epoxy flooring last in San Antonio?

A professionally installed epoxy system lasts 10 to 20 years in a residential garage, depending on traffic volume and maintenance. Polyaspartic coatings tend toward the upper end of that range, often 15 to 20 years, because they resist UV degradation and chemical exposure slightly better than standard epoxy. The [lifespan guide](/guides/how-long-does-an-epoxy-floor-last/) goes deeper on what affects durability in this climate.

DIY paint kits from the hardware store typically last 2 to 5 years before they peel, chip or wear through. The difference comes down to surface preparation and product thickness. A professional crew grinds the slab, tests moisture, applies primer and builds the coating system to 15 mils or more. A paint kit goes on with a roller at 3 to 5 mils over an acid-etched surface and does not have the same bond strength.

### Do I need a permit for epoxy flooring in San Antonio?

No. Coating an existing concrete floor with epoxy is considered cosmetic work in San Antonio, [Converse](/areas/converse/), [Live Oak](/areas/live-oak/), [Helotes](/areas/helotes/) and [Schertz](/areas/schertz/). No building permit is required. Texas does not have a state-level license for epoxy floor installers either. If a project involves structural changes to the slab, a permit may apply, but a standard garage floor coating does not trigger that threshold.

### What is the best time of year to install epoxy in San Antonio?

The fall-through-spring window, roughly October to April, gives you lower humidity, cooler slab temperatures and more consistent curing conditions. San Antonio's humidity averages 67 percent annually and peaks above 70 percent in May, which can cause amine blush and slow curing on standard epoxy. Summer installations are common, but most contractors recommend polyaspartic systems or run dehumidifiers to control the environment.

### How much does epoxy flooring cost in San Antonio?

Residential garage floor projects typically fall between $1,600 and $3,500 installed. Solid-color and flake coatings run $3 to $7 per square foot. Metallic finishes jump to $5 to $12 per square foot. The [cost guide](/cost/) breaks down pricing by finish type and garage size, and the [calculator](/floor-coating-cost-calculator/) gives an instant ballpark.

### Can you apply epoxy over cracked concrete?

Yes, after the cracks are repaired. Contractors fill cracks with flexible polyurea or epoxy-based filler that moves with the slab as Houston Black clay expands and contracts. Coating directly over an unrepaired crack guarantees the crack will telegraph through the finish and eventually peel. Crack repair is standard practice in Bexar County, not an optional extra.

### What is the difference between epoxy and polyaspartic coatings?

Standard epoxy costs less per square foot and has a proven track record stretching back decades. It cures in 24 to 72 hours and can yellow slightly under heavy UV exposure. Polyaspartic coatings cure in 1 to 4 hours, resist UV yellowing, tolerate higher ambient humidity during application and last 15 to 20 years. They cost $1 to $3 more per square foot in material. The [epoxy vs. polyaspartic guide](/guides/epoxy-vs-polyaspartic-garage-floors/) compares the two systems side by side.

### Why is moisture testing important before epoxy installation?

San Antonio's clay soil forces moisture through concrete slabs, especially after the heavy spring rains that push the clay into full expansion. If the moisture vapor emission rate exceeds 3 to 5 pounds per 1,000 square feet per 24 hours, standard epoxy will blister and delaminate. An estimated 40 percent of epoxy failures in Texas trace back to moisture issues. Testing takes less than an hour and determines whether the slab needs a mitigation primer before coating.

### How long before I can park on a new epoxy floor?

Standard epoxy needs about 72 hours before foot traffic and a full week before vehicles. Polyaspartic systems move much faster: foot traffic in four hours and vehicles within 24 hours. Temperature, humidity and product formulation all affect these timelines, so your contractor will give you a specific window based on the conditions on install day.
