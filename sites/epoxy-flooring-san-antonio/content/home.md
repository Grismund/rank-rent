### Why Choose Epoxy Flooring?

Bare concrete stains, dusts and cracks. Epoxy bonds to the slab at a molecular level, forming a surface that resists oil, chemical spills, tire marks and abrasion. In a city where summer highs push past 95 degrees and afternoon storms drop an inch of rain in twenty minutes, a sealed floor keeps moisture from wicking through the slab and ruining whatever sits above it.

Professional epoxy systems last 10 to 20 years depending on traffic and maintenance. Compare that to the two-to-five-year life of a hardware-store paint kit and the math becomes straightforward, especially when the typical San Antonio garage floor job runs between $1,600 and $3,500 installed.

### Where Epoxy Flooring Works Best

Garages are the most common application. San Antonio's sprawling suburbs, Stone Oak, Alamo Ranch, the entire northeast military corridor, are built around homes with two- and three-car garages that see daily parking, weekend projects and seasonal storage.

Beyond the garage, epoxy protects [commercial floors](/commercial-epoxy-flooring/) in warehouses, auto shops and restaurant prep areas. It seals laundry rooms, covered patios and workshop spaces. Any concrete slab that takes foot traffic, rolling loads or chemical exposure is a candidate.

### Step 2: Surface Prep and Color Selection

Your contractor inspects the slab, tests for moisture vapor transmission and grinds or shot-blasts the surface to open the pores of the concrete. This preparation step is the single biggest factor in how long the coating lasts. You will also pick your finish at this stage, solid color, decorative flake, metallic or polyaspartic.

### Step 3: Installation and Curing

The crew applies primer, base coat and topcoat over one to two days depending on the system. Standard epoxy needs 24 to 72 hours to cure before foot traffic and about a week before you park on it. Polyaspartic systems cure in as little as four hours, which is a real advantage during San Antonio's humid summer months when extended cure windows invite moisture problems.
