## Why Schertz Homeowners Choose Epoxy Flooring

### Climate and Concrete Conditions

Schertz is squarely in what engineers call the I-35 Expansive Clay Corridor. The soils, Eddy very gravelly clay loam and Houston Black clay, swell 10 to 30 percent by volume when wet and crack up to four inches wide when dry. Houston Black runs 46 to 60 percent smectite, which puts it among the most reactive soils on the continent.

So slabs crack here, and not just old ones. Homeowners in Belmont Park, Rhine Valley and similar subdivisions report cracked slabs, cracked walls and doors that stop closing square as the foundation rides the seasons. For an epoxy floor that means crack repair is not an upsell and a flexible system is not a luxury. A rigid coating over an unaddressed crack re-cracks and then peels.

Humidity averages around 64 percent, peaking near 75 in May. That is enough to make moisture vapor testing mandatory before anyone coats a slab. Readings above 3 to 5 pounds per 1,000 square feet per 24 hours call for a moisture-mitigating primer. Homes built before about 2010 often lack a proper under-slab vapor barrier, and skipping the primer on one of those tends to produce blistering, clouding or delamination inside a year.

## Local Considerations

**Permits:** No building permit is required to coat an existing concrete floor in Schertz. The city does permit new flatwork such as driveways and sidewalks ($100, two inspections), but coating an existing slab is not new flatwork. Every contractor working in Schertz must register through the city's online portal; registration is free. Planning and Community Development: 210-619-1750. [City of Schertz — Building Permits](https://www.schertz.com/456/Building-Permits)

**Building codes:** Schertz is on the 2018 International Building Code, International Residential Code and related 2018 I-codes. Texas has no state license for epoxy installers and the city has no epoxy-specific certification beyond the general registration.

**HOAs:** The Crossvine, Homestead, Forest Ridge and the other master-planned communities have architectural guidelines. Garage floors are interior work and usually fall outside review, but some associations regulate contractor hours or where crews can stage. Check your covenants.

**Three-county note:** Since Schertz spans three counties, property tax rates and a few jurisdictional details vary by address. Permit requirements for epoxy work do not.
