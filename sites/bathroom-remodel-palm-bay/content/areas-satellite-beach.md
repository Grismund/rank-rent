Satellite Beach is a barrier island city of 11,365 residents, positioned between the Atlantic Ocean and the Banana River, adjacent to Patrick Space Force Base. ### Home Ages and Common Bathroom Issues

Satellite Beach's neighborhoods reveal a housing stock rooted in the 1960s and 1970s:

- **Tortoise Island:** A guard-gated waterfront community near the Pineda Causeway with 686 residents and an average household income of $199,000. Despite the high property values, many homes retain original-era plumbing that benefits from a comprehensive remodel.
- **Waterway Estates:** Canal-front homes along the Grand Canal. These waterfront properties face exposure from both the canal-side humidity and salt air, intensifying the wear on bathroom fixtures and plumbing.
- **South Patrick Shores:** Mixed housing near Patrick Space Force Base. Many residents are military families or civilian base employees who need functional, updated bathrooms during their time at the installation.
- **DeSoto Park area:** Homes surrounding the DeSoto Park recreation complex, with construction dating predominantly to the 1960s and 1970s.

Many Satellite Beach homes from the 1955 to 1985 era have been updated with impact-resistant windows and metal roofs, but the original cast iron drain pipes and galvanized steel supply lines remain behind the walls. These materials corrode in Brevard County's hard water (182 mg/L, trending upward in recent years), restricting flow and eventually requiring replacement. Polybutylene supply pipes, installed in homes from the late 1970s through mid-1990s, also have a documented history of premature failure. Repiping with PEX or copper is a common component of full bathroom remodels in Satellite Beach.

## Satellite Beach Bathroom Remodel Permits

The City of Satellite Beach Community Development Department at 1390 S Patrick Drive issues building permits. Contact the department at 321-773-4409 or permits@satellitebeach.gov.

Permits are required for any bathroom remodel involving plumbing relocation, electrical changes, structural modifications, or shower and tub retiling. Cosmetic changes are exempt. Permit review is typically completed within 3 business days. A certified Notice of Commencement is required for all work valued at $2,500 or more.

As of July 1, 2026, Florida HB 803 allows building permit exemptions for small home improvement projects valued under $7,500 on owner-occupied homes. Permit fees for a standard bathroom remodel run $200 to $700.

Satellite Beach enforces the Florida Building Code, 8th Edition (2023). Bathroom exhaust must terminate outside the building envelope. The waterproofing membrane behind new tile must be code-listed and inspected before tile installation. Barrier island properties in flood zones may face additional elevation and construction requirements for ground-floor renovations. All plumbing and electrical work requiring permits must be performed by Florida-licensed contractors.

Specific clearance requirements apply — for example, 15 inches minimum from the toilet center to any side wall or fixture, per Florida Building Code minimums. The contractor confirms all clearance and code requirements during the planning phase.

## What Satellite Beach Homeowners Are Choosing

Satellite Beach homeowners make material and fixture choices influenced by the barrier island environment:

- **Corrosion-resistant fixtures** in stainless steel or brushed nickel. Chrome finishes deteriorate faster in salt-air conditions. Ceramic-disc valves resist the mineral buildup from hard water better than standard valve types.
- **Large-format porcelain tile** that minimizes grout lines. Fewer joints means less surface area for hard water scale and mold to accumulate.
- **Walk-in showers** replacing old tub-shower combos. Aging in place is a practical consideration in Satellite Beach, and the [tub-to-shower conversion](/tub-to-shower-conversion/) is among the most requested projects.
- **Frameless glass with protective coatings** to resist both mineral deposits and salt residue. Standard glass in a barrier island bathroom requires frequent cleaning without these treatments.
- **Complete repiping** during full remodels, replacing original galvanized steel or polybutylene with PEX supply lines and PVC drains — addressing the root plumbing issue rather than working around it.
