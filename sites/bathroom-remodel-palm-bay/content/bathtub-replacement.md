## What Is Bathtub Replacement?

Bathtub replacement removes the existing tub and installs a new one. The scope can be as simple as swapping an alcove tub for a new unit in the same footprint, or as involved as changing the tub type entirely — moving from a standard alcove to a freestanding soaking tub or a walk-in tub with grab bars and a low-threshold door.

When the new tub fits the existing plumbing connections (same drain and overflow position), the project is straightforward. When the tub type changes — requiring a new drain location, different supply line routing, or structural reinforcement for a heavier unit — the scope expands and a permit from the City of Palm Bay Building Department is required.

## The Bathtub Replacement Process

### Planning and Design

The contractor assesses the existing tub, checks the plumbing and drain condition, and discusses your options. Key decisions include tub type (alcove, freestanding, or walk-in), material (acrylic, cast iron, or fiberglass), and whether the surrounding tile or surround panels need replacement. If the drain or supply lines must be rerouted, the contractor factors that into the scope and timeline.

### Demolition and Rough-In

Removing the old tub takes a few hours to a full day depending on the type and how it was installed. The contractor inspects the subfloor and wall framing behind the tub for water damage — a common discovery in Palm Bay bathrooms that have spent 25 to 30 years exposed to humidity and hard water. Any rot or moisture damage is addressed before the new tub goes in.

If plumbing changes are needed, the rough-in work — drain relocation, supply line rerouting, valve adjustments — is completed and inspected before the new tub is placed.

### Finishing and Inspection

The new tub is set, leveled, and connected to the drain and supply lines. Surrounding walls are waterproofed, tiled or paneled, and sealed. For walk-in tubs, the door seal is tested to verify it holds water without leaking. A final plumbing inspection confirms all connections are secure and leak-free.

A standard same-footprint bathtub replacement in Palm Bay takes two to four days. Projects that involve plumbing relocation or surrounding tile work extend to one to two weeks.

## Choices for Your Bathtub Replacement

- **Alcove tub:** The most common type, fitting between three walls. Standard acrylic alcove tubs start around $200 for the fixture. This is a direct swap when the existing space accommodates an alcove design.
- **Freestanding tub:** Soaking tubs in acrylic or cast iron, placed as a standalone fixture. Prices range from $500 to $3,000+ for the tub alone. Requires adequate floor support, especially for heavier cast-iron models.
- **Walk-in tub:** Designed for accessibility, with a low-threshold door, grab bars, a built-in seat, and sometimes hydrotherapy jets. Fixture prices run $2,000 to $5,000. Walk-in tubs are increasingly requested in Palm Bay, where aging in place is a common priority.
- **Material considerations:** Acrylic is lightweight and retains heat well. Fiberglass is the most affordable but scratches more easily. Cast iron is durable and heavy — confirm that the floor structure can support it. In all cases, selecting finishes that resist hard water mineral deposits extends the useful life in Brevard County's water conditions.

Bathtub replacement in the Palm Bay area typically costs **$1,500 to $5,000** for a standard swap in the same footprint. Walk-in tubs with accessibility features run higher, from $3,000 to $8,000 installed, depending on the model and plumbing requirements.
