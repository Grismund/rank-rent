The shower sees more daily water contact than any other bathroom fixture. In Brevard County, where annual humidity averages 75 percent and water hardness reaches 182 mg/L, that exposure takes a measurable toll. Grout lines break down, caulk joints fail, mineral deposits coat glass and fixtures, and moisture can penetrate behind tile into the wall cavity. A shower that was installed in the early 1990s — the era when most Palm Bay homes were built — has absorbed three decades of this environment.

## What Is Shower Remodel?

A shower remodel replaces or upgrades the shower enclosure, walls, floor, fixtures, and sometimes the plumbing behind them. The scope ranges from cosmetic (new tile and fixtures over sound substrate) to structural (tear out everything to the framing, address water damage, re-waterproof, and rebuild).

Common shower remodel projects in the Palm Bay area include:

- Replacing a prefabricated fiberglass insert with a custom-tiled walk-in shower
- Retiling an existing shower with updated porcelain or large-format tile
- Adding a frameless or semi-frameless glass enclosure
- Installing a curbless zero-threshold shower for accessibility
- Upgrading the showerhead, valve, and controls to a thermostatic system
- Rebuilding a shower that has developed leaks behind the walls

## The Shower Remodel Process

### Planning and Design

The contractor inspects the existing shower, checks for moisture damage behind the walls, and discusses your goals. Key decisions include tile material and pattern, shower dimensions and threshold type (curbed or curbless), glass enclosure style, showerhead configuration, and niche placement. If the project involves relocating the drain or adding a second showerhead line, a permit is required from the City of Palm Bay Building Department.

### Demolition and Rough-In

Demo removes the old tile, backer board, shower pan, and fixtures. The contractor inspects the framing and subfloor for water damage. In Palm Bay's humid climate, moisture that worked past failed grout or caulk can deteriorate studs and subflooring over years without any visible sign from the outside. Damaged framing is replaced before the rebuild starts.

Plumbing rough-in repositions supply lines and valves as needed. If the drain location changes, the plumber reroutes it. A rough plumbing inspection must pass before the walls can be closed.

### Finishing and Inspection

The waterproofing membrane covers the shower pan and walls first. Florida Building Code Section R702.4 requires a code-listed membrane behind new tile, verified by inspection before tile installation begins. Backer board, then tile, then grout. The showerhead, valve trim, glass enclosure, and accessories are installed last. A shower pan flood test may be required during the inspection process.

Most shower remodels in Palm Bay take one to three weeks depending on scope, tile complexity, and whether custom glass is ordered.

## Choices for Your Shower Remodel

- **Tile:** Large-format porcelain is the top choice for Brevard County — fewer grout lines means less surface for hard water mineral deposits to accumulate. Subway tile remains a standard option. Mosaic tile works well for accent bands and niches. Natural stone requires regular sealing.
- **Glass enclosure:** Frameless glass ($800 to $2,000+ installed) provides a clean, open appearance. Semi-frameless offers a mid-range balance. Protective coatings on the glass help resist both mineral buildup and salt-air corrosion for homeowners closer to the coast.
- **Showerhead:** Single fixed head, handheld, rain head, or multi-head configurations. Thermostatic valves maintain temperature consistency when water is running elsewhere in the house.
- **Niche:** A recessed shelf built into the shower wall. More practical and durable than a hanging caddy, and built to last the life of the shower.
- **Bench:** A built-in tile bench adds function and an accessibility element, particularly popular in Palm Bay's aging-in-place projects.

## Shower Remodel Cost in Palm Bay

Shower remodels in the Palm Bay area typically range from **$2,000 to $7,000**. A straightforward retile with stock fixtures falls at the lower end. A complete tear-out and rebuild with custom tile, frameless glass, and upgraded plumbing lands at the upper end.

The primary cost drivers are tile material and coverage area, glass enclosure type, the extent of plumbing modifications, and whether water damage repair is needed behind the walls.
