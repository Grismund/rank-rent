## West Melbourne Bathroom Remodel Permits

The City of West Melbourne Building Department at 2240 Minton Road issues permits for all construction within city limits. Contact the department at 321-837-7776 or buildingpermits@westmelbourne.gov.

Building permits are required for plumbing work, electrical work, window replacement, and any structural modifications. Cosmetic changes — painting, flooring replacement, hardware swaps — are exempt. Permit applications are valid for 180 days from the date of submission. Plan review checks compliance with both City ordinances and the Florida Building Code.

As of July 1, 2026, Florida HB 803 allows certain building permit exemptions for home improvement projects valued under $7,500 on owner-occupied homes. A certified Notice of Commencement is required for all work valued at $2,500 or more.

West Melbourne enforces the Florida Building Code, 8th Edition (2023). Bathroom exhaust must terminate outside the building envelope. When shower or tub-surround tile is removed and replaced, Section R702.4 requires a code-listed waterproofing membrane behind the new tile, inspected before tile installation begins. All plumbing and electrical work requiring permits must be performed by Florida-licensed contractors.

Permit fees for a standard bathroom remodel run $200 to $700 depending on scope and project valuation.

## What West Melbourne Homeowners Are Choosing

West Melbourne homeowners select upgrades suited to both their housing era and the local climate conditions:

- **Walk-in shower conversions** are the leading request in older homes where the tub-shower combo is no longer practical. The [tub-to-shower conversion](/tub-to-shower-conversion/) service page covers this project in detail.
- **Porcelain tile in large format** for both newer and older homes. Brevard County's hard water (182 mg/L) deposits minerals on every grout joint — fewer grout lines means less maintenance.
- **Quartz countertops** on vanities. Quartz requires no sealing, resists water stains, and handles the daily moisture of a bathroom better than natural stone in a humid climate.
- **Improved exhaust ventilation** in older homes that may have inadequate or code-noncompliant fan installations. Florida Building Code requires 50 CFM minimum, terminating outdoors.
- **Newer homes in Sawgrass Lakes and Addison Village** tend toward aesthetic upgrades — frameless glass shower enclosures, freestanding tubs, and statement lighting.
