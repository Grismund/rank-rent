What does a **bathroom remodel cost Palm Bay** homeowners should expect? Most bathroom renovations in this area fall between $3,000 and $15,000. A cosmetic update of a guest bath runs $3,000 to $6,000. A mid-range renovation of a primary bathroom lands between $6,000 and $10,000. A complete reconstruction with new plumbing, electrical, and layout changes reaches $10,000 to $15,000 or higher.

These figures reflect what Brevard County contractors are quoting in the current market. Your specific bathroom dimensions, fixture selections, and chosen contractor will shift the final number.

*Last updated: November 2026. Costs vary by contractor and site conditions.*

## Bathroom Remodel Price Ranges

### Budget Bathroom Remodel

**$3,000 to $6,000.** This scope covers a guest bath, half bath, or powder room where the objective is a visual refresh without altering the layout. Existing plumbing stays in place. Typical work includes a new vanity, faucet, toilet, light fixtures, mirror, floor tile, and paint.

Because plumbing is not relocated, a permit is usually not required for this scope under Florida rules. Work valued under $7,500 on an owner-occupied single-family home may qualify for exemption under HB 803. The timeline runs approximately one week. This is the most common starting point for Palm Bay homeowners who want a noticeable change without a significant commitment.

### Mid-Range Bathroom Remodel

**$6,000 to $10,000.** The standard master bath update. New tile on the floor and in the shower, a new vanity with countertop, updated fixtures throughout, a new toilet, lighting, and possibly a shower remodel with a glass enclosure. Some plumbing modifications may be involved — relocating a shower valve, for instance — which triggers a permit from the City of Palm Bay Building Department.

This is where most Palm Bay homeowners land. It addresses the functional issues that accumulate over 25 to 30 years — deteriorating grout, mineral-encrusted fixtures, inadequate ventilation — while giving the room a current look. Expect two to three weeks from demolition to completion.

### High-End Bathroom Remodel

**$10,000 to $15,000+.** A complete reconstruction. Everything is removed to the studs and the room is rebuilt with new plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, tile, fixtures, and potentially a reconfigured layout. This scope typically involves relocating drains, adding features like a curbless shower or freestanding tub, and upgrading to premium tile and fixtures.

A permit is required. The timeline runs three to six weeks depending on scope and inspection scheduling. See the [full bathroom remodel](/full-bathroom-remodel/) page for details on what this project involves.

## Why Bathroom Remodel Prices Vary

Five factors, roughly ordered by their effect on the total:

**Extent of plumbing work.** Keeping fixtures in their existing positions costs significantly less than moving them. Rerouting a drain line in a slab-on-grade home — which many Palm Bay homes are — means cutting concrete, repositioning the pipe, and patching. That work alone can add $1,000 to $3,000 to the project.

**Tile material and coverage.** Ceramic tile material runs $1 to $5 per square foot. Porcelain is $3 to $10. Natural stone starts at $5 and goes up from there. A bathroom with tile on the floor, all shower walls, and a tub surround requires a substantial quantity, and material cost compounds with area.

**Fixture grade.** A basic faucet costs $30. A mid-range name-brand set runs $150 to $400. Multiply that across faucet, showerhead, toilet, vanity hardware, and towel bars, and fixture quality becomes a real budget line.

**Bathroom dimensions.** A 40-square-foot half bath requires less material and labor than a 100-square-foot master. However, fixed costs like mobilization, permits, and inspections do not scale down proportionally. Smaller bathrooms cost more per square foot even though the total is lower.

**Concealed damage.** Water damage behind tile, mold in the subfloor, deteriorated plumbing that needs replacement — these appear during demolition and add to the final cost. In Brevard County, where humidity runs 75 percent and water hardness reaches 182 mg/L, finding moisture damage behind a decades-old tub surround is a routine discovery.

## Cost Breakdown by Component

Where a mid-range bathroom remodel budget is typically distributed:

- **Labor:** 40 to 50 percent. Tile installation, plumbing, electrical, and carpentry represent the trades involved.
- **Tile and countertops:** 15 to 25 percent. Floor tile, shower wall tile, and vanity countertop.
- **Fixtures and hardware:** 10 to 15 percent. Faucet, showerhead, toilet, towel bars, hooks, and trim.
- **Vanity and cabinetry:** 10 to 15 percent. The vanity is typically the highest-cost single fixture in the room.
- **Plumbing and electrical:** 5 to 10 percent. Supply lines, drain work, GFCI outlets, ventilation fan, and lighting connections.
- **Permits and inspections:** 1 to 3 percent. Required for any project that modifies plumbing or electrical in Palm Bay. Fees typically run $200 to $700.

These proportions shift with scope. A cosmetic update has proportionally less labor because there is no demolition or rough-in. A high-end reconstruction with premium tile increases the material share.
