## Local Regulations and Permits

Cedar Park's curbside service through Republic Services provides weekly garbage and bi-weekly recycling. The bulk trash program allows up to 7 oversized items placed curbside next to the green cart on regular service day. Accepted items include mattresses, appliances without doors, and bundled brush under 4 feet and 40 pounds. Construction debris, hazardous waste, tires, TVs, and computers are prohibited from bulk pickup.

Dumpster placement on private property does not require a permit, but Cedar Park city code mandates that containers sit at least 50 feet from any single-family residential property line and 15 feet from existing or proposed trees. Placing a roll-off on a public right-of-way requires a permit from Cedar Park Public Works at $50 to $200, with 1-3 business days for processing. All containers must be screened from public view and the view of adjoining properties.

HOA communities throughout Cedar Park — which is most of the city — layer additional rules on top of city code. Many require dumpsters on driveways only and mandate privacy screens for visibility from the street.

A hauler pickup sidesteps every one of these restrictions. No container stays on your property, no permit is needed, and no HOA notification is required.
