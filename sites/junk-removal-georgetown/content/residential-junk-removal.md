## What Is Residential Junk Removal?

Residential junk removal is the full-service clearance of unwanted household items from inside your home, garage, attic, shed, or yard. Unlike curbside pickup — which limits you to what fits in a bin and requires scheduling through Texas Disposal Systems — a hauler takes everything that is not hazardous, in any quantity, from wherever it sits on your property.

The service covers everything from a single room cleanout to an entire estate. It is especially useful during moves, after a family member passes, before a home renovation, or simply when years of accumulation reach the point where you need a fresh start.

## What We Haul for Residential Junk Removal

Local haulers take away a wide range of household items:

- Furniture: couches, recliners, mattresses, bed frames, dining sets, desks, bookshelves, entertainment centers
- Appliances: refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, microwaves, window AC units (Freon units require recovery — see [appliance hauling](/appliance-hauling/))
- Electronics: TVs, monitors, computers, printers
- Clothing, toys, and household goods in bulk
- Exercise equipment, hot tub shells, and play structures
- Boxes, bags, and miscellaneous clutter from garages, attics, and storage rooms

Items not accepted include hazardous chemicals, paint cans with liquid paint, medical waste, and propane tanks. The [guide on what haulers will not take](/guides/what-junk-removal-companies-wont-take/) explains where to bring these items in Williamson County.
