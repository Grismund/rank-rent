## Frequently Asked Questions About Junk Removal

### How much does junk removal cost in Georgetown?

Most jobs range from $100 for a single item to $700 for a full truckload. Pricing is based on how much of the truck bed your items fill — not by the hour or by weight (though very heavy loads may add a surcharge). A quarter truckload runs $150 to $250, a half truck is $250 to $400, and a full truck is $450 to $700. The [cost guide](/cost/) has the complete breakdown, and the [cost estimator](/junk-removal-cost-estimator/) gives a quick ballpark.

### What items do junk removal haulers take?

Haulers take furniture, appliances, electronics, yard debris, construction waste, and general household junk. The short version: if it is not hazardous, a hauler can probably take it. Items they will not take include chemicals, liquid paint, medical waste, asbestos, and certain tires. The [guide on what haulers will not take](/guides/what-junk-removal-companies-wont-take/) covers the full exclusion list and explains where to bring each item in Williamson County.

### Do I need to move items to the curb?

No. The hauler crew comes inside the home and carries items from whatever room, floor, or area they are located in. You walk them through the property, point at what needs to go, and they handle all the lifting, carrying, and loading. Attics, garages, basements, backyards — it all gets cleared.

### Where does my junk go after pickup?

Usable items go to donation centers. The Habitat for Humanity ReStore at 3916 Gattis School Road in Round Rock accepts furniture, appliances, and building materials. Goodwill locations in Georgetown, Round Rock, and Cedar Park take clothing, household goods, and electronics. Recyclable metals are separated for scrap. The remainder goes to the Georgetown Transfer Station on W.L. Walden Drive or the Williamson County Landfill at 600 Landfill Road in Hutto.

### How quickly can I schedule a pickup?

Most haulers serving the Georgetown area can schedule a pickup within a few days. Same-day or next-day service may be available depending on the hauler's current schedule. Weekday pickups tend to have more availability than weekends.

### Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Georgetown?

If the dumpster sits entirely on private property — your driveway, for example — no permit is needed. Placing a dumpster on a public street or right-of-way requires a permit from the city. Note that in Taylor, Waste Connections holds an exclusive solid waste franchise, so all dumpster placement must be coordinated through them. A hauler pickup avoids the permit question entirely since no container sits on your property.

### Is there a minimum charge?

Yes. Most haulers charge a minimum of $100 to $150 for a single item or very small load. The minimum covers the fixed costs of driving the truck to your location, loading the item, and making a disposal run. If you have multiple small items, combining them into one pickup keeps the per-item cost down.

### Do haulers recycle or donate items?

Yes. Independent haulers in Williamson County check whether items are in donatable or recyclable condition before anything goes to the landfill. Furniture, appliances, and building materials in usable shape go to the ReStore or Goodwill. Metals get separated for scrap recycling at the Williamson County Recycling Center. This matters beyond environmental impact — diverting items from the landfill can reduce the disposal portion of your bill.
