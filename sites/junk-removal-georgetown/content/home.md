The practical reasons come down to weight, volume, and disposal logistics. A sectional sofa does not fit in an SUV. A water heater requires disconnection and potentially Freon recovery. A hauler quotes the full job upfront, brings the right truck, handles the heavy lifting, and takes care of disposal at the correct facility.
