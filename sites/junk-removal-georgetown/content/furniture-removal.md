## What Is Furniture Removal?

Furniture removal is the pickup and disposal of large, heavy, or bulky household furniture that standard trash service will not accept. A hauler comes to your home, carries the item out from whatever room or floor it is on, loads it into the truck, and takes it to the proper destination — donation center, recycling facility, or landfill.

The service is used during moves, after replacing worn-out pieces, when downsizing (especially common in Georgetown's Sun City community), or when clearing an estate.

### Responsible Disposal

Before anything goes to the landfill, haulers check whether items are in donatable condition. Furniture in decent shape goes to the Habitat for Humanity ReStore at 3916 Gattis School Road in Round Rock, which accepts furniture, appliances, and building materials. Goodwill locations in Georgetown, Round Rock, and Cedar Park also accept usable furniture. Metal frames and hardware get separated for scrap recycling. Items that are damaged beyond reuse go to the Georgetown Transfer Station or the Williamson County Landfill.

This matters beyond goodwill — diverting usable items reduces the load on the truck, which can lower the price of the job.
