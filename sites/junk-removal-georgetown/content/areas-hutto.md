### Local Disposal Options

## Local Regulations and Permits

Hutto's curbside garbage service runs through Al Clawson Disposal (512-930-5490). Bulk and brush removal is scheduled on a quarterly basis with a limit of 18 cubic feet or 18 bags per pickup. Accepted items include furniture, appliances, extra garbage bags, yard clippings, and tree trimmings placed curbside. That quarterly schedule and the 18-bag cap mean anything beyond a small load needs a hauler or a self-haul trip to the landfill.

Dumpster placement on private property does not require a permit. Placing a container on a public right-of-way requires a city permit, and Hutto limits dumpster permits to a maximum of two per year per property. The city's MS4 stormwater permit prohibits disposing of debris or waste in storm drains, creeks, or drainage ditches — a rule worth noting during construction projects near Brushy Creek or other waterways.

HOA communities — which account for the vast majority of Hutto's housing — have their own rules on dumpster placement, exterior storage, and curbside staging. Star Ranch, Emory Farms, and Huttoparke all require trash bins stored out of sight and containers restricted to driveways. Keep Hutto Beautiful hosts an annual Junk Round Up in October at 1600 Innovation Blvd, accepting electronics, metal, brush, furniture, tires, and bulk trash for free with proof of residency — a useful supplement but not a substitute for year-round service.

A hauler pickup bypasses the quarterly wait, the 18-bag limit, the dumpster permit cap, and the HOA rules. The truck arrives, loads, and leaves within hours.
