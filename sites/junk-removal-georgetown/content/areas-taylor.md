## Local Regulations and Permits

Taylor's solid waste situation is unique in Williamson County. Waste Connections holds an exclusive franchise for solid waste collection and disposal within city limits. This means:

- **No other dumpster company** can legally service Taylor. For temporary roll-off dumpsters, residents must call Waste Connections at 512-282-3508.
- **Curbside service** allows one bulk item per week on regular trash day — call Utility Billing at 512-352-2066 to schedule in advance.
- **No construction material** (wood, sheetrock, carpet) is accepted for curbside bulk removal.
- **Freon-containing appliances** require a certificate proving Freon removal before the city will collect them.

A junk removal hauler sidesteps the exclusive franchise entirely. The hauler loads items onto their own truck and transports them to the Williamson County Landfill or Georgetown Transfer Station. No roll-off container is placed on your property, so the franchise restriction does not apply.

Taylor hosts an annual Citywide Spring Cleanup with free disposal at multiple drop-off locations for bulky items, tires, electronics, and brush. This is a useful resource for smaller loads, but it happens once a year. For anything that cannot wait, a hauler is the practical solution.
