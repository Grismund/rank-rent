## Local Regulations and Permits

Round Rock's curbside service through Republic Services provides weekly garbage and bi-weekly recycling. In-city residents receive one free bulk pickup per year covering up to 5 cubic yards — call Republic Services at 512-255-4980 at least one week before collection day to schedule. Accepted bulk items include non-Freon appliances, furniture, scrap metal, and lumber under 4 feet. Construction debris is not accepted curbside.

Dumpster placement on private property requires no permit. Placing a container on a public street or right-of-way requires a right-of-way obstruction permit from the Round Rock Engineering Department, with 3-7 business days processing. Unauthorized placement can result in fines up to $2,000 per day. HOA communities — including Teravista, Paloma Lake, and Walsh Ranch — commonly require advance notice for dumpster placement and restrict containers to driveways only.

A hauler pickup avoids both the permit requirement and the HOA restrictions, since no container stays on the property.
