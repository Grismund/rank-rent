A single bulky item starts around $100. A full truckload of household junk or construction debris ranges from $450 to $700. ## Junk Removal Price Ranges

Junk removal in Georgetown is priced by the fraction of the truck your load occupies, not by the hour or by weight (though exceptionally heavy items can add a surcharge). Here is how the ranges break down:

### Single Item Removal

One couch, one mattress, one appliance. Expect around **$100 to $150**. The minimum exists because the hauler still drives a truck to your property, loads the piece, and makes a disposal run to the Georgetown Transfer Station or the Williamson County Landfill — the fixed overhead is roughly the same whether they haul one item or three.

### Partial Truck Load

This is where most Georgetown residential jobs fall.

- **Quarter bed** (a room's worth of furniture or a stack of boxes): **$150 to $250**
- **Half bed** (a large cleanout, garage purge, or small renovation): **$250 to $400**
- **Three-quarter bed** (multi-room clearance or a full garage): **$350 to $500**

A quarter to half truckload covers the typical Georgetown job: clearing out a spare bedroom in Sun City, hauling a living room set and miscellaneous items from Berry Creek, or removing a pile of drywall scraps from a bathroom renovation.

### Full Truck Load

A full truckload handles a whole-house cleanout, an estate cleanup, or a large renovation's worth of debris. Expect **$450 to $700**. Standard junk removal trucks hold 10 to 15 cubic yards. If the job exceeds one truckload, the hauler quotes for multiple loads upfront.

## Why Junk Removal Prices Vary

Five factors, roughly in order of impact:

**Volume.** The more truck space your junk occupies, the more it costs. This is the primary variable. A stack of boxes and a couple of chairs fills a quarter truck. A three-bedroom Sun City cleanout may need a full truck or more.

**Weight.** Most loads are priced on volume alone, but exceptionally heavy materials — concrete, tile, wet construction debris — push the price higher because they increase fuel cost and trigger heavier landfill fees at the Georgetown Transfer Station.

**Location and access.** Items on a ground floor with a clear path to the truck cost less than items on a second floor, in an attic, or in a tight storage closet. If the crew needs extra time or additional people to extract items, the quote reflects that labor.

**Material type.** Appliances with refrigerant (refrigerators, freezers, AC units) require Freon recovery, adding to disposal cost. Electronics carry separate recycling fees. Standard furniture and household goods are the simplest to dispose of.

**Disposal fees.** The Georgetown Transfer Station on W.L. Walden Drive and the Williamson County Landfill in Hutto each have their own fee schedules. The landfill charges $45 for a standard residential load. Haulers build these costs into the quote, but heavy or high-volume loads cost more at the scale.

## Tips to Lower Your Junk Removal Cost

**Consolidate into one trip.** A half-full truck in a single visit costs less than two separate quarter-truck pickups. If you are planning a cleanout, do it all at once rather than spacing pickups out over weeks.

**Separate donatable items.** Furniture and goods in usable condition can go to the Habitat for Humanity ReStore in Round Rock (3916 Gattis School Road, 512-863-4344) or Goodwill locations throughout Williamson County. The less that goes on the truck, the smaller the bill.

**Get multiple quotes.** Haulers in the Georgetown market price competitively. Call two or three with the same job description. The spread between bids is usually informative.

**Be flexible on timing.** Haulers may have better availability and pricing on weekdays versus weekends. If your schedule allows it, ask about mid-week slots.

For large or slow-paced renovation projects, renting a dumpster may be cheaper than a full-service hauler. The [junk removal vs. dumpster rental guide](/guides/junk-removal-vs-renting-a-dumpster/) compares costs and logistics for Georgetown homeowners.
