## What Is Appliance Hauling?

Appliance hauling is the removal and proper disposal of large household appliances. The service covers disconnection assistance, carrying the unit out of the house, loading it onto the truck, and transporting it to a recycling center or landfill. For appliances that contain refrigerant — such as refrigerators, freezers, and air conditioning units — haulers coordinate Freon recovery before disposal, as required by EPA regulations.

### Pickup Day

The hauler arrives with the right equipment — a hand truck, appliance dolly, and straps — to safely move the unit out of your home. They navigate tight kitchens, laundry closets, and staircases without damaging floors or walls. You do not need to disconnect the appliance yourself, though having the water and power supply shut off beforehand speeds things up.

For refrigerators and freezers, the hauler ensures the unit is defrosted or manages transport of units with residual ice. Doors are secured during transport to prevent them from swinging open.

### Responsible Disposal

Appliances contain recyclable metals — steel, copper, aluminum — that have scrap value. Haulers deliver appliances to metal recyclers or the Williamson County Recycling Center at 600 Landfill Road in Hutto, which accepts scrap metal and electronics. Units containing Freon go through certified refrigerant recovery before the shell is recycled.

Water heaters, washers, and dryers in working condition may be donated to the Habitat for Humanity ReStore in Round Rock. Appliances beyond repair are stripped for recyclable components, keeping the bulk out of the landfill.
