## How We Calculate These Ranges

The price ranges in this tool are based on the volume-based pricing structure used by independent haulers serving Georgetown, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Taylor, and Hutto. Haulers price by the fraction of the truck bed your items fill. A standard junk removal truck holds 10 to 15 cubic yards.

Key inputs behind the ranges:

- **Minimum pickup** (1-2 small items): $100 to $150 — covers the fixed cost of the truck, crew, and a disposal run to the Georgetown Transfer Station or Williamson County Landfill
- **Quarter bed**: $150 to $250 — a room's worth of furniture or a stack of boxes and bags
- **Half bed**: $250 to $400 — a full garage cleanout or moderate renovation debris
- **Three-quarter bed**: $350 to $500 — multi-room clearance or a large estate cleanout
- **Full bed**: $450 to $700 — whole-house cleanout or a major renovation's worth of waste

Heavy materials (concrete, tile, appliances with refrigerant) may add surcharges above these ranges. The [junk removal cost guide](/cost/) explains all the factors that move the price.
