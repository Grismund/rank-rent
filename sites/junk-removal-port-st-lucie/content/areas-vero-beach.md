Vero Beach sits at the northern edge of the Treasure Coast with a population of about 17,100. ## Why Vero Beach Residents Choose Professional Junk Removal

### Local Disposal Options

Indian River County updated its collection system effective October 1, 2025. Unincorporated county residents now receive weekly curbside collection for trash, recycling, yard waste, and up to 4 bulk trash items per week. Acceptable bulk items include furniture and white goods (appliances). Prohibited curbside items include batteries, gasoline, motor oil, chemicals, propane tanks, and carpet.

Within the City of Vero Beach itself, the city provides twice-weekly garbage pickup with separate weekly recycling and yard waste collection. Bulk pickup requires calling the Solid Waste division in advance to schedule.

The weekly 4-item bulk limit works for gradual disposal but does not solve a full cleanout. When you need a bedroom emptied, a garage cleared, or an estate cleaned out in one day, 4 items per week means weeks of staging. A hauler takes the entire load at once.

## Permits and Ordinances in Vero Beach

A dumpster on private property in Vero Beach generally does not require a permit. Placement on a public street, sidewalk, or alley requires a Right-of-Way Obstruction Permit from the City of Vero Beach Public Works Department under Chapter 59 of the city code. Right-of-way dumpsters must not block fire hydrants, bus stops, driveways, or crosswalks. Reflective tape or traffic cones may be required for containers on public property.

Standard junk removal service does not require any permit.

For HOA communities: under Florida HB 1203, an HOA cannot fine a homeowner for leaving garbage receptacles at the curb within 24 hours before or after the designated collection day. Most Vero Beach HOAs require dumpsters on private driveways and prohibit piling trash outside containers or leaving loose debris around bins.
