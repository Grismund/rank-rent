### Local Disposal Options

Fort Pierce has solid curbside collection: residential garbage is collected twice weekly, recycling once weekly, and yard waste plus bulk waste once weekly. Bulk waste — furniture, appliances, mattresses, carpet, and large items that do not fit in the 96-gallon green cart — is collected curbside on a monthly schedule.

But curbside has limits. Bulk pickup is monthly, not on demand. Items must meet placement rules: at the curb by 7 AM on collection day, not more than 24 hours early, and not remaining more than 24 hours after collection. All doors must be removed from refrigerators, washers, dryers, and stoves before curbside placement. And the monthly cycle means a full-room cleanout could mean staging items at the curb for weeks.

A hauler takes it all in one visit, handles the door removal and Freon recovery for appliances, and disposes of everything properly the same day.

## Permits and Ordinances in Fort Pierce

A dumpster placed on private property in Fort Pierce does not require a permit. A dumpster placed on a public right-of-way — street, alley, or sidewalk — requires a Right-of-Way Permit from the City of Fort Pierce Department of Engineering at (772) 467-3773, located at 100 N. US Highway 1. Placement must maintain at least 15 feet of clearance from fire hydrants and 10 feet from storm drain inlets.

Standard junk removal — where a truck arrives, loads, and leaves the same day — does not require any permit.

For HOA communities: under Florida HB 1203, an HOA cannot fine a homeowner for leaving garbage receptacles at the curb within 24 hours before or after the designated collection day. Most HOAs still require dumpsters to remain on private driveways rather than on the street, and bulk debris may be subject to advance notice requirements per community CC&Rs.
