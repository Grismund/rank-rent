Need **appliance removal Port St. Lucie** homeowners can depend on? Port St. Lucie Junk Haulers connects you with independent local haulers who remove refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, water heaters, and other heavy appliances from anywhere in the house and handle disposal properly — including Freon recovery for units that contain refrigerant.

Appliances are some of the heaviest single items in a home. A standard refrigerator weighs 200 to 400 pounds. A front-load washer hits 200 easily. Getting one to the curb safely, without damaging floors or doorframes, is a two-person job at minimum. Getting it disposed of correctly adds another layer, because St. Lucie County requires appliance doors to be removed or secured before curbside placement, and refrigerant-containing units need certified Freon recovery before they reach the landfill.

## What Is Appliance Hauling?

A hauler disconnects the appliance (if requested), carries it out of the house, loads it onto the truck, and delivers it to the proper disposal or recycling facility. Units containing refrigerant — refrigerators, freezers, window AC units, dehumidifiers — go through EPA-compliant Freon recovery before final disposal. Usable appliances in working condition are diverted to donation centers when possible.

This service covers removal only. If you need a new appliance installed at the same time, coordinate with your delivery service or plumber separately.

The crew arrives with the right equipment for the weight. Appliance dollies, furniture pads for doorframes, and straps for the truck. They carry the unit out without you having to touch it. If the appliance is in a tight laundry closet, a second-floor kitchen, or a detached garage with a narrow doorway, mention it when booking so the crew plans accordingly.

### Responsible Disposal

Appliances contain recyclable metals — steel, copper, aluminum — that have value at scrap facilities. Haulers separate recyclable units from general waste. The Habitat for Humanity ReStore in Fort Pierce accepts working appliances and offers free pickup. Atlantic Coast Recycling and Recycling Services of Florida, both in Fort Pierce, handle metal recycling.

Refrigerant-containing appliances go through certified Freon recovery before the shell reaches the recycling stream. This is not optional — the EPA mandates it, and responsible haulers build it into the process.
