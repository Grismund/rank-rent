### Why Hire a Junk Removal Service?

The practical reasons come down to time, labor, and disposal rules. A couch does not fit in a sedan. A refrigerator needs Freon recovery before it can go to a landfill. Construction debris has weight and volume that overwhelm a pickup truck in a single load. And St. Lucie County's landfill charges a $10 minimum for up to 250 pounds — which sounds reasonable until you factor in the drive, the unloading, and the second trip for whatever did not fit.

A hauler prices the whole job upfront. They bring the right size truck, do the lifting, sort what can be donated or recycled, and handle disposal at the proper facility. You point at what goes and the space is clear when they leave.
