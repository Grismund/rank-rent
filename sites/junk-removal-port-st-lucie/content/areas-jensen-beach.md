### Local Disposal Options

Martin County allows residents to place bulk waste at the curb on their scheduled garbage collection day. Oversized items that the regular crew cannot handle are tagged and collected within 5 business days. Placement rules require all bulk waste within 6 feet of the curb, loose debris containerized, glass panels taped, and nothing placed under utility lines, on meters, or within 3 feet of mailboxes or fire hydrants. Appliance doors must be sealed or removed before curbside placement.

These rules handle occasional items. They do not handle a full garage cleanout, an estate clearing, or a renovation debris pile. A hauler takes the full load in one visit, follows Martin County's disposal requirements, and diverts usable items to local donation centers.

## Permits and Ordinances in Jensen Beach

A dumpster placed on private property in Jensen Beach does not typically require a permit. Because Jensen Beach is unincorporated Martin County, any placement on a public right-of-way requires a permit from Martin County. HOA-governed communities may have additional restrictions on dumpster placement — check with your specific community.

Standard junk removal service does not require any permit.

Household hazardous waste is handled through Martin County's HazMobile program. Contact Martin County Solid Waste at (772) 288-5772 for the current collection schedule.

For HOA communities: under Florida HB 1203, an HOA cannot fine a homeowner for leaving garbage receptacles at the curb within 24 hours before or after the designated collection day. Bulk trash restrictions may require advance notice or adherence to specific pickup days per community CC&Rs.
