## What Is Furniture Removal?

A hauler arrives with a truck and crew, carries the furniture out of wherever it sits — living room, bedroom, second floor, lanai — loads it, and takes it away. Usable pieces go to donation centers. The rest goes to the landfill.

This is not a moving service. The furniture leaves, it does not get relocated. If a couch is going to a specific person or address, that is a moving job. If it is going away permanently, that is furniture removal.

## Furniture Removal Step by Step

### Booking and Estimates

Call **[PHONE]** or submit a request. List the pieces and where they are in the house. Most furniture pickups are straightforward enough to quote over the phone. For a full room or multiple rooms, an on-site visit gives the hauler a clear picture of the volume and any access challenges (narrow hallways, stairs, tight turns).

### Pickup Day

The crew handles all the carrying. You do not need to move furniture to the garage, the curb, or even the front door. If the dresser is upstairs and the couch is in the back room, the crew brings it all out. Doors, banisters, and walls are protected — haulers who do this daily know how to angle a sectional through a hallway without leaving marks.

### Responsible Disposal

Port St. Lucie and the surrounding Treasure Coast have several donation options for furniture in good condition. The Habitat for Humanity ReStore at 2904 Okeechobee Road in Fort Pierce accepts furniture, appliances, and building materials with free donation pickup. Goodwill locations in Fort Pierce, Stuart, and Jensen Beach accept household goods. Haulers check these options before sending anything to the St. Lucie County landfill.

For pieces that are stained, broken, or otherwise not donation-worthy, the landfill on Glades Cut-Off Road in Fort Pierce is the final stop. The drop-off fee is $10 minimum for up to 250 pounds.

## Furniture Removal Cost in Port St. Lucie

Furniture removal is priced by how much truck space the pieces occupy. Costs vary by hauler, but typical ranges in the Port St. Lucie area:

- **Single item** (one couch, one mattress, one dresser): around $100
- **Several pieces** (quarter truckload): $150 to $250
- **Room full** (half truckload): $250 to $400
- **Whole-house furnishings** (full truckload): $450 to $700

Pieces on upper floors or in tight spaces may add to the quote if extra labor or crew members are needed. The hauler gives you the number before any work starts. Full pricing breakdown is on the [cost guide](/cost/).
