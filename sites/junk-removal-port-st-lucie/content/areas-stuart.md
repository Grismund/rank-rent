## Local Junk Removal in Stuart

Stuart is the county seat of Martin County with a population of roughly 19,500. The city stretches from its historic downtown waterfront — added to the National Register of Historic Places in August 2026 — through established neighborhoods like North River Shores and East Stuart to the exclusive gated community of Sailfish Point on Hutchinson Island.

Martin County operates its own waste system separate from St. Lucie County. Unincorporated Martin County residential curbside service is contracted to Waste Management at (772) 546-7700. The Martin County Solid Waste Transfer and Recycling Facility at 9101 SW Busch Street in Palm City, approximately 8 miles from downtown Stuart, is the primary disposal destination. It is open Monday through Friday from 8 AM to 5 PM and Saturday from 8 AM to noon.

### Local Disposal Options

Martin County allows residents to place bulk waste at the curb on their scheduled garbage collection day. Oversized items tagged by the driver will be collected within 5 business days. The placement rules are specific: all bulk waste must be within 6 feet of the curb, loose debris must be containerized to prevent scattering, and glass panels on doors or windows must be taped. Waste must not be placed under utility lines, on water or gas meters, or within 3 feet of mailboxes or fire hydrants. Appliance doors must be sealed or removed before curbside placement.

These rules work for a piece or two. They do not work for a garage full of furniture, a post-renovation debris pile, or an estate cleanout. A hauler handles the full load in one visit, follows Martin County's disposal rules, and routes usable items to donation centers.

Stuart also offers a Neighborhood Clean Up program for community-organized debris removal events — a useful option for neighborhoods coordinating a shared cleanup, though individual households with immediate needs are better served by a hauler.

## Permits and Ordinances in Stuart

A dumpster on private property in Stuart does not require a permit. Placement on a public street or right-of-way requires a right-of-way permit from the City of Stuart. Dumpsters must be covered at all times, and the rental company is responsible for keeping the surrounding area clean.

Standard junk removal service does not require any permit.

Illegal dumping is strictly prohibited in Stuart, and violators face fines from the City Council. If you have items that need disposal, the proper channels are curbside bulk pickup, self-haul to the Martin County transfer facility, or hiring a licensed hauler.

For HOA communities: under Florida HB 1203, an HOA cannot fine a homeowner for leaving garbage receptacles at the curb within 24 hours before or after the designated collection day. Most HOAs require dumpsters on private driveways and may restrict bulk debris staging to specific pickup days per community CC&Rs.
