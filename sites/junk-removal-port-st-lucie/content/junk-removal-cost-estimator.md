This **junk removal cost estimator Port St. Lucie** page gives you a price range before you talk to anyone. Select your load size, the type of material, and whether there are access challenges. The estimate appears immediately.

## How to Use This Tool

Three inputs:

1. **Load size.** Pick the fraction of a truck your junk would fill: a few items (eighth), a room's worth (quarter), a garage cleanout (half), or a full house (full truck). That sets the volume.
2. **Material type.** Household items, furniture, appliances, construction debris, or yard waste. Heavier materials cost more to haul and dispose of.
3. **Access difficulty.** Ground floor with a clear path, stairs involved, or tight spaces. More labor means a higher number.

From those three answers you get a low and a high. The range covers labor, truck space, and standard disposal fees.

## Where Our Estimates Come From

The numbers behind this tool reflect what Treasure Coast haulers are quoting today. A single item starts around $100. A quarter truckload runs $150 to $250. A half truck lands at $250 to $400. A full truckload of household junk or furniture comes in between $450 and $700.

Those figures assume standard access — ground floor, clear path to the truck, no stairs. They include labor, truck space, and disposal at the St. Lucie County landfill or Martin County transfer facility.

What the tool cannot account for is your specific situation. A 500-pound concrete pile, a refrigerator on the second floor, or a hoarder cleanout will come in above the range it shows. Treat the result as a starting point for a conversation with a hauler, not a binding quote. The [junk removal cost guide](/cost/) explains each pricing factor in more detail.

## Common Questions About Hauling Costs

### How accurate is this junk removal cost estimator?

Accurate enough to set expectations, not accurate enough to replace a hauler's quote. It uses typical Port St. Lucie pricing and assumes a standard residential job. The hauler's on-site or phone estimate will account for your specific load, weight, and access conditions.

### Does the estimate include labor and disposal?

Yes. The range covers the crew's time, truck space, and standard disposal fees at local facilities. It does not include specialty disposal like Freon recovery for refrigerators (usually built into the hauler's appliance removal price separately) or hazardous waste handling (which haulers do not perform).

### What counts as a full truckload?

A standard junk removal truck holds 10 to 15 cubic yards — roughly the volume of a large pickup truck bed stacked six feet high. A full house of furniture, the contents of a two-car garage, or a large renovation debris pile typically fills one truck. If your load exceeds that, the hauler quotes for multiple loads.

### Is there a minimum charge?

Most haulers in the Port St. Lucie area have a minimum around $100, which covers a single item like a couch, mattress, or appliance. The truck still has to drive to your location and make a disposal run, so the minimum exists regardless of load size.

### What if my load is heavier than normal?

Concrete, tile, dirt, and wet construction debris weigh significantly more per cubic yard than furniture or household items. If the truck hits its weight capacity before it fills by volume, the hauler may charge more or limit the load. Mention heavy materials when requesting a quote so the estimate reflects reality.
