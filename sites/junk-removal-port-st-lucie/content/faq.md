## Frequently Asked Questions About Junk Removal

### How much does junk removal cost in Port St. Lucie?

Most jobs fall between $150 and $700. The price depends on how much of the truck your items fill. A single piece of furniture or one appliance runs around $100. A quarter truckload is $150 to $250. A full truckload of household junk lands at $450 to $700. Costs vary by hauler and load conditions. The [cost guide](/cost/) breaks it down in detail.

### What items do junk removal companies take?

Furniture, appliances, electronics, mattresses, yard debris, construction waste, and general household clutter. Essentially anything that is not hazardous. Refrigerators and freezers are accepted — haulers handle the Freon recovery. The [guide on what haulers will not take](/guides/what-junk-removal-companies-wont-take/) covers the exceptions.

### What items do junk removal companies refuse?

Hazardous materials: paint, chemicals, motor oil, gasoline, pesticides, medical waste, asbestos, and biological waste. These require specialized disposal. St. Lucie County accepts household hazardous waste at the facility on Glades Cut-Off Road in Fort Pierce at no charge with proof of residency.

### How fast can I get a pickup scheduled?

Most haulers in the Port St. Lucie area can schedule within a few days, sometimes next-day for smaller jobs. Availability depends on the season — hurricane cleanup and snowbird move-in periods are the busiest. Call early in the week for the best selection of time slots.

### Do I need to move items to the curb?

No. The crew enters the house and carries items out from wherever they sit — bedrooms, attics, garages, lanais. You point at what goes and they handle the rest. If access is tight (narrow stairs, low ceilings, long hallways), mention it when booking so the hauler sends the right crew size.

### What happens to the items after pickup?

Usable items in good condition go to donation centers — the Habitat for Humanity ReStore in Fort Pierce and Goodwill locations across the Treasure Coast are common destinations. Recyclable metals and electronics are separated. Everything else goes to the St. Lucie County landfill on Glades Cut-Off Road or the Martin County Transfer and Recycling Facility on Busch Street in Palm City.

### Is junk removal better than renting a dumpster?

It depends on the project. Junk removal is better when you want everything gone in a single visit without doing any loading yourself. A dumpster rental is better for multi-day renovation projects where debris accumulates gradually. The [junk removal vs. dumpster guide](/guides/junk-removal-vs-renting-a-dumpster/) compares the two in detail.

### Do I need a permit for junk removal?

Not for the removal itself. If a dumpster is placed on a public right-of-way (street, sidewalk, alley), a permit is required from the city or county. Dumpsters on private driveways do not require a permit. Standard junk removal service — where a truck arrives, loads, and leaves — requires no permit. If you live in an HOA community, check your CC&Rs for rules about staging items at the curb.
