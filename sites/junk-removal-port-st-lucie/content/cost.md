## Average Junk Removal Prices

Junk removal is priced by the fraction of the truck your items fill, not by the hour or by weight (though exceptionally heavy loads may carry a surcharge). Here is how the ranges break down in the Port St. Lucie area:

### Single Item Removal

One couch, one mattress, one appliance. Expect around **$100 to $150**. The minimum exists because the hauler still drives a truck to your house, loads the item, and makes a disposal run — the overhead is roughly the same whether they pick up one piece or three.

### Partial Truck Load

This is where most residential jobs land.

- **Eighth of a truck** (a few small items): **$100 to $150**
- **Quarter truck** (a room's worth of furniture or a garage corner): **$150 to $250**
- **Half truck** (a large cleanout, garage purge, or small renovation): **$250 to $400**
- **Three-quarter truck** (whole-room or multi-room clearance): **$350 to $500**

A quarter to half truckload covers the typical Port St. Lucie job: clearing out a spare bedroom, hauling away a living room set and miscellaneous items, or removing a stack of construction debris from a bathroom renovation.

### Full Truck Load

A full truckload handles a whole-house cleanout, an estate cleanup, or a large renovation's worth of debris. Expect **$450 to $700**. Haulers measure their trucks by cubic yards — a standard junk removal truck holds 10 to 15 cubic yards. If the job exceeds one truckload, the hauler quotes for multiple loads upfront.

## Factors That Influence Junk Removal Cost

Five things, roughly in order of impact:

**Volume.** The more truck space your junk occupies, the more it costs. This is the primary variable. A stack of boxes and a couple of chairs is a quarter truck. A three-bedroom cleanout may need a full truck or more.

**Weight.** Most loads are priced on volume alone, but exceptionally heavy materials — concrete, tile, dirt, wet construction debris — can push the price above the standard range because they add fuel cost and may incur higher landfill fees.

**Location and access.** Items on a ground floor with a clear path to the truck cost less labor than items on a second floor, in a tight attic, or behind a narrow hallway. If the crew needs extra time or additional people to get items out of the house, the quote reflects it.

**Material type.** Appliances with refrigerant (refrigerators, freezers, AC units) require Freon recovery, which adds to the disposal cost. Electronics may carry separate recycling fees. Standard furniture and household items are the simplest and cheapest to dispose of.

**Disposal fees.** The St. Lucie County landfill charges a $10 minimum for up to 250 pounds. Martin County's transfer facility on Busch Street in Palm City has its own fee schedule. Haulers build these costs into the quote, but heavy or high-volume loads at the landfill cost more than light ones.

## Ways to Reduce Your Hauling Bill

**Consolidate.** One trip with a half-full truck costs less than two trips with a quarter-full truck each. If you are planning a cleanout, do it all at once rather than scheduling multiple pickups weeks apart.

**Separate donations.** Items in good condition can go to Habitat for Humanity ReStore in Fort Pierce (free pickup at 772-249-0792) or Goodwill locations in the area. Haulers check for donation eligibility, but if you sort usable items ahead of time, the load on the truck shrinks and the price drops.

**Compare quotes.** Haulers in the Port St. Lucie market price competitively. Call two or three and describe the same job. The range between the low and high bid is usually informative.

**Consider timing.** Haulers may offer better availability and pricing on weekdays versus weekends. If your schedule is flexible, ask.
