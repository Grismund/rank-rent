## What Is Residential Junk Removal?

A hauler arrives with a truck and a crew. You walk through the house and point at what goes. The crew carries it out, loads the truck, and takes it away. Anything in usable condition gets checked for donation eligibility at local centers like the Habitat for Humanity ReStore on Okeechobee Road in Fort Pierce or the Goodwill locations in the area. The rest goes to the St. Lucie County landfill on Glades Cut-Off Road.

The difference between this and curbside pickup is scope. Curbside takes what fits in your bin, plus scheduled bulk items. A hauler takes everything in one visit — couch, mattress, boxes of old clothes, broken exercise equipment, the pile of lumber in the garage corner — and handles it start to finish.

### Responsible Disposal

Usable items are diverted to donation centers when possible. Metals and electronics are separated for recycling. The balance goes to the county landfill. Haulers in the Port St. Lucie area are familiar with the county's disposal requirements, including the $10 minimum drop-off fee and the separate handling protocols for e-waste and fluorescent bulbs.
