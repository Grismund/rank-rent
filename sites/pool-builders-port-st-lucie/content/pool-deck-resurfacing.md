Port St. Lucie Pool Builders connects homeowners with local contractors for **pool deck resurfacing Port St. Lucie** projects. The pool deck is the most used surface around your pool, and in the Treasure Coast's subtropical climate it takes constant punishment — UV exposure, standing water, chlorine splash, and foot traffic on surfaces that reach 150 degrees or more in direct sun. When the existing concrete is cracked, stained, or uncomfortably hot underfoot, resurfacing restores the deck without the cost of full demolition and replacement.

## What Is Pool Deck Resurfacing?

Pool deck resurfacing applies a new finish layer over the existing concrete deck. The existing slab stays in place. The contractor repairs cracks and low spots, preps the surface for adhesion, and applies a new coating or overlay system that restores the appearance and improves the texture. It is a faster and less expensive alternative to tearing out the old deck and pouring new concrete.

Resurfacing is appropriate when the existing slab is structurally sound — no major heaving, settling, or structural cracks. If the deck has shifted significantly or sections have sunk, those areas may need mudjacking or partial replacement before resurfacing. The contractor assesses the slab condition during the initial evaluation.

## Pool Deck Resurfacing Materials and Styles

**Cool-deck (Kool Deck)** is a cementitious coating sprayed and troweled onto the existing concrete. Its porous texture stays significantly cooler than bare concrete in direct sun — a critical feature on the Treasure Coast where deck surface temperatures can exceed 150 degrees on a summer afternoon. Cool-deck is the most common pool deck finish in Florida. It is available in a range of colors and provides good slip resistance when wet.

**Spray-deck (acrylic overlay)** is a textured acrylic coating sprayed in thin layers over the existing surface. It offers more color and pattern options than cool-deck, including stamped and stenciled designs that mimic stone, brick, or tile. Spray-deck is slightly more expensive than cool-deck but provides a more customized appearance.

**Paver overlay** installs thin concrete or porcelain pavers directly over the existing deck using a modified thin-set or polymeric sand base. Pavers offer the most design flexibility and a premium appearance. They cost more than coatings but are individually replaceable if a section is damaged.

**Stamped concrete overlay** applies a 1/4 to 3/8 inch polymer-modified concrete layer and stamps it with a pattern while wet. The result mimics flagstone, slate, or tile at a fraction of the cost of natural stone.

All of these options can be applied over the existing deck surface. None require full demolition. The contractor recommends the best option based on the existing deck condition, the desired appearance, and the budget.

## Pool Deck Resurfacing Step by Step

### Surface Assessment

The contractor inspects the existing deck for structural cracks, settling, spalling, and adhesion issues. Minor cracks (hairline to 1/4 inch) are repairable. Wider cracks or areas where the slab has shifted may need routing, filling, or partial replacement before resurfacing. The deck surface is tested for adhesion by checking whether the existing coating is delaminating from the concrete.

### Prep and Application

The deck is pressure-washed to remove dirt, algae, old sealant, and loose material. Cracks are routed and filled. Low spots are built up. For coating systems (cool-deck, spray-deck), a bonding primer is applied, followed by the finish coat in one or more layers. For paver overlays, the surface is leveled and the pavers are set. The pool area is typically sectioned off so part of the deck remains usable during the work.

### Curing and Sealing

Coatings need 24 to 48 hours to cure before foot traffic and 3 to 5 days before furniture is replaced. A sealer is applied to protect the new surface from UV, chlorine, and water penetration. Paver installations can be walked on immediately after the joint sand or mortar sets. Total project time for a standard residential pool deck is 2 to 5 days.
