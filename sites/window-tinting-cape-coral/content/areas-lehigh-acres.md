## Legal Tint Limits in Florida

No permit is required for vehicle window tinting in Florida. Tint shops must comply with Florida Statute §316.2953:

- **Front side windows:** At least 28 percent VLT, no more than 25 percent reflectance
- **Rear side windows (sedans):** At least 15 percent VLT
- **Rear side windows (SUVs/vans/trucks):** As dark as 6 percent VLT
- **Rear windshield (sedans):** At least 15 percent VLT
- **Rear windshield (SUVs/vans/trucks):** As dark as 6 percent VLT
- **Windshield:** Non-reflective tint strip above the AS-1 line only

A sticker identifying legal tinting is required on the driver's side door jamb. Medical exemptions for darker front tint are available under Florida Statute §316.2954 with a physician's prescription. Full details are in the [Florida tint laws guide](/guides/state-window-tint-laws-explained/).
