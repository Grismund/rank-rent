## Legal Tint Limits in Florida

No specialty trade license is required for window tinting in Florida, and no permit is needed for vehicle installations. Tint shops must comply with Florida Statute §316.2953:

- **Front side windows:** At least 28 percent VLT, no more than 25 percent reflectance
- **Rear side windows (sedans):** At least 15 percent VLT
- **Rear side windows (SUVs/vans/trucks):** As dark as 6 percent VLT
- **Rear windshield (sedans):** At least 15 percent VLT
- **Rear windshield (SUVs/vans/trucks):** As dark as 6 percent VLT
- **Windshield:** Non-reflective tint strip above the AS-1 line only

All VLT readings carry a plus or minus 3 percent tolerance. Illegal tint is a primary offense with a $116 base fine and a 30-day fix-it window. Medical exemptions are available under Florida Statute §316.29545 via FLHSMV Form 83390 with a physician's signature.
