## Local Bathroom Remodel in Schertz

Schertz straddles three counties — Guadalupe, Comal, and Bexar — along the I-35 corridor approximately 20 miles south of New Braunfels and 18 miles northeast of downtown San Antonio. The city has grown from 31,465 residents in 2010 to an estimated 46,597 in 2026, fueled by its position in the San Antonio-Austin growth corridor and proximity to Joint Base San Antonio-Randolph.

Roughly 22 percent of Schertz residents are veterans, and the city has been ranked among the top 10 safest cities in Texas and among the best for military families. That military connection shapes the bathroom remodel market in a specific way: Permanent Change of Station (PCS) turnover drives homeowners to remodel bathrooms before listing, often on tight three-to-six-week timelines. Remodelers serving Schertz are familiar with PCS-paced projects.

Schertz water comes from two groundwater sources: the Schertz-Seguin well field on the Carrizo-Wilcox Aquifer and Naco Wells on the Edwards Aquifer. Water hardness measures 176 to 212 mg/L (10 to 12 grains per gallon) — classified as hard. That mineral content causes moderate scale on shower fixtures, glass enclosures, and tile, and reduces water heater efficiency over time.

### Home Ages and Common Bathroom Issues

Schertz's housing stock reflects steady, decades-long growth:

- **Pre-1970 homes (Aviation Heights, original townsite — approximately 8.2 percent of stock):** These homes may have galvanized steel supply pipes and cast-iron drains that need full replacement during a bathroom remodel. Small, compartmentalized bathroom layouts with single-sink vanities are common.
- **1970s through 1990s homes (Northcliffe, Greenfield Village, Woodland Oaks — 37.4 percent of stock):** Builder-grade bathrooms now 25 to 50 years old. Worn tile, corroded fixtures, and inadequate ventilation are the typical triggers for a remodel.
- **Post-2000 homes (The Crossvine, Homestead, The Parklands, Forest Ridge — 54 percent of stock):** Modern PEX plumbing and PVC drains in good condition. Remodels in these neighborhoods are primarily cosmetic — upgrading builder-grade finishes to custom tile, glass enclosures, and premium fixtures.

### Local Housing Market Trends

Median household income in Schertz is approximately $99,948 with a home value-to-income ratio of 3.1x, meaning homeowners have disposable income for bathroom upgrades. Master-planned communities with 2,400- to 3,500-square-foot homes — The Crossvine, Homestead, and The Parklands — are prime candidates for master bathroom remodels that bring the bath up to the standard of the rest of the home.

The PCS cycle creates a predictable remodel pattern: military families buy, live in the home for two to four years, then remodel the bathroom and kitchen before listing for the next rotation. Quick-turnaround mid-range remodels in the $6,000 to $10,000 range are the most common scope in this market.

## Schertz Bathroom Remodel Permits

A building permit is required in Schertz for bathroom remodels involving structural changes, plumbing relocation, or electrical modifications. The city follows the 2018 International Building Code and 2018 International Residential Code. A plumbing permit is specifically required when existing plumbing is relocated, when replacing water heaters or underground piping, or when altering piping inside a wall, ceiling, or beneath a floor. The plumbing repair and replacement permit fee is $60.

Replacement of accessible fixtures in their existing locations — a vanity swap, a toilet replacement, a showerhead change — does not require a permit. All contractors doing business in Schertz must register through the city's online portal at schertz.com; registration is free.

Homeowners who own and occupy their homestead may pull their own permit. Contact Planning and Community Development at 210-619-1750 for permit questions. Permits are valid for 180 days.
