Showers take more daily abuse than any other bathroom fixture — constant water contact, steam, and hard water mineral buildup. In New Braunfels, where the municipal supply tests at 20 grains per gallon, mineral scale accumulates on showerheads, glass, and grout faster than in most Texas cities. After 15 to 20 years, the signs are clear: white scale deposits, discolored grout, caulk failure, and water stains on adjacent drywall.

## Understanding Shower Remodel

A shower remodel replaces or upgrades the shower enclosure, walls, floor, fixtures, and sometimes the plumbing behind it. The scope ranges from cosmetic (new tile over sound substrate, new fixtures) to structural (tear out everything to the framing, repair moisture damage, re-waterproof, and rebuild).

Common shower remodel projects in the New Braunfels area include:

- Replacing a prefabricated fiberglass insert with a custom-tiled walk-in shower
- Retiling an existing shower with updated porcelain tile
- Adding a frameless or semi-frameless glass enclosure
- Installing a curbless shower for accessibility
- Upgrading to a thermostatic valve and multi-head system
- Rebuilding a leaking shower from the studs out

## What to Expect During Shower Remodel

### Planning and Design

The contractor inspects the existing shower, checks for water damage behind the walls, and discusses your goals. Key decisions include tile material, shower size and threshold type, glass enclosure style, showerhead configuration, and niche placement. If plumbing changes are involved — moving the drain, adding a second showerhead, changing valve locations — a permit is required from the City of New Braunfels Building Safety department.

### Demolition and Rough-In

Demo removes the old tile, backer board, shower pan, and fixtures. The crew inspects the framing for moisture damage. In New Braunfels' hard water environment, slow leaks past failed grout or caulk often leave mineral-crusted damage on studs and subfloor that only appears once walls are opened. Damaged framing gets replaced before the rebuild begins.

Plumbing rough-in repositions supply lines and valves as needed. If the drain location changes, the contractor cuts and re-plumbs it through the slab. A rough plumbing inspection is required before closing the walls.

### Finishing and Inspection

Waterproofing goes on first — shower pan liner, wall membrane, and sealed seams. This is the most critical step in any shower build. Backer board follows, then tile installation. The showerhead, valve trim, glass enclosure, and accessories go in last. A shower pan flood test may be required during inspection.

Most shower remodels in New Braunfels take one to three weeks depending on scope, tile complexity, and whether custom glass is ordered.

## Shower Remodel Materials and Styles

- **Tile:** Large-format porcelain tile is the top choice for shower walls in hard-water areas — fewer grout lines means less surface for mineral scale to grip. Subway tile remains popular. Natural stone requires sealing and more upkeep, which is a larger commitment when water hardness is 20 GPG.
- **Glass enclosure:** Frameless glass ($800 to $2,000+ installed) gives a clean, open look and is easier to squeegee than framed doors. Semi-frameless offers a mid-range option. Coated glass that resists water spots is worth considering in this water.
- **Showerhead:** Single fixed head, handheld, rain head, or multi-head systems. Specify models with easy-clean nozzles that resist mineral clogging.
- **Niche:** A recessed shelf built into the shower wall. More practical than hanging caddies and built to last the life of the shower.
- **Bench:** A built-in tile bench adds function and serves as an accessibility feature for aging-in-place homeowners.
