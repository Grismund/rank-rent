This **bathroom remodel cost calculator New Braunfels** homeowners use gives you a price range for your project before you talk to anyone. Select your bathroom type, the scope of work, and the material grade you have in mind. The estimate appears immediately.

## How to Use This Tool

Three inputs:

1. **Bathroom type.** Master bath, guest bath, or half bath/powder room. Each has a different typical size and fixture count that affects the total.
2. **Scope.** Surface refresh (fixtures and cosmetics only), standard remodel (new tile, shower, and vanity), or gut-and-rebuild (strip to studs and reconstruct). Scope determines how much labor and material the project requires.
3. **Material grade.** Builder grade (stock materials), mid-grade (name-brand porcelain and fixtures), or designer grade (natural stone, frameless glass, premium hardware). The grade shifts material costs significantly.

From those three answers you get a low and a high. The range covers materials, standard labor, and basic permitting for the New Braunfels area.

## Where Our Estimates Come From

The numbers behind this calculator reflect what New Braunfels-area contractors are quoting today. Surface refreshes run $3,000 to $6,000, covering cosmetic updates without layout changes. Standard remodels land between $6,000 and $10,000 for new tile, an updated shower, and upgraded fixtures. Gut-and-rebuild projects reach $10,000 to $15,000 or more when the room is stripped and reconstructed.

Those figures assume a bathroom in reasonable condition with no major surprises behind the walls. What the calculator cannot account for is hidden water damage, deteriorated plumbing, or mineral-crusted connections — conditions that are common in New Braunfels after 15 to 20 years of 20 GPG hard water exposure.

Treat the result as a budget number, not a bid. The [bathroom remodel cost guide](/cost/) explains each pricing factor in detail.

*Costs vary by contractor and site conditions.*

## Common Questions About Remodel Costs

### How accurate is this bathroom remodel cost calculator?

Accurate enough to plan a budget, not accurate enough to skip a contractor visit. It uses typical New Braunfels-area pricing and assumes a bathroom in standard condition. The contractor's on-site estimate will account for the actual state of your plumbing, subfloor, and walls.

### Does the estimate include permits and inspections?

It includes basic permit fees for projects that require them (plumbing relocation, electrical changes). In New Braunfels, cosmetic-only refreshes typically do not need a permit. For permitted work, note that the city charges separate fees for plumbing, electrical, mechanical, and fuel gas permits — they are not bundled.

### What is the difference between builder grade, mid-grade, and designer grade?

Builder grade uses stock materials: ceramic tile, a basic vanity, and standard fixtures. Mid-grade steps up to porcelain tile, a semi-custom vanity, and name-brand fixtures with hard-water-resistant features like ceramic disc cartridges. Designer grade includes natural stone tile, custom vanities, frameless glass shower enclosures, and premium finishes throughout.

### How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A surface refresh takes about one week. A standard remodel runs two to three weeks. A full gut-and-rebuild takes three to six weeks. Those timelines include permit inspections where required. The [timeline guide](/guides/how-long-does-a-bathroom-remodel-take/) has a detailed breakdown.

### Does the estimate cover plumbing changes?

It covers basic plumbing connections — connecting a new fixture to an existing supply and drain line in the same location. Relocating a drain, moving a toilet, or rerouting supply lines involves additional labor reflected in the standard and gut-and-rebuild estimates but varies significantly by project. A contractor on site provides the specific number.
