Small bathrooms — typically 25 to 50 square feet — are the workhorses of a household. The guest bath handles visitor traffic and double duty when the master is occupied. The half bath on the main floor gets used by everyone. These rooms accumulate hard water damage just as fast as the master bath. At 20 grains per gallon in New Braunfels, mineral scale on a 15-year-old faucet, discolored grout, and a dated vanity make the room look worse than its age.

## Understanding Small Bathroom Remodel

A small bathroom remodel updates the visible fixtures and finishes without changing the room's layout. The existing plumbing stays in place. The scope typically includes: a new vanity with faucet, a new toilet, updated light fixtures, a new mirror, floor tile, and paint. Some projects add a fresh shower surround or bathtub refinishing if the room has one.

The defining characteristic of a small remodel is what it does not include: no plumbing relocation, no wall removal, no electrical circuit changes. By keeping supply and drain lines where they are, the project avoids the need for a permit in most cases under the City of New Braunfels building code. That reduces cost, shortens the timeline, and keeps disruption to about one week.

## What to Expect During Small Bathroom Remodel

### Planning and Design

The contractor visits the bathroom, verifies the plumbing and electrical condition, and discusses what you want to change. Decisions focus on vanity style, faucet finish, toilet model, tile for the floor, and lighting. The goal is selecting materials that transform the room's appearance while staying within budget.

In New Braunfels' hard water, finish selection matters. Brushed nickel and matte black faucets hide water spots better than polished chrome. Ceramic disc cartridges resist mineral buildup inside the valve.

### Demolition and Rough-In

Demo is minimal in a small remodel. The old vanity, toilet, mirror, and light fixtures come out. Floor tile is removed if being replaced. The contractor inspects the exposed wall and floor areas for moisture damage — particularly around the toilet base and behind the vanity, where slow leaks in a hard-water environment leave mineral-crusted stains and sometimes rot.

No rough-in is needed if plumbing stays in place. The existing shut-off valves, supply lines, and drain connections are verified and updated if corroded.

### Finishing and Inspection

New floor tile goes down. The vanity is installed and plumbed. The toilet is set with a new wax ring. Light fixtures and mirror are mounted. Paint and trim complete the project. Because no plumbing or electrical was relocated, a city inspection is typically not required.

A small bathroom remodel takes about one week from start to finish.

## Small Bathroom Remodel Materials and Styles

- **Vanity:** A 24- to 36-inch vanity with an integrated top and sink is standard for a small bath. Stock vanities from home improvement stores run $200 to $600. A floating wall-mount vanity opens up floor space and makes the room feel larger.
- **Toilet:** Comfort-height models are increasingly standard. Elongated bowls fit in most half baths. Dual-flush toilets save water.
- **Floor tile:** Porcelain is the best value for durability and moisture resistance. Ceramic is the budget option. A 25- to 50-square-foot floor does not use much tile, so upgrading material quality has a small impact on total cost.
- **Lighting:** A single overhead fixture plus a vanity light is the standard layout. LED fixtures reduce heat output in a small enclosed space.
- **Paint:** Semi-gloss or satin finish for moisture resistance. Light colors make a small room feel open.

## Small Bathroom Remodel Pricing in New Braunfels

Small bathroom remodels in the New Braunfels area typically range from **$3,000 to $6,000**. The final cost depends on fixture quality, tile selection, and the condition of what is behind the existing finishes.

A half bath with a new vanity, toilet, mirror, light fixture, and paint falls at the low end. A guest bath that includes floor tile, a new tub surround, and upgraded fixtures reaches the higher range.

The fixed costs of a bathroom project — mobilization, material delivery, disposal — do not scale down proportionally for a smaller room. A small bathroom costs more per square foot than a large one, but the total remains significantly lower than a full remodel.
