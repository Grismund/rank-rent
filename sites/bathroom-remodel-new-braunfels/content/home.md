New Braunfels sits in Comal County along the I-35 growth corridor between San Antonio and Austin. The city has grown from a population of roughly 57,000 in 2010 to an estimated 122,492 in 2025, and that expansion shows in the housing mix. The median home here was built around 2006, which means the oldest bathrooms in those subdivisions are approaching two decades of daily wear. But the city's roots go back much further — German settlers founded New Braunfels in 1845, and established neighborhoods near downtown and the Gruene Historic District include homes from every era since the mid-1800s.

Water quality shapes every bathroom remodel in this area. New Braunfels municipal water tests at 20 grains per gallon — classified as very hard. That mineral load causes $1,380 to $2,230 in annual appliance damage per household, exhausts water heater anode rods in two to three years instead of the typical six to eight, and deposits visible scale on fixtures within months. Any remodel here must specify fixtures and finishes that withstand hard water from day one.

The climate adds another layer. New Braunfels averages 35 inches of rain per year with summer highs routinely in the mid-90s. That combination of heat and moisture puts sustained pressure on bathroom surfaces, caulk, and grout. Proper ventilation, waterproof membranes behind tile, and low-porosity materials are baseline requirements in this area, not optional upgrades.

## Bathroom Remodel Services for Your Home

### Why Remodel Your Bathroom?

Three factors drive most bathroom remodels in the New Braunfels area. First, wear from hard water: at 20 grains per gallon, the municipal supply deposits mineral scale on every surface it touches. Showerheads clog, faucet aerators calcify, and grout absorbs minerals that discolor it over time. Second, aging housing stock: a bathroom built in 2006 with builder-grade finishes was constructed to a price point, and after nearly 20 years the fixtures show it. Third, accessibility: an older population in areas like Canyon Lake and Gruene wants curbless showers, grab bars, and comfort-height toilets to age in place safely.

### Types of Bathroom Remodels

Bathroom remodeling covers a wide range of scope and cost:

- **Surface refresh** ($3,000 to $6,000): New vanity, faucet, toilet, and light fixtures. The existing layout stays the same, and no plumbing is relocated.
- **Standard remodel** ($6,000 to $10,000): New tile throughout, an updated shower or tub, a new vanity with countertop, and cosmetic upgrades. Some plumbing changes may be involved.
- **Gut-and-rebuild** ($10,000 to $15,000+): Strip the room to the studs and reconstruct with new plumbing, electrical, tile, fixtures, and potentially a different layout.

## Remodeling Options Available in New Braunfels

New Braunfels Bath Works connects you with contractors who handle five categories of bathroom work:

**[Full Bathroom Remodel](/full-bathroom-remodel/)** is the complete rebuild. Everything comes out, the layout changes if needed, and the room gets rebuilt from the framing up. This is the right scope when a master bath needs to be rethought entirely.

**[Shower Remodel](/shower-remodel/)** covers shower replacement, retiling, glass enclosure upgrades, and converting an aging fiberglass insert into a tiled walk-in. Shower work is the most common single project in a bathroom renovation, especially in an area where hard water degrades grout and fixtures.

**[Bathtub Replacement](/bathtub-replacement/)** means swapping an old tub for a new soaking tub, freestanding model, or walk-in tub. When a tub is cracked, stained beyond cleaning, or simply outdated, replacement is more practical than refinishing — particularly in hard water that shortens refinishing lifespan.

**[Tub-to-Shower Conversion](/tub-to-shower-conversion/)** removes the bathtub entirely and replaces it with a walk-in shower. This is the most requested accessibility upgrade and one of the most popular projects in the New Braunfels area.

**[Small Bathroom Remodel](/small-bathroom-remodel/)** covers guest baths, half baths, and powder rooms where the goal is a meaningful update on a controlled budget.

## The Bathroom Remodel Process

Three steps from first call to finished bathroom.

### Step 1: Request a Quote

Call New Braunfels Bath Works or submit a request through the site. Tell us which bathroom, what you want to change, and your general budget range. We connect you with a local contractor who schedules a free on-site visit. No pressure, no commitment.

### Step 2: Design and Planning

The contractor inspects the bathroom, checks the plumbing and electrical, and discusses your goals. You make decisions about tile, fixtures, layout, and finish level. If the project requires a permit — anything involving plumbing relocation, new drain lines, or electrical changes does in New Braunfels under the 2021 IPC — the contractor handles that before work begins. Cosmetic updates like a new vanity, toilet, or paint typically do not need a permit.

### Step 3: Construction and Completion

Demo, rough-in, waterproofing, tile, fixtures, paint, final inspection. A surface refresh takes about one week. A standard remodel runs two to three weeks. A full gut-and-rebuild can take three to six weeks depending on scope and permit inspection scheduling. The contractor manages the timeline and coordinates all inspections with the City of New Braunfels Building Safety department.
