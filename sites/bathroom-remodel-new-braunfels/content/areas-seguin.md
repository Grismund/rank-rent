## Local Bathroom Remodel in Seguin

Seguin is the Guadalupe County seat, 16 miles east of New Braunfels along I-10. Founded in 1838 by Texas Rangers and known as the "Mother of Concrete Cities" for its concentration of early limecrete buildings, Seguin blends deep history with explosive recent growth. The 2020 Census recorded 29,433 residents; the 2025 estimate of 41,048 represents 39.3 percent growth in five years — making it the fastest-growing city in the San Antonio metro area.

That growth created a housing mix unlike most Texas suburbs. The city has 58 subdivisions under active construction alongside established neighborhoods with homes dating to the 1850s. Bathroom remodel demand here comes from both ends of that timeline: new homeowners upgrading builder-grade finishes in Emerald Forest or Cotton Crossing, and longtime residents modernizing bathrooms in Peirson Park or the downtown Historic District.

Seguin water hardness measures approximately 240 mg/L as CaCO3 (14 grains per gallon) — very hard. The Guadalupe River surface water supply carries heavy calcium and magnesium concentrations that leave mineral scale on fixtures, shorten water heater life, and clog low-flow showerheads. Bathroom remodels in Seguin should factor in hard-water-resistant finishes and potentially a water softener installation.

### Home Ages and Common Bathroom Issues

Seguin's housing stock spans a range most Texas cities do not match:

- **Historic Downtown and limecrete buildings (1850s–1900s):** Seguin has 19 surviving limecrete buildings from the 1850s and substantial housing stock from the late 1800s through early 1900s. These homes present unique remodeling challenges: thick concrete and limecrete walls make plumbing routing difficult, and original cast-iron drain systems and galvanized supply lines need full replacement.
- **Established neighborhoods (Peirson Park, Blair, Bruns, Leaning Oaks):** Mid-century homes with outdated single-sink vanities, small layouts, and aging plumbing. No HOA restrictions in these neighborhoods give homeowners full freedom in renovation scope.

- **New subdivisions (Emerald Forest, Cotton Crossing, Arroyo Ranch, Nolte Farms):** Modern construction with PEX plumbing requiring mainly cosmetic upgrades. Nearly 5,000 new residential building permits have been issued since 2020.

## Seguin Bathroom Remodel Permits

The City of Seguin requires a building permit for bathroom remodels involving plumbing relocation, electrical modifications, structural changes, or ventilation system alterations. A like-for-like fixture swap does not require a permit. Seguin adopted the 2018 International Building Codes and 2020 National Electrical Code via Ordinance No. 2022-040.

Property owners who own and occupy their homestead can pull their own permit for self-performed work. All hired contractors must register with the City of Seguin's Development Services Center before permit issuance. Building permits in the Downtown Historic District have fees waived, though remodels in historic limecrete buildings may require additional review.

All permits are valid for 180 days with one 180-day extension available. Seguin's 39.3 percent population growth has strained inspection resources — homeowners should plan for potential scheduling delays with the Permits and Inspections department.
