# Local Facts: Hutto, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-15

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Star Ranch: One of Hutto's largest master-planned communities, spanning 3,000+ homesites on 1,000 acres along the SH-130 corridor; built around the Star Ranch Golf Club with resort-style amenities, community pools, and trail networks; homes from $275K to $500K. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/star-ranch-hutto-neighborhood-guide/)
- Emory Farms: Master-planned community two miles north of downtown Hutto with one- and two-story homes featuring brick and stone exteriors, parks, and common green spaces; homes from the low $280Ks to mid-$400Ks. [Bramlett Partners](https://bramlettpartners.com/blog/18-best-neighborhoods-in-hutto)
- Hutto Commercial Historic District: Listed on the National Register of Historic Places; the original downtown centered on East and Farley streets with small shops and community character. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hutto_Commercial_Historic_District)
- Huttoparke: Master-planned community with family-oriented amenities and newer construction; one of the three largest communities in Hutto alongside Star Ranch and Emory Farms. [Cain Realty Group](https://www.cainrealtygroup.com/hutto/subdivisions/)

### Climate
- Average temperatures range from 40 °F to 95 °F over the year; August is the hottest month with an average high of 94 °F and low of 74 °F; January is the coldest with an average high of 60 °F and low of 41 °F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/8020/Average-Weather-in-Hutto-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual rainfall of 36 inches; May is the wettest month at 3.9 inches and July the driest at 1.6 inches. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/hutto)
- Summers are hot and humid; winters are short, cold, and windy; partly cloudy year round; humid subtropical climate. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/8020/Average-Weather-in-Hutto-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Permit Requirements
- No permit is required to place a dumpster on private property in Hutto; placing a dumpster on a public street or right-of-way requires a permit from the City of Hutto. [Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/service-area/austin-tx/hutto)
- The permit application requires the date range of the rental, location of the dumpster, and proof of liability insurance; the city allows a maximum of two dumpster permits per year per property. [Dumpsters.com](https://www.dumpsters.com/service-area/austin-tx/hutto)
- The city's MS4 stormwater permit under the Clean Water Act prohibits disposal of debris or waste in storm drains, creeks, or drainage ditches. [City of Hutto](https://ecode360.com/39881930)

### Local Regulations
- Hutto offers scheduled bulk and brush removal on a quarterly basis; bulk pickup has a limit of 18 cubic feet or 18 bags; items include furniture, appliances, extra garbage bags, yard clippings, and tree trimmings placed curbside outside containers. [City of Hutto](https://www.huttotx.gov/413/Residential-Garbage)
- Garbage containers must be placed at curbside with lids closed by 6 a.m. on collection day and removed from the curb by the next morning per city ordinance. [City of Hutto](https://www.huttotx.gov/413/Residential-Garbage)
- Code Enforcement enforces ordinances relating to dangerous structures, debris, high grass and weeds, inoperable vehicles, and sign code violations; violations can lead to Municipal Court action including search warrants and seizure warrants for removing debris. [City of Hutto](https://www.huttotx.gov/303/Code-Enforcement)
- Junked vehicles visible from public right-of-way must be removed; the owner or occupant of any property within the city shall keep it free of junked vehicles. [eCode360 Hutto](https://ecode360.com/39879852)
- Al Clawson Disposal provides residential garbage service; for additional pickup requests call 512-930-5490. [City of Hutto](https://www.huttotx.gov/413/Residential-Garbage)

### Landfill Location
- Williamson County Landfill is located at 600 Landfill Road, Hutto, TX 78634 — directly adjacent to the city; operated by WM; open Monday-Friday 7 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 7 a.m.-noon. [WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/facilities/williamson-county-landfill)
- Residential disposal fee is $45.00 for cars, SUVs, or pickups without a trailer; brush is $9.50 per cubic yard; mattresses $20.00 each; safety vests mandatory at $5.31. [Dump Fee Finder](https://dumpfeefinder.com/places/williamson-county-landfill-hutto/)
- Georgetown Transfer Station at 250 W.L. Walden Drive, Georgetown, TX 78626 (approximately 12 miles northwest) is an alternative facility. [City of Georgetown](https://directory.curbwaste.com/transfer-stations/tx/williamson-county/city-of-georgetown-transfer-station-facility-georgetown/)

### Recycling Programs
- Goodwill Store & Donation Center at 560 US-79, Hutto, TX 78634; phone 512-846-1131; open Monday-Saturday 10 a.m.-8 p.m. and Sunday 10 a.m.-7 p.m.; drive-thru donation drop-off at rear (no pickups). [Goodwill Central Texas](https://www.goodwillcentraltexas.org/retail-stores/)
- Williamson County Recycling Center at 600 Landfill Road, Hutto, TX 78634 accepts oil, oil filters, scrap metal, and old computers/printers; phone 512-759-8881. [WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/facilities/williamson-county-landfill/recycling-center-and-daily-operations)
- Keep Hutto Beautiful hosts an annual free Junk Round Up (October 2026) at 1600 Innovation Blvd, Hutto; accepts electronics, metal, brush, furniture, tires, and general bulk trash; must show ID or utility bill. [Keep Hutto Beautiful](https://www.keephuttobeautiful.org/junkrounduptemplate)
- Habitat for Humanity ReStore in Round Rock at 3916 Gattis School Road, Suite 104 (8 miles west) accepts furniture, appliances, and building materials; phone 512-863-4344. [Habitat for Humanity of Williamson County](https://www.findhelp.org/habitat-for-humanity-of-williamson-county--round-rock-tx--restore-/5652677467308032?postal=78613)

### HOA Rules
- Master-planned communities in Hutto (Star Ranch, Emory Farms, Huttoparke, Brooklands) have HOA governance with rules on exterior maintenance, curbside storage, and dumpster placement; residents should check CC&Rs before scheduling junk removal. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-hutto-tx/)
- Common HOA rules in Williamson County require trash bins stored out of sight (usually in the garage) and removed from the curb within 24 hours of pickup. [The Bin Spa](https://thebinspa.com/blog/trash-bin-cleaning-amp-hoa-rules-in-central-texas-what-you-need-to-know)
- Most HOAs require dumpsters to remain on private driveways or designated service areas; containers visible from the road may need privacy screens or fencing. [Fuzion Field Services](https://fuzionfs.com/resources/hoa-dumpster-rental/)
