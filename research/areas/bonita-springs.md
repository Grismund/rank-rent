# Local Facts: Bonita Springs, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring, paver-sealing, window-tinting
**County:** Lee County
**Population:** 57,112 (2024) — [Census Bureau QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/bonitaspringscityflorida,US/PST045222)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Bonita Bay: One of the most prestigious gated communities in Bonita Springs; 2,400 acres with five golf courses, a marina, tennis facilities, walking trails, and a private beach park. [Life in Bonita Springs](https://www.lifeinbonitasprings.com/bonita-springs-communities/)
- Pelican Landing: Sought-after gated community with over 3,000 residences across 2,300 acres; amenities include a private beach island, tennis center, and sailing club. [Life in Bonita Springs](https://www.lifeinbonitasprings.com/bonita-springs-communities/)
- Downtown / Old 41: The city center along Old 41 Road near Riverside Park and the Imperial River; features colorful murals, a food truck park, and the new Imperial Crossing multi-use development. [PODS](https://www.pods.com/blog/living-in-bonita-springs-florida)
- Imperial Shores: Quiet neighborhood along the Imperial River with direct Gulf access; a mix of older Florida-style homes and newer construction popular with boaters. [Palm Paradise Realty](https://www.palmparadiserealty.com/bonita-springs/bonita-springs-neighborhood-directory/)

### Climate
- Subtropical climate with an average annual temperature of 75 °F; summer highs reach 91 °F and winter lows average 53 °F. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/bonita-springs-climate)
- Average annual rainfall of 55 inches, with the majority falling between June and September; August is the wettest month at 4.29 inches (109 mm). [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/bonita-springs-climate)
- Distinct dry season (November–April) with monthly rainfall of 1–2 inches, and wet season (June–September) driven by afternoon thunderstorms. [McGreevy and Comisar](https://mcgreevyandcomisar.com/blog/weather-in-bonita-springs)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- Most residential epoxy coating over existing concrete does not require a building permit in Bonita Springs. The City of Bonita Springs administers its own building permits through the Community Development Department. [PermitMint](https://permitmint.com/permits/florida/lee-county-fl/)
- Commercial installations involving structural modifications, floor drains, or fire-rating changes may require a permit. [PermitPlace](https://permitplace.com/county/lee-county-fl-building-permits/)
- Contractors must hold either a State of Florida certification or a Lee County Certificate of Competency, plus current liability and workers' compensation insurance. [Lee County Contractor Registration](https://www.leefl.gov/329/Contractor-Registration)
- Florida HB 735 (effective July 1, 2025) eliminated the local flooring license requirement, but Florida Statute 489.103 requires a state-licensed contractor for work above $2,500 or requiring a building permit. [Flooring Clarity](https://www.flooringclarity.com/do-you-need-a-license-to-do-epoxy-flooring/)
- Many Bonita Springs communities (Bonita Bay, Pelican Landing, The Colony) have HOA architectural review requirements that may apply to visible garage modifications. [WC Pavers](https://wcpavers.com/hoa-paver-approval-checklist-for-southwest-florida/)

### Local Regulations
- Florida Administrative Code Rule 62-296.500 regulates VOC emissions from coating operations at stationary commercial facilities; residential applications are generally exempt. [FL DEP Rule 62-296.500](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/florida/Fla-Admin-Code-r-62-296-500)
- Professional installers in southwest Florida increasingly use low-VOC polyaspartic and polyurea coatings that cure faster and produce fewer fumes. [All Floors Restoration](https://allfloorsrestoration.com/blog/polished-concrete-vs-epoxy-florida)
- Bonita Springs enforces the Florida Building Code (7th Edition, 2023) for all construction and renovation work within city limits. [PermitMint](https://permitmint.com/permits/florida/lee-county-fl/)
- Unpermitted commercial work can result in stop-work orders, fines, complications with property sales, and insurance claim denials. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/florida-painting-permit-questions-for-fort-myers-and-naples)
- Contractors performing work in Bonita Springs must register with Lee County's regional contractor registration system. [Lee County Contractor Registration](https://www.leefl.gov/329/Contractor-Registration)

### Concrete Condition
- Bonita Springs homes sit on concrete slab-on-grade foundations; sandy soils over marine limestone and a high water table cause moisture to wick upward through the slab. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/how-moisture-barriers-work-under-florida-epoxy-floors/)
- Southwest Florida building code requires concrete moisture to be no more than 3 pounds per 1,000 square feet in 24 hours for floor coatings. [Top Gun Garage](https://topgun-garage.com/what-concrete-moisture-content-is-acceptable-for-epoxy/)
- ASTM F2170 requires relative humidity readings below 75% in concrete slabs before applying epoxy; 85% of epoxy failures stem from sub-surface moisture. [Top Gun Garage](https://topgun-garage.com/why-concrete-moisture-matters-for-garage-epoxy/)
- Bonita Springs' coastal location means higher ambient humidity and salt air exposure than inland communities, increasing slab moisture risks. [Self-Leveling Florida](https://www.selflevelingflorida.com/blog/concrete-moisture-problems)
- Older Bonita Springs homes (pre-2000) may lack modern vapor barriers under slabs, requiring moisture mitigation systems before epoxy application. [Epoxy Pro Solutions](https://www.epoxyprosolutions.com/post/understanding-moisture-content-in-concrete-and-its-impact-on-epoxy-flooring)

### Humidity Impact
- Annual humidity in Bonita Springs ranges from 65–80%, with summer peaks exceeding 85%; south Florida air carries roughly 20% more moisture than the national average. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/why-diy-epoxy-garage-floors-fail-in-florida-humidity)
- High humidity slows epoxy curing times, leaving coatings vulnerable to contamination, bubbling, and adhesion failure. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/why-diy-epoxy-garage-floors-fail-in-florida-humidity)
- Coastal humidity and temperature fluctuations can reduce standard garage floor coating lifespans by up to 40% without proper mitigation. [Top Gun Garage](https://topgun-garage.com/how-seasonal-changes-affect-naples-epoxy-floors/)
- Professional installers use 10–14 day curing windows with vapor-retarding membranes instead of the standard 7-day protocol. [Top Gun Garage](https://topgun-garage.com/how-seasonal-changes-affect-naples-epoxy-floors/)
- Polyaspartic coatings are increasingly preferred because they cure in 1–4 hours regardless of humidity, reducing the window for moisture-related failures. [All Floors Restoration](https://allfloorsrestoration.com/blog/polished-concrete-vs-epoxy-florida)

### Garage Usage
- Bonita Springs' upscale communities feature 2- and 3-car garages; homeowners use them for vehicle parking, golf cart storage, workshops, and home gyms. [Cutting Edge HNR](https://www.cuttingedgehnr.com/cape-coral-garage-storage-for-golf-carts-and-workshops)
- Hurricane season demands dedicated garage space for emergency supplies — shutters, plywood, generators, fuel cans, and emergency equipment. [Premier Garage](https://www.premiergarage.com/near-me/miami-fl/local-blog/miami-hurricane-season-garage-storage/)
- Seasonal residents (snowbirds) are particularly common in Bonita Springs' retirement and 55+ communities; they use garages for overflow storage during months away. [Modern Garage FL](https://moderngaragefl.com/blog/garage-storage-solutions-in-fort-myers-florida)
- Florida's heat and humidity stress garage interiors, causing moisture settling on cabinets, shelves, and hardware; epoxy floors help manage moisture and simplify cleanup. [Hamilton & Carter](https://hamiltonandcarter.com/fort-myers-garage-cabinets/)
- Many homeowners do not park vehicles inside; garages become de facto storage units for kayaks, paddleboards, fishing gear, and seasonal decorations. [The Smarter Garage](https://www.thesmartergarage.com/blog/garage-storage-tampa-bay-lifestyles/)

## Niche: paver-sealing

### Permit Requirements
- Re-sanding or sealing existing pavers generally does not require a building permit in Bonita Springs. [MJC Pavers](https://www.mjcpavers.com/blog/do-you-need-a-permit-for-pavers-in-florida/)
- New paver installations at grade level that do not attach to the home structure typically do not require a permit, though setback requirements still apply. [MJC Pavers](https://www.mjcpavers.com/blog/do-you-need-a-permit-for-pavers-in-florida/)
- Paver driveways, retaining walls over 4 feet, and projects affecting drainage or the public right-of-way require a city permit. [PermitMint](https://permitmint.com/permits/florida/lee-county-fl/)
- Work in the city right-of-way (e.g., driveway aprons) requires a separate right-of-way permit from the City of Bonita Springs. [Lee County DOT](https://www.leegov.com/dot/Documents/Permit%20documents/AC-11-12.pdf)
- HOA communities (Bonita Bay, Pelican Landing, The Colony) require architectural review board approval for hardscape changes including paver materials, colors, and patterns. [WC Pavers](https://wcpavers.com/hoa-paver-approval-checklist-for-southwest-florida/)

### Local Regulations
- Bonita Springs follows NPDES stormwater regulations; hardscape projects must account for impervious surface coverage and stormwater runoff. [FL DEP NPDES Manual](https://floridadep.gov/sites/default/files/MS4_Permit_Resource_Manual_0.pdf)
- Coal-tar based sealants produce PAH levels in stormwater runoff 65 times higher than unsealed surfaces; water-based, low-VOC alternatives are increasingly used by local contractors. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2018-02/documents/pah_-coal-tar_sealcoat-swpoll.pdf)
- Solvent-based sealers carry stronger fumes and more environmental restrictions; water-based sealers offer low VOC emissions and reduced ecological impact. [First Coast Property Experts](https://firstcoastpropertyexperts.com/blog/paver-sealing-florida-guide/)
- The Downtown Bonita Springs area is adding brick pavers to roads as part of its streetscape improvement project, burying wires and adding roundabouts. [PODS](https://www.pods.com/blog/living-in-bonita-springs-florida)
- Lee County enforces fertilizer and chemical use blackout periods (June 1–September 30) near waterways; Bonita Springs properties near the Imperial River or Estero Bay may face additional compliance requirements. [Lee County Ordinance](https://www.leegov.com/bocc/ordinances/00-26.pdf)

### Soil Type
- Lee County soil is classified as sand with 87.4% sand composition and a low pH of 5.45, sitting over marine limestone and shell-hash substrate. [Soil By County](https://soilbycounty.com/florida/lee-county)
- Sandy soils compress unevenly under load; proper paver base preparation requires 4–6 inches of compacted crushed shell or gravel. [SW Pavers](https://swpavers.com/paver-base-guide/)
- The most common paver failure in Lee County is improper sub-base preparation for sandy soil combined with intense rainy seasons that wash out base material. [Craft Pavers](https://craft-pavers.com/areas-served/lee-county/)
- Capillary moisture wicking from the water table is more aggressive in southwest Florida's coastal areas like Bonita Springs than on the east coast. [Soil By County](https://soilbycounty.com/florida/lee-county)
- Bonita Springs properties near the Imperial River and Estero Bay have particularly high water tables, requiring extra base depth for stable paver installations. [Fort Myers Landscapes](https://fortmyerslandscapes.com/paver-patios-hardscaping-fort-myers-fl)

### Paver Styles
- Travertine pavers are the most popular choice for Bonita Springs pool decks because they remain cool underfoot in direct Florida sun and offer natural slip resistance. [Tuscan Paving Stone](https://swpavers.com/travertine)
- Concrete pavers dominate the driveway market, available in a wide range of colors, textures, and interlocking patterns. [Progressive Design Build](https://progressivedesignbuild.com/how-to-choose-and-maintain-the-perfect-pavers-for-your-southwest-florida-home-blog/)
- Clay brick pavers provide a timeless look for driveways and walkways; properly maintained, they can last 50–100 years but heat up in Florida sunshine. [EPR Landscaping](https://eprlandscaping.com/blog/best-pavers-florida-driveways-pool-decks/)
- The 45° herringbone pattern is the top choice for driveways due to its structural interlock under heavy vehicle traffic. [Brevard Pavers](https://www.brevardpavers.com/blog/paver-pattern-options-florida-driveways-pool-decks/)
- Bonita Springs' upscale neighborhoods favor premium paver materials — marble, limestone, and designer-series concrete pavers with textured finishes. [Progressive Design Build](https://progressivedesignbuild.com/how-to-choose-and-maintain-the-perfect-pavers-for-your-southwest-florida-home-blog/)

### Salt Exposure
- Oceanfront and canal-side properties in Lee County see paver deterioration 40–60% faster than inland homes. [HD Surface Solutions](https://www.hdsurfacesolutions.com/lee-county-paver-sealing/)
- Bonita Springs properties — many within a few miles of the Gulf and Estero Bay — are exposed to constant salt-laden air that can cut sealer lifespan roughly in half. [Palm Shine Pros](https://www.palmshinepros.com/post/weather-impacts-paver-sealing-florida)
- Salt crystallization from Gulf air fractures paver stone from the inside out as one of the key deterioration mechanisms. [Premier Coatings FL](https://www.premiercoatingsfl.com/single-post/the-ultimate-florida-paver-care-guide-how-humidity-salt-air-and-sunshine-affect-your-brick-pavers)
- The combination of UV, salt air, tropical rain, and year-round humidity creates conditions that degrade unsealed pavers faster than virtually any other region in the continental United States. [Premier Coatings FL](https://www.premiercoatingsfl.com/single-post/the-ultimate-florida-paver-care-guide-how-humidity-salt-air-and-sunshine-affect-your-brick-pavers)
- Waterfront Bonita Springs properties need paver sealing every 2–3 years; breathable, salt-resistant sealers are recommended for maximum protection. [Rain Home Service](https://www.fortmyerspaversealing.com/)

## Niche: window-tinting

### Permit Requirements
- No permit is required for vehicle window tinting in Florida. Tint shops must comply with Florida Statute §316.2953 for legal VLT limits. [Florida Court Rules](https://floridacourtrules.com/florida-window-tint-law/)

### Local Regulations
- Front side windows on all vehicles must allow at least 28% VLT (visible light transmittance) with no more than 25% reflectance. [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/florida/)
- Rear side windows and rear windshield on sedans require at least 15% VLT; SUVs, vans, and trucks may go as dark as 6% VLT. [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/florida/)
- Windshields may have a non-reflective tint strip above the manufacturer's AS-1 line only; full windshield tinting is not legal. [Exzact Window Tinting](https://exzactwindowtinting.com/florida-window-tint-law/)
- Colored tint that changes the normal color of the window is prohibited in Florida. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-data/)
- A sticker identifying legal tinting is required on the inside of the driver's side door jamb. [Law Walls](https://lawwalls.com/florida-window-tint-laws)
- Medical exemptions for darker tint are available under Florida Statute §316.2954 with a physician's prescription. [Florida Tint Exemption](https://www.floridatintexemption.com/florida-window-tint-laws)

### Sun Exposure
- The Cape Coral–Fort Myers metro area (including Bonita Springs) averages 264–271 sunny days per year, well above the US average of 205. [Current Results](https://www.currentresults.com/Weather/Florida/annual-days-of-sunshine.php)
- The UV index averages between 7 and 11+ from April through October, classified as Very High to Extreme by the EPA. [UV Blocker](https://uv-blocker.com/blogs/sun-protection/florida-sun-protection)
- Maximum UV index reaches 12 (Extreme category) during April, May, and August. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)
- Fort Myers has some of the highest UV index readings in the continental United States, with an annual average UV index of 7.6–7.7. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)
- Intense year-round sun exposure causes interior vehicle temperatures to exceed 150 °F in summer, accelerating dashboard and upholstery deterioration. [Vitally Doc](https://vitallydoc.com/2026/07/27/vitallydoc-com-blog-sun-damage-skin-aging-fort-myers/)

### Vehicle Types
- SUVs and trucks dominate the Florida market, accounting for nearly 75% of new vehicle sales; sedans make up about 22%. [AutoLand Jax](https://autolandjax.com/blog/most-popular-autos-in-florida)
- Lee County had 607,021 autos and pickups registered plus 70,587 heavy trucks as of January 2024. [FL HSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/pdf/vehicle-vesselreports/cvr_01_2024_ada.pdf)
- Top-selling vehicles in Florida include the Toyota RAV4 (3.3% market share), Honda CR-V, Toyota Camry, Ford F-150, and Chevrolet Silverado. [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/best-selling-cars-in-florida)
- Bonita Springs' affluent demographics favor luxury SUVs (BMW X5, Mercedes GLE, Lexus RX) and premium sedans alongside the ubiquitous pickup trucks. [Florida Farm Bureau](https://floridafarmbureau.com/2023/07/the-most-popular-vehicles-in-florida/)
- Golf carts are common in Bonita Springs' gated communities; tint shops may also offer UV-protective films for golf cart enclosures and home windows. [Cutting Edge HNR](https://www.cuttingedgehnr.com/cape-coral-garage-storage-for-golf-carts-and-workshops)
