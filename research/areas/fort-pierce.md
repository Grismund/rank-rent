# Local Facts: Fort Pierce, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-15

**County:** St. Lucie County
**Population:** 51,737 (2025 est.) — [U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fortpiercecityflorida/PST045224)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Downtown Fort Pierce: Artsy and eclectic district known for vibrant murals, the Sunrise Theatre, galleries, and restaurants along the Indian River waterfront. [Homes.com](https://www.homes.com/local-guide/fort-pierce-fl/)
- Lincoln Park Historic District: One of six historic districts in Fort Pierce, previously named Avenue D; offers historic homes at budget-friendly prices appealing to first-time buyers and investors. [ArcGIS StoryMaps](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/1c07cd90c937407dad2232d1a00c35c8)
- Hutchinson Island: Barrier island offering oceanfront condos and beach houses with direct Atlantic access; split into Hutchinson Island North and South. [Treasure Coast Home Spot](https://www.treasurecoasthomespot.com/fort-pierce-fl-homes-for-sale/neighborhoods/)
- Lakewood Park: Peaceful, family-friendly neighborhood to the north known for serene lakeside setting and well-maintained parks. [Move With Momentum](https://movewithmomentum.com/best-neighborhoods-fort-pierce)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate with temperatures typically ranging from 55 °F to 89 °F year-round. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/18754/Average-Weather-in-Fort-Pierce-Florida-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual rainfall of 55 inches; June is the wettest month at 5.5 inches and December the driest at 1.7 inches. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/fl/fort_pierce)
- Humidity peaks in September at about 81% and is lowest in April at about 66%. [Weather and Climate](https://weatherandclimate.com/united-states/florida-united-states/fort-pierce)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Permit Requirements
- A dumpster placed on private property (driveway or inside a fence line) does not require a permit from the City of Fort Pierce. [Prime Dumpster](https://primedumpster.com/dumpster-rental-fort-pierce-florida-34950/)
- A dumpster placed in a public right-of-way (street, alley, sidewalk) requires a Right-of-Way Permit from the City of Fort Pierce Department of Engineering at (772) 467-3773, located at 100 N. US Highway 1. [City of Fort Pierce Permits](https://cityoffortpierce.com/163/Permits-Additional-Documents)
- Placement must maintain at least 15 feet of clearance from fire hydrants and 10 feet from storm drain inlets; travel lanes and bike paths must remain unblocked. [Prime Dumpster](https://primedumpster.com/dumpster-rental-fort-pierce-florida-34950/)

### Local Regulations
- Residential garbage (green carts) is collected twice weekly; recycling (blue carts) once weekly; yard waste (black carts) and bulk waste once weekly. [City of Fort Pierce Garbage Collection](https://cityoffortpierce.com/204/Garbage-Collection)
- Bulk waste includes items that do not fit in the 96-gallon green cart — furniture, appliances, mattresses, carpet, large toys — and is collected curbside monthly. [City of Fort Pierce Solid Waste](https://www.cityoffortpierce.com/203/Solid-Waste-Division)
- Trash and recycling containers must be at the curb by 7 AM on collection day; containers must not be placed more than 24 hours before or remain more than 24 hours after collection. [City of Fort Pierce Garbage Collection](https://cityoffortpierce.com/204/Garbage-Collection)
- All doors must be removed from refrigerators, washers, dryers, and stoves before placing them curbside. [City of Fort Pierce Garbage Collection](https://cityoffortpierce.com/204/Garbage-Collection)
- Fort Pierce residents pay for garbage and recycling collection via their Fort Pierce Utilities bill. [City of Fort Pierce Solid Waste](https://www.cityoffortpierce.com/203/Solid-Waste-Division)

### Landfill Location
- The St. Lucie County Sanitary Landfill is located at 6120 Glades Cut Off Road, Fort Pierce, FL 34981; phone (772) 462-1768. [St. Lucie County Solid Waste](https://www.stlucieco.gov/departments-and-services/solid-waste)
- The county's 331-acre Baling & Recycling Facility at the same address receives solid waste from all of St. Lucie County. [WasteMap](https://wastemap.org/Facility-Profile.cfm?idsFacility=92&iconType=LF1)
- Waste Pro serves unincorporated St. Lucie County from their facility at 4100 Selvitz Road, Fort Pierce, FL 34981. [St. Lucie County Waste Pro](https://www.stlucieco.gov/departments-and-services/solid-waste/waste-pro-customer-service)

### Recycling Programs
- Habitat for Humanity ReStore at 2904 Okeechobee Rd, Fort Pierce, FL 34947; accepts furniture, appliances, building materials; open Tue–Sat 9:30 AM – 5:30 PM; free donation pickup at (772) 249-0792. [Habitat for Humanity St. Lucie](https://www.habitat.org/fl/fort-pierce/st-lucie-hfh)
- Goodwill Donation Center Indrio at 5009 Turnpike Feeder Rd, Fort Pierce, and Goodwill NOPS at 815 S Kings Hwy, Fort Pierce — both accept gently used household goods and clothing. [MapQuest](https://www.mapquest.com/us/florida/habitat-for-humanity-restore-261569595)
- Atlantic Coast Recycling Inc at 3301 Ave D, Fort Pierce, provides metal recycling services. [Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com/fort-pierce-fl/recycling-centers)
- Recycling Services of Florida (RSF) at 4205 Metzger Road, Fort Pierce, offers commercial recycling with front-load, roll-off containers, and compactors. [Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com/fort-pierce-fl/recycling-centers)
- Household hazardous waste (batteries, fluorescents, chemicals) is accepted at the county facility on Glades Cut Off Road at no charge with proof of residency. [St. Lucie County Hazardous Waste](https://www.stlucieco.gov/departments-and-services/solid-waste/household-hazardous-waste)

### HOA Rules
- Under Florida HB 1203, an HOA cannot fine a homeowner for leaving garbage receptacles at the curb within 24 hours before or after the designated collection day. [Condo Control](https://www.condocontrol.com/blog/florida-hoas-can-no-longer-enforce-these-rules-hb-1203/)
- Most HOAs require dumpsters to remain on private driveways or within designated service areas rather than on the street; a city permit may still be needed for right-of-way placement. [Fuzion FS](https://fuzionfs.com/resources/hoa-dumpster-rental/)
- HOAs may require advance notice or restrict bulk debris placement to specific pickup days; loose debris must be containerized. [NRP Management](https://www.nrp.management/hoa-trash-can-rules/)
- HOAs may fine up to $100 per violation and $1,000 in aggregate under Fla. §720.30851 unless governing documents allow more. [Florida Statutes](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/)

## Niche: pool-builders

### Permit Requirements
- A building permit is required for any in-ground or above-ground swimming pool in Fort Pierce/St. Lucie County under the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023). [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)
- Pool plans must be signed and sealed by a Florida-licensed architect or engineer; a signed and sealed survey showing the proposed pool location is also required. [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)
- Electrical, plumbing, and (if applicable) gas sub-permits are required; a separate structural permit is needed if a screen enclosure is built around the pool. [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)
- Pool permit fee is approximately $461 (including state surcharge) based on the St. Lucie area fee schedule; pool enclosure permits run approximately $180. [St. Lucie Village Permit Fees](https://stlucievillagefl.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PermitFees.pdf)
- Prefabricated above-ground pools under 24 inches deep and under 5,000 gallons are exempt from permitting. [Florida Building Code](http://www.floridabuilding.org/)

### Local Regulations
- Swimming pools must maintain a minimum setback of 5–7.5 feet from side and rear property lines in standard single-family residential zones. [Site Plan Creator](https://www.siteplancreator.com/site-plans/port-st-lucie-fl)
- Public Works engineering approval of the site drainage is required before a pool permit is issued in Port St. Lucie/St. Lucie County. [City of PSL Residential Pool Handout](https://www.cityofpsl.com/files/assets/public/v/5/departments/public-works/documents/residential-pool-handout_2026.pdf)
- Fort Pierce's pool construction permitting timeline typically ranges from 14 to 45 days for plan review, depending on project complexity. [Do I Need a Permit](https://www.doineedapermit.org/permits/cities/fort-pierce-fl)

### Water Table
- St. Lucie County has low ground surface elevations with a high groundwater table; the water table in the Fort Pierce area generally ranges from 4–12 feet below ground surface. [USGS Water Resources Data – St. Lucie County](https://pubs.usgs.gov/wdr/2005/wdr-fl-05-2b/pdf/pp551-564-St-Lucie-County.pdf)
- Sandy soil over limestone with a shallow water table means an empty or partially empty pool shell can lift and crack when the ground saturates; hydrostatic relief valves are standard. [South Florida Pool Authority](https://southfloridapoolauthority.com/high-water-table-pool-issues-south-florida/)
- Dewatering, over-excavation, and French drain systems are standard construction scope near the coast to manage groundwater during pool installation. [Agua Construction](https://aguaconstruction.com/coastal-florida-pool-building-challenges-solutions/)

### Pool Barrier Laws
- Florida Statute 515 (Residential Swimming Pool Safety Act) requires every residential pool or spa to have a compliant safety barrier before final inspection. [Florida Senate – Statute 515.29](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/515.29)
- Pool barriers must be at least 48 inches (4 feet) high on the exterior side, with no gaps larger than 4 inches between components and no more than 2 inches between the fence and the ground. [Pool Fence](https://poolfence.com/florida-pool-fence-requirements-explained-what-section-515-actually-says/)
- All pool gates must be self-closing and self-latching, with the gate swinging outward (away from the pool); the latch release must be at least 54 inches above grade. [Allied Fence and Gate](https://alliedfenceandgate.com/blog/pool-fence-requirements-florida)
- The exterior face of the barrier must be non-climbable; horizontal members spaced between 1.75 and 45 inches apart must be positioned on the pool side of the fence. [HW Fence](https://hwfence.com/blog/florida-pool-fence-laws-requirements-in-2025)

### HOA Pool Rules
- Most Florida HOAs require Architectural Review Committee (ARC) approval before pool construction; verbal approvals from board members are not binding. [Pool Installer Authority](https://poolinstallerauthority.com/hoa-rules-and-pool-installation)
- HOAs frequently enforce setback rules (e.g., pools must sit 10 feet from property lines), fencing heights (minimum 6 feet), and noise limits (below 60 decibels during evening hours). [Pool Installer Authority](https://poolinstallerauthority.com/hoa-rules-and-pool-installation)
- CC&Rs may prohibit above-ground pools; Florida SB 630 (2021) limits some HOA restrictions but does not override existing covenants. [I Moving to Florida](https://imovingtoflorida.com/guide/florida-pool-laws-guide/)
- Private residential pool construction requires a building permit, barrier/fence documentation, and a site plan regardless of HOA status. [Site Plan Creator](https://www.siteplancreator.com/site-plans/port-st-lucie-fl)
