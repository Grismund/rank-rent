## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Pinecrest Lakes: Long regarded as one of the best neighborhoods in Jensen Beach; established community with mature landscaping and family-friendly atmosphere. [Treasure Coast Home Spot](https://www.treasurecoasthomespot.com/blog/exploring-best-neighborhoods-jensen-beach-florida/)
- The Pines (Pineapple Plantation): Many homes back up to preserve areas, providing privacy and an "Old Florida" feel with large lots and native vegetation. [The Haigh Group](https://www.thehaighgroup.com/blog/-floridas-best-kept-secret-jensen-beach-homes-communities-tour/)
- Leilani Heights: Centrally located, budget-friendly neighborhood close to schools, parks, and downtown Jensen shopping areas. [Treasure Coast Home Spot](https://www.treasurecoasthomespot.com/blog/exploring-best-neighborhoods-jensen-beach-florida/)
- Jensen Beach Country Club: Established community with golf course amenities; a mix of single-family homes and condos within walking distance of the club. [Treasure Coast Neighborhoods](https://treasurecoastneighborhoods.com/jensen-beach-florida-neighborhoods/)

### Climate
- Tropical monsoon climate with temperatures ranging from 55 °F to 90 °F year-round; August is the hottest month (avg high 90 °F, avg low 76 °F) and January the coolest (avg high 74 °F, avg low 55 °F). [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/18760/Average-Weather-in-Jensen-Beach-Florida-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual rainfall of 59 inches; September is the wettest month at 5.5 inches, December the driest at 1.7 inches. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/florida/jensen_beach)
- Jensen Beach enjoys 239 sunny days per year; humidity is uncomfortable from May through October. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/florida/jensen_beach)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Permit Requirements
- A dumpster placed on private property (driveway or yard) in Jensen Beach does not typically require a permit. [Go Load Up – Jensen Beach](https://goloadup.com/dumpster-rental/jensen-beach-fl/)
- Jensen Beach is an unincorporated census-designated place in Martin County; any placement on a public right-of-way requires a permit from Martin County. [Wikipedia – Jensen Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Beach,_Florida)
- Permits are also typically needed if the dumpster sits in an area governed by an HOA; check with your specific community for placement rules. [Go Load Up – Jensen Beach](https://goloadup.com/dumpster-rental/jensen-beach-fl/)

### Local Regulations
- Martin County residents may place bulk waste at the curb on their scheduled garbage collection day; items too large for immediate pickup will be tagged and collected within 5 business days. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)
- All bulk waste must be within 6 feet of the curb; loose debris must be containerized to prevent scattering; glass panels must be taped. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)
- Waste must not be placed under utility lines, on water/gas meters, or within 3 feet of mailboxes or fire hydrants. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)
- Unincorporated Martin County residential curbside service (including Jensen Beach) is contracted to Waste Management; call (772) 546-7700 or email wm.martin@wm.com to schedule bulk pickup. [Martin County Garbage, Recycling and Yard Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/Garbage-Recycling-Yard-Waste)
- Appliance doors must be sealed or removed before curbside placement. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)

### Landfill Location
- Martin County Solid Waste Transfer and Recycling Facility at 9101 SW Busch Street, Palm City, FL 34990; phone (772) 288-5772; approximately 10 miles from Jensen Beach. [Martin County Landfill – WasteMap](https://wastemap.org/Facility-Profile.cfm?idsFacility=164)
- Open Mon–Fri 8 AM – 5 PM and Sat 8 AM – 12 PM; self-haul, self-unload drop-off facility. [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/florida/palm-city/county-government-office/2015169134-martin-county-solid-waste-transfer-and-recycling-landfill-facility)
- Household hazardous waste is handled through Martin County's HazMobile program. [City of Stuart HazMobile](https://www.stuartfl.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1034/HazMobile-Household-Hazardous-Waste-Program-PDF)

### Recycling Programs
- Habitat for Humanity of Martin County ReStore at 2090 NW Federal Hwy, Jensen Beach; accepts furniture, appliances, building materials; has diverted over 1,200 tons of material from landfills in under 5 years. [Habitat Martin ReStore](https://www.habitatmartin.org/p/166/about-the-restore)
- Goodwill Donation Center at 3491 NW Federal Hwy, Jensen Beach, FL 34957; accepts clothing, household goods, and electronics with drive-thru drop-off. [MapQuest](https://www.mapquest.com/us/florida/goodwill-donation-center-jensen-beach-777946070)
- Treasure Coast E-Waste serves Jensen Beach for electronics recycling and disposal. [Treasure Coast E-Waste](https://treasurecoastewaste.biz/)
- Martin County provides curbside recycling collection through Waste Management for all unincorporated areas including Jensen Beach. [Martin County Garbage, Recycling and Yard Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/Garbage-Recycling-Yard-Waste)

### HOA Rules
- Under Florida HB 1203, an HOA cannot fine a homeowner for leaving garbage receptacles at the curb within 24 hours before or after the designated collection day. [Condo Control](https://www.condocontrol.com/blog/florida-hoas-can-no-longer-enforce-these-rules-hb-1203/)
- The Jensen Beach Country Club HOA provides specific waste management guidance to residents, including designated pickup days and container storage rules. [JBC HOA Waste Management](https://www.jbcchoa.com/waste-management)
- Most HOAs require dumpsters to remain on private driveways or designated service areas; a county permit may still be needed for right-of-way placement. [Fuzion FS](https://fuzionfs.com/resources/hoa-dumpster-rental/)
- HOA bulk trash restrictions may require advance notice or adherence to specific pickup days per community CC&Rs. [NRP Management](https://www.nrp.management/hoa-trash-can-rules/)

## Niche: pool-builders

### Permit Requirements
- A building permit is required for any in-ground or above-ground pool in Martin County under the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023). [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)
- Pool plans must be signed and sealed by a Florida-licensed architect or engineer; barrier/fence documentation and a site plan are required. [Martin County Pool Checklist](https://www.martin.fl.us/resources/checklist-swimming-pool-deck)
- Jensen Beach is unincorporated Martin County; pool permits are processed through the Martin County Building Department at 900 SE Ruhnke Street, Stuart, FL 34994, phone (772) 288-5916. [Martin County Building Department](https://building-department.org/martin-county-building-department/)
- Pool projects also require electrical, plumbing, and (if applicable) gas sub-permits; a separate structural permit is needed for screen enclosures. [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)
- Florida pool permit fees typically range from $200–$900 for residential projects; Martin County fees are calculated from the value of work under the county's fee schedule. [Permit Requirements](https://permitrequirements.com/pool-permit/florida/)

### Local Regulations
- Martin County pool enclosure and slab projects must meet checklist requirements under the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023). [Martin County Pool Enclosure Checklist](https://www.martin.fl.us/resources/checklist-pool-enclosure-slab-requirement-pdf)
- For waterfront properties in subdivisions approved after April 1982, pools must be set back at least 5 feet from the Shoreline Protection Zone or wetland buffer. [Martin County Land Development – Sec. 3.211](https://library.municode.com/fl/martin_county/codes/land_development_regulations_?nodeId=LADERE_ART3ZODI_DIV4MIDEST_S3.211DIERE)
- Plan review for pool projects in Martin County typically takes 2–6 weeks depending on complexity. [Alliance Permitting](https://alliancepermitting.com/guides/florida/martin-county-building-permit)
- Fence barriers 6 feet or less in height are exempt from district setback limitations under Martin County Land Development Regulations Section 3.204. [Martin County Fence Rules](https://flfencerules.com/fence-rules-martin-county-florida/)

### Water Table
- Jensen Beach's coastal Treasure Coast location creates a high water table, with groundwater commonly encountered at 4–6 feet below grade during pool excavation. [Agua Construction](https://aguaconstruction.com/coastal-florida-pool-building-challenges-solutions/)
- An empty or partially empty pool shell can float when the water table rises after sustained rainfall; hydrostatic pressure against the structure is the primary risk. [South Florida Pool Authority](https://southfloridapoolauthority.com/high-water-table-pool-issues-south-florida/)
- Standard mitigation includes dewatering systems, French drains, gravel beds, hydrostatic relief valves, and in some cases raising the pool elevation with fill dirt. [Pool Brokers USA](https://poolbrokersusa.com/pool-high-water-table/)
- The mild climate supports nearly year-round pool use; the prime unheated outdoor swimming season runs March through November (approximately 10 months). [Pool Pad – Fort Pierce](https://www.poolpad.com/pool-service/fort-pierce-fl/)

### Pool Barrier Laws
- Florida Statute 515 requires every residential pool or spa to have a barrier at least 48 inches high on the exterior side, with no gaps larger than 4 inches. [Florida Senate – Statute 515.29](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/515.29)
- All pool gates must be self-closing and self-latching, swinging outward (away from the pool); latch release must be at least 54 inches above grade. [Allied Fence and Gate](https://alliedfenceandgate.com/blog/pool-fence-requirements-florida)
- No more than 2 inches of gap between the fence and the ground; the exterior face of the barrier must be non-climbable. [Pool Fence](https://poolfence.com/florida-pool-fence-requirements-explained-what-section-515-actually-says/)
- Martin County building inspectors administer barrier inspections locally; a Pool Alarm/Barrier Affidavit and FBC 4101.7 compliance inspection may be required. [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)

### HOA Pool Rules
- Most Florida HOAs require Architectural Review Committee (ARC) approval before pool construction; written ARC approval is required — verbal approvals are not binding. [Pool Installer Authority](https://poolinstallerauthority.com/hoa-rules-and-pool-installation)
- HOAs may enforce setback rules, fencing heights (often minimum 6 feet, exceeding the state's 4-foot minimum), and noise limits for pool equipment. [Pool Installer Authority](https://poolinstallerauthority.com/hoa-rules-and-pool-installation)
- CC&Rs may prohibit above-ground pools; Florida SB 630 (2021) limits some HOA restrictions but does not override existing covenants. [I Moving to Florida](https://imovingtoflorida.com/guide/florida-pool-laws-guide/)
- County permit requirements for pools over 24 inches deep are separate from HOA rules; homeowners must comply with both. [I Moving to Florida](https://imovingtoflorida.com/guide/florida-pool-laws-guide/)
