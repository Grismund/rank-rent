## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Buttercup Creek: Classic Cedar Park subdivision developed in the late 1990s with mature landscaping, community pool, and walking trails; served primarily by Leander ISD. [Cedar Park TX Living](https://cedarparktxliving.com/top-neighborhoods-cedar-park/)
- Twin Creeks: Master-planned community with the Twin Creeks Country Club, golf course, resort-style pool, and homes ranging from the $300Ks to $600Ks; one of Cedar Park's flagship neighborhoods. [Bramlett Partners](https://bramlettpartners.com/blog/17-best-neighborhoods-in-cedar-park)
- Ranch at Brushy Creek: Family-friendly community in east Cedar Park with parks, playgrounds, a community center, and nature trails along Brushy Creek. [Cain Realty Group](https://www.cainrealtygroup.com/cedar-park/subdivisions/)
- Cedar Park Town Center: Mixed-use area near the H-E-B Center at Cedar Park (arena), anchoring the city's entertainment and shopping district with restaurants, retail, and newer housing. [Austin Real Estate Homes Blog](https://www.austinrealestatehomesblog.com/cedar-park/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate with hot summers and mild winters; average temperatures range from 42 °F in January to 96 °F in August; average annual high of 79 °F. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/cedar_park)
- Average annual rainfall of 36 inches; May is the wettest month at 4 inches; approximately 73 days of precipitation per year with 231 sunny days. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/cedar_park)
- Essentially no measurable snowfall; winters are short and mild with occasional freezes. [Weather and Climate](https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Rainfall-Temperature-Sunshine,cedar-park-texas-us,United-States-of-America)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Permit Requirements
- No permit is required when a dumpster sits entirely on private property such as a driveway or yard. [Prime Dumpster](https://primedumpster.com/dumpster-rental-cedar-park-texas-78613/)
- Placing a roll-off container in a public right-of-way requires a permit from the Cedar Park Public Works Department; cost is typically $50-$200 with 1-3 business days processing; permits are valid for 7-14 days. [Prime Dumpster](https://primedumpster.com/dumpster-rental-cedar-park-texas-78613/)
- Dumpsters must be located a minimum of 50 feet from any single-family residential property line and 15 feet from existing or proposed trees per city code. [eCode360 Cedar Park](https://ecode360.com/38611190)
- All refuse and recycling containers must be completely screened from public view and the view of adjoining properties. [eCode360 Cedar Park](https://ecode360.com/38611190)

### Local Regulations
- Republic Services provides weekly curbside garbage collection and bi-weekly recycling; carts must be at the curb by 7 a.m. on collection day. [City of Cedar Park](https://cityrulelookup.com/trash-recycling/pickup-rules/cedar-park-tx)
- Bulk trash pickup allows up to 7 oversized items placed curbside next to the green cart on normal service day; accepted items include mattresses, appliances (without doors), and bundled brush under 4 feet and 40 lbs. [City of Cedar Park (@CedarParkTX)](https://x.com/CedarParkTX/status/2066868395002229224)
- Prohibited bulk items include construction debris, hazardous waste (paint, chemicals), tires or auto parts, and TVs or computers. [City of Cedar Park](https://cityrulelookup.com/trash-recycling/pickup-rules/cedar-park-tx)
- Property maintenance violations include accumulation of household trash, brush piles, debris, used building materials, and stagnant water; violations are enforced by Code Compliance (512-401-5208). [City of Cedar Park](https://www.cedarparktexas.gov/518/Common-Violations)
- Junked vehicles visible from public right-of-way are a misdemeanor punishable by a fine up to $200.00 per Texas Transportation Code, enforced locally by Cedar Park Code Compliance. [eCode360 Cedar Park](https://ecode360.com/38611190)

### Landfill Location
- Williamson County Landfill is located at 600 Landfill Road, Hutto, TX 78634 (approximately 25 miles east); operated by WM; open Monday-Friday 7 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 7 a.m.-noon. [WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/facilities/williamson-county-landfill)
- Residential disposal fee is $45.00 for cars, SUVs, or pickups without a trailer; brush is $9.50 per cubic yard; mattresses $20.00 each. [Dump Fee Finder](https://dumpfeefinder.com/places/williamson-county-landfill-hutto/)
- Georgetown Transfer Station (TDS) at 250 W.L. Walden Drive, Georgetown, TX 78626 is closer (approximately 15 miles northeast); open Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m.-3 p.m. [City of Georgetown](https://directory.curbwaste.com/transfer-stations/tx/williamson-county/city-of-georgetown-transfer-station-facility-georgetown/)

### HOA Rules
- Cedar Park has widespread HOA coverage across master-planned communities; HOAs may impose separate rules on dumpster placement, screening, or rental duration beyond city code. [Prime Dumpster](https://primedumpster.com/dumpster-rental-cedar-park-texas-78613/)
- Common HOA rules in Williamson County require trash bins to be stored out of sight (typically in the garage) and removed from the curb within 24 hours of pickup. [The Bin Spa](https://thebinspa.com/blog/trash-bin-cleaning-amp-hoa-rules-in-central-texas-what-you-need-to-know)
- Many HOAs require dumpsters to remain on private driveways or designated service areas and may mandate privacy screens or fencing for containers visible from the street. [Fuzion Field Services](https://fuzionfs.com/resources/hoa-dumpster-rental/)
