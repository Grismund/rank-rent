## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Brushy Creek: Established community dating to 1978 with homes on half-acre lots, mature oak trees, four parks, a community pool, and the Brushy Creek Regional Trail; served by Round Rock ISD. [Austin Real Estate Homes Blog](https://www.austinrealestatehomesblog.com/round-rock/brushy-creek/)
- Teravista: Master-planned community with resort-style amenities, pools, and playgrounds; homes from the mid-$400Ks into the $500Ks; split between Round Rock ISD and Georgetown ISD. [Texas Home Talk](https://texashometalk.com/round-rock-neighborhoods/)
- La Frontera: Mixed-use district along the I-35 corridor with modern apartments, townhomes, and walkable access to La Frontera Village shopping center; Round Rock's most transit-adjacent area. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-to-live-in-round-rock-tx/)
- Old Town Round Rock: Historic downtown area around Main Street with local shops, restaurants, and community character; more affordable entry point with older home stock. [My Texas Home Resource](https://www.mytexashomeresource.com/round-rock/neighborhoods/)

### Climate
- Average annual temperature of 68.1 °F (20.1 °C); August is the hottest month at an average of 85.2 °F and January the coldest at 50.2 °F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/round-rock-764474/)
- Average annual rainfall of 36 inches; May is the wettest month at 4.6 inches and July the driest at 1.8 inches; precipitation occurs on roughly 78 days per year. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/round-rock-764474/)
- Average annual humidity of approximately 67%, peaking at 71% in May; humid subtropical climate (Köppen Cfa). [Weather and Climate](https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Humidity-perc,round-rock-texas-us,United-States-of-America)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Permit Requirements
- No permit is required when a dumpster or roll-off container is placed entirely on private property such as a driveway or parking lot. [Dumpsters on Demand](https://dumpstersondemand.com/states/texas/round-rock-tx/)
- Placing a dumpster on a public street, sidewalk, or right-of-way requires a right-of-way obstruction permit from the City of Round Rock Engineering Department; processing takes 3-7 business days and requires a site plan showing placement location, dates, and traffic control measures. [City of Round Rock](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/city-departments/community-and-neighborhood-services/code-enforcement/)
- Williamson County regulations require keeping roll-off dumpsters at least 10 feet from overhead utility lines. [Dumpsters on Demand](https://dumpstersondemand.com/states/texas/round-rock-tx/)
- Unauthorized street-side placement can result in fines of up to $2,000 per day under Round Rock's solid waste and right-of-way ordinances. [Dumpster Champs](https://www.dumpsterchamps.com/dumpster-rental-round-rock-tx)

### Local Regulations
- Garbage is collected weekly and recycling every other week; carts must be at the curb before 7 a.m. on pickup day. [City of Round Rock](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/city-departments/utilities-and-environmental-services/garbage-and-recycling/)
- In-city residents receive one free bulk pickup per year (up to 5 cubic yards); call Republic Services at 512-255-4980 one week before collection day to schedule. [City of Round Rock](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/city-departments/utilities-and-environmental-services/garbage-and-recycling/)
- Accepted bulk items include non-Freon appliances, furniture, scrap metal, lumber under 4 feet with metal removed, and mattresses; construction debris is not accepted curbside. [Olive Fox Properties](https://olivefoxproperties.com/blog/trash-recycling-and-bulk-pickup-tips-for-round-rock)
- Brush and rubbish accumulation on private property is a code violation; heavy accumulation (broken concrete, appliances, dead trees, limbs over 3 feet) must be removed at owner's expense. [City of Round Rock](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/departments/planning-and-development-services/code-enforcement/environmental-nuisances/)
- Grass and weeds over 12 inches high are a code violation subject to enforcement and contractor abatement charged to the property owner. [City of Round Rock](https://www.roundrocktexas.gov/departments/planning-and-development-services/code-enforcement/environmental-nuisances/)

### Landfill Location
- Williamson County Landfill is located at 600 Landfill Road, Hutto, TX 78634 (approximately 15 miles east of Round Rock); operated by WM; open Monday-Friday 7 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 7 a.m.-noon. [WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/facilities/williamson-county-landfill)
- Residential disposal fee is $45.00 for cars, SUVs, or pickups without a trailer; brush disposal is $9.50 per cubic yard; mattresses are $20.00 each. [Dump Fee Finder](https://dumpfeefinder.com/places/williamson-county-landfill-hutto/)
- Georgetown Transfer Station (TDS) is also available at 250 W.L. Walden Drive, Georgetown, TX 78626; open Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m.-3 p.m. [City of Georgetown](https://directory.curbwaste.com/transfer-stations/tx/williamson-county/city-of-georgetown-transfer-station-facility-georgetown/)

### HOA Rules
- Newer subdivisions including Teravista, Paloma Lake, Walsh Ranch, and Siena have HOA rules that commonly require advance notice for dumpster placement and restrict containers to driveways only. [Dumpster Champs](https://www.dumpsterchamps.com/dumpster-rental-round-rock-tx)
- Most Williamson County HOAs require trash bins to be stored out of sight (typically in the garage) and removed from the curb within 24 hours of pickup. [The Bin Spa](https://thebinspa.com/blog/trash-bin-cleaning-amp-hoa-rules-in-central-texas-what-you-need-to-know)
- Containers visible from the main road may require privacy screens or fencing to reduce visual impact under HOA covenants. [Fuzion Field Services](https://fuzionfs.com/resources/hoa-dumpster-rental/)
