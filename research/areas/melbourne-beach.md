# Local Facts: Melbourne Beach, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** bathroom-remodel
**County:** Brevard County
**Population:** 3,177 (2025 est.) — [Florida Demographics](https://www.florida-demographics.com/melbourne-beach-demographics)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Aquarina Beach and Country Club: Gated ocean-to-river luxury community at the southern end of the barrier island, featuring an 18-hole golf course, private beach access, fishing dock, boat launch on the Indian River Lagoon, and fitness center. [Aquarina CC](https://www.aquarinacc.com/)
- Floridana Beach: Quaint, primarily residential community on the barrier island between the Atlantic Ocean and the Indian River Lagoon, known for affordable beachside living and a laid-back atmosphere. [Homes.com](https://www.homes.com/local-guide/melbourne-fl/floridana-beach-neighborhood/)
- Crystal Lakes / Ocean Ridge: Central Melbourne Beach neighborhoods offering a mix of single-family homes and condos, with some of the town's higher-value properties and direct ocean access. [Move with Momentum](https://movewithmomentum.com/best-neighborhoods-melbourne-beach)
- Beach Woods: One of the most affordable communities in the Melbourne Beach area, set among natural preserves on the western side of the island near the Indian River. [Move with Momentum](https://movewithmomentum.com/best-neighborhoods-melbourne-beach)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate with an average annual temperature of 72.3 deg F; barrier island location provides slightly moderating ocean breezes compared to the mainland. [Weather by County (NOAA)](https://weatherbycounty.com/florida/brevard-county)
- Average annual rainfall of 54 inches, concentrated in summer thunderstorms (June-September); the barrier island is also exposed to tropical storm and hurricane wind and surge. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/county/florida/brevard)
- Annual average humidity of 75%, peaking at 80% in August; ocean proximity adds salt-air exposure that accelerates fixture corrosion. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/@4148826/climate)
- July average high of 90.5 deg F; January average low of 50.5 deg F. [US Climate Data](https://usclimatedata.com/climate/melbourne/florida/united-states/usfl0985)

## Niche: bathroom-remodel

### Permit Requirements
- The Town of Melbourne Beach Building Department at 507 Ocean Avenue issues permits for all construction within town limits; contact 321-724-5860 or building@melbournebeachfl.org. [Town of Melbourne Beach Building](https://www.melbournebeachfl.org/Departments/Building)
- Permits are required for any bathroom remodel involving plumbing relocation, electrical changes, structural modifications, or shower/tub retiling. Cosmetic changes (paint, hardware) are exempt. [FL Permit Help](https://flpermithelp.com/blog/bathroom-remodel-permits-florida)
- Online permitting is available through the Town's BS&A portal; emailed submissions are also accepted. [Town of Melbourne Beach Building](https://www.melbournebeachfl.org/Departments/Building)
- Permit fees for a standard bathroom remodel in Florida run $200-$700 depending on project scope. [FL Permit Help](https://flpermithelp.com/blog/bathroom-remodel-permits-florida)
- As of July 1, 2026, Florida HB 803 allows certain building permit exemptions for small home improvement projects valued under $7,500 on owner-occupied homes. [D&C Homes](https://www.dandcohomes.com/bathroom/bathroom-permit-requirements-florida)

### Local Regulations
- Melbourne Beach enforces the Florida Building Code, 8th Edition (2023), for all disciplines. The town also has additional coastal construction regulations under Chapter 4A of its municipal code. [Melbourne Beach Municipal Code](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/melbournebeach/latest/melbournebeach_fl/0-0-0-29534)
- Bathroom exhaust must terminate outside the building envelope; venting into an attic or soffit cavity is prohibited under the 8th Edition. [Elso Contracting](https://elsocontracting.com/blog/florida-building-code-changes-2026-remodel)
- When shower or tub-surround tile is removed and replaced, Florida Building Code Section R702.4 requires a code-listed waterproofing membrane behind the new tile, inspected before tile installation. [D&C Homes](https://www.dandcohomes.com/bathroom/bathroom-permit-requirements-florida)
- Barrier island properties in flood zones may have additional elevation and construction requirements affecting ground-floor bathroom renovations. [Town of Melbourne Beach Building](https://www.melbournebeachfl.org/Departments/Building)
- All plumbing and electrical work requiring permits must be performed by Florida-licensed contractors. [CMK Construction](https://www.cmkconstructioninc.com/do-you-need-a-permit-to-remodel-a-bathroom-in-florida/)

### Plumbing Codes
- Florida follows its own plumbing code (Florida Building Code — Plumbing, 8th Edition); rough-in plumbing must be completed and inspected before drywall is installed. [Drain Wizard Plumbing](https://drainwizardplumbing.com/brevard-county-plumbing-permits-avoid-costly-mistakes/)
- Minimum bathroom ventilation: 50 CFM intermittent or 20 CFM continuous; exhaust must terminate outdoors. [Florida Building Commission Ventilation Fact Sheet](https://www.floridabuilding.org/fbc/commission/FBC_0706/Education_POC/Fact_Sheets/VentilationandACSystems061606_V3.pdf)
- Rough-in inspection checks drain slope, venting, supply line sizing, and waterproofing of shower pans before tile installation. [Drain Wizard Plumbing](https://drainwizardplumbing.com/services/bathroom-remodeling/)
- A final plumbing inspection verifies fixture connections, water pressure, and leak-free operation before the permit is closed. [H&H Plumbing](https://www.hhplumb.com/bathroom-remodeling-plumbing-brevard-county-fl)
- Inspections in Brevard County typically require 24-48 hours advance notice for scheduling. [Drain Wizard Plumbing](https://drainwizardplumbing.com/brevard-county-plumbing-permits-avoid-costly-mistakes/)

### Typical Home Age
- Median construction year for Melbourne Beach homes is 1974; the average year built is 1990, reflecting a predominantly older housing stock. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/FL/Melbourne-Beach-Demographics.html)
- Approximately 33.8% of homes were built between 1940-1969, and a large additional share dates from the 1970s-1990s; only 7.7% were built after 2000. [Neighborhood Scout](https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/fl/melbourne-beach/real-estate)
- The town has 1,809 housing units, 75.4% of which are detached single-family homes on the narrow barrier island between the Atlantic and the Indian River Lagoon. [Neighborhood Scout](https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/fl/melbourne-beach/real-estate)
- Homes from the 1960s-1970s frequently have original cast iron drain pipes and galvanized steel supply lines that corrode in Brevard County's hard water. Whole-home repiping is common during full bathroom remodels. [Inlet Mechanical](https://inletfl.com/residential-services/residential-plumbing-services/water-line-gas-line-services/residential-re-piping-partial-or-whole-house-services-in-brevard-county-fl/)
- Salt-air exposure on the barrier island accelerates corrosion of metal plumbing components and fixture finishes compared to mainland homes. [Inlet Mechanical](https://inletfl.com/how-hard-water-damages-plumbing-in-brevard-county/)

### Water Quality
- Brevard County water is classified as "hard" to "very hard" at 182 mg/L (10.8 grains per gallon) average hardness in 2025, sourced from the Floridan Aquifer. [Brevard County MUD Water Hardness Report 2025](https://bcmud.org/filestorage/13136/13345/13442/Water_Hardness_-_2025.pdf)
- Hard water causes white scale buildup on showerheads, faucets, glass shower doors, and inside water heaters, reducing lifespan and increasing maintenance. [Inlet Mechanical](https://inletfl.com/how-hard-water-damages-plumbing-in-brevard-county/)
- Barrier island homes face dual exposure: hard water mineral deposits from interior plumbing plus salt-air corrosion from exterior climate, making corrosion-resistant fixtures essential. [Anna's Air and Heat](https://www.annasairandheat.com/blog/why-hard-water-impacts-accelerate-during-summer)
- Water softeners or whole-home filtration are commonly installed; ceramic-disc valve faucets and spot-resistant finishes are recommended. [Inlet Mechanical](https://inletfl.com/water-softener-benefits-for-melbourne-fl-homes-with-hard-water/)
- Water meets all EPA Safe Drinking Water Act standards despite high mineral content. [CertPAH](https://www.certpah.com/what-brevard-county-homeowners-should-know-about-water-quality/)

### Climate and Humidity Factors
- With 75% annual average humidity, 80% summer peaks, and direct ocean exposure, Melbourne Beach bathrooms face the highest mold and corrosion risk in the Palm Bay metro area. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/@4148826/climate)
- Florida Building Code requires bathroom exhaust (50 CFM minimum) to terminate outdoors; on the barrier island, a window alone admits salt air and humidity rather than removing it. [Alliance Construction FL](https://allianceconstructionfl.com/bathroom-ventilation-upgrades-swfl/)
- Recommended materials: porcelain tile (not natural stone), quartz or solid-surface countertops, PVC/composite trim, and mold-resistant drywall (green board or cement board) in all wet areas. [CMK Construction](https://www.cmkconstructioninc.com/best-bathroom-materials-for-florida-humidity-what-lasts-and-what-fails/)
- Stainless-steel or brushed-nickel fixtures hold up better than chrome in salt-air environments; frameless glass shower doors should use protective coatings against both mineral deposits and salt. [Haven HRG](https://www.havenhrg.com/blog/bathrooms/florida-bathroom-mold-prevention)
- Bathroom remodel costs in Brevard County range from $8,000-$18,000 (essential) to $18,000-$40,000 (mid-range) to $40,000-$80,000+ (luxury). [Elso Contracting](https://elsocontracting.com/bathroom-remodel-cost-brevard-county-fl)
