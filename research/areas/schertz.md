# Local Facts: Schertz, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-13
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring
**Counties:** Guadalupe (primary), Comal, Bexar
**Population:** 46,597 (2026 est.) — [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-cities/texas/schertz)

## City Overview

### Location and Geography
- Schertz straddles three counties — Guadalupe, Comal, and Bexar — with City Hall in Guadalupe County; the historic core sits in western Guadalupe County while newer development extends north into Comal and south into Bexar. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schertz,_Texas)
- Located on the I-35 corridor approximately 18 miles northeast of downtown San Antonio and 14 miles south of New Braunfels. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schertz,_Texas)
- Schertz is the largest city in the Randolph Metrocom, the cluster of suburbs surrounding Joint Base San Antonio–Randolph. [Schertz EDC](https://www.schertzedc.com/why-schertz)

### Population and Growth
- Population grew from 31,465 (2010) to 42,267 (2020) to an estimated 46,597 in 2026 — a 10.24% increase since 2020. [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-cities/texas/schertz)
- The city more than doubled from 21,208 residents in 2000, driven by its position in the San Antonio–Austin growth corridor. [Texas Demographics](https://www.texas-demographics.com/schertz-demographics)
- Median household income is approximately $99,948; median home value is $315,578 (Zillow ZHVI, 2026). [HomeSnacks](https://www.homesnacks.com/tx/schertz/) · [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/237555/schertz-tx/)

- 224 sunny days per year; zero inches of annual snowfall. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/zip-code/texas/schertz/78154)

## Epoxy Flooring — Local Facts

### Permit Requirements
- A building permit is required in Schertz for new concrete flatwork (driveways, sidewalks, parking surfaces) at a cost of $100 with two required inspections. Applying an epoxy coating over an existing concrete floor does not require a building permit. [City of Schertz — Concrete Flatwork](https://www.schertz.com/DocumentCenter/View/5235/Concrete-Flatwork---Residential?bidId=)
- The City of Schertz has adopted the 2018 International Building Code (IBC), International Residential Code (IRC), and related 2018 I-codes. [City of Schertz — Building Permits](https://www.schertz.com/456/Building-Permits)
- All contractors doing business in Schertz must register through the city's online portal; registration is free. [City of Schertz — Contractor Registration](https://www.schertz.com/173/Building-Permits-Inspections-Registratio)
- Texas does not require a specific state-level license for epoxy flooring installers; no local epoxy-specific certification exists beyond the general contractor registration. [Flooring Clarity](https://www.flooringclarity.com/do-you-need-a-license-to-do-epoxy-flooring/)
- Planning and Community Development office: 210-619-1750. [City of Schertz — FAQ](https://www.schertz.com/2314/Frequently-Asked-Questions)

### Concrete Condition
- Schertz sits within the I-35 Expansive Clay Corridor; predominant soils include Eddy very gravelly clay loam and Houston Black clay, which can swell 10–30% in volume when wet and crack up to 4 inches wide when dry. [Foundation Risk](https://foundationrisk.org/services/foundation-repair/schertz-tx/) · [Wikipedia — Houston Black Soil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_black_(soil))
- Houston Black clay is a Vertisol with 46–60% smectite (montmorillonite) clay content, making it one of the most reactive soil types in North America. [Hill Country Groundworks](https://hillcountrygroundworks.com/blackland-prairie-clay-guide/) · [USDA Soil Survey](https://ttu-ir.tdl.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/0c1b0ff0-f1b7-4140-bccb-12efa61ed873/content)
- Foundation movement from clay shrink-swell is common even in relatively new Schertz construction; homeowners in Belmont Park, Rhine Valley, and other subdivisions report slab cracking, wall cracks, and door misalignment. [Schertz Concrete](https://schertzconcrete.com/services/foundation-repair-schertz/) · [DuraPier](https://durapier.com/service-areas/schertz-tx/)
- An estimated 70% of Texas foundation failures are caused by expansive clay soil. [DuraPier](https://durapier.com/blog/why-texas-clay-soil-causes-so-many-foundation-problems-and-what-actually-helps/)
- For epoxy flooring, slab movement means proper crack repair and flexible coating systems are essential; rigid coatings over unaddressed slab cracks will re-crack and delaminate. [FS Foundation & Construction](https://fsfoundationandconstruction.com/slab-foundations)

### Humidity Impact
- Schertz's average annual humidity of 64% (peaking at 75% in May) places it in the zone where concrete moisture vapor transmission testing is critical before any epoxy application. [Area Vibes](https://www.areavibes.com/schertz-tx/weather/)
- Epoxy installs optimally when ambient humidity is 40–70% and concrete slab relative humidity is below 75% RH; humidity above 85% can interfere with adhesion. In Schertz, May through September humidity frequently exceeds 65%, narrowing the ideal installation window. [Epoxy of Texas](https://epoxyoftexas.com/how-to-prepare-garage-floor-for-epoxy-in-texas-complete-2026-installation-guide/)
- Professional installers perform moisture vapor emission rate (MVER) tests before coating; slabs above 3–5 lbs per 1,000 sq ft per 24 hours require moisture mitigation primers. An estimated 40% of Texas epoxy failures are attributed to moisture vapor transmission exceeding safe levels. [Epoxy of Texas](https://epoxyoftexas.com/garage-floor-coating-in-texas-complete-2026-guide-to-durable-premium-flooring-so/)
- New concrete should cure at least 30 days before epoxy application. [FloorChem Depot](https://floorchemdepot.com/epoxy-garage-floor-temperature-curing-application-tips/)
- Without a proper under-slab vapor barrier — an issue in some pre-2010 Schertz construction — moisture migrating through the slab causes blistering, clouding, and delamination within 6–12 months. [At Home Flooring Solutions](https://athomeflooringsolutions.com/blog/epoxy-flooring-garage-texas)

### Garage Usage
- Schertz is a bedroom community where the vast majority of homes sit on concrete slab foundations with attached garages; two-car garages are standard, with three-car options increasingly available in newer communities like Homestead, The Parklands, and The Crossvine. [Coventry Homes](https://www.coventryhomes.com/new-homes/tx/schertz/homestead/)
- With 22% of residents being veterans and the proximity to Randolph AFB, many Schertz garages serve dual duty as workshops and storage — military families on PCS rotations use garage space for equipment, tools, and household goods between moves. [VA Loan Network](https://valoannetwork.com/best-neighborhoods-in-schertz-tx-for-veterans/)
- Median household income of ~$100K supports discretionary home upgrades; the Home Value-to-Income ratio of 3.1x means homeowners have disposable income for improvements like garage floor coatings. [HomeSnacks](https://www.homesnacks.com/tx/schertz/)
- New construction homes in The Crossvine and Homestead feature "Texas-sized" 8-foot garage doors and tandem three-car configurations with 400–600 sq ft of total garage floor area — prime candidates for epoxy coating. [Highland Homes](https://www.highlandhomes.com/san-antonio/schertz/the-crossvine)
