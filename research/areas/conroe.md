## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Downtown Conroe: Historic district with local shops, restaurants, and the Crighton Theatre along the main square. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conroe,_Texas)
- Grand Central Park: Master-planned community on the south side of Conroe with trails, lakes, and mixed-use development. [Homes.com](https://www.homes.com/neighborhood-search/conroe-tx/)
- The Woodlands North: Northern extension of The Woodlands master-planned community bordering Conroe. [Neighborhood Scout](https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/tx/conroe/woodlands-north)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Cfa) with a mean annual temperature of 68.7 °F (20.4 °C). [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/conroe-17175/)
- Average annual rainfall of 47.9 inches, with June as the wettest month (4.5 inches); significant rainfall year-round. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/conroe-17175/)
- August is the hottest month at 83.9 °F average; January is the coldest at 51.8 °F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/conroe-17175/)

## Niche: bathroom-remodel

### Plumbing Codes
- The City of Conroe has adopted the 2012 International Residential Code and 2012 International Building Code as adopted by the State of Texas, plus the 2015 International Energy Conservation Code. [Conroe Code of Ordinances](https://library.municode.com/tx/conroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_CH14BUBURE_ARTIIBUCO_DIV4EXBUCO_SS14-91--14-110RE)
- Permits for electrical, mechanical, plumbing, or other technical code work must be obtained in addition to the building permit. [Conroe Code of Ordinances](https://library.municode.com/tx/conroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_CH14BUBURE_ARTIIBUCO_DIV4EXBUCO_SS14-91--14-110RE)
- Plumbing contractors must hold a current Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners license. [Alliance Permitting](https://alliancepermitting.com/guides/texas/conroe-building-permit)
- Cosmetic work (retiling, replacing fixtures, swapping vanities) typically does not require a permit; structural, plumbing re-route, or electrical changes do. [Alliance Permitting](https://alliancepermitting.com/guides/texas/conroe-building-permit)
- No work may commence until a building permit has been issued by the building inspection office. [Conroe Code of Ordinances](https://library.municode.com/tx/conroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_CH14BUBURE_ARTIIBUCO_DIV4EXBUCO_SS14-91--14-110RE)

### Typical Home Age
- Median construction year for Conroe homes is approximately 2006; median home age is roughly 20 years. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Conroe-Demographics.html)
- Only 3.2% of homes were built before 1960; 17.4% built before 1980; the majority from the 1990s and 2000s, reflecting rapid growth. [Best Neighborhood](https://bestneighborhood.org/housing-data-in-conroe-tx/)

### Water Quality
- Conroe water has an average hardness of 8 grains per gallon (moderate-to-hard), sourced from Lake Conroe and the Trinity Aquifer. [Soft Pro Water Systems](https://www.softprowatersystems.com/pages/conroe-water-quality-report-contamination-risks-solutions)
- The Trinity Aquifer's marine sediment composition creates mineral-rich water with elevated calcium and magnesium that accumulates in pipes and fixtures. [Houston Culligan](https://houstonculligan.com/locations/conroe/)
- The San Jacinto River Authority tests the water supply per EPA and TCEQ standards; water is deemed safe for consumption. [Soft Pro Water Systems](https://www.softprowatersystems.com/pages/conroe-water-quality-report-contamination-risks-solutions)

## Niche: pressure-washing

### Mold/Mildew Risk
- Conroe sits on the edge of the East Texas Piney Woods; humidity combined with abundant rainfall and tree cover creates ideal conditions for algae, mold, and mildew on roofs, siding, fences, and concrete. [Prestige Exterior Cleaning](https://prestigeexteriorcleaning.com/2026/04/10/pressure-washing-conroe-tx-2/)
- Homeowners deal with a relentless combination of heat, humidity, pine pollen, mold, and mildew that builds up faster than almost anywhere else in the country. [Prestige Exterior Cleaning](https://prestigeexteriorcleaning.com/2026/04/10/pressure-washing-conroe-tx-2/)
- Sidewalks and front entries develop algae and mildew along shaded edges and around tree root areas. [Space City Washing](https://spacecitywashing.com/pressure-washing-conroe-tx/)

### Tree Coverage
- Conroe roofs develop algae and organic growth faster than many areas because of sustained humidity and organic debris from the surrounding tree canopy. [Prestige Exterior Cleaning](https://prestigeexteriorcleaning.com/2026/07/15/house-washing-in-conroe-tx-professional-exterior-cleaning/)
- Homes under heavy tree cover or with north-facing walls may need service twice annually. [1st Impressions Home Services](https://www.1stimpressionshs.com/pressure-washing-articles/how-often-should-you-pressure-wash-your-home-in-conroe-tx-conroe-power-washing)
- Homes near Lake Conroe or along shaded greenbelts often need exterior cleaning every 6–9 months. [Prestige Exterior Cleaning](https://prestigeexteriorcleaning.com/2026/08/17/how-often-pressure-wash-home-texas/)

### Water Restrictions
- From May 1 through October 31, landscape irrigation is limited to 6:00 PM–10:00 AM; odd addresses on Tuesdays/Fridays, even addresses on Wednesdays/Saturdays. [City of Conroe](https://cityofconroe.org/services/water_conservation/weekly_watering_advice.php)
- Hand watering with a nozzle-equipped hose is allowed any time and any day. [City of Conroe](https://cms3.revize.com/revize/conroetx/Documents/Services/Utility%20Billing/Utility%20Billing%20Inserts/2025/Watering%20Regulations%20Brochure.pdf)
- The city implemented restrictions because rapid growth has taxed water and sewer infrastructure. [Houston Chronicle](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/neighborhood/conroe/article/conroe-water-irrigation-infrastructure-19506664.php)

## Niche: window-tinting

### Tint Laws
- Texas Transportation Code §547.613 requires front side windows to allow at least 25% VLT. [PS Trial Law](https://www.pstriallaw.com/legal-news/texas-window-tint-laws)
- Rear and back side windows have no minimum VLT restriction. [Rayno Film](https://www.raynofilm.com/blog/texas-tint-laws)
- Windshield tint of 25% VLT or more is allowed above the manufacturer's AS-1 line (top 5 inches). [Sun Stoppers](https://sunstoppers.com/tint_laws/texas-tint-laws/)
- Red, blue, and amber tint colors are prohibited on all vehicle windows. [Tint Laws](https://www.tintlaws.com/states/texas/)
- A sticker identifying the installer and manufacturer must be placed between the film and glass on the driver's side window. [Rayno Film](https://www.raynofilm.com/blog/texas-tint-laws)

### Sun Exposure
- Conroe receives approximately 2,800–2,900 hours of sunshine per year with July averaging 10.1 hours of sunshine per day. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/texas-usa/conroe-climate)
- UV index reaches Extreme levels (11) in May; June through August average a UV index of 7. [Weather Online](https://www.weatheronline.in/Texas/Conroe/UVindex.htm)
- Annual clear-sky UV index average is approximately 6.6–7.0. [HomeFacts](https://www.homefacts.com/uvindex/Texas/Montgomery-County/Conroe.html)

### Vehicle Types
- Montgomery County is part of the Houston–The Woodlands metro area; popular vehicles include full-size trucks (Ford F-150, Chevy Silverado, RAM 1500) common in Texas, plus SUVs and sedans. [Niche](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/conroe-montgomery-tx/)
- Texas has the most registered vehicles of any state; trucks and SUVs dominate the mix in suburban and exurban communities like Conroe. [Texas DMV](https://www.txdmv.gov)
