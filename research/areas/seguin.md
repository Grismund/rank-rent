# Local Facts: Seguin, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-13
**Niche:** epoxy-flooring
**County:** Guadalupe County (county seat)
**Population:** ~41,048 (2025 Census est.) — [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/seguincitytexas/PST045225)

## City Overview

### Location and Identity
- Seguin (pronounced sih-GHEEN) is the county seat of Guadalupe County, located along I-10 roughly 35 miles east of San Antonio. Founded in 1838 by Texas Rangers, renamed for Colonel Juan Seguin, a Tejano hero of the Texas Revolution. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seguin,_Texas)
- Known as the "Pecan Capital of Texas" — home to the World's Largest Pecan sculpture (16 ft x 8 ft) on the Guadalupe County Courthouse square. [Texas Highways](https://texashighways.com/travel/roadside-oddity-worlds-largest-pecan/)
- Also nicknamed the "Mother of Concrete Cities" for having the largest concentration of early concrete (limecrete) buildings in the U.S. at the time of construction. [Texas Time Travel](https://texastimetravel.com/cities/seguin/)
- Home to Texas Lutheran University, a private four-year liberal-arts institution. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seguin,_Texas)

### Population and Growth
- 2020 Census: 29,433. 2025 Census estimate: 41,048 — a 39.3% increase in five years. [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/seguincitytexas/PST045225)
- Fastest-growing city in the San Antonio metro area (2023-2024), 12th fastest in Texas. [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/news-and-media/p/item/57551/seguins-population-surges-to-new-heights-in-latest-census-estimates)
- Over 17,000 residential units in pipeline and 58 subdivisions under construction as of January 2026. Nearly 5,000 new residential building permits issued since 2020. [Legacy Broker Group](https://www.legacybrokergroup.com/seguin-texas-commercial-growth-whats-opening-zoning-traffic/)

- **Northeast Growth Quadrant:** Newer master-planned subdivisions including Emerald Forest, Cotton Crossing, Arroyo Ranch, Nolte Farms, and Hiddenbrooke. [10 Federal Storage](https://10federalstorage.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-seguin-tx)
- **North Seguin / Hwy 46 Corridor:** Newer subdivisions, typical home prices $270K-$350K. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-in-seguin-tx/)

- **Lake McQueeney / Lake Placid:** Waterfront communities south and east of town. [10 Federal Storage](https://10federalstorage.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-seguin-tx)

### Economy
- Manufacturing accounts for nearly 30% of the workforce — over 8,000 manufacturing jobs, more than four times the national average. Highest manufacturing jobs per capita in Texas. [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/business/industry-profiles/advanced-manufacturing)
- Top employers: Schaeffler (~1,750 employees), Caterpillar (~2,000), Seguin ISD (~1,100), CMC Steel Texas (~900), Guadalupe Regional Medical Center (~900), Tyson Foods (~830). [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/site-selection/primary-employers)
- Caterpillar's 1-million-sq-ft engine assembly plant opened 2010; $90 million expansion announced 2024. [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/business/industry-profiles/advanced-manufacturing)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Koppen Cfa). Average annual temperature: 69.4°F. [Weatherbase](https://www.weatherbase.com/weather/weather-summary.php3?s=49537)
- Summer highs: average 97°F in August; winter lows: average 39°F in January. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/seguin)
- Average annual precipitation: 34 inches, with May the wettest month (~3.5 in) and January the driest (~1.8 in). [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/seguin)
- Average annual humidity: 78.8%. Summer months June-September are uncomfortably humid. [Plant Maps](https://www.plantmaps.com/en/clim/f/us/texas/seguin/climate-data)

## Epoxy Flooring — Local Facts

### Permit Requirements
- Residential epoxy coating over an existing concrete slab generally does not trigger a building permit in Seguin, as it does not alter structure, plumbing, electrical, or mechanical systems. Commercial projects involving floor drains, structural modifications, or occupancy changes do require permits. [City of Seguin](https://www.seguintexas.gov/554/Permits-and-Inspections)
- Property owners who own and occupy their homestead can pull their own permit for self-performed work (Seguin Code of Ordinances, Section 18-34). Hired contractors must be licensed with the City of Seguin. [City of Seguin](https://www.seguintexas.gov/554/Permits-and-Inspections)
- Building permit fees are waived in the Downtown Historic District. Building permits are valid for 180 days with one 180-day extension available. [City of Seguin](https://www.seguintexas.gov/554/Permits-and-Inspections)
- Texas does not require a state-level epoxy or floor coating certification; no Seguin-specific license beyond general contractor registration with the city. [City of Seguin](https://www.seguintexas.gov/554/Permits-and-Inspections)
- TCEQ VOC limit for floor coatings is 250 g/L under 30 TAC Chapter 115, Section 115.421. However, Guadalupe County is not in a designated enforcement area, so state-level floor coating VOC limits are not actively enforced here. Standard low-VOC products remain best practice. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/stationary-rules/voc/coating)

### Concrete Condition
- Seguin sits in the Blackland Prairie ecoregion east of I-35. The dominant soil is Houston Black clay — 40-60% clay content with high shrink-swell potential from smectite (montmorillonite) minerals. Locally called "black gumbo." [VoidForm](https://voidform.com/soil-education/blackland-prairie-soil/)
- Houston Black clay cracks 0.5 to 4 inches wide at one foot depth, staying open 90-150 days per year during dry periods. This cyclical expansion and contraction is the leading cause of slab foundation damage in Texas. [Solid Base Foundations](https://solidbasefoundations.com/expansive-clay-soil-texas-foundations/)
- Expansive clay soil causes significant foundation movement in the Guadalupe County area. Slabs may shift, crack, or settle unevenly, directly affecting epoxy adhesion — installers must assess slab condition and complete crack repair before coating. [Two Bros Foundation Repair](https://www.twobrosfoundationrepair.com/resources/soil-types-in-texas)
- New construction typically uses post-tension slab foundations to resist clay movement, but older homes on conventional slabs are more susceptible to cracking and heaving. [StabilTech](https://stabiltechsoil.com/podcast/the-texas-clay-problem-why-post-tension-and-piers-arent-enough/)
- With nearly 5,000 new building permits since 2020, a large share of Seguin's concrete slabs are less than 5 years old — newer slabs are generally better candidates for epoxy, but must still cure at least 30 days before coating. [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/news-and-media/p/item/57551/seguins-population-surges-to-new-heights-in-latest-census-estimates)

### Humidity Impact
- Seguin's average annual humidity of 78.8% is above the national average. Summer months (June-September) are the most problematic for epoxy installation, with ambient humidity regularly exceeding 70% and temperatures above 95°F. [Plant Maps](https://www.plantmaps.com/en/clim/f/us/texas/seguin/climate-data)
- Epoxy installs optimally when concrete moisture is below 75% RH, ambient humidity is 40-65%, and slab temperature is between 55-90°F. Professional installers in Texas target application days with temperatures between 60-85°F and humidity below 65%. [NES Flooring](https://www.nesflooring.com/why-spring-is-the-best-season-to-install-epoxy-floors)
- Best installation seasons: spring (March-May) and fall (September-November), when temperatures are moderate and humidity swings are less extreme. Mid-summer (June-August) extends cure times by 20-40%. [Craft and Coating Epoxy](https://epoxyoftexas.com/professional-epoxy-floor-installation-in-austin-tx-complete-guide/)
- Moisture testing is critical before application: calcium chloride test (ASTM F1869) or in-situ relative humidity probe (ASTM F2170). An estimated 40% of Texas epoxy failures are attributed to moisture vapor transmission exceeding safe levels. [Grizzly Floor Coatings](https://grizzlyfloorcoatings.com/how-temperature-and-humidity-affect-concrete-coating-installation)
- Blackland Prairie clay beneath slabs retains moisture and drives vapor transmission upward through concrete, especially after rain events following dry periods. Moisture-blocking primers or vapor barriers are recommended for slabs on grade in this soil type. [One Stop Epoxy](https://onestopepoxy.com/pages/how-does-humidity-affect-epoxy-flooring-installation)

### Garage Usage
- Seguin's 58 active subdivisions feature predominantly single-family homes with attached two-car garages (typically 20x20 to 24x24 ft, 400-576 sq ft) in the $240K-$350K price range. Median home price approximately $290K — lower than San Antonio ($324K) and Austin ($557K). [TPR](https://www.tpr.org/economy-and-labor/2025-07-17/how-seguin-became-the-areas-3rd-fastest-growing-city)
- Many Seguin residents commute to San Antonio (35 min) or the Austin/San Marcos corridor (55-65 min), using garages as daily-driver parking and workshop space. The Guadalupe River recreation culture means garages also store outdoor equipment. [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/site-selection/location-and-transportation)
- Seguin has 35 registered HOAs concentrated in newer subdivisions. HOA communities typically maintain higher exterior standards, driving demand for garage floor upgrades as a finishing touch on new homes. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-in-seguin-tx/)
- Industrial and commercial floor coating demand is exceptionally strong: Seguin's manufacturing base includes Caterpillar (1M sq ft plant), Schaeffler (1,750 employees), CMC Steel, Tyson Foods, Hexcel, and Georgia Pacific. New spec buildings of 17,000-140,000 sq ft are being developed along I-10. [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/business/industry-profiles/advanced-manufacturing)
- The Tyson Foods plant (~830 employees) and other food processing facilities require USDA-compliant floor coatings — antimicrobial epoxy and urethane systems with coved bases and slip-resistant finishes. [Seguin EDC](https://www.seguinedc.com/site-selection/primary-employers)
