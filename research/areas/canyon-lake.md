# Local Facts: Canyon Lake, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-13
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring
**County:** Comal County
**Population:** 33,883 (2020–2024 ACS 5-Year Estimate) — [U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/canyonlakecdptexas/POP645224)

## City Overview

### Character
- Canyon Lake is an unincorporated census-designated place (CDP) in Comal County, centered on the 8,230-acre Canyon Lake reservoir on the Guadalupe River. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canyon_Lake,_Texas)
- The reservoir was created by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers when Canyon Dam was completed in 1964; the dam rises 224 feet above the streambed and is the highest earthen dam in Texas. [U.S. Army Corps of Engineers](https://www.swf.usace.army.mil/Portals/47/docs/Lakes/Canyon/MasterPlan/Canyon_MP_Final-Signed.pdf)
- The lake has over 80 miles of shoreline, 23 public boat ramps, and eight Corps-maintained park areas for camping, swimming, boating, and picnicking. [Canyon Lake Texas](https://canyonlaketexas.org/)
- Canyon Lake sits at approximately 965 feet elevation on the Edwards Plateau in the Texas Hill Country, about 16 miles northwest of New Braunfels and 45 miles north of San Antonio. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canyon_Lake_(Texas))
- Part of the San Antonio–New Braunfels MSA; served by Comal Independent School District. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canyon_Lake,_Texas)

### Neighborhoods and Communities
- The CDP is a cluster of unincorporated communities rather than a single municipality, including Sattler, Startzville, Canyon City, Cranes Mill, and Hancock. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canyon_Lake,_Texas)
- Sattler and Startzville function as the area's commercial hubs with grocery stores, restaurants, and services along FM 306 and FM 2673. [Herald-Zeitung](https://herald-zeitung.com/opinion/article_b7a8886e-40d0-11eb-ab87-8f898d0d8e9f.html)
- More than 80 subdivisions existed by the mid-1980s; notable current neighborhoods include Mystic Shores (gated, 2,200+ homes), Vintage Oaks at the Vineyard (acreage lots), Canyon Lake Estates, and Oak Shores. [10 Federal Storage](https://10federalstorage.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-canyon-lake-tx)
- Fringe communities include Fischer, Spring Branch, and Smithson Valley. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canyon_Lake,_Texas)

### Population and Growth
- 2010 Census: 21,262; 2020 Census: 31,124 — a 46.4% increase over the decade. [U.S. Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/canyonlakecdptexas/POP645224)
- Currently growing at approximately 2.6% annually; 2024 ACS estimate: 33,883. [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-cities/texas/canyon-lake)
- 85% homeownership rate; median age of 48.2 years (35% higher than Texas median); median household income of $97,301. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Canyon-Lake-Demographics.html)
- 19.9% housing vacancy rate reflects the high proportion of vacation and seasonal homes. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Canyon-Lake-Demographics.html)

### Housing Stock
- Median construction year is approximately 2001; 51% of housing built since 2000. [NeighborhoodScout](https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/tx/canyon-lake/real-estate)
- Residential development began after the dam's completion in 1964; only 1.6% of homes predate 1939, and 7.3% were built between 1940–1969. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Canyon-Lake-Demographics.html)

## Climate

- Hot and humid subtropical climate (Koppen Cfa) with an average annual temperature of 67.9°F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/7105/Average-Weather-in-Canyon-Lake-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- August is the hottest month: average high of 96°F, low of 75°F. January is the coldest: average high of 60°F, low of 43°F. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/canyon_lake)
- Hot season (daily highs above 88°F) runs from June 1 through September 19 — approximately 3.6 months. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/7105/Average-Weather-in-Canyon-Lake-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual precipitation: 36 inches; wettest month is May (3.7 inches), driest is January (1.5 inches). [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/canyon_lake)
- Average annual humidity: 68%. Summer dew points reach 70°F in June–August. January dew point averages 38°F. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/@4678901/climate)
- Lake-adjacent microclimate: the 8,230-acre reservoir adds moisture to the local air; properties on lower coves experience meaningfully higher humidity than surrounding Hill Country communities or homes on the bluffs. [Jon Wayne Service Company](https://jonwayne.com/service-area/canyon-lake-cooling/)

## Epoxy Flooring — Local Facts

### Permit Requirements
- Canyon Lake is unincorporated and falls under Comal County jurisdiction — there is no municipal government, no city building department, and no city-level permit process. [Herald-Zeitung](https://herald-zeitung.com/news/article_db473fb2-9cee-11e0-8142-001cc4c03286.html)
- Unincorporated Comal County does not require a general building permit for residential work. The county only requires permits for septic (OSSF), floodplain development, driveway/right-of-way work (PIPROW), and Fire Marshal review for commercial or multifamily buildings. [Comal County](https://www.comalcounty.gov/286/Permits)
- Residential garage floor coating (epoxy over existing concrete) does not trigger any Comal County permit requirement. [Comal County](https://www.comalcounty.gov/286/Permits)
- Texas does not require a state-level license for flooring or coating contractors; there is no specialty epoxy certification at the state or county level. [Flooring Clarity](https://www.flooringclarity.com/do-you-need-a-license-to-do-epoxy-flooring/)
- Property owners should check subdivision deed restrictions and POA covenants, as some gated communities (Mystic Shores, Oak Shores) may have rules about exterior modifications or contractor access. [Comal County](https://www.comalcounty.gov/286/Permits)
- County Engineer's Office for permit questions: 830-608-2090. [AR Design Build](https://ardesign.build/resources/building-in-bulverde-city-etj-comal-county/)

### Concrete Condition
- Canyon Lake sits on the Edwards Plateau — Hill Country limestone with thin topsoil (often 2–6 inches over solid rock) and intermixed clay pockets. [Hill Country Homesteads](https://hillcountryhomesteads.com/infrastructure/limestone-caliche/)
- Most homes use either post-tension slab-on-grade or pier-and-beam foundations engineered for the rocky limestone substrate. Post-tensioned cables are standard for newer construction. [Valor Concrete](https://valorconcretecompany.com/foundation-types-canyon-lake/)
- The mix of limestone strata and clay creates uneven moisture distribution beneath slabs — during drought the edges dry and shrink while the center stays stable, causing differential movement and hairline cracks that must be filled before epoxy application. [Central TX Foundation Repair](https://centraltxfoundationrepair.com/foundation-repair-canyon-lake-tx/)
- Lakeside properties face additional moisture pressure from the reservoir's water table; more than 60% of homes in the area experience some foundation moisture issue during their lifetime. [Groundworks](https://www.groundworks.com/service-areas/foundation-repair-canyon-lake-tx/)
- Because 51% of homes were built after 2000, many slabs are relatively new and in good condition — but all concrete should be tested for moisture vapor transmission before coating. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Canyon-Lake-Demographics.html)
- Older homes (1960s–1980s lakeside cabins) may have thinner, unreinforced slabs that require grinding and crack repair before epoxy can bond properly. [NeighborhoodScout](https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/tx/canyon-lake/real-estate)

### Humidity Impact
- The 8,230-acre reservoir creates a localized moisture effect: lower-cove properties near the waterline experience higher ambient humidity than homes on the bluffs or in surrounding Hill Country communities. [Jon Wayne Service Company](https://jonwayne.com/service-area/canyon-lake-cooling/)
- Summer dew points average 69–70°F from June through August, regularly exceeding the 60°F threshold where air feels noticeably humid. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/@4678901/climate)
- Epoxy requires application between 60–85°F ambient temperature with relative humidity under 75%; the slab surface must be at least 5°F above the dew point (per ASTM D3276) to prevent condensation under the film. [Concrete Network](https://www.concretenetwork.com/bob_cain/)
- In Canyon Lake's summer heat (90°F+ days from June through September), the application window often shifts to early morning before lake-effect humidity peaks, or to fall/spring when conditions are optimal. [Wes Vollmer Epoxy](https://www.wesvollmer.com/post/how-to-choose-the-right-epoxy-flooring-for-your-hill-country-property-a-comprehensive-guide)
- Moisture vapor transmission from the slab is elevated in lake-proximate properties where groundwater pressure is higher. A calcium chloride test or in-situ relative humidity probe should be standard before every install. [Texas Tough Concrete Coatings](https://texastoughconcretecoatings.com/why-concrete-coatings-fail-and-what-smart-texas-homeowners-should-watch-for/)
- For high-MVT slabs near the lake, a moisture-tolerant epoxy primer or polyurea base coat is recommended over standard epoxy systems. [Garage Floor Coatings Dallas](https://www.garagefloorcoatingsdallas.com/how-garage-floor-epoxy-in-dallas-stops-moisture-vapor-transmission-at-the-source/)
- Ideal application months are October–November and March–April when temperatures are 60–85°F and humidity is moderate. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/7105/Average-Weather-in-Canyon-Lake-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)

### Garage Usage
- Canyon Lake's lake-recreation lifestyle drives heavy garage demand for boat, jet ski, kayak, and paddleboard storage. Multiple dedicated boat/RV storage facilities exist because homeowners need to store watercraft. [Canyon Lake Texas](https://canyonlaketexas.org/boat-and-rv-storage/)
- Many HOAs in deed-restricted subdivisions ban long-term boat or RV storage on driveways, pushing watercraft into home garages — exactly the high-wear, moisture-prone environment where epoxy coatings provide the most value. [10 Federal Storage](https://10federalstorage.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-canyon-lake-tx)
- The 19.9% housing vacancy rate reflects a large stock of vacation homes, weekend lake houses, and seasonal cabins — these properties sit empty for extended periods, making low-maintenance, sealed epoxy floors ideal versus bare concrete that collects dust and moisture while unoccupied. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Canyon-Lake-Demographics.html)
- Garages in lakeside communities regularly handle wet gear (life jackets, wetsuits, fishing equipment, dripping coolers), tracked-in sand and lake sediment, and occasional flooding from heavy rain events — epoxy's seamless, non-porous surface resists water damage and cleans easily. [VRBO](https://www.vrbo.com/vacation-rentals/usa/texas/hill-country/canyon-lake)
- RV and tow vehicle storage is common — Canyon Lake is a destination community where many residents own recreational vehicles, boats with trailers, and off-road vehicles that drip oil, fluids, and lake water onto garage floors. Chemical-resistant epoxy protects against these stains. [RecNation](https://www.recnationstorage.com/boat-storage/tx/canyon-lake/)
