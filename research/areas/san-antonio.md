## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Downtown / River Walk: The city's urban core centered on the San Antonio River Walk, a network of walkways along the San Antonio River with restaurants, shops, and hotels. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Antonio)
- Alamo Heights: Affluent enclave surrounded by San Antonio with tree-lined streets and historic homes. [Niche](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/san-antonio-bexar-tx/)
- Monte Vista: Historic neighborhood north of downtown with early 20th-century architecture and a designated historic district. [Niche](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/san-antonio-bexar-tx/)
- Stone Oak: Large master-planned community on the far north side with newer construction and family-oriented amenities. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-cities-to-live-in-bexar-county/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Cfa) with hot summers and short, cool winters; annual average temperature of 69.8 °F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/san-antonio-417/)
- August is the hottest month with an average high of 95 °F and low of 75 °F; January averages 64 °F high and 43 °F low. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/7137/Average-Weather-in-San-Antonio-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Annual precipitation of approximately 34 inches, peaking in May (4.4 inches). [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/san-antonio-417/)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- San Antonio has adopted the 2024 Edition of the International Building Code; the Development Services Department issues permits and runs inspections. [City of San Antonio](https://docsonline.sanantonio.gov/DSDUploads/2024Ch10Building-RelatedCodesFinal.pdf)
- Residential epoxy over existing concrete generally does not require a permit; commercial projects may require permits depending on scope, fire safety, and ventilation considerations. [Build Spec](https://specta.build/codes/san_antonio_tx)
- Most contractors handle the permit application as part of their service when required. [Home You](https://www.homeyou.com/tx/epoxy-flooring-san-antonio-costs)

### Local Regulations
- Texas does not require a state-specific license for epoxy flooring or concrete coating work. [Prestige 360 Design](https://prestige360design.com/blog/best-epoxy-flooring-companies-san-antonio/)
- School and commercial facilities must meet fire-rated flooring standards: Class I (0.45 W/cm²) or Class II (0.22 W/cm²) depending on occupancy. [Pro Epoxy San Antonio](https://proepoxysanantonio.com/blog/school-university-flooring-san-antonio/)

### Concrete Condition
- San Antonio's caliche-heavy soil expands and contracts with moisture changes, causing slab movement that can crack rigid coatings over time. [Pro Epoxy San Antonio](https://proepoxysanantonio.com/blog/epoxy-flooring-new-construction-san-antonio/)
- San Antonio summers push concrete slab temperatures above 100 °F by mid-morning, affecting coating adhesion and curing. [Global Garage Flooring](https://centraltexas.globalgarageflooring.com/garage-floor-coatings-rainy-weather/)
- Professional installers use ASTM F1869 calcium chloride and ASTM F2170 relative humidity testing on every slab before applying coatings. [San Antonio Epoxy Floor](https://sanantonioepoxyfloor.com/)

### Humidity Impact
- San Antonio averages 74% relative humidity, which significantly impacts epoxy installation; excess moisture in the slab causes bubbling, peeling, or delamination within months. [San Antonio Epoxy Floor](https://sanantonioepoxyfloor.com/)
- October through April is the best window for garage floor epoxy in San Antonio, when lower humidity and cooler temps create ideal adhesion conditions. [Pro Epoxy San Antonio](https://proepoxysanantonio.com/blog/garage-floor-epoxy-san-antonio-installation/)
- Polyurea and polyaspartic systems are more humidity-tolerant and often preferred for summer installs over standard epoxy. [San Antonio Epoxy Floor](https://sanantonioepoxyfloor.com/blog/epoxy-vs-polyaspartic-san-antonio.html)

### Garage Usage
- San Antonio's sprawling suburban neighborhoods (Stone Oak, Alamo Ranch, Helotes) feature homes with two- and three-car garages commonly used for vehicle parking, workshops, and storage. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-cities-to-live-in-bexar-county/)
- Heavy rain events (34 inches annual average) drive demand for sealed garage floors that prevent water damage and standing moisture. [Global Garage Flooring](https://centraltexas.globalgarageflooring.com/garage-floor-coatings-rainy-weather/)
- Military families from Joint Base San Antonio (Fort Sam Houston, Lackland, Randolph) are a significant market segment for garage upgrades. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Antonio)
