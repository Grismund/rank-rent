# Local Facts: San Marcos, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-13
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring
**County:** Hays County (county seat)
**Population:** 91,082 (Jan 2026 city estimate) — [City of San Marcos](https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/1534/Population)

## City Overview

### Geography and Demographics
- San Marcos sits along the Balcones Escarpment where the Edwards Plateau meets the Blackland Prairie, straddling the I-35 corridor between Austin (30 mi north) and San Antonio (47 mi south). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marcos,_Texas)
- Hays County seat; Hays County population approximately 314,441 (2026 est.) with 3.3% annual growth — one of the fastest-growing counties in the US. [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-counties/texas/hays-county)
- Home to Texas State University with 47,513 enrolled students (Fall 2026), making the university population nearly half the city's size. [Texas State Newsroom](https://news.txst.edu/university-statements/2026/fall-record-enrollment.html)
- Median age is 25.5 years — significantly younger than the Texas median — driven by the university population. [Data Commons](https://datacommons.org/place/geoId/4865600)
- Only 30.8% of housing is owner-occupied; 69.2% renter-occupied, reflecting the large student population. [Census QuickFacts via Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/San-Marcos-Demographics.html)
- Median home value approximately $310,000–$320,000 (mid-2026). [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/92599/san-marcos-tx-78667/)
- The San Marcos Premium Outlets and Tanger Outlets together form one of the largest outlet shopping destinations in the US (~1.2 million sq ft, 240+ stores, ~11 million visitors annually). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marcos_Outlet_Malls)

### Neighborhoods and Subdivisions
- **Trace:** Modern master-planned community with resort-style amenities, trails, and I-35 access; active new construction. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/top-neighborhoods-to-live-in-san-marcos-tx/)
- **La Cima:** Master-planned community adjacent to San Marcos Academy; located in a Public Improvement District (PID) with annual special assessments. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/top-neighborhoods-to-live-in-san-marcos-tx/)
- **Kissing Tree:** Gated 55+ active-adult community with golf course, resort amenities, and lock-and-leave living. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/top-neighborhoods-to-live-in-san-marcos-tx/)
- **Blanco Vista:** Master-planned on the north edge toward Kyle; only San Marcos-area subdivision in Hays CISD. [Texas Home Talk](https://texashometalk.com/communities/san-marcos/)
- **Cottonwood Creek:** Newer construction at accessible price points; popular with I-35 commuters. [Texas Home Talk](https://texashometalk.com/communities/san-marcos/)
- **River Bridge Ranch:** New subdivision with single-family homes, pool, dog park, and fishing pond near the San Marcos River. [New Home Source](https://www.newhomesource.com/communities/tx/austin-area/san-marcos)
- Established neighborhoods closer to downtown include Dunbar, Blanco Gardens, Westover, Southwest Hills, and Franklin Square. [HAR.com](https://www.har.com/sanmarcos/realestate/neighborhoods)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Koppen Cfa) with an average annual temperature of 68.9°F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/san-marcos-5930/)
- August is the hottest month: average high 96.5°F, average low 73.3°F. January is the coldest: average high 62.3°F, average low 39.3°F. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/san-marcos/climate)
- Average annual precipitation of 35 inches, with May being the wettest month (~4.8 in) and July the driest (~2.0 in). [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/san_marcos)
- Average annual relative humidity of 67%; highest in May (~72%), lowest in August (~64%). Morning humidity can reach 85%, dropping to 47–59% by afternoon. [Weather and Climate](https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Humidity-perc,san-marcos-texas-us,United-States-of-America)

## Epoxy Flooring — Local Facts

### Permit Requirements
- Residential epoxy coating over existing concrete generally does not require a building permit in San Marcos. Minor updates such as painting, flooring replacement, and fixture swaps do not require approval. [City of San Marcos — Permits](https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/1000/Permits-Applications)
- A permit is required when the work involves structural modifications, subfloor changes, or electrical/plumbing alterations. [City of San Marcos — Permits](https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/1000/Permits-Applications)
- Commercial projects involving floor drains, structural modifications, or fire-rating changes may require building permits. Contact Planning & Development Services at 630 E. Hopkins, 2nd Floor, Building 3; phone 512-805-2630. [City of San Marcos](https://www.sanmarcostx.gov/1000/Permits-Applications)
- San Marcos follows the 2021 International Residential Code (IRC) with local amendments (Ordinance 2023-09). [City of San Marcos — Building Code](https://sanmarcostx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3330/Building-Code-Ordinance-02072023-PDF)
- Texas does not require a state-level license or epoxy-specific certification for flooring contractors. [Check Licensed](https://checklicensed.com/blog/licensed-flooring-contractor-texas)
- Hays County is part of the TCEQ Austin-Round Rock area subject to VOC control rules under 30 TAC Chapter 115. Standard residential epoxy and polyaspartic products are low-VOC and do not trigger reporting requirements. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/stationary-rules/voc/coating)

### Concrete Condition
- San Marcos straddles the Balcones Escarpment, creating two distinct foundation zones. West of I-35: Edwards Plateau limestone and caliche with thin topsoil — slabs rarely heave from clay movement. East of I-35: Houston Black clay (Blackland Prairie) with very high shrink-swell characteristics (Heiden series, linear extensibility ~12.1%). [Olshan Foundation](https://www.olshanfoundation.com/regions/texas/san-marcos/)
- The Balcones Fault runs directly through the city; ground conditions can shift from swelling clay to shallow limestone within a single lot. Epoxy installers must assess each slab individually. [Pinnacle Foundation Repair](https://pinnaclefoundationrepair.com/locations/san-marcos/)
- Expansive clay on the east side creates seasonal slab movement: wet seasons cause heave, dry summers cause contraction and cracking. Visible soil gaps at the foundation perimeter are common in late summer. [Ground Lock Foundation](https://groundlockfoundation.com/service-areas/san-marcos-tx/)
- Homes less than five years old on recently disturbed fill soil in active development corridors (Trace, La Cima, Cottonwood Creek) may show new cracks as fill settles. [Texas Home Talk](https://texashometalk.com/texas-foundation-problems-guide/)
- The San Marcos Springs / San Marcos River corridor adds a third moisture variable: water table conditions near the river create unique moisture cycling that affects slab conditions in nearby neighborhoods. [Olshan Foundation](https://www.olshanfoundation.com/regions/texas/san-marcos/)
- Slab cracks and movement from expansive clay require thorough concrete prep — crack repair, grinding, and moisture testing before coating. Flexible coating systems (polyaspartic or polyurea) may outperform rigid epoxy on slabs with active movement. [New Era Epoxy](https://neweraepoxy.com/faq)

### Humidity Impact
- Annual average relative humidity of 67%, with monthly averages ranging from 64% (August) to 72% (May). Morning humidity frequently reaches 85%. [Weather and Climate](https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Humidity-perc,san-marcos-texas-us,United-States-of-America)
- Epoxy installs best when ambient humidity is below 85% and ideally in the 40–70% range; concrete moisture should be below 75% RH with slab temperatures between 55–90°F. [New Era Epoxy](https://neweraepoxy.com/faq)
- Best seasons for application: spring (March–May) offers moderate temperatures (60–80°F) and stable curing conditions. Fall (October–November) is also favorable. Summer is workable but high heat accelerates curing — installers use additives and fans to manage. [NES Flooring](https://www.nesflooring.com/why-spring-is-the-best-season-to-install-epoxy-floors)
- The concrete slab should remain at least 5°F above the dew point during application and early cure to prevent condensation and adhesion failure. [Epoxy Coat Masters](https://epoxycoatmasters.com/winter-vs-spring-best-time-to-install-garage-flooring-in-texas/)
- During humid summer months, drying period may extend to 72 hours to ensure moisture content falls below 4%. High humidity slows solvent evaporation and can cause cloudy or hazy finish if sealed too early. [Craft and Coating Epoxy](https://epoxyoftexas.com/epoxy-flooring-installation-process-steps-in-texas-the-complete-expert-guide/)

### Garage Usage
- New construction in San Marcos (Trace, La Cima, Blanco Vista, Cottonwood Creek, River Bridge Ranch) overwhelmingly features two-car garages; typical dimensions are 20x20 ft (400 sq ft) to 24x24 ft (576 sq ft). Three-car garages are uncommon. [Pacesetter Homes](https://pacesetterhomestexas.com/new-homes-for-sale-austin/san-marcos-tx/)
- Texas State's 47,513 students create a large rental market (69% of housing is renter-occupied). Student rentals rarely invest in garage floor upgrades, but the 30.8% owner-occupied segment — particularly in master-planned communities — represents the primary epoxy customer base. [Census QuickFacts via Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/San-Marcos-Demographics.html)
- The 55+ Kissing Tree community is a strong epoxy prospect: downsizers converting garages to workshops, hobby rooms, or extra living space value durable, low-maintenance flooring. Lock-and-leave residents want sealed floors that resist dust, moisture, and staining. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/top-neighborhoods-to-live-in-san-marcos-tx/)
- The outlet malls (~11 million visitors/year) and university generate significant commercial and retail flooring demand beyond residential garages — warehouse, restaurant, and retail spaces require industrial-grade epoxy or polished concrete. [Wikipedia — San Marcos Outlet Malls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Marcos_Outlet_Malls)
- San Marcos's outdoor recreation culture (San Marcos River tubing and kayaking, nearby trails) means many garages store sporting equipment, making durable and easy-to-clean flooring attractive. [Visit San Marcos](https://www.visitsanmarcos.com/plan-your-visit/about-san-marcos/destination-cities/)
- Approximately 1,424 construction projects worth $4.23B are active in San Marcos (589 new builds, 835 renovations/remodels), indicating a healthy pipeline of both new-construction and retrofit epoxy opportunities. [Zabalist](https://www.zabalist.com/projects/city/san-marcos)
