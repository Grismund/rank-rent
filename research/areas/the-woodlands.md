# Local Facts: The Woodlands, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** bathroom-remodel, pressure-washing, window-tinting
**County:** Montgomery County (southern portions extend into Harris County)
**Population:** 122,240 (2026 est.) — [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-cities/texas/the-woodlands)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Grogan's Mill: The oldest village in The Woodlands, established in 1974 and named after the Grogan-Cochran Lumber Mill; offers 4,194 homes built between 1971 and 2025 with mature tree canopy and walkable access to shops and parks. [HAR.com](https://www.har.com/blog_132400_the-best-neighborhoods-and-suburbs-of-the-woodlands-tx)
- Town Center: The commercial and entertainment hub of The Woodlands featuring The Woodlands Mall, Market Street, The Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion, and Waterway Square with restaurants, offices, and high-rise residences. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Woodlands,_Texas)
- Indian Springs: A serene 2,200-acre village known for rolling hills, forested landscapes, and diverse housing from single-family homes to luxury custom residences. [Perry Homes](https://www.perryhomes.com/blog/the-woodlands-texas-guide-and-insights)
- Carlton Woods: One of the most exclusive gated communities in the greater Houston area with two private golf courses and the Carlton Woods Clubhouse. [Jo & Co Realty](https://byjoandco.com/2024/12/15/the-best-neighborhoods-and-suburbs-of-the-woodlands-tx/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Cfa) with a mean annual temperature of 68.7 °F; August averages a high of 93 °F and January averages a low of 42 °F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/9298/Average-Weather-in-Conroe-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual rainfall of 49 inches; October is the wettest month at 5.4 inches. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/texas-usa/conroe-climate)
- Relative humidity remains 73%–77% year-round, with September reaching the peak at 77%. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/9298/Average-Weather-in-Conroe-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)

## Niche: bathroom-remodel

### Permit Requirements
- The Woodlands lies in unincorporated Montgomery County; building permits are issued by Montgomery County, not a city building department. [PermitPlace](https://permitplace.com/city/the-woodlands-tx-building-permits/)
- For a typical interior bathroom remodel on public water and sewer, Montgomery County does not require a building permit; permits are triggered when plumbing is relocated, new drain lines are added, or electrical circuits are changed. [Marvel Works](https://www.marvel-works.com/bathroom-remodel-permit-woodlands-montgomery-county/)
- Standard bathroom remodel permits with complete documentation are typically approved in 3–7 business days. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/remodeling-permits-zoning-guide-montgomery-county-2026)
- Cosmetic work such as replacing a vanity, toilet, or flooring typically does not need a permit. [AC Remodeling](https://acremodeling.com/home-remodeling-blog-montgomery-county-remodeling-permits)
- The Woodlands Residential Design Review Committee (RDRC) must approve exterior changes and some structural interior changes that affect the exterior appearance. [Arte Architecture](https://arte-architecture.com/building-or-adding-on-in-the-woodlands-tx-what-homeowners-need-to-know-before-starting-a-home-project/)

### Local Regulations
- Building permits are issued under the International Building Code (IBC) and International Residential Code (IRC), updated on a three-year cycle. [LicensePro](https://licensepro.io/building-permit/the-woodlands-tx/)
- The RDRC enforces The Woodlands Residential Development Standards for all exterior modifications including windows, siding, and roofing visible from the street. [The Woodlands Township](https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf)
- Renovations require two separate approvals: a Montgomery County building permit for structural/mechanical/electrical/plumbing work, and RDRC approval for exterior-affecting changes. [Arte Architecture](https://arte-architecture.com/building-or-adding-on-in-the-woodlands-tx-what-homeowners-need-to-know-before-starting-a-home-project/)
- Bathroom exhaust fans must provide a minimum of 1 CFM per square foot per NEC bathroom specifications. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/what-to-prepare-before-rough-in-inspection-for-bathroom-remodels)
- All bathroom outlets require GFCI protection per National Electrical Code standards. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/what-to-prepare-before-rough-in-inspection-for-bathroom-remodels)

### Plumbing Codes
- Plumbing contractors must hold a current license from the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE). [TSBPE](https://www.tsbpe.texas.gov/)
- Permitted remodels require a rough plumbing inspection before walls are closed and a final inspection after all fixtures are installed. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/what-to-prepare-before-rough-in-inspection-for-bathroom-remodels)
- Shower pan and waterproofing inspections are required before tile is installed. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)
- Texas plumbing code specifies requirements for pipe sizing, venting, fixture spacing, water pressure, and accessibility. [The Woodlands Plumbing Team](https://thewoodlandsplumbingteam.com/bathroom-plumbing/)
- 2025 plumbing standards mandate low-flow fixtures for water efficiency across all new installations. [On Point Plumber](https://onpointplumber.com/plumbing-code-updates/)

### Typical Home Age
- The Woodlands was founded in 1974 by George P. Mitchell; the oldest village, Grogan's Mill, has homes dating to 1971 with a median year built of 1981. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/The-Woodlands/Grogan-s-Mill-Demographics.html)
- Montgomery County's overall median construction year is 2004, reflecting rapid suburban expansion from the 1990s onward. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Montgomery-County-Demographics.html)
- Homes in older villages like Grogan's Mill (1970s–1980s) often have original cast-iron drains and galvanized supply lines, increasing remodel scope. [Houston Chronicle](https://www.houstonchronicle.com/projects/2025/the-woodlands-grogans-mill-house-listing/)
- Newer villages like Creekside Park (2007+) have modern PEX plumbing and PVC drains that simplify renovations. [Perry Homes](https://www.perryhomes.com/blog/the-woodlands-texas-guide-and-insights)
- Less than 3% of Montgomery County housing stock was built before 1960. [Montgomery County CDBG Plan](https://cms1files.revize.com/montgomerycountytx/document_center/Community%20Development/Action%20Plans/Section2AnalysisofNeeds.pdf)

### Water Quality
- Montgomery County water hardness varies widely: municipal supplies test around 4–9 grains per gallon (moderately hard), while well water outside the Conroe supply zone can reach 20–40 grains per gallon. [CSS Water](https://csswater.com/areas-we-serve/montgomery)
- Southeast Texas groundwater commonly runs 300–800 mg/L Total Dissolved Solids (TDS); the EPA recommended limit is 500 mg/L. [Texas Water Development Board](https://www.twdb.texas.gov/publications/reports/numbered_reports/doc/R136/R136.pdf)
- Iron levels above 2 mg/L are routine in some Montgomery County well areas, causing rust staining on fixtures and tile. [MyTapWater.org](https://mytapwater.org/pws/tx1700458/montgomery-county-mud-47/montgomery-tx/)
- Hard water mineral deposits (calcium, magnesium) accumulate in pipes and fixtures, shortening the lifespan of faucets and showerheads. [We The Plumbers TX](https://www.wetheplumberstx.com/signs-you-need-water-softener-texas/)
- Montgomery County MUD 47 reports water hardness of 72 mg/L as CaCO3 (4.2 grains per gallon), classified as moderately hard. [MyTapWater.org](https://mytapwater.org/pws/tx1700458/montgomery-county-mud-47/montgomery-tx/)

## Niche: pressure-washing

### Permit Requirements
- Texas does not require a specific contractor's license for pressure washing; a general business registration and EIN are sufficient in most jurisdictions. [BellaFSM](https://www.bellafsm.com/need-license-to-start-pressure-washing-business/)
- Montgomery County does not have a separate pressure-washing business permit; operators need a standard business license and may need a sales tax permit from the Texas Comptroller. [StartPermit](https://startpermit.com/blog/how-to-start-a-pressure-washing-business/)
- Commercial operations generating significant wastewater volumes may need an individual TPDES (Texas Pollutant Discharge Elimination System) discharge permit from TCEQ. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/downloads/permitting/wastewater/general/txg670000-issued-2025.pdf)
- The Woodlands Township does not impose additional business licensing requirements beyond Montgomery County's. [The Woodlands Township](https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/Community/Business-Resources/Economic-Development/Area-Demographics)
- Operators working on properties within HOA-managed villages should verify that their equipment and schedule comply with community noise and hours-of-operation rules. [The Woodlands Township Residential Standards](https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/files/sharedassets/main/v/2/covenant-administration/documents/2024-residential-standards-recorded-12-10-2024.pdf)

### Local Regulations
- Under the Clean Water Act and Texas TPDES program, pressure washing wastewater may not be discharged into storm drains; it must be captured and disposed of via sanitary sewer or hauled to a disposal facility. [Hotsy South Texas](https://hotsysouthtexas.com/stormwater-compliance-solutions-for-commercial-pressure-washing/)
- Federal civil penalties under the Clean Water Act can exceed $50,000 per violation per day for illegal discharge. [StartPermit](https://startpermit.com/blog/how-to-start-a-pressure-washing-business/)
- Wastewater containing paint chips or lead from pre-1978 surfaces is classified as hazardous material under EPA rules and cannot be routed to the sanitary sewer. [City of Denton BMP Manual](https://www.cityofdenton.com/DocumentCenter/View/388/Pressure-Wash-Manual-PDF)
- The Woodlands' extensive storm drain network flows to Spring Creek and Lake Woodlands; operators must prevent wash water from entering these waterways. [The Woodlands Township](https://www.thewoodlandstownship-tx.gov/)
- Houston-area enforcement can levy fines up to $10,000 per day for violations of stormwater discharge rules. [Launch Local](https://www.launchlocal.shop/blogs/news/houston-pressure-washing-wastewater-rules)

### Mold Mildew Risk
- The Houston metro's humid subtropical climate creates the highest mold-risk classification in the continental US, driven by Gulf of Mexico moisture and year-round warm temperatures. [MoldCo](https://www.moldco.com/blog/mold-is-a-pervasive-issue-that-can-affect-any-indo)
- Average summer humidity levels hover between 70% and 90%, precisely the range in which mold spores thrive and colonize exterior surfaces. [HouMold](https://houmold.com/blog/houston-climate-and-mold/)
- High humidity causes dirt, dust, and pollen to stick to exterior surfaces, creating an ideal base for algae and mildew growth. [Advantage Pro Services](https://advantageproservices.com/blog/algae-and-mildew-removal-in-humid-texas-weather/)
- Homes near Lake Woodlands, Spring Creek, or dense vegetation require more frequent pressure washing — typically every 6–12 months. [Advantage Pro Services](https://advantageproservices.com/blog/algae-and-mildew-removal-in-humid-texas-weather/)
- North-facing walls and shaded areas under the heavy tree canopy develop mold and algae significantly faster. [Advantage Pro Services](https://advantageproservices.com/blog/algae-and-mildew-removal-in-humid-texas-weather/)

### Tree Coverage
- The Woodlands Township tree canopy covers 17,067 acres, representing 60.9% of the township's land area per 2021 Land Cover Classification data. [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/houston/the-woodlands/environment/2024/07/09/maintaining-tree-canopy-sustainability-part-of-the-woodlands-50-year-history)
- Over 35% of The Woodlands has been preserved as open space and forest, a core design principle since founder George Mitchell's 1974 master plan. [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/houston/city-county/2018/11/14/the-woodlands-targets-tree-preservation-at-build-out/)
- At build-out, The Woodlands is expected to maintain approximately 28% green space including forest preserve, parks, and canopy. [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/houston/city-county/2018/11/14/the-woodlands-targets-tree-preservation-at-build-out/)
- By comparison, the City of Houston's tree canopy covers only 18.4% of its land area. [USDA Forest Service](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/54109)
- The dense loblolly pine and hardwood canopy drops needles, leaves, and organic debris onto roofs, driveways, and sidewalks year-round, accelerating algae and staining. [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/houston/the-woodlands/environment/2024/07/09/maintaining-tree-canopy-sustainability-part-of-the-woodlands-50-year-history)

### Water Restrictions
- Montgomery County MUDs enforce tiered drought contingency plans; Stage 2 triggers mandatory conservation measures and Stage 3 can prohibit landscape irrigation entirely. [MC MUD 88](https://www.mcmud88.org/2023/09/drought-stage-2-water-conservation-measures-for-residents/)
- Typical Stage 2 restrictions limit outdoor water use to assigned days: even-numbered addresses on Monday/Wednesday/Friday, odd-numbered on Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday. [MC MUD 105](https://www.mcmud105.org/category/water/)
- Hand watering with a nozzle-equipped hose is generally permitted even during Stage 2 restrictions. [MC MUD 88](https://www.mcmud88.org/2023/09/drought-stage-2-water-conservation-measures-for-residents/)
- Pressure washing is typically categorized as a non-irrigation activity but operators should confirm with the local MUD during active drought stages. [TCEQ Drought List](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/drinkingwater/trot/droughtw.html)
- The Woodlands Joint Powers Agency coordinates water supply; rapid population growth has increased pressure on regional water infrastructure. [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/conroe-montgomery/government/montgomery-county-water-officials-say-data-centers-aren-t-likely-to-target-county-for-development/)

## Niche: window-tinting

### Tint Laws
- Texas Transportation Code Section 547.613 requires front side windows to allow at least 25% visible light transmission (VLT). [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/texas/)
- Windshield tint is restricted to the area above the manufacturer's AS-1 line (or top 5 inches if no AS-1 line); the film plus glass must allow at least 25% VLT and must not exceed 25% reflectance. [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/texas/)
- Back side windows and rear window have no minimum VLT requirement when the vehicle has an outside mirror on each side. [Tint Laws](https://www.tintlaws.com/states/texas/)
- Red, blue, and amber tint colors are prohibited on all vehicle windows. [Tint Laws](https://www.tintlaws.com/states/texas/)
- A compliance sticker identifying the installer and manufacturer must be placed between the film and glass on the driver's side window; installing tint without this label is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to $1,000. [Traffic Ticket Fine](https://www.trafficticketfine.com/en/texas/texas-window-tint-ticket-fines)
- Medical exemptions are available under Section 547.613 for persons requiring sun protection. [Law Walls](https://lawwalls.com/texas-window-tint-laws/)

### Sun Exposure
- The Conroe/Woodlands area receives approximately 2,889 hours of sunshine per year. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/texas-usa/conroe-climate)
- UV index reaches 7 (High) from June through August and peaks at Extreme levels (11) in May. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/texas-usa/conroe-climate)
- August averages 10.2 hours of sunshine per day; December averages 4.9 hours. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/texas-usa/conroe-climate)
- The area averages approximately 205–267 sunny days per year depending on the measurement methodology. [BestPlaces](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/conroe)
- Annual clear-sky UV index average is approximately 6.6–7.0, making UV protection a year-round concern. [Nomad Season](https://nomadseason.com/weather/united-states/texas/conroe-may.html)

### Vehicle Types
- Texas has 24.92 million registered vehicles: 38.5% SUVs/utility vehicles, 27.2% pickup trucks, 30.4% cars, and 3.8% vans/minivans (December 2024). [Alliance for Automotive Innovation](https://autosinnovate.org/resources/insights/tx)
- The Ford F-Series is the top-selling vehicle in Texas, followed by the RAM 1500 and Chevrolet Silverado 1500. [iSeeCars](https://www.iseecars.com/popular-vehicle-type-by-state-study)
- In 2024 new vehicle sales, pickup trucks and SUVs combined for 78.6% of all new registrations in Texas. [Alliance for Automotive Innovation](https://autosinnovate.org/resources/insights/tx)
- The Woodlands' suburban commuter profile means high concentrations of full-size trucks, midsize SUVs, and luxury vehicles. [Niche](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/the-woodlands-montgomery-tx/)
- Trucks and SUVs with larger glass surface areas are prime candidates for window tinting due to greater sun exposure inside the cabin. [CarSoup](https://www.carsoup.com/blog/2024/07/26/untitled-675/)
