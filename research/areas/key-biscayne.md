# Local Facts: Key Biscayne, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring
**County:** Miami-Dade County
**Population:** 14,809 (2020 Census) — [Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/keybiscaynevillageflorida)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Key Colony: Gated, resort-style community on the north end of the island offering multiple residential towers, direct beach access, tennis courts, pools, and landscaped grounds; one of the most popular addresses on Key Biscayne. [The Move Genie](https://themovegenie.com/florida/key-biscayne/best-neighborhoods)
- Ocean Club: Premier luxury residential community with sweeping ocean and bay views, beach access, dining facilities, fitness centers, and extensive recreation amenities; features high-rise condominiums. [The Move Genie](https://themovegenie.com/florida/key-biscayne/best-neighborhoods)
- Mashta Island: Ultra-exclusive enclave of approximately 99 single-family waterfront properties off the southwest corner of Key Biscayne, with private boat docks and open-ocean access; the most expensive neighborhood on the island. [NeighborhoodScout](https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/fl/key-biscayne)
- Village Center / Crandon Boulevard: The commercial and civic spine of the island running north-south; includes shops, restaurants, the Village Green park, and the community center along Crandon Boulevard. [Compass](https://www.compass.com/neighborhood-guides/miami/key-biscayne/)

### Climate
- Tropical monsoon climate with an annual mean temperature of 78 °F; the island's location between the Atlantic Ocean and Biscayne Bay moderates temperature extremes slightly compared to mainland Miami. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Miami)
- Average annual rainfall of 61.9 inches (1,570 mm), concentrated May through October; the island is exposed to tropical storms and hurricanes during the Atlantic hurricane season (June-November). [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/miami/climate)
- Average humidity of 73% year-round due to the island's surrounded-by-water geography; ocean breezes provide some relief but also carry salt-laden moisture inland. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Miami)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- Residential interior floor coating in single-family homes and duplexes is generally permit-exempt in Miami-Dade County; surfacing existing interior floors with carpet, tile, or similar coverings does not require a permit. [City of Miami](https://www.miami.gov/Permits-Construction/Work-Exempt-from-Permit)
- All commercial floor coating projects require building permits in Miami-Dade, including condominium common areas; Key Biscayne's many condo towers (Key Colony, Ocean Club) fall under commercial permitting. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/epoxy-flooring-permit-requirements-miami/)
- Contractors must hold either a Florida DBPR Certified license or a Miami-Dade Certificate of Competency; the Village of Key Biscayne issues building permits through the Miami-Dade County system. [Miami-Dade County](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/license.page?Mduid_license=lic1677272391446892)
- Miami-Dade is within the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ) with design wind speeds of 175 mph; Key Biscayne's barrier-island exposure means structures face the full force of storm surge and wind-borne debris. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Building_Code)
- Every industrial floor coating installation in Miami-Dade requires ASTM F2170 in-situ relative humidity testing before a coating system is applied. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/epoxy-flooring-permit-requirements-miami/)

### Local Regulations
- Florida regulates VOC emissions for surface coatings under Rules 62-296.500 through 62-296.516, F.A.C.; coatings must contain 5.0% or less VOC by volume, or total usage must not exceed 6.0 gallons per day averaged monthly. [Florida DEP](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/florida/Fla-Admin-Code-r-62-296-500)
- The federal EPA AIM rule (40 CFR Part 59, Subpart D) caps floor coatings at 400 g/L VOC and industrial maintenance coatings at 450 g/L. [EPA eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-59/subpart-D)
- Miami-Dade County requires laboratory test reports for low-VOC components using ASTM D 6886 testing methods for waterborne air-dry coatings. [Miami-Dade County](https://www.miamidade.gov/building/library/checklists/low-voc-components.pdf)
- Key Biscayne's residential community includes HOAs with their own rules on contractor access, work hours, and materials; Ocean Club, Key Colony, and Grand Bay each carry planned-unit zoning districts. [Key Biscayne Zoning](https://www.zoneomics.com/code/key-biscayne-FL/chapter_1)
- ADA compliance and fire code adherence apply to all commercial floor coating installations, including condo parking garages and common areas. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/epoxy-flooring-permit-requirements-miami/)

### Concrete Condition
- Key Biscayne is a barrier island sitting directly on porous oolitic limestone; the water table can be as shallow as 12 inches below grade during rainy season, making moisture vapor drive through slabs extreme. [USGS](https://fl.water.usgs.gov/PDF_files/wsp2458_merritt.pdf)
- Chloride ions from salt air and sea spray directly impact concrete on the island; salt-laden air penetrates unsealed concrete and accelerates rebar corrosion, with rust occupying 2-6 times the volume of steel. [FTL Concrete](https://www.ftlconcrete.com/residential/common-concrete-issues-in-florida-and-how-to-address-them/)
- Slab moisture vapor transmission in coastal Miami-Dade averages 4-8 lbs per 1,000 sq ft per 24 hours — well above the 3 lbs/1,000 sq ft maximum for standard epoxy application without a mitigation primer. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/concrete-moisture-mitigation-florida/)
- Hydrostatic pressure pushes moisture through concrete when the water table rises to within inches of the slab, a frequent occurrence on Key Biscayne during the wet season (May-October). [CL Wizard](https://clwizard.com/concrete-leak-repair/concrete-slab-waterproofing-south-florida-methods/)
- Efflorescence and spalling are common on Key Biscayne concrete due to constant salt exposure; proper surface preparation including diamond grinding is critical before any epoxy application. [King Concrete Hollywood](https://kingconcretehollywood.com/2025/12/06/waterproofing-reinforcing-coastal-slabs-in-hollywood-fl/)

### Humidity Impact
- Key Biscayne's surrounded-by-water geography pushes local humidity above the Miami-Dade average of 73%; ocean breezes carry moisture that keeps surfaces damp and slows epoxy cure times. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Miami)
- At 70% relative humidity, epoxy tensile strength drops by 25%; the island rarely sees humidity below 65% even during the dry season. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/how-humidity-affects-epoxy-floors-in-miami/)
- High humidity combines with moisture vapor rising from the slab to break the epoxy bond; amine blush (a waxy film) forms on uncured surfaces, destroying inter-coat adhesion. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/why-epoxy-floors-fail-in-florida-climate-explained/)
- The optimal installation window is October through April (dry season); professional installers use climate-controlled tents maintaining 45-55% relative humidity during application. [Top Gun Garage](https://topgun-garage.com/the-3-month-window-when-garage-floor-coating-works-best-in-florida/)
- Moisture-mitigating primers are mandatory on Key Biscayne slabs; a standard system consists of moisture-mitigation primer, ASTM-tested base coat, and UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat tuned for the subtropical climate. [SPF Epoxy](https://spfepoxy.com/garage-epoxy-flooring/)

### Garage Usage
- Florida homes are built on slab-on-grade foundations with no basements; the water table beneath Key Biscayne is too high for below-grade construction, making the garage the primary storage real estate. [Alpha Foundations](https://www.alphafoundations.com/resources/foundation-repair/wondering-why-homes-in-the-south-have-no-basements/)
- Key Biscayne's single-family homes on Mashta Island and the southern residential streets typically have 2-3 car garages that serve as storage, workshop, and overflow living space. [Compass](https://www.compass.com/neighborhood-guides/miami/key-biscayne/)
- Nationally, 76% of homeowners use their garage for vehicle storage, but 36% say it is too packed to park in; 62% name the garage the most cluttered space in their home. [Craftsman Survey](https://press.craftsman.com/2024-03-06-A-Peek-Behind-the-Garage-Door-More-than-1-in-4-Homeowners-Surveyed-Say-Their-Garage-Helped-Start-a-Side-Hustle,-According-to-Newly-Released-CRAFTSMAN-R-Survey)
- Key Biscayne's condo towers use parking garages as a major shared infrastructure; epoxy coatings in these garages must withstand vehicle traffic, tire marks, oil drips, and salt tracked in from coastal roads. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/garage-epoxy-flooring-miami/)
- The median home value in Key Biscayne is $1,575,300 (2020-2024 ACS), positioning garage floor upgrades as a value-add improvement for a high-income market. [Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/keybiscaynevillageflorida)
