## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Tradition: Master-planned community with walkable design, Tradition Square, single-family homes, townhomes, and villas; recognized as one of the top 100 places to live in the US. [On the Treasure Coast](https://www.onthetreasurecoast.com/psl/neighborhoods)
- St. Lucie West: Mixed-use development near I-95 with shopping, dining, entertainment, and the Mets spring training stadium; ideal for commuters. [Jeannie Homes](https://jeanniehomesforsale.com/port-st-lucie-communities/)
- Torino: Growing neighborhood in northwest Port St. Lucie known for larger lots, newer construction, and HOA-free subdivisions. [Homes.com](https://www.homes.com/local-guide/port-saint-lucie-fl/torino-neighborhood/)
- The city has 33 officially named neighborhoods as of 2020. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port_St._Lucie,_Florida)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate with hot, humid summers and mild, comfortable winters. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/18767/Average-Weather-in-Port-Saint-Lucie-Florida-United-States-Year-Round)
- Temperature typically varies from 55 °F to 89 °F year-round, rarely below 41 °F or above 92 °F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/18767/Average-Weather-in-Port-Saint-Lucie-Florida-United-States-Year-Round)
- Summers are long, oppressive, and wet with frequent thunderstorms; winters are short and partly cloudy. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/18767/Average-Weather-in-Port-Saint-Lucie-Florida-United-States-Year-Round)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Landfill Location
- The St. Lucie County Solid Waste, Baling & Recycling Facility is located at 6120 Glades Cut-Off Road, Fort Pierce, FL 34981 — a 331-acre site that receives solid waste from all of St. Lucie County year-round. [City of PSL Office of Solid Waste](https://www.cityofpsl.com/Government/Your-City-Government/Departments/Office-of-Solid-Waste)
- Drop-off fee is $10 minimum for up to 250 pounds of yard waste, household garbage, and recyclables; hazardous materials and e-waste accepted at no charge with proof of residency. [City of PSL Drop-off Center](https://www.cityofpsl.com/Government/Your-City-Government/Departments/Office-of-Solid-Waste/Convenient-Drop-off-Center)
- The City's Convenient Drop-off Center at 1501 SW Cameo Boulevard was permanently closed effective January 5, 2026, due to declining usage. [City of PSL Office of Solid Waste](https://www.cityofpsl.com/Government/Your-City-Government/Departments/Office-of-Solid-Waste)

### Recycling Programs
- The city provides green recycling carts with wheels for weekly curbside collection; recyclables must be loose (not bagged), empty, clean, and dry. [City of PSL Office of Solid Waste](https://www.cityofpsl.com/Government/Your-City-Government/Departments/Office-of-Solid-Waste)
- Accepted curbside recyclables: paper, boxes, cans, bottles, tubs, jugs, jars, and glass bottles or jars. [On the Treasure Coast](https://www.onthetreasurecoast.com/psl/port-st-lucie-recycling-guide)
- FCC Environmental Services Florida, LLC became Port St. Lucie's solid waste hauler on September 5, 2022. [FCC Environmental](https://fccenvironmental.com/pslresi/)
- Rechargeable batteries, fluorescent bulbs, and household hazardous waste must go to the county facility in Fort Pierce. [City of PSL Hazardous Waste](https://www.cityofpsl.com/Government/Your-City-Government/Departments/Office-of-Solid-Waste/Household-Hazardous-Waste)

### HOA Rules
- Florida HOAs are governed by Fla. §720.30851 (Florida HOA Act); Architectural Review Committee approval is required for exterior changes including fences, additions, and solar. [Kind HOA](https://kindhoa.com/compliance/florida/port-st-lucie)
- HOAs may fine up to $100 per violation and $1,000 in aggregate unless governing documents allow more. [Kind HOA](https://kindhoa.com/compliance/florida/port-st-lucie)
- Modern Port St. Lucie HOA rules increasingly address solar panels, EV charging stations, and recycling programs. [PSL 55+ Communities](https://portstlucie55communities.com/55-community-news-updates/hoa-rules-changing-for-modern-55-communities-in-port-st-lucie/)
- Florida Statute §163.04 prohibits deed restrictions that ban solar equipment, though reasonable location rules may apply. [Florida Statutes](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/)

## Niche: pool-builders

### Water Table
- St. Lucie County has low ground surface elevations, high groundwater table, low soil storage capacity — sand over limestone with water table sitting high, especially on canal and waterfront lots. [FAU Engineering Research](https://www.fau.edu/engineering/research/cwr3/pdf/27.st.lucie--loxahatchee.pdf)
- Pool installation in Port St. Lucie takes 5–6 weeks from start to finish, slightly longer than inland Florida cities due to high water table management. [Cool Pool FL](https://coolpoolfl.com/installation-port-st-lucie)
- Sandy soil and shallow water table mean an empty or partly empty pool shell can lift and crack when the ground saturates; hydrostatic relief systems are standard. [South Florida Pool Authority](https://southfloridapoolauthority.com/high-water-table-pool-issues-south-florida/)

### Pool Barrier Laws
- Port St. Lucie enforces Florida's Residential Swimming Pool Safety Act (Statute 515), requiring every residential pool or spa to have a compliant safety barrier before final inspection or use. [Treasure Coast Pool Fences](https://treasurecoastpoolfences.com/need-pool-safety-fence-in-port-st-lucie/)
- Pool enclosure fences must be a minimum of 4 feet high and a maximum of 6 feet, measured from the side away from the pool. [City of PSL Building Dept.](https://pandapublicweb.cityofpsl.com/Files/PDF/PermitApps/residential-fence-permit-with-ordinance.pdf)
- Swimming pools must maintain a minimum setback of 5–7.5 feet from side and rear property lines in standard single-family residential zones. [Site Plan Creator](https://www.siteplancreator.com/site-plans/port-st-lucie-fl)
- Pool Alarm/Barrier Affidavit and FBC 4101.7 compliance inspection are required for fences used as pool barriers. [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)

### HOA Pool Rules
- Florida HOAs have authority to establish reasonable rules governing community pool amenities; rules must be applied fairly and consistently and cannot conflict with state law. [HOA Experts USA](https://hoaexpertsusa.com/florida-hoa-pool-rules/)
- Most HOA community pools in Florida do not provide lifeguards. [Hamilton Mikes Law](https://hamiltonmikes.com/resources/hoa-law/swimming-pool-rules-risk/)
- Private residential pools require a building permit, barrier/fence documentation, and a site plan showing pool distance from property lines and the home. [Site Plan Creator](https://www.siteplancreator.com/site-plans/port-st-lucie-fl)
