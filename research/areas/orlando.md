## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Downtown Orlando: Includes North Quarter, Eola Heights, Thornton Park, South Eola, Lake Cherokee Historic District, and the Central Business District. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Orlando)
- Winter Park: Upscale suburb just north of Orlando with tree-lined streets and Park Avenue shopping. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida)
- Dr. Phillips / MetroWest: Western Orlando neighborhoods with a mix of residential communities and restaurant row. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida)
- Pine Hills: Large residential community in western Orange County. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando,_Florida)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Cfa) with an average annual temperature of 72 °F and more than 300 days of sunshine. [Visit Orlando](https://www.visitorlando.com/plan/weather/)
- Average annual rainfall of 51.45 inches, with the majority falling June through September; 114 precipitation days per year. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/orlando-1262/)
- Summer highs average 92 °F in August with nighttime lows of 76 °F; winter temperatures typically in the 60s–low 70s. [Visit Orlando](https://www.visitorlando.com/plan/weather/)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- Most residential epoxy floor installations over existing concrete do not require building permits in Orlando or Orange County. [Orlando Epoxy Flooring](https://orlandoepoxyflooring.net/)
- Commercial projects must comply with local building codes related to fire safety, slip resistance, and ventilation during installation. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/location/orlando-fl-epoxy-floors/)
- Florida does not require a specific state epoxy certification, though professional training builds credibility. [Xtreme Polishing Systems](https://xtremepolishingsystems.com/pages/orlando-epoxy-training)

### Local Regulations
- Commercial food service facilities require coved bases and health-code-compliant coatings per DBPR standards. [Kongcrete Coatings](https://kongcretecoatingsco.com/health-code-compliant-epoxy-flooring-in-orlando-inside-sanford-airports-commercial-kitchen/)
- Orlando building inspectors can require compliance with Florida Building Code for ventilation during coating application in enclosed commercial spaces. [Prime Resin FL](https://primeresinfl.com/service-areas/orange-county/orlando/)

### Concrete Condition
- Orlando sits on sandy soil over a shallow water table; this combination drives moisture vapor up through slab-on-grade concrete year-round. [Gio Floors FL](https://www.giofloorsfl.com/post/subfloor-slab-prep-in-orlando-why-it-matters-first)
- Most Orlando homes are slab-on-grade construction; moisture from below moves up through concrete via capillary action. [My Garage Floors](https://mygaragefloors.com/garage-mold-mildew-florida/)
- Concrete surfaces in Orlando develop cracks, spalling, and discoloration from heat cycling and moisture exposure if left unsealed. [Concrete Solutions FL](https://www.concretesolutions-fl.com/blog/signs-worn-concrete-surfaces-in-orlando-are-getting-worse)

### Humidity Impact
- Outdoor humidity in Orlando averages 87% on summer mornings and rarely drops below 60% even in the driest winter months. [Gio Floors FL](https://www.giofloorsfl.com/post/subfloor-slab-prep-in-orlando-why-it-matters-first)
- High humidity combined with Florida's water table creates "moisture vapor transmission" — water vapor rising through the concrete slab itself. [Garaginization](https://garaginization.com/orlando/)
- In-situ relative humidity (RH) testing (ASTM F2170) is recommended before any epoxy or coating application to verify slab moisture levels. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/how-to-test-concrete-slab-moisture-before-floor-coating-in-florida)

### Garage Usage
- Many Orlando homeowners use garages primarily for storage, stacking belongings on moisture-sweating concrete slabs. [Garaginization](https://garaginization.com/orlando/garage-overhead-storage/)
- Garage interiors in Central Florida regularly exceed 110 °F in summer, accelerating corrosion of standard steel storage systems. [Garaginization](https://garaginization.com/orlando/)
- Standard MDF-core or particleboard cabinets are not engineered for Florida's combination of high humidity, daily heat cycling, and moisture vapor from the slab. [Garaginization](https://garaginization.com/orlando/)
- Mold and mildew thrive in unconditioned Florida garages, which supply moisture, warmth, and organic material year-round. [My Garage Floors](https://mygaragefloors.com/garage-mold-mildew-florida/)
