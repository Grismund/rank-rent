# Local Facts: Live Oak, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring
**County:** Bexar County
**Population:** 16,426 (2025 est.) — [U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/liveoakcitytexas/PST045225)

## City Overview

### Location and Geography
- Live Oak is an incorporated city approximately 15 miles northeast of downtown San Antonio in Bexar County, situated off I-35 and Loop 1604 North. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Oak,_Texas)
- Incorporated in 1960 as residential development spread northeast from San Antonio; the city evolved from a farming and ranching community into a suburban city with its own municipal services, shopping centers, a major hospital and medical complex, and college campuses. [City of Live Oak](https://www.liveoaktx.net/history/lo-original-houses)

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- **Auburn Hills at Woodcrest:** A peaceful neighborhood between Toepperwein Road and O'Connor Road with approximately 750 homes at an average price of $225,000; features walking trails connecting to Woodcrest Park and is served by Northeast ISD. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-in-live-oak-tx/)
- **Bridlewood Park:** Approximately 450 homes at an average price of $275,000 near the Gateway Shopping Plaza, with easy access to Loop 1604 and I-35; known for its modern feel and accessible pricing, popular with first-time buyers. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-to-live-in-live-oak-tx/)
- **Woodcrest:** One of Live Oak's oldest subdivisions, originally platted before the town incorporated in 1954; mature trees, established lots, and adjacent walking trails to the city's Woodcrest Park. [My San Antonio](https://www.mysanantonio.com/realestate/article/live-oak-17830454.php)
- **Coronado Village / Live Oak Village:** Established neighborhoods in the $200K-$220K range, centrally located within the city with quick access to Pat Booker Road commercial corridor. [TX Property](https://txproperty.com/live-oak/communities/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Cfa) with an average annual temperature of approximately 70 degrees F. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/san-antonio/climate)
- August is the hottest month with an average high of 95 degrees F and low of 75 degrees F; January is the coldest with an average high of 63 degrees F and low of 43 degrees F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/7137/Average-Weather-in-San-Antonio-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Annual precipitation of approximately 34 inches, peaking in May (4.4 inches); average annual humidity of 67%, with morning readings averaging 84% and afternoon readings dropping to 52%. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/san-antonio/climate)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- Live Oak is located within the San Antonio metropolitan area but has its own city government; epoxy coating over existing concrete is generally classified as cosmetic surface treatment. The City of San Antonio lists "painting, plastering, repairing of sheet rock, wallpapering and tiling" on its No Permit Required List for residential work. [City of San Antonio](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/DSD/Constructing/Residential/Permits/No-Permit-Required)
- Texas does not require a state-level license for epoxy flooring installers; the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) regulates electricians, HVAC, and plumbers but not flooring contractors. [Flooring Clarity](https://www.flooringclarity.com/do-you-need-a-license-to-do-epoxy-flooring/)
- Interior floor coating on an existing slab typically needs no building permit; many coatings-only companies never trigger the contractor registration requirement. [Get On The Map](https://getonthemap.sbs/start-a-business/epoxy-flooring-business-texas)
- For projects that do require permits (structural changes, commercial work), San Antonio permit fees typically run $70-$140, and contractors must register as Home Improvement Contractors ($150 for two years) with $300,000 minimum liability insurance. [City of San Antonio](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/DSD/Contractor/Building-Home-Improvement)
- Homeowners in Live Oak should verify whether their property falls within city limits or unincorporated Bexar County, as permit jurisdiction varies. [City of Live Oak](https://www.liveoaktx.net/government/mayor-and-city-council)

### Local Regulations
- TCEQ Chapter 115 regulates VOC emissions from surface coatings in Texas; floor coatings are subject to a VOC content limit of 250 grams per liter, more restrictive than the federal EPA limit of 400 g/L. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/stationary-rules/voc/coating)
- San Antonio's adopted 2024 International Building Code requires interior floor finishes in commercial and institutional buildings to meet fire performance standards per IBC Section 804, tested per ASTM E648; residential garage floors are exempt from these commercial fire-rating requirements. [Up Codes](https://up.codes/viewer/san-antonio/ibc-2021/chapter/8/interior-finishes)
- Surface coating facilities emitting more than 25 tons per year of VOC require a TCEQ Permit by Rule; individual residential garage coatings are well below this threshold. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/downloads/rules/current/25006115_pro.pdf)

### Concrete Condition
- Live Oak sits on Bexar County's expansive clay soils, primarily Houston Black clay — the Texas state soil — a deep Vertisol with 46-60% smectite content that swells 10-30% in volume when wet and cracks up to 4 inches wide when dry. [GL Hunt Foundation Repair](https://glhunt.com/san-antonio-foundation-repair-by-soil-neighborhood/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston_black_(soil))
- Houston Black clay has a plasticity index in the 40s (values above 35 indicate very high movement); the soil can move 4-6 inches between wet and dry seasons, causing slab heave in spring and shrinkage cracks during summer drought. [Texas Foundation Repair TX](https://texasfoundationrepairtx.com/texas/bexar-county/san-antonio/)
- An estimated 70% of Texas foundation failures are caused by expansive clay soil; proper crack repair and flexible coating systems are essential before any epoxy application. [DuraPier](https://durapier.com/blog/why-texas-clay-soil-causes-so-many-foundation-problems-and-what-actually-helps/)
- Moisture vapor transmission is the leading cause of coating failure in Central Texas; after heavy rains, clay soil swells and forces moisture upward through the slab, spiking beyond the 3-4 lbs per 1,000 sq ft per 24 hours that standard epoxy can tolerate. [Garage Floor Coatings Dallas](https://www.garagefloorcoatingsdallas.com/texas-concrete-isnt-like-the-rest-of-the-country-what-that-means-for-garage-floor-epoxy-installations/)
- Some pre-2010 construction in the San Antonio metro lacks a proper under-slab vapor barrier, which causes blistering, clouding, and delamination within 6-12 months of epoxy application. [At Home Flooring Solutions](https://athomeflooringsolutions.com/blog/epoxy-flooring-garage-texas)
- Professional installers perform ASTM F1869 (calcium chloride, surface MVER) and ASTM F2170 (in-situ RH probe at 40% slab depth) tests before coating; slabs reading above 75% internal RH require moisture mitigation primers. [One Stop Epoxy](https://onestopepoxy.com/pages/how-does-humidity-affect-epoxy-flooring-installation)

### Humidity Impact
- San Antonio area humidity averages 67% annually, with May peaking at 71% and morning readings averaging 84%; humidity above 75% dramatically increases the risk of amine blush and intercoat delamination on epoxy surfaces. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/san-antonio/climate) · [One Stop Epoxy](https://onestopepoxy.com/pages/how-does-humidity-affect-epoxy-flooring-installation)
- The ideal humidity range for epoxy application is 40-60%; October through April is the best installation window in the San Antonio area, when humidity and temperatures create favorable adhesion conditions. [Pro Epoxy San Antonio](https://proepoxysanantonio.com/blog/garage-floor-epoxy-san-antonio-installation/)
- High humidity slows curing and can cause clouding, white haze (amine blush), loss of gloss, and compromised intercoat adhesion; dehumidifiers or forced ventilation are recommended to maintain below 70% RH during application. [Crash of Rhinos Painting](https://www.crashofrhinospainting.com/blog/mastering-concrete-humidity-and-epoxy-coatings)
- An estimated 40% of Texas epoxy failures are attributed to moisture issues — either ambient humidity during curing or moisture vapor transmission from below the slab. [Epoxy of Texas](https://epoxyoftexas.com/garage-floor-coating-in-texas-complete-2026-guide-to-durable-premium-flooring-so/)
- Polyurea and polyaspartic coatings cure in 1-4 hours vs. 24-72 hours for traditional epoxy, making them more humidity-tolerant and often preferred for summer installs in the San Antonio metro. [San Antonio Epoxy Floor](https://sanantonioepoxyfloor.com/blog/epoxy-vs-polyaspartic-san-antonio.html)

### Garage Usage
- Live Oak is a suburban bedroom community where homes are built on concrete slab foundations with attached garages; two-car garages are standard in established neighborhoods like Auburn Hills and Woodcrest, with three-car options available in newer construction. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-in-live-oak-tx/)
- Most Live Oak neighborhoods are within 5-15 minutes of JBSA-Randolph's main gate; military families are a significant market segment, using garage space for workshops, storage, and equipment between PCS rotations. [Living in SATX](https://livinginsatx.com/live-oak/)
- Nationally, 76% of homeowners use garages for vehicle parking, 47% for home improvement projects, and 38% for automotive projects; 36% of homeowners say their garage is too packed to park a vehicle. [CRAFTSMAN Survey 2024](https://press.craftsman.com/2024-03-06-A-Peek-Behind-the-Garage-Door-More-than-1-in-4-Homeowners-Surveyed-Say-Their-Garage-Helped-Start-a-Side-Hustle,-According-to-Newly-Released-CRAFTSMAN-R-Survey)

[Builder Online](https://www.builderonline.com/design/consumer-trends/when-it-comes-to-garages-buyers-want-space-for-at-least-two-cars_o)
