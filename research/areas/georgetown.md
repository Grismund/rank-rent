## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Historic Downtown Georgetown: Victorian-era square with shops, restaurants, and the Williamson County Courthouse; designated as "The Most Beautiful Town Square in Texas." [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown,_Texas)
- Sun City Texas: 4,750-acre age-restricted (55+) active adult community with golf courses, pools, and recreation centers. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_City_Texas)
- Berry Creek: Established neighborhood with a country club and golf course in northeast Georgetown. [10 Federal Storage](https://10federalstorage.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-georgetown-tx)
- Cimarron Hills: Luxury Hill Country community in western Georgetown. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-cities-neighborhoods-to-live-williamson-county-tx/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate with hot summers and cool winters; temperature typically varies from 39 °F to 96 °F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/8015/Average-Weather-in-Georgetown-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- August is the hottest month with an average high of 95 °F and low of 74 °F; January is the coldest. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/8015/Average-Weather-in-Georgetown-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Rainfall concentrated in the fall; the area is fairly wet overall for Central Texas. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/georgetown-16849/)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Landfill Location
- Georgetown Transfer Station operated by Texas Disposal Systems (TDS) is located at 250 W.L. Walden Road, Georgetown, TX 78626; phone 512-930-1715. [Texas Disposal Systems](https://www.yelp.com/biz/texas-disposal-systems-georgetown-georgetown)
- The facility incorporates solid waste disposal, compost production, and recycling operations. [TDS Georgetown](https://www.texasdisposal.com/processing/austin-landfill/)
- The larger Austin and Travis County Landfill operated by TDS serves the broader region for bulk disposal. [Texas Disposal Systems](https://www.texasdisposal.com/processing/austin-landfill/)

### Recycling Programs
- The City of Georgetown provides weekly curbside trash collection and bi-weekly single-stream recycling through Texas Disposal Systems. [City of Georgetown](https://georgetowntexas.gov/utilities/garbage_and_recycling/)
- Single-stream recycling means no separation required — all recyclables go in one bin. [City of Georgetown](https://www.georgetowntexas.gov/utilities/garbage_and_recycling/residential_services/single_stream_recycling.php)
- Curbside yard trimming collection occurs on the first recycling day of each month; bundles must be under 4 feet long, 35 lbs, with a 20-item maximum. [City of Georgetown](https://www.georgetowntexas.gov/utilities/garbage_and_recycling/curbside_yard_trimming_collections.php)
- TDS provides a Waste Wizard tool showing trash, recycling, and green-waste calendars with item-specific disposal guidance. [City of Georgetown](https://georgetowntexas.gov/utilities/garbage_and_recycling/)
- Georgetown offers holiday recycling programs for Christmas tree recycling and seasonal packaging. [Hello Georgetown](https://www.hellogeorgetown.com/city-of-georgetown-news/georgetown-launches-2025-holiday-recycling-programs-for-lights-trees-and-more/)

### HOA Rules
- Georgetown has over 40 distinct neighborhoods, many with HOAs that enforce architectural standards and exterior appearance rules. [10 Federal Storage](https://10federalstorage.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-georgetown-tx)
- Sun City Texas, the city's largest community, has active HOA governance with rules on exterior maintenance, storage, and landscaping. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_City_Texas)
- HOA communities may have specific rules about leaving items curbside; residents should confirm with their HOA before scheduling bulk pickups. [Go Load Up](https://goloadup.com/junk-removal/tx/georgetown/)
