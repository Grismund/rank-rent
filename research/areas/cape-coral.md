# Local Facts: Cape Coral, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring, paver-sealing, window-tinting
**County:** Lee County
**Population:** 236,264 (2025) — [Data Commons](https://datacommons.org/place/geoId/1210275)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Southwest Cape Coral: Established residential area with many canal-front properties and mature landscaping. [Cape Coral GIS Maps](https://www.capecoral.gov/department/information_technology_services/interactive_gis_maps.php)
- Northwest Cape Coral: Rapidly developing section with newer subdivisions and commercial growth. [Cape Coral GIS Maps](https://www.capecoral.gov/department/information_technology_services/interactive_gis_maps.php)
- Southeast Cape Coral: Includes the Cape Harbour marina district and waterfront dining. [Cape Coral City Council](https://www.capecoral.gov/government/city_council/index.php)
- Northeast Cape Coral: Mix of established and new-build neighborhoods; includes Burnt Store corridor. [Cape Coral City Council](https://www.capecoral.gov/government/city_council/index.php)

### Climate
- Warm subtropical climate with an annual mean temperature of 74.2 °F (23.4 °C); August averages 81.9 °F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/cape-coral-1654/)
- Average annual rainfall of 53 inches across 108 precipitation days, with June as the wettest month at 9.4 inches. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/cape-coral-1654/)
- Average humidity of 72%, remaining high year-round and especially in autumn. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/cape-coral-1654/)
- Classified as a "Very High Risk Area" for UV exposure with an annual average UV index of 7.6–7.7 and 3,622 hours of sunshine per year. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- Residential garage floor epoxy coatings are classified as cosmetic finish work under Florida Building Code Section 105.2, which exempts "painting, wallpapering, and similar cosmetic finish work" from building permits. Cosmetic flooring over an existing slab does not require a permit in Cape Coral unless structural modifications or utility changes are involved. [PermitPlace](https://permitplace.com/city/cape-coral-fl-building-permits/)
- Over 60% of Cape Coral lies within a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area, making most properties ineligible for the HB 803 exemption that otherwise allows flooring replacement without permits on projects under $7,500. [City of Cape Coral - HB 803](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/development_services/permitting_services_division/hb_803/index.php)
- Commercial epoxy flooring projects involving structural modifications, electrical work, or plumbing changes require a building permit from Cape Coral's Building Division. [PermitPlace](https://permitplace.com/city/cape-coral-fl-building-permits/)
- The City of Cape Coral no longer issues local contractor licenses (Ordinance 10-23), consistent with Florida HB 735. Contractors must register with the city through the EnerGov CSS portal and carry general liability insurance. [City of Cape Coral - Building Division](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/development_services/building_division/index.php)
- Under Florida Statute 489.103, any construction work with a contract value above $2,500 or requiring a building permit must be performed by a state-licensed contractor. [Claro Studio Web](https://clarostudioweb.com/blog/licenca-flooring-florida/)

### Local Regulations
- Cape Coral enforces the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023), effective December 31, 2023 through December 30, 2026. [City of Cape Coral - Building Division](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/development_services/building_division/index.php)
- The EPA's national Architectural Coatings Rule (40 CFR Part 59, Subpart D) sets a VOC content limit of 400 grams per liter for floor coatings; Florida does not impose stricter limits. [eCFR - 40 CFR Part 59](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-59/subpart-D)
- Contractors must register with the City of Cape Coral through the EnerGov CSS online portal before pulling permits. Registration inquiries: (239) 574-0870. [City of Cape Coral - Building Division](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/development_services/building_division/index.php)
- Cape Coral requires impact-rated garage doors throughout the city, rated for 140–150 mph wind speeds with large missile impact resistance per Florida Building Code hurricane provisions. [DAB Door](https://www.dabdoor.com/lee-approved-garage-doors/)

### Concrete Condition
- Cape Coral sits on the Fort Thompson Formation — very permeable, fossiliferous, sandy marine limestones and calcareous sandstones that allow moisture to move freely upward through the ground. [USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/wri/wri034208/html/)
- The surficial aquifer in Cape Coral is shallow: water levels recover to near land surface during the summer wet season. Aquifer thickness ranges between 20 and 40 feet. [USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/wri/1995/4003/report.pdf)
- Florida slabs routinely measure 5–15+ pounds of moisture vapor emission per 1,000 sq ft per 24 hours, well above the standard epoxy threshold of 3 lbs. Moisture vapor driving up through slab-on-grade foundations is the number-one cause of epoxy that bubbles or peels in Lee County. [imovingtoflorida.com](https://imovingtoflorida.com/guide/florida-epoxy-flooring-guide/)
- ASTM F2170 is the industry standard for measuring relative humidity inside concrete slabs. Most epoxy manufacturers set the threshold at 75% RH; slabs reading above that require a moisture vapor barrier before any coating. [Xtreme Polishing Systems](https://xtremepolishingsystems.com/blogs/decorative-concrete-and-epoxy-blog/epoxy-floor-fails-why-moisture-testing-mvbs-matter)
- Cape Coral has 400+ miles of canals, and the city holds a Class 5 Community Rating System rating — the pervasive flood zone status correlates with high subslab moisture conditions. [City of Cape Coral - Flood Protection](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/development_services/building_division/flood_protection.php)
- Without a moisture vapor barrier, trapped vapor creates osmotic blisters that cause epoxy to swell, bubble, and pull away, with complete system failure possible within 12 to 24 months. [Xtreme Polishing Systems](https://xtremepolishingsystems.com/blogs/decorative-concrete-and-epoxy-blog/epoxy-floor-fails-why-moisture-testing-mvbs-matter)

### Humidity Impact
- Cape Coral humidity ranges from 69–79% depending on the month, peaking at 78–79% in July through September and dropping to 69–71% in March. [Climates to Travel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/united-states/cape-coral)
- Above 85% relative humidity, moisture condenses on epoxy surfaces and triggers amine blushing — white patches or greasy layers that block intercoat adhesion. [PCI Magazine](https://www.pcimag.com/articles/85460-amine-blushing-and-blooming-of-epoxy-binder-systems-in-protective-coatings)
- The substrate temperature must stay at least 5 °F above the dew point before, during, and after epoxy application to prevent condensation between coating and concrete. [PCI Magazine](https://www.pcimag.com/articles/85460-amine-blushing-and-blooming-of-epoxy-binder-systems-in-protective-coatings)
- At 86 °F, epoxy pot life drops to approximately 15 minutes versus 25 minutes at 68 °F, forcing installers to work in smaller batches. Cape Coral summer slab temperatures can reach 90–110 °F. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/how-humidity-affects-epoxy-floors-in-miami/)
- Professional installers use industrial dehumidifiers, thermostatically controlled workspace conditions, and dew point monitoring; applications are typically scheduled for early morning hours. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/how-humidity-affects-epoxy-floors-in-miami/)

### Garage Usage
- Cape Coral has more than 400 miles of navigable canals — more than any other city in the world — with approximately 40,000 waterfront estates, many providing direct Gulf access. [City of Cape Coral](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/parks_recreation/outdoor_recreation/boating.php)
- Lee County had approximately 49,000–51,000 registered recreational vessels as of 2023–2024, ranking among the top three Florida counties for boat registrations. [Lee County Government](https://www.leefl.gov/459/Safe-Boating)
- Cape Coral city code requires boats and trailers to be stored in the rear yard or entirely within a garage; RVs must be parked entirely within a garage or carport. [City of Cape Coral - Code Compliance](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/development_services/code_compliance_division/common_code_violations.php)
- All single-family homes in Cape Coral must include a garage with minimum dimensions of 14 × 20 feet of unobstructed space; carports are prohibited on single-family detached properties. [Cape Coral Land Development Code](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/capecoral-fl/ereader/files/basic-html/page123.html)
- Canal-front properties experience accelerated corrosion on metal garage components due to salt air exposure, making corrosion-resistant floor coatings particularly valuable. [Champions Garage Door Service](https://championsgaragedoorservice.com/locations/florida/cape-coral/)
- Garages serve as hurricane staging areas in Cape Coral; impact-rated doors (140–150 mph) create protected enclosures during hurricane season. [Champions Garage Door Service](https://championsgaragedoorservice.com/locations/florida/cape-coral/)

## Niche: paver-sealing

### Permit Requirements
- Paver brick installation requires a permit from the City of Cape Coral; pavers must be approximately 2¾" thick placed on 1" of sand over 4" minimum of limerock. [Cape Coral Permitting](https://www.capecoral.gov/Documents/Document%20Hub/Permit%20Document%20Center/Applications/Packet_One%20or%20Two%20Family%20Dwelling%20Application.pdf)
- Decorative surface pavers (¼" plus thin set) on driveways do not require a permit unless it is a swale drive. [Cape Coral Permitting](https://www.capecoral.gov/Documents/Document%20Hub/Permit%20Document%20Center/Applications/Packet_One%20or%20Two%20Family%20Dwelling%20Application.pdf)
- Contractors must register with the City of Cape Coral to apply for permits; a state license is no longer required for paver block or paving work. [Cape Coral Permitting](https://www.capecoral.gov/department/community_development/customer_service-permitting/private_provider.php)

### Local Regulations
- The City requires an impervious surface calculation sheet for driveway and hardscape projects to manage stormwater runoff. [Cape Coral Stormwater Division](https://www.capecoral.gov/Documents/Document%20Hub/Permit%20Document%20Center/Miscellaneous%20Documents/ImperviousSurfaceCalculationSheet.pdf)

### Soil Type
- Cape Coral sits on the Fort Thompson Formation, characterized by sandy soils over limestone bedrock with poor load-bearing capacity and high susceptibility to erosion. [Foundation Pile Co. SWFL](https://fpcswfl.com/southwest-florida-soils)
- Sandy soil drains fast and settles unevenly; heavy storms carry sand away from beneath pavers, causing settling, rocking corners, and surface waves. [Outdoor Life Pros](https://www.outdoorlifepros.com/paver-base-depth-guide-for-cape-coral-patios-and-driveways)
- Crushed limestone is the recommended base material for Cape Coral paver installations, with geotextile separator fabric used when soils are soft. [Outdoor Life Pros](https://www.outdoorlifepros.com/paver-base-depth-guide-for-cape-coral-patios-and-driveways)

### Paver Styles
- Concrete pavers are the most popular choice for Cape Coral driveways, available in a wide range of colors, textures, and patterns. [Blue Collar Q](https://bluecollarq.net/blog/driveway-pavers-cape-coral)
- Travertine pavers are a popular natural stone option for pool decks and patios — cool to the touch and slip-resistant. [Tuscan Paving Stone](https://www.tuscanpavingstone.com/home/search/pavers-in-cape-coral/)
- Classic clay brick pavers are used for driveways and walkways; they can last 50–100 years with proper maintenance. [Blue Collar Q](https://bluecollarq.net/pavers-cape-coral)
- The 45° herringbone pattern is the top choice for driveways due to its performance under heavy vehicle traffic. [Outdoor Life Pros](https://www.outdoorlifepros.com/paver-pattern-guide-for-cape-coral-driveways-and-patios)

### Salt Exposure
- Salt particles carried by coastal breezes settle on paver surfaces, seeping into porous materials and causing corrosion that shortens paver lifespan. [Alliance Pavers](https://www.alliancepavers.com/blog/how-florida-climate-affects-your-pavers-and-hardscaping)
- Salt residue accelerates surface wear and weakens joint sand, leading to shifting pavers and weed growth. [Paver Protectors](https://www.paverprotectors.com/paver-cleaning-sealing-cape-coral-fl/)
- Efflorescence (white powdery residue) forms when salt and minerals are carried to the surface by moisture; spalling causes surfaces to flake and crumble. [Alliance Pavers](https://www.alliancepavers.com/blog/how-florida-climate-affects-your-pavers-and-hardscaping)
- Most Cape Coral properties need sealing every 2–3 years; waterfront properties with higher salt exposure benefit from the shorter cycle. [AAA Pressure Washing](https://www.aaapressurewashingnow.com/post/why-paver-sealing-for-cape-coral-driveways-and-pool-decks)

## Niche: window-tinting

### Permit Requirements
- No permit is required for vehicle window tinting in Florida. Tint shops must comply with Florida Statute §316.2953 for legal VLT limits. [Florida Statute §316.2953](https://floridacourtrules.com/florida-window-tint-law/)

### Local Regulations
- Front side windows on all vehicles must allow at least 28% VLT (visible light transmittance) with no more than 25% reflectance. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-laws/)
- Rear side windows and rear windshield on sedans require at least 15% VLT; SUVs, vans, and trucks may go as dark as 6% VLT. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-laws/)
- Windshields may have a non-reflective tint strip above the manufacturer's AS-1 line only; full windshield tinting is not legal. [Howell Window Tinting](https://howellwindowtinting.com/legal-window-tinting-in-florida/)
- Colored tint that changes the normal color of the window is not allowed in Florida. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-laws/)
- A sticker identifying legal tinting is required on the inside of the driver's side door jamb. [Howell Window Tinting](https://howellwindowtinting.com/auto-window-tinting-guide-in-cape-coral-florida/)

### Sun Exposure
- Cape Coral is classified as a "Very High Risk Area" for UV exposure with an annual average UV index of 7.6–7.7. [WeatherOnline](https://www.woweather.com/Florida/CapeCoral/UVindex.htm)
- Maximum UV index reaches 12 (Extreme category) during April, May, and August. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)
- Cape Coral enjoys approximately 3,622 hours of sunshine per year, making it an exceptionally sunny location. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)

### Vehicle Types
- Popular vehicles in Cape Coral include sedans (Honda Civic, Toyota Camry, Nissan Altima), trucks (Ford F-150, Chevy Silverado, Toyota Tacoma), and SUVs (Jeep Cherokee, Ford Explorer, Chevy Tahoe). [AutoTrader Cape Coral](https://www.autotrader.com/cars-for-sale/certified-cars/suv-crossover/cape-coral-fl)
- Florida has over 18 million registered vehicles statewide; Lee County's DMV handles all vehicle registration and titling services for Cape Coral residents. [Florida License Plate](https://floridalicenseplate.org/registration-statistics)
