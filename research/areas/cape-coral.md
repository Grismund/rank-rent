# Local Facts: Cape Coral, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-09
**Niches:** paver-sealing, window-tinting
**County:** Lee County
**Population:** 236,264 (2025) — [Data Commons](https://datacommons.org/place/geoId/1210275)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Southwest Cape Coral: Established residential area with many canal-front properties and mature landscaping. [Cape Coral GIS Maps](https://www.capecoral.gov/department/information_technology_services/interactive_gis_maps.php)
- Northwest Cape Coral: Rapidly developing section with newer subdivisions and commercial growth. [Cape Coral GIS Maps](https://www.capecoral.gov/department/information_technology_services/interactive_gis_maps.php)
- Southeast Cape Coral: Includes the Cape Harbour marina district and waterfront dining. [Cape Coral City Council](https://www.capecoral.gov/government/city_council/index.php)
- Northeast Cape Coral: Mix of established and new-build neighborhoods; includes Burnt Store corridor. [Cape Coral City Council](https://www.capecoral.gov/government/city_council/index.php)

### Climate
- Warm subtropical climate with an annual mean temperature of 74.2 °F (23.4 °C); August averages 81.9 °F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/cape-coral-1654/)
- Average annual rainfall of 53 inches across 108 precipitation days, with June as the wettest month at 9.4 inches. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/cape-coral-1654/)
- Average humidity of 72%, remaining high year-round and especially in autumn. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/cape-coral-1654/)
- Classified as a "Very High Risk Area" for UV exposure with an annual average UV index of 7.6–7.7 and 3,622 hours of sunshine per year. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)

## Niche: paver-sealing

### Permit Requirements
- Paver brick installation requires a permit from the City of Cape Coral; pavers must be approximately 2¾" thick placed on 1" of sand over 4" minimum of limerock. [Cape Coral Permitting](https://www.capecoral.gov/Documents/Document%20Hub/Permit%20Document%20Center/Applications/Packet_One%20or%20Two%20Family%20Dwelling%20Application.pdf)
- Decorative surface pavers (¼" plus thin set) on driveways do not require a permit unless it is a swale drive. [Cape Coral Permitting](https://www.capecoral.gov/Documents/Document%20Hub/Permit%20Document%20Center/Applications/Packet_One%20or%20Two%20Family%20Dwelling%20Application.pdf)
- Contractors must register with the City of Cape Coral to apply for permits; a state license is no longer required for paver block or paving work. [Cape Coral Permitting](https://www.capecoral.gov/department/community_development/customer_service-permitting/private_provider.php)

### Local Regulations
- The City requires an impervious surface calculation sheet for driveway and hardscape projects to manage stormwater runoff. [Cape Coral Stormwater Division](https://www.capecoral.gov/Documents/Document%20Hub/Permit%20Document%20Center/Miscellaneous%20Documents/ImperviousSurfaceCalculationSheet.pdf)

### Soil Type
- Cape Coral sits on the Fort Thompson Formation, characterized by sandy soils over limestone bedrock with poor load-bearing capacity and high susceptibility to erosion. [Foundation Pile Co. SWFL](https://fpcswfl.com/southwest-florida-soils)
- Sandy soil drains fast and settles unevenly; heavy storms carry sand away from beneath pavers, causing settling, rocking corners, and surface waves. [Outdoor Life Pros](https://www.outdoorlifepros.com/paver-base-depth-guide-for-cape-coral-patios-and-driveways)
- Crushed limestone is the recommended base material for Cape Coral paver installations, with geotextile separator fabric used when soils are soft. [Outdoor Life Pros](https://www.outdoorlifepros.com/paver-base-depth-guide-for-cape-coral-patios-and-driveways)

### Paver Styles
- Concrete pavers are the most popular choice for Cape Coral driveways, available in a wide range of colors, textures, and patterns. [Blue Collar Q](https://bluecollarq.net/blog/driveway-pavers-cape-coral)
- Travertine pavers are a popular natural stone option for pool decks and patios — cool to the touch and slip-resistant. [Tuscan Paving Stone](https://www.tuscanpavingstone.com/home/search/pavers-in-cape-coral/)
- Classic clay brick pavers are used for driveways and walkways; they can last 50–100 years with proper maintenance. [Blue Collar Q](https://bluecollarq.net/pavers-cape-coral)
- The 45° herringbone pattern is the top choice for driveways due to its performance under heavy vehicle traffic. [Outdoor Life Pros](https://www.outdoorlifepros.com/paver-pattern-guide-for-cape-coral-driveways-and-patios)

### Salt Exposure
- Salt particles carried by coastal breezes settle on paver surfaces, seeping into porous materials and causing corrosion that shortens paver lifespan. [Alliance Pavers](https://www.alliancepavers.com/blog/how-florida-climate-affects-your-pavers-and-hardscaping)
- Salt residue accelerates surface wear and weakens joint sand, leading to shifting pavers and weed growth. [Paver Protectors](https://www.paverprotectors.com/paver-cleaning-sealing-cape-coral-fl/)
- Efflorescence (white powdery residue) forms when salt and minerals are carried to the surface by moisture; spalling causes surfaces to flake and crumble. [Alliance Pavers](https://www.alliancepavers.com/blog/how-florida-climate-affects-your-pavers-and-hardscaping)
- Most Cape Coral properties need sealing every 2–3 years; waterfront properties with higher salt exposure benefit from the shorter cycle. [AAA Pressure Washing](https://www.aaapressurewashingnow.com/post/why-paver-sealing-for-cape-coral-driveways-and-pool-decks)

## Niche: window-tinting

### Permit Requirements
- No permit is required for vehicle window tinting in Florida. Tint shops must comply with Florida Statute §316.2953 for legal VLT limits. [Florida Statute §316.2953](https://floridacourtrules.com/florida-window-tint-law/)

### Local Regulations
- Front side windows on all vehicles must allow at least 28% VLT (visible light transmittance) with no more than 25% reflectance. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-laws/)
- Rear side windows and rear windshield on sedans require at least 15% VLT; SUVs, vans, and trucks may go as dark as 6% VLT. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-laws/)
- Windshields may have a non-reflective tint strip above the manufacturer's AS-1 line only; full windshield tinting is not legal. [Howell Window Tinting](https://howellwindowtinting.com/legal-window-tinting-in-florida/)
- Colored tint that changes the normal color of the window is not allowed in Florida. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-laws/)
- A sticker identifying legal tinting is required on the inside of the driver's side door jamb. [Howell Window Tinting](https://howellwindowtinting.com/auto-window-tinting-guide-in-cape-coral-florida/)

### Sun Exposure
- Cape Coral is classified as a "Very High Risk Area" for UV exposure with an annual average UV index of 7.6–7.7. [WeatherOnline](https://www.woweather.com/Florida/CapeCoral/UVindex.htm)
- Maximum UV index reaches 12 (Extreme category) during April, May, and August. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)
- Cape Coral enjoys approximately 3,622 hours of sunshine per year, making it an exceptionally sunny location. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)

### Vehicle Types
- Popular vehicles in Cape Coral include sedans (Honda Civic, Toyota Camry, Nissan Altima), trucks (Ford F-150, Chevy Silverado, Toyota Tacoma), and SUVs (Jeep Cherokee, Ford Explorer, Chevy Tahoe). [AutoTrader Cape Coral](https://www.autotrader.com/cars-for-sale/certified-cars/suv-crossover/cape-coral-fl)
- Florida has over 18 million registered vehicles statewide; Lee County's DMV handles all vehicle registration and titling services for Cape Coral residents. [Florida License Plate](https://floridalicenseplate.org/registration-statistics)
