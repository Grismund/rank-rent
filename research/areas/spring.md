# Local Facts: Spring, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** bathroom-remodel, pressure-washing, window-tinting
**County:** Primarily Harris County; northern portions extend into Montgomery County
**Population:** 70,058 (2026 est.) — [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-cities/texas/spring)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Old Town Spring: A walkable historic district built around the original 1900s railroad town, featuring 150+ independent shops, art galleries, restaurants, and seasonal community festivals along Spring-Cypress and Hardy roads. [Spring News](https://springnews.org/about)
- Gleannloch Farms: A master-planned community in Klein ISD with a resort-style pool, golf course, community lake, and 1,600+ homes; one of the most popular Spring neighborhoods. [HAR.com](https://www.har.com/spring/realestate/neighborhoods)
- Imperial Oaks: A newer master-planned community in Conroe ISD in the northeast Spring area with trails, splash pads, and family-oriented amenities. [Revilo Real Estate](https://revilorealestate.com/blog/moving-to-spring-texas-neighborhoods-schools-commutes)
- Champion Forest / Spring Creek Forest: Established subdivisions from the 1970s–1980s with large lots, mature trees, and proximity to Spring Creek Greenway trails. [Discover Spring Texas](https://discoverspringtexas.com/spring-texas-neighborhoods/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Cfa) with a mean annual temperature of 68.7 °F; August averages a high of 93 °F and January averages a low of 42 °F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/9298/Average-Weather-in-Conroe-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual rainfall of 49 inches with significant rainfall year-round; October is the wettest month at 5.4 inches. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/texas-usa/conroe-climate)
- Relative humidity remains 73%–77% year-round, peaking in September at 77%. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/9298/Average-Weather-in-Conroe-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)

## Niche: bathroom-remodel

### Permit Requirements
- Spring is unincorporated; the portion in Harris County is governed by Harris County permits, and the portion in Montgomery County is governed by Montgomery County permits. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)
- Bathroom remodels in Spring require permits when plumbing is relocated, new drain lines are added, or electrical circuits are changed; cosmetic-only work does not require a permit. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)
- Standard bathroom remodel permits with complete documentation are typically approved in 3–7 business days in both Harris County and Montgomery County. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)
- A permit must be issued and active before the rough-in inspection can be scheduled. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)
- Most permitted remodels require at least a rough inspection (before walls close) and a final inspection. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/what-to-prepare-before-rough-in-inspection-for-bathroom-remodels)

### Local Regulations
- Harris County has adopted the International Building Code (IBC) and International Residential Code (IRC) as the basis for building standards. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)
- Electrical installations must meet National Electrical Code (NEC) bathroom specifications, including GFCI protection for all outlets. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/what-to-prepare-before-rough-in-inspection-for-bathroom-remodels)
- Bathroom exhaust fans must provide a minimum of 1 CFM per square foot to address humidity concerns. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/what-to-prepare-before-rough-in-inspection-for-bathroom-remodels)
- Spring straddles four school districts (Spring ISD, Klein ISD, Conroe ISD, Tomball ISD) and two counties, so permit jurisdiction depends on exact property location. [Wikipedia — Spring, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring,_Texas)
- Inspections are scheduled within 24–48 hours of request during active construction phases. [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)

### Plumbing Codes
- Plumbing contractors must hold a current license from the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE). [TSBPE](https://www.tsbpe.texas.gov/)
- Permitted bathroom remodels in Spring require: rough plumbing inspection (before walls are closed), shower pan/waterproofing inspection (before tile is installed), rough electrical inspection (before walls are closed), and final inspection (after all fixtures are installed). [Select Remodeling](https://selectremodelingandconstruction.com/blogs/permit-requirements-for-bathroom-remodel-in-spring-tx)
- 2025 plumbing standards mandate low-flow fixtures for water efficiency across all new installations. [On Point Plumber](https://onpointplumber.com/plumbing-code-updates/)
- Texas plumbing code specifies requirements for pipe sizing, venting, fixture spacing, water pressure, and accessibility. [Austin Remodeling Experts](https://austinremodelingexperts.com/does-bathroom-remodel-require-permit-texas-2025/)
- Hot water heater installations and relocations require a separate plumbing permit and inspection. [JBN Bathroom Remodeling](https://jbnbathroomremodeling.com/bathroom-remodel-permit-texas/)

### Typical Home Age
- Spring's median construction year is approximately 1993–2002, depending on the specific area measured, making the median home age roughly 24–33 years. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/Spring-Demographics.html)
- Established neighborhoods like Champion Forest and Spring Creek Forest date to the 1970s–1980s and often have original galvanized plumbing that may need replacement during remodels. [City-Data](https://www.city-data.com/housing/houses-Spring-Texas.html)
- Only 0.5% of Spring homes were built before 1960; 29.9% were built before 1980. [City-Data](https://www.city-data.com/housing/houses-Spring-Texas.html)
- Newer master-planned communities like Imperial Oaks and Harmony (2010s+) have modern PEX plumbing and energy-efficient fixtures. [Discover Spring Texas](https://discoverspringtexas.com/spring-texas-neighborhoods/)
- Spring's 12.5% population growth from 2019 to 2024 has driven substantial new construction with updated building codes. [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/us-cities/texas/spring)

### Water Quality
- Spring draws water from both Harris County MUD systems and Montgomery County MUDs depending on location; hardness ranges from 4–9 grains per gallon on municipal supply. [CSS Water](https://csswater.com/areas-we-serve/montgomery)
- Southeast Texas groundwater commonly runs 300–800 mg/L Total Dissolved Solids (TDS); the EPA recommended limit is 500 mg/L. [Texas Water Development Board](https://www.twdb.texas.gov/publications/reports/numbered_reports/doc/R136/R136.pdf)
- Hard water mineral deposits (calcium, magnesium) accumulate in pipes and fixtures, shortening the lifespan of faucets and showerheads. [We The Plumbers TX](https://www.wetheplumberstx.com/signs-you-need-water-softener-texas/)
- Iron content in some areas causes rust staining on bathroom fixtures and tile grout. [MyTapWater.org](https://mytapwater.org/pws/tx1700458/montgomery-county-mud-47/montgomery-tx/)
- Water quality varies by MUD district; homeowners should check their specific district's annual water quality report for localized hardness and contaminant data. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/drinkingwater/trot/droughtw.html)

## Niche: pressure-washing

### Permit Requirements
- Texas does not require a specific contractor's license for pressure washing; a general business registration and EIN are sufficient. [BellaFSM](https://www.bellafsm.com/need-license-to-start-pressure-washing-business/)
- Spring does not have a municipal pressure-washing permit because it is unincorporated; county-level business licensing applies. [StartPermit](https://startpermit.com/blog/how-to-start-a-pressure-washing-business/)
- Commercial operations generating significant wastewater volumes may need an individual TPDES discharge permit from TCEQ. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/downloads/permitting/wastewater/general/txg670000-issued-2025.pdf)
- A sales tax permit from the Texas Comptroller is required for businesses charging for pressure washing services. [StartPermit](https://startpermit.com/blog/how-to-start-a-pressure-washing-business/)
- HOA-managed communities (Gleannloch Farms, Imperial Oaks, etc.) may impose their own vendor registration and insurance requirements. [HAR.com](https://www.har.com/spring/realestate/neighborhoods)

### Local Regulations
- Under the Clean Water Act and Texas TPDES program, pressure washing wastewater may not be discharged into storm drains; it must be captured and disposed of via sanitary sewer or hauled to a disposal facility. [Hotsy South Texas](https://hotsysouthtexas.com/stormwater-compliance-solutions-for-commercial-pressure-washing/)
- Harris County storm drains flow untreated to Spring Creek and Cypress Creek; wash water containing soap, degreaser, oil, or grime counts as illegal discharge. [Sugar Land TX](https://www.sugarlandtx.gov/676/Pressure-Washing)
- Federal civil penalties under the Clean Water Act can exceed $50,000 per violation per day for illegal discharge. [StartPermit](https://startpermit.com/blog/how-to-start-a-pressure-washing-business/)
- Houston-area enforcement can levy fines up to $10,000 per day for stormwater discharge violations. [Launch Local](https://www.launchlocal.shop/blogs/news/houston-pressure-washing-wastewater-rules)
- Wastewater containing paint chips or lead from pre-1978 surfaces is classified as hazardous material under EPA rules and cannot be routed to the sanitary sewer. [City of Denton BMP Manual](https://www.cityofdenton.com/DocumentCenter/View/388/Pressure-Wash-Manual-PDF)

### Mold Mildew Risk
- Southeast Texas and the Gulf Coast consistently rank as the highest-risk regions for mold exposure due to year-round humidity, heavy rainfall, and warm temperatures. [MoldCo](https://www.moldco.com/blog/mold-is-a-pervasive-issue-that-can-affect-any-indo)
- Average summer humidity levels hover between 70% and 90%, the range in which mold spores thrive and colonize exterior surfaces. [HouMold](https://houmold.com/blog/houston-climate-and-mold/)
- Homes near Spring Creek or along shaded greenbelts face elevated moisture exposure and typically need exterior cleaning every 6–12 months. [Advantage Pro Services](https://advantageproservices.com/blog/algae-and-mildew-removal-in-humid-texas-weather/)
- High humidity causes dirt, dust, and pollen to stick to exterior surfaces, creating an ideal base for algae and mildew growth. [Advantage Pro Services](https://advantageproservices.com/blog/algae-and-mildew-removal-in-humid-texas-weather/)
- Older neighborhoods like Champion Forest with mature tree canopy and aging siding are especially susceptible to mold and algae buildup. [Advantage Pro Services](https://advantageproservices.com/blog/algae-and-mildew-removal-in-humid-texas-weather/)

### Tree Coverage
- Spring sits at the southern edge of the East Texas Piney Woods; loblolly pine and hardwood forests are prevalent. [Wikipedia — Spring, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring,_Texas)
- Established neighborhoods like Champion Forest, Spring Creek Forest, and Gleannloch Farms feature heavy mature tree canopy providing shade but also dropping debris year-round. [Discover Spring Texas](https://discoverspringtexas.com/spring-texas-neighborhoods/)
- For regional context, Houston's urban forest covers 18.4% of the city's land area; northern suburbs like Spring have significantly higher canopy coverage due to proximity to the Piney Woods. [USDA Forest Service](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/54109)
- Dense canopy drops needles, leaves, and organic debris onto roofs, driveways, and sidewalks, accelerating algae and staining. [Advantage Pro Services](https://advantageproservices.com/blog/algae-and-mildew-removal-in-humid-texas-weather/)
- Spring Creek Greenway, a 16-mile nature corridor through Spring, adds significant tree cover to adjacent properties. [Wikipedia — Spring, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring,_Texas)

### Water Restrictions
- Spring-area water is supplied by various MUDs in both Harris and Montgomery counties, each with its own drought contingency plan. [TCEQ Drought List](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/drinkingwater/trot/droughtw.html)
- Montgomery County MUDs enforce tiered drought stages; Stage 2 limits outdoor water use to assigned days, and Stage 3 can prohibit landscape irrigation entirely. [MC MUD 88](https://www.mcmud88.org/2023/09/drought-stage-2-water-conservation-measures-for-residents/)
- Harris County MUDs similarly implement mandatory conservation during drought conditions with odd/even watering schedules. [MC MUD 105](https://www.mcmud105.org/category/water/)
- Hand watering with a nozzle-equipped hose is generally permitted even during Stage 2 restrictions. [MC MUD 88](https://www.mcmud88.org/2023/09/drought-stage-2-water-conservation-measures-for-residents/)
- Pressure washing is typically categorized as a non-irrigation activity but operators should confirm with the local MUD during active drought stages. [TCEQ Drought List](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/drinkingwater/trot/droughtw.html)

## Niche: window-tinting

### Tint Laws
- Texas Transportation Code Section 547.613 requires front side windows to allow at least 25% visible light transmission (VLT). [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/texas/)
- Windshield tint is restricted to the area above the manufacturer's AS-1 line (or top 5 inches if no AS-1 line); the film plus glass must allow at least 25% VLT and must not exceed 25% reflectance. [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/texas/)
- Back side windows and rear window have no minimum VLT requirement when the vehicle has an outside mirror on each side. [Tint Laws](https://www.tintlaws.com/states/texas/)
- Red, blue, and amber tint colors are prohibited on all vehicle windows. [Tint Laws](https://www.tintlaws.com/states/texas/)
- A compliance sticker identifying the installer and manufacturer must be placed between the film and glass on the driver's side window; installing tint without this label is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to $1,000. [Traffic Ticket Fine](https://www.trafficticketfine.com/en/texas/texas-window-tint-ticket-fines)
- Medical exemptions are available under Section 547.613 for persons requiring sun protection. [Law Walls](https://lawwalls.com/texas-window-tint-laws/)

- August averages 10.2 hours of sunshine per day; December averages 4.9 hours. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/texas-usa/conroe-climate)
- The area averages approximately 205–267 sunny days per year depending on measurement methodology. [BestPlaces](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/conroe)
- Annual clear-sky UV index average is approximately 6.6–7.0, making UV protection a year-round concern for vehicle interiors. [Nomad Season](https://nomadseason.com/weather/united-states/texas/conroe-may.html)
