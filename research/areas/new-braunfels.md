## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Historic Downtown / Gruene: German-heritage district and the adjacent Gruene Historic District with dance halls, shops, and the Comal and Guadalupe Rivers. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Braunfels,_Texas)
- Canyon Lake: Unincorporated community northwest of New Braunfels centered on the reservoir; popular for recreation and Hill Country living. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-cities-neighborhoods-to-live-in-comal-county-tx/)
- Vintage Oaks: Master-planned community in the Hill Country west of New Braunfels with acreage lots and resort amenities. [Texas Ally](https://www.texasally.com/neighborhoods/guadalupe/new-braunfels/)

### Climate
- Hot and humid subtropical climate with an average annual temperature of 68 °F (20 °C); temperatures range from 39 °F to 97 °F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/new-braunfels-1556/)
- August is the hottest month with an average high of 96 °F and low of 72 °F; January lows average 39 °F. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/new_braunfels)
- Average annual precipitation of 35 inches. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/texas/new_braunfels)

## Niche: bathroom-remodel

### Plumbing Codes
- New Braunfels has adopted the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) and ICC Code, 2021 Edition. [City of New Braunfels](https://newbraunfels.gov/3430/Building-Safety)
- A permit is required any time a bathroom remodel alters plumbing, electrical, gas, or mechanical systems; every contractor must be registered with the city. [City of New Braunfels](https://newbraunfels.gov/3433/Building-Permits)
- Adding a new fixture (sink, toilet, shower) requires a plumbing permit; rerouting supply or drain lines requires plumbing and potentially building permits. [Permit Mint](https://permitmint.com/guides/plumbing/texas/new-braunfels/)
- Replacing an existing fixture in the same location does not require a plumbing permit. [Permit Mint](https://permitmint.com/guides/plumbing/texas/new-braunfels/)
- Electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and fuel gas permits are separate schedules and NOT bundled into the building permit. [City of New Braunfels](https://newbraunfels.gov/3433/Building-Permits)

### Typical Home Age
- Median construction year for New Braunfels homes is approximately 2006; about 3.1% built before the 1940s. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/TX/New-Braunfels-Demographics.html)
- The city's annual growth rate of 4–5% over the past decade means a significant share of housing stock is less than 15 years old. [Niche](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/new-braunfels-comal-tx/)

### Water Quality
- New Braunfels water hardness is 20 GPG (very hard), causing $1,380–2,230 annual damage to appliances per household; 14 detected contaminants with 9 exceeding health guidelines. [Soft Pro Water Systems](https://www.softprowatersystems.com/pages/new-braunfels-texas-water-quality-report-treatment-solutions)
- Very hard water accelerates appliance failure: water heater anode rods exhaust in 2–3 years instead of 6–8; scale deposits form at fixture connections within months. [Soft Pro Water Systems](https://www.softprowatersystems.com/pages/new-braunfels-texas-water-quality-report-treatment-solutions)
- Tankless water heater heat exchangers accumulate mineral buildup that can reduce lifespan by half without regular descaling. [Soft Pro Water Systems](https://www.softprowatersystems.com/pages/new-braunfels-texas-water-quality-report-treatment-solutions)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- The City of New Braunfels requires permits for construction and alteration work affecting structure or systems; residential epoxy over existing concrete generally does not require a building permit. [City of New Braunfels](https://newbraunfels.gov/3433/Building-Permits)
- Commercial projects involving floor drains, structural modifications, or fire-rating changes require permits. [City of New Braunfels](https://newbraunfels.gov/3430/Building-Safety)

### Local Regulations
- Building Safety department located at 550 Landa St, New Braunfels, TX 78130; accepts online applications. [City of New Braunfels](https://newbraunfels.gov/3430/Building-Safety)
- Texas does not require a specific state epoxy certification; no local licensing for epoxy flooring contractors beyond general contractor registration. [City of New Braunfels](https://newbraunfels.gov/3433/Building-Permits)

### Concrete Condition
- New Braunfels sits on the Balcones Fault where Hill Country limestone meets expansive clay of the Blackland Prairie; this causes slab movement from soil expansion and contraction. [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/austin/new-braunfels/housing-real-estate/2023/07/14/foundation-issues-on-the-rise-in-new-braunfels/)
- Expansive clay soil causes 78% of foundation failures in the area; moisture fluctuations create constant pressure leading to shifting, cracking, and uneven settling. [Best Foundation Repair New Braunfels](https://bestfoundationrepairnewbraunfels.com/how-new-braunfels-soil-affects-your-foundation-19-years-of-data/)
- Polished concrete and rigid coatings can be problematic due to soil movement; flexible coating systems are recommended. [FS Foundation & Construction](https://fsfoundationandconstruction.com/slab-foundations)

### Humidity Impact
- Epoxy installs best when concrete moisture is below 75% RH and slab temperatures are 55–90 °F. [New Era Epoxy](https://neweraepoxy.com/faq)
- Vacation homes and lake cabins around Canyon Lake are popular candidates for epoxy garage floors because the coating handles humidity, occasional flooding, and infrequent maintenance. [Triple C Epoxy Flooring](https://www.triplecepoxyflooring.com/)

### Garage Usage
- New Braunfels' rapid growth has produced large subdivisions with standard two- and three-car garages used for vehicle parking, workshop space, and recreation storage. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-cities-neighborhoods-to-live-in-comal-county-tx/)
- The area's outdoor recreation culture (tubing, kayaking, hiking) means many garages store significant sporting equipment. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Braunfels,_Texas)
