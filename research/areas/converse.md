# Local Facts: Converse, Texas

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring
**County:** Bexar County
**Population:** 32,481 (2025 est.) — [Census Bureau via Data Commons](https://datacommons.org/place/geoId/4816468)

## City Overview

### Location and Geography
- Converse is a suburb approximately 15 miles northeast of downtown San Antonio in Bexar County, situated along FM 78 between Loop 1604 and Loop 410. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse,_Texas)
- The city grew as a residential community serving nearby Joint Base San Antonio-Randolph; military families, veterans, and civilian DoD employees make up a significant share of residents. [Texas Home Talk](https://texashometalk.com/communities/converse/)

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- **Northampton:** The most established neighborhood in Converse, located off FM 78 near Crestway and Converse City Park; features brick ranch homes with mature red oak trees, a community swimming pool, playground, and park. [10 Federal Storage](https://10federalstorage.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-in-converse-tx)
- **Quiet Creek:** Premium-lot subdivision near Gibbs Sprawl Rd and Loop 410, with lots averaging 0.57 acres (vs. 0.14-0.16 acre citywide average); homes built around 2014, averaging 3,193 sq ft with three-car garages; zoned to East Central ISD. [My Texas Home Resource](https://www.mytexashomeresource.com/converse/quiet-creek-homes-for-sale/)
- **The Wilder / Texas Palm Drive corridor:** Northeastern Converse with the city's most recent residential development, featuring contemporary construction and access to newer commercial development along Loop 1604. [HAR.com](https://www.har.com/ri/4632/converse-tx-areas-new-residents-are-choosing-to-settle)
- **Meadow Brook / Knox Ridge / Ventura Heights:** Mid-range subdivisions in central Converse served by Judson ISD; popular with military families for their proximity to JBSA-Randolph (5-minute commute) and homes that fit E-5 through O-3 BAH. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/best-neighborhoods-in-converse-tx/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate (Cfa) with an average annual temperature of 69 degrees F; August is the hottest month with an average high of 95 degrees F and low of 74 degrees F; January is the coldest with a high of 63 degrees F and low of 43 degrees F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/7111/Average-Weather-in-Converse-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Annual rainfall of approximately 34 inches, peaking in May (4.4 inches); hot season lasts 3.7 months (late May through mid-September). [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/7111/Average-Weather-in-Converse-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual relative humidity of approximately 67%, peaking at 71% in May and dropping to 59% in August; morning humidity averages 84% before falling to 52% by afternoon. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/san-antonio/climate)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- The City of San Antonio's No Permit Required List includes "painting, plastering, repairing of sheet rock, wallpapering and tiling" for residential properties; epoxy coating over an existing concrete garage floor is classified as a cosmetic surface treatment and generally does not require a building permit. [City of San Antonio](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/DSD/Constructing/Residential/Permits/No-Permit-Required)
- If a project involves structural changes or is part of a larger renovation, a permit may be required; permit fees typically run $70-$140. [ContractorPlus](https://contractorplus.app/resources/construction-costs/install-epoxy-flooring/san-antonio-tx)
- Texas does not require a state-level license for epoxy flooring or floor coating installers; the TDLR contractor registration program covers electricians, HVAC, and plumbers but not flooring work. [Flooring Clarity](https://www.flooringclarity.com/do-you-need-a-license-to-do-epoxy-flooring/)
- Within San Antonio city limits, contractors performing permitted work must register as Home Improvement Contractors ($150 for two years) and carry a minimum of $300,000 per occurrence liability insurance. [City of San Antonio](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/DSD/Contractor/Building-Home-Improvement)
- Converse has its own municipal government; homeowners should verify whether their property falls within San Antonio or Converse city limits for permit jurisdiction. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse,_Texas)

### Local Regulations
- Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) Chapter 115 regulates VOC emissions from surface coatings; floor coatings are subject to a VOC content limit of 250 grams per liter, which is more restrictive than the federal EPA standard of 400 g/L. [TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/stationary-rules/voc/coating)
- San Antonio has adopted the 2024 International Building Code; interior floor finishes in commercial and institutional buildings must meet fire performance standards: Class I (0.45 W/sq cm) or Class II (0.22 W/sq cm) per IBC Section 804, tested per ASTM E648. [Up Codes](https://up.codes/viewer/san-antonio/ibc-2021/chapter/8/interior-finishes)
- For residential garage applications, no specific VOC or fire-rating inspections are triggered when epoxy is applied over existing concrete without structural changes. [City of San Antonio](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/DSD/Constructing/Residential/Permits/No-Permit-Required)

### Concrete Condition
- Converse sits on Bexar County's expansive clay soils, primarily Houston Black clay — a Vertisol with 46-60% smectite (montmorillonite) content and a plasticity index in the 40s, making it one of the most reactive soil types in North America. [GL Hunt Foundation Repair](https://glhunt.com/san-antonio-foundation-repair-by-soil-neighborhood/)
- Houston Black clay can move 4-6 inches between wet and dry seasons, causing slab heave in spring and shrinkage cracks in summer drought; San Antonio homes are typically built on post-tension slab-on-grade foundations to mitigate this movement. [Texas Foundation Repair TX](https://texasfoundationrepairtx.com/texas/bexar-county/san-antonio/)
- An estimated 70% of Texas foundation failures are caused by expansive clay soil; slab movement means proper crack repair and flexible coating systems are essential before epoxy application. [DuraPier](https://durapier.com/blog/why-texas-clay-soil-causes-so-many-foundation-problems-and-what-actually-helps/)
- Moisture vapor transmission through the slab is the leading cause of epoxy coating failure in Central Texas; after heavy rains, clay soil swells and forces moisture upward through the slab, creating pressure that can exceed the 3-4 lbs per 1,000 sq ft per 24 hours that standard epoxy can tolerate. [Garage Floor Coatings Dallas](https://www.garagefloorcoatingsdallas.com/texas-concrete-isnt-like-the-rest-of-the-country-what-that-means-for-garage-floor-epoxy-installations/)
- Professional installers perform ASTM F1869 (calcium chloride) and ASTM F2170 (in-situ relative humidity probe) testing on every slab before coating; slabs above 75% internal RH or 3-5 lbs MVER require moisture mitigation primers. [One Stop Epoxy](https://onestopepoxy.com/pages/how-does-humidity-affect-epoxy-flooring-installation)

### Humidity Impact
- San Antonio area humidity averages 67% annually, with morning readings often reaching 84% and May peaks at 71%; humidity above 75% dramatically increases the risk of amine blush and intercoat delamination on epoxy. [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/san-antonio/climate) · [One Stop Epoxy](https://onestopepoxy.com/pages/how-does-humidity-affect-epoxy-flooring-installation)
- Epoxy installs optimally when ambient humidity is 40-60% and the concrete surface is dry; October through April is the best window for garage floor epoxy in the San Antonio area, when lower humidity and cooler temperatures create ideal adhesion conditions. [Pro Epoxy San Antonio](https://proepoxysanantonio.com/blog/garage-floor-epoxy-san-antonio-installation/)
- High humidity slows the curing process and can cause clouding, blushing (white haze), loss of gloss, and compromised intercoat adhesion; dehumidifiers or forced ventilation are recommended to maintain below 70% RH during application. [Crash of Rhinos Painting](https://www.crashofrhinospainting.com/blog/mastering-concrete-humidity-and-epoxy-coatings)
- Polyurea and polyaspartic coatings are more humidity-tolerant than standard epoxy and are often preferred for summer installs in the San Antonio metro; they cure in 1-4 hours vs. 24-72 hours for traditional epoxy. [San Antonio Epoxy Floor](https://sanantonioepoxyfloor.com/blog/epoxy-vs-polyaspartic-san-antonio.html)
- An estimated 40% of Texas epoxy failures are attributed to moisture issues — either ambient humidity during curing or moisture vapor transmission from below the slab. [Epoxy of Texas](https://epoxyoftexas.com/garage-floor-coating-in-texas-complete-2026-guide-to-durable-premium-flooring-so/)

### Garage Usage
- Converse is a bedroom community where the vast majority of homes sit on concrete slab foundations with attached garages; two-car garages are standard, with three-car options increasingly available in newer subdivisions like Quiet Creek and The Wilder. [My Texas Home Resource](https://www.mytexashomeresource.com/converse/quiet-creek-homes-for-sale/)
- Nationally, 76% of homeowners use garages for vehicle parking, 47% for home improvement projects, and 38% for automotive projects; 36% of homeowners report their garage is too packed with belongings to park a vehicle inside. [CRAFTSMAN Survey 2024](https://press.craftsman.com/2024-03-06-A-Peek-Behind-the-Garage-Door-More-than-1-in-4-Homeowners-Surveyed-Say-Their-Garage-Helped-Start-a-Side-Hustle,-According-to-Newly-Released-CRAFTSMAN-R-Survey)
- Military families from JBSA-Randolph are a significant market segment; many Converse garages serve dual duty as workshops and storage, with military families using garage space for equipment, tools, and household goods between PCS rotations. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/where-military-families-are-moving-near-san-antonio-and-austin/)
- In the Southwest/Texas region, 49% of potential homeowners prefer a three-car garage over a two-car (41%), reflecting the use of garages as multipurpose spaces beyond just vehicle parking. [Builder Online](https://www.builderonline.com/design/consumer-trends/when-it-comes-to-garages-buyers-want-space-for-at-least-two-cars_o)
- Median household income of approximately $94,222 (2024) supports discretionary home upgrades; median home value of $248,400 with a 74.2% homeownership rate. [Data USA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/converse-tx)
