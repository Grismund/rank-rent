## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Historic Downtown Taylor: Main Street corridor with turn-of-the-century commercial buildings, local shops, and restaurants; center of the city's Czech and German heritage. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor,_Texas)
- Heritage Square: Newer master-planned subdivision on the west side along FM 973 with contemporary homes, HOA-maintained amenities, and family-oriented design. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/taylor-tx-neighborhood-guide/)
- Eastwood: Master-planned community with mature trees, parks, and a mix of traditional and modern home styles east of downtown. [Austin Real Estate Homes Blog](https://www.austinrealestatehomesblog.com/taylor/)
- North Park / Summerfield: Established residential areas north of downtown with mid-range pricing and proximity to Taylor ISD schools. [Homes.com](https://www.homes.com/local-guide/taylor-tx/)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate with a mean annual temperature of 68.7 °F (20.4 °C); August is the hottest month averaging a high of 94 °F and low of 74 °F; January is the coldest averaging a high of 60 °F and low of 41 °F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/8045/Average-Weather-in-Taylor-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual rainfall of 36.1 inches (918 mm); May is the wettest month at 3.9 inches and July the driest at 1.5 inches. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/texas/taylor-767944/)
- Hot season lasts 3.6 months (June through mid-September); cool season lasts 2.9 months (late November through late February); partly cloudy year round. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/8045/Average-Weather-in-Taylor-Texas-United-States-Year-Round)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Permit Requirements
- Waste Connections holds an exclusive franchise for solid waste collection and disposal within Taylor city limits; no other roll-off dumpster services are allowed. For temporary roll-off dumpsters, residents must call Waste Connections at 512-282-3508. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/faq.aspx?TID=8)
- If dumpster placement requires use of a public right-of-way, a permit must be obtained through the Road & Bridge Department. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/269/Trash-and-Recycling)
- Texas municipalities may award exclusive solid waste franchises under state law; Taylor's arrangement means junk removal operators hauling to a landfill must coordinate with Waste Connections for container placement. [Texas Municipal League](https://www.tml.org/DocumentCenter/View/3321/Solid-Waste--2026-Update)

### Local Regulations
- The trash company removes one bulk item per week on the regular trash day; residents must contact Utility Billing at 512-352-2066 to request a bulk pickup prior to their scheduled day. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/faq.aspx?TID=8)
- No construction material (wood, sheetrock, carpet, etc.) is accepted for curbside bulk removal. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/faq.aspx?TID=8)
- Any item that may have contained Freon (refrigerators, freezers, A/C units) requires a certificate stating Freon has been removed before the city will collect it. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/faq.aspx?TID=8)
- Limbs must be cut to 4 feet or shorter and tied in bundles of no more than 20 pounds; loose piles are not collected. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/faq.aspx?TID=8)
- The City of Taylor hosts an annual Citywide Spring Cleanup (April 2026) with free disposal for residents at multiple drop-off locations for bulky items, tires, electronics, and brush; proof of residency required. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/calendar.aspx?EID=7627)

### Landfill Location
- Williamson County Landfill is located at 600 Landfill Road, Hutto, TX 78634 (approximately 12 miles west); operated by WM; open Monday-Friday 7 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 7 a.m.-noon. [WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/facilities/williamson-county-landfill)
- Residential disposal fee is $45.00 for cars, SUVs, or pickups without a trailer; brush is $9.50 per cubic yard; mattresses $20.00 each. [Dump Fee Finder](https://dumpfeefinder.com/places/williamson-county-landfill-hutto/)
- Georgetown Transfer Station at 250 W.L. Walden Drive, Georgetown, TX 78626 (approximately 17 miles northwest) is an alternative; open Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-5 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m.-3 p.m. [City of Georgetown](https://directory.curbwaste.com/transfer-stations/tx/williamson-county/city-of-georgetown-transfer-station-facility-georgetown/)

### Recycling Programs
- Goodwill Store & Donation Center at 3100 N. Main St, Suite 137, Taylor, TX 76574; phone 512-547-3748; open Monday-Saturday 10 a.m.-8 p.m. and Sunday 10 a.m.-7 p.m. [Goodwill Central Texas](https://www.goodwillcentraltexas.org/retail-stores/)
- Williamson County Recycling Center at 600 Landfill Road, Hutto, TX 78634 accepts oil, oil filters, scrap metal, and electronics; phone 512-759-8881. [WM](https://www.wm.com/us/en/facilities/williamson-county-landfill/recycling-center-and-daily-operations)
- City of Taylor recycling is collected bi-weekly curbside; single-stream recycling. [City of Taylor](https://taylortx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14667/2026-CITY-OF-TAYLOR-RECYCLING-SCHEDULE?bidId=)
- Williamson County Household Hazardous Waste Cleanup Events are held periodically for paint, chemicals, batteries, and electronics; Taylor residents are eligible. [City of Taylor](https://www.taylortx.gov/calendar.aspx?PREVIEW=YES&EID=7626)
- Habitat for Humanity ReStore in Round Rock at 3916 Gattis School Road, Suite 104 (20 miles southwest) accepts furniture and building materials; phone 512-863-4344. [Habitat for Humanity of Williamson County](https://www.findhelp.org/habitat-for-humanity-of-williamson-county--round-rock-tx--restore-/5652677467308032?postal=78613)

### HOA Rules
- Newer subdivisions on Taylor's west side (Heritage Square, Taylor Commons) have HOAs with rules on exterior appearance, curbside storage, and maintenance; residents should check their CC&Rs before placing dumpsters. [LRG Realty](https://lrgrealty.com/lrg-blog/taylor-tx-neighborhood-guide/)
- Common HOA rules in Williamson County require trash bins stored out of sight and removed from the curb within 24 hours of pickup. [The Bin Spa](https://thebinspa.com/blog/trash-bin-cleaning-amp-hoa-rules-in-central-texas-what-you-need-to-know)
- Older neighborhoods near downtown Taylor generally do not have HOA restrictions on dumpster placement. [Austin Real Estate Homes Blog](https://www.austinrealestatehomesblog.com/taylor/)
