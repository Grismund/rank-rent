## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Downtown Stuart: Historic waterfront district added to the National Register of Historic Places in August 2026; anchored by Old Florida charm with shopping, dining, galleries, and cultural events. [Palm Martin Real Estate](https://www.palmmartin.com/stuart/stuart-communities/)
- East Stuart: Founded in 1913 as Lincoln Park and the center of Martin County's African American community, renamed East Stuart in 1964; historic neighborhood with deep local roots. [Wikipedia – Stuart, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuart,_Florida)
- North River Shores: One of the most sought-after neighborhoods in Martin County, situated along the St. Lucie River with waterfront homes and a quiet, suburban feel. [The Haigh Group](https://www.thehaighgroup.com/blog/where-to-live-in-stuart-florida/)
- Sailfish Point: Exclusive gated community on Hutchinson Island with large luxury homes, private marina, and direct ocean access. [Meyer Lucas Real Estate](https://meyerlucas.com/blog/best-neighborhoods-stuart-florida)

### Climate
- Tropical rainforest climate with an average year-round temperature of 74.7 °F; hot season (highs above 86 °F) lasts from late May through late September. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/18778/Average-Weather-in-Stuart-Florida-United-States-Year-Round)
- Average annual rainfall of 60 inches with 131 days of precipitation per year; June is the wettest month at 5.6 inches, December the driest at 1.7 inches. [Best Places](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/fl/stuart)
- Cool season (highs below 76 °F) runs from early December through late February; January average low is 57 °F, high is 73 °F. [Weather Spark](https://weatherspark.com/y/18778/Average-Weather-in-Stuart-Florida-United-States-Year-Round)

## Niche: junk-removal

### Permit Requirements
- A dumpster on private property in Stuart does not require a permit; placement on a public street or right-of-way requires a right-of-way permit from the City of Stuart. [Dumpster.co – Stuart FL](https://dumpster.co/stuart-fl/)
- Dumpsters must be covered at all times, and the rental company is responsible for keeping the area around the dumpster clean and free of debris. [Dumpster.co – Stuart FL](https://dumpster.co/stuart-fl/)
- Illegal dumping is strictly prohibited in Stuart and violators face fines from the City Council. [Junk Garbage Removal – Stuart](https://junkgarbageremoval.com/Junk_Removal_Stuart_FL.html)

### Local Regulations
- Martin County residents may place bulk waste at the curb on their scheduled garbage collection day; oversized items tagged by the driver will be collected within 5 business days. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)
- All bulk waste must be placed within 6 feet of the curb; loose debris must be containerized; glass panels on doors or windows must be taped to prevent breakage. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)
- Waste must not be placed under utility lines, on water/gas meters, telephone/cable boxes, or within 3 feet of mailboxes or fire hydrants. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)
- Unincorporated Martin County residential curbside service is contracted to Waste Management; to schedule bulk pickup or for questions, call (772) 546-7700 or email wm.martin@wm.com. [Martin County Garbage, Recycling and Yard Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/Garbage-Recycling-Yard-Waste)
- Appliance doors must be sealed or removed before curbside placement. [Martin County Bulk Waste](https://www.martin.fl.us/BulkWaste)

### HOA Rules
- Under Florida HB 1203, an HOA cannot fine a homeowner for leaving garbage receptacles at the curb within 24 hours before or after the designated collection day. [Condo Control](https://www.condocontrol.com/blog/florida-hoas-can-no-longer-enforce-these-rules-hb-1203/)
- Most HOAs require dumpsters to remain on private driveways or designated service areas; a city permit may still be required for right-of-way placement. [Fuzion FS](https://fuzionfs.com/resources/hoa-dumpster-rental/)
- HOAs cannot permanently store trash cans on the street or curbside and typically require bins to be kept out of sight in a garage or behind the house. [NRP Management](https://www.nrp.management/hoa-trash-can-rules/)
- Bulk trash restrictions (mattresses, furniture, construction debris) may require advance notice or adherence to specific pickup days per community CC&Rs. [NRP Management](https://www.nrp.management/hoa-trash-can-rules/)

## Niche: pool-builders

### Permit Requirements
- A building permit is required for any in-ground or above-ground swimming pool in Martin County under the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023). [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)
- Pool plans must be signed and sealed by a Florida-licensed architect or engineer; barrier/fence documentation and a site plan are also required. [Martin County Pool Checklist](https://www.martin.fl.us/resources/checklist-swimming-pool-deck)
- Properties inside Stuart city limits require permits from the City of Stuart Building Department; properties in unincorporated Martin County go through the county building department at 900 SE Ruhnke Street, Stuart, FL 34994, phone (772) 288-5916. [Martin County Building Department](https://building-department.org/martin-county-building-department/)
- Pool projects also require electrical, plumbing, and (if applicable) gas sub-permits, plus a separate structural permit for screen enclosures. [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)
- Florida pool permit fees typically range from $200–$900, with most residential projects around $500; Martin County fees are calculated from the value of work under the county's adopted fee schedule. [Permit Requirements](https://permitrequirements.com/pool-permit/florida/)

### Local Regulations
- Pool enclosure and slab projects in Martin County must meet the checklist requirements under the Florida Building Code 8th Edition (2023). [Martin County Pool Enclosure Checklist](https://www.martin.fl.us/resources/checklist-pool-enclosure-slab-requirement-pdf)
- For waterfront properties in subdivisions approved after April 1982, pools must be set back at least 5 feet from the Shoreline Protection Zone or wetland buffer. [Martin County Land Development – Sec. 3.211](https://library.municode.com/fl/martin_county/codes/land_development_regulations_?nodeId=LADERE_ART3ZODI_DIV4MIDEST_S3.211DIERE)
- More complex projects like pools, room additions, and new construction typically take 2–6 weeks for plan review in Martin County. [Alliance Permitting](https://alliancepermitting.com/guides/florida/martin-county-building-permit)
- Fence barriers 6 feet or less in height are exempt from district setback limitations under Martin County Land Development Regulations Section 3.204. [Martin County Fence Rules](https://flfencerules.com/fence-rules-martin-county-florida/)

### Water Table
- The Treasure Coast's proximity to the Atlantic Ocean creates a high water table, with pool contractors typically hitting groundwater at 4–6 feet below grade in coastal areas. [Agua Construction](https://aguaconstruction.com/coastal-florida-pool-building-challenges-solutions/)
- Hydrostatic pressure from rising groundwater can lift an empty pool shell; the buoyant force can exceed the shell's weight after sustained rainfall. [South Florida Pool Authority](https://southfloridapoolauthority.com/high-water-table-pool-issues-south-florida/)
- Standard mitigation includes dewatering, French drain installation, gravel systems, and hydrostatic relief valves built into the pool shell. [Integrity Pool Development](https://www.integritypooldevelopment.com/building-a-pool-in-florida-potential-problems-to-look-out-for/)
- Some builders raise the pool's elevation by bringing in fill dirt, which lifts the shell above the high-water mark and reduces seepage risk. [Pool Brokers USA](https://poolbrokersusa.com/pool-high-water-table/)

### Pool Barrier Laws
- Florida Statute 515 requires every residential pool or spa to have a barrier at least 48 inches high on the exterior side, with no gaps larger than 4 inches. [Florida Senate – Statute 515.29](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2024/515.29)
- All pool gates must be self-closing and self-latching, swinging outward (away from the pool), with the latch release at least 54 inches above grade. [Allied Fence and Gate](https://alliedfenceandgate.com/blog/pool-fence-requirements-florida)
- The exterior face of the barrier must be non-climbable; no more than 2 inches of gap between the fence and the ground. [Pool Fence](https://poolfence.com/florida-pool-fence-requirements-explained-what-section-515-actually-says/)
- Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River building departments administer barrier inspections locally, so field interpretations may vary even though the state framework controls basic requirements. [Endless Life Design](https://www.endlesslifedesign.com/post/pool-permits-martin-st-lucie)

### HOA Pool Rules
- Most Florida HOAs require Architectural Review Committee (ARC) approval before pool construction begins; written approval is required — verbal approvals are not binding. [Pool Installer Authority](https://poolinstallerauthority.com/hoa-rules-and-pool-installation)
- HOAs frequently enforce setback rules, fencing heights (minimum 6 feet), and noise limits for pool equipment. [Pool Installer Authority](https://poolinstallerauthority.com/hoa-rules-and-pool-installation)
- CC&Rs may prohibit above-ground pools; Florida SB 630 (2021) limits some HOA restrictions but does not override existing covenants or local regulations. [I Moving to Florida](https://imovingtoflorida.com/guide/florida-pool-laws-guide/)
- County permit requirements for pools over 24 inches deep are separate from HOA rules; homeowners must comply with both. [I Moving to Florida](https://imovingtoflorida.com/guide/florida-pool-laws-guide/)
