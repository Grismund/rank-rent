# Local Facts: Pine Island Center, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-15
**Niches:** paver-sealing, window-tinting
**County:** Lee County
**Population:** 1,834 (2024) — [Data USA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/pine-island-center-fl/)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Matlacha: Tiny island community between the mainland and Pine Island, known for funky art galleries, seafood shacks, and waterfront shops; the Matlacha drawbridge is nicknamed "the fishingest bridge in the world." [Florida Backroads Travel](https://www.florida-backroads-travel.com/pine-island-florida.html)
- St. James City: Residential community at the southern end of Pine Island; a boater's paradise with quick Gulf access, waterfront dining, and dock-accessible homes. [Always Island Time Realty](https://alwaysislandtimerealty.com/pine-island-st-james-city-bokeelia-matlacha/)
- Bokeelia: Quiet community at the north tip of the island, known for palm farms, Charlotte Harbor views, and as the launch point for boat trips to Cayo Costa State Park. [GPICA](https://gpica.org/about-us/about-pine-island/)
- Pineland: Small historic community 1.6 miles from Pine Island Center with the Randell Research Center (Calusa heritage site) and some of the island's oldest homes. [Museum of the Islands](https://www.museumoftheislands.com/pine-island-information/)

### Climate
- Subtropical climate with an average annual temperature of 75 °F; summer highs average 91 °F and winter lows average 46 °F. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/pine-island-center-climate)
- Average annual rainfall of 53.2 inches, with July as the wettest month at 8.1 inches and November the driest at 2.0 inches. [BestPlaces](https://www.bestplaces.net/climate/city/florida/pine_island_center)
- Island location surrounded by Matlacha Pass and Pine Island Sound provides constant breezes but also higher salt air exposure and humidity than mainland communities. [Dwellics](https://dwellics.com/florida/climate-in-pine-island)

### Nearby Cities
- Cape Coral (15 miles east, across the Matlacha bridge)
- North Fort Myers (16 miles east via Pine Island Road / CR 78)
- Fort Myers (17 miles southeast)
- Lehigh Acres (30 miles east)
- Bonita Springs (30 miles south)
- Punta Gorda (25 miles north)

## Niche: paver-sealing

### Permit Requirements
- Re-sanding or sealing existing pavers generally does not require a building permit in unincorporated Lee County. Pine Island Center is unincorporated and uses Lee County's permitting system. [MJC Pavers](https://www.mjcpavers.com/blog/do-you-need-a-permit-for-pavers-in-florida/)
- New paver installations at grade level that do not attach to the home structure typically do not require a permit, though setback requirements from property lines and utility easements still apply. [MJC Pavers](https://www.mjcpavers.com/blog/do-you-need-a-permit-for-pavers-in-florida/)
- Paver driveways, retaining walls over 4 feet, and projects affecting drainage or the right-of-way require a Lee County permit. [PermitMint](https://permitmint.com/permits/florida/lee-county-fl/)
- Work within Lee County right-of-way requires a separate right-of-way permit. [Lee County DOT](https://www.leegov.com/dot/Documents/Permit%20documents/AC-11-12.pdf)
- Pine Island has limited HOA governance compared to mainland communities, but individual subdivisions may still have deed restrictions on hardscape materials. [WC Pavers](https://wcpavers.com/hoa-paver-approval-checklist-for-southwest-florida/)

### Local Regulations
- Lee County follows NPDES stormwater regulations; hardscape projects near Pine Island's sensitive waterways (Matlacha Pass Aquatic Preserve, Pine Island Sound) must account for impervious surface coverage and stormwater runoff. [FL DEP NPDES Manual](https://floridadep.gov/sites/default/files/MS4_Permit_Resource_Manual_0.pdf)
- Coal-tar based sealants produce PAH levels in stormwater runoff 65 times higher than unsealed surfaces; this is especially concerning on a barrier island with direct waterway proximity. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2018-02/documents/pah_-coal-tar_sealcoat-swpoll.pdf)
- Water-based, low-VOC sealers are preferred for island applications due to reduced environmental impact on surrounding marine ecosystems. [First Coast Property Experts](https://firstcoastpropertyexperts.com/blog/paver-sealing-florida-guide/)
- Florida Statute 489.103 exempts paver sealing from state contractor licensing when the project does not exceed $2,500 and no building permit is required. [FL Senate HB 735 Analysis](https://flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2021/735/Analyses/h0735z1.RRS.PDF)
- Pine Island is within the Calusa Blueway Paddling Trail system and Charlotte Harbor National Estuary Program area; chemical runoff regulations are more closely monitored than mainland locations. [Visit Florida](https://www.visitflorida.com/places-to-go/southwest/pine-island/)

### Soil Type
- Lee County soil is classified as sand with 87.4% sand composition and a low pH of 5.45, sitting over marine limestone and shell-hash substrate. [Soil By County](https://soilbycounty.com/florida/lee-county)
- Pine Island's soil is particularly sandy with a high water table due to its barrier island geography; paver base preparation requires extra depth (6+ inches) of compacted crushed shell. [SW Pavers](https://swpavers.com/paver-base-guide/)
- Sandy soils compress unevenly under load; heavy summer storms carry sand away from beneath pavers, causing settling, rocking corners, and surface waves. [Craft Pavers](https://craft-pavers.com/areas-served/lee-county/)
- Capillary moisture wicking from the water table is more aggressive on barrier islands than inland Lee County communities. [Soil By County](https://soilbycounty.com/florida/lee-county)
- Hurricane Ian (September 2022) caused significant erosion and soil displacement on Pine Island; properties rebuilt since 2022 may have disturbed soil conditions requiring extra base stabilization. [WGCU](https://www.wgcu.org/section/hurricanerecovery/2025-06-02/pine-island-rebuilds-from-damage-by-several-storms-as-another-hurricane-season-begins)

### Paver Styles
- Travertine pavers are the top choice for Pine Island pool decks and patios because they remain cool underfoot and offer natural slip resistance — important for waterfront properties. [Tuscan Paving Stone](https://swpavers.com/travertine)
- Concrete pavers dominate the driveway market on Pine Island, available in colors and textures that complement the island's Old Florida aesthetic. [Progressive Design Build](https://progressivedesignbuild.com/how-to-choose-and-maintain-the-perfect-pavers-for-your-southwest-florida-home-blog/)
- Clay brick pavers provide a traditional look for driveways and walkways; properly maintained, they can last 50–100 years but heat up in direct sun. [EPR Landscaping](https://eprlandscaping.com/blog/best-pavers-florida-driveways-pool-decks/)
- The running bond pattern is popular for Pine Island walkways; it complements the island's casual architectural style. [Brevard Pavers](https://www.brevardpavers.com/blog/paver-pattern-options-florida-driveways-pool-decks/)
- Shell aggregate pavers and natural stone are also used on Pine Island to blend with the island's tropical landscaping and waterfront setting. [Fort Myers Landscapes](https://fortmyerslandscapes.com/paver-patios-hardscaping-fort-myers-fl)

### Salt Exposure
- Pine Island is surrounded by saltwater on all sides (Matlacha Pass, Pine Island Sound, Charlotte Harbor); salt air exposure is among the most intense in Lee County. [GPICA](https://gpica.org/about-us/about-pine-island/)
- Oceanfront and waterfront properties in Lee County see paver deterioration 40–60% faster than inland homes; Pine Island properties are squarely in this category. [HD Surface Solutions](https://www.hdsurfacesolutions.com/lee-county-paver-sealing/)
- Coastal salt air can cut a paver sealer's effective lifespan roughly in half compared to inland applications. [Palm Shine Pros](https://www.palmshinepros.com/post/weather-impacts-paver-sealing-florida)
- Salt crystallization from Gulf air fractures paver stone from the inside out; constant breezes across the island ensure continuous salt deposition. [Premier Coatings FL](https://www.premiercoatingsfl.com/single-post/the-ultimate-florida-paver-care-guide-how-humidity-salt-air-and-sunshine-affect-your-brick-pavers)
- Pine Island properties need paver sealing every 2 years or more frequently due to extreme salt exposure; breathable, salt-resistant sealers are essential. [Rain Home Service](https://www.fortmyerspaversealing.com/)

## Niche: window-tinting

### Permit Requirements
- No permit is required for vehicle window tinting in Florida. Tint shops must comply with Florida Statute §316.2953 for legal VLT limits. [Florida Court Rules](https://floridacourtrules.com/florida-window-tint-law/)

### Local Regulations
- Front side windows on all vehicles must allow at least 28% VLT (visible light transmittance) with no more than 25% reflectance. [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/florida/)
- Rear side windows and rear windshield on sedans require at least 15% VLT; SUVs, vans, and trucks may go as dark as 6% VLT. [Tint Legal](https://tintlegal.com/window-tint-laws/florida/)
- Windshields may have a non-reflective tint strip above the manufacturer's AS-1 line only; full windshield tinting is not legal. [Exzact Window Tinting](https://exzactwindowtinting.com/florida-window-tint-law/)
- Colored tint that changes the normal color of the window is prohibited in Florida. [SG Tint](https://sgtint.com/florida-window-tint-data/)
- A sticker identifying legal tinting is required on the inside of the driver's side door jamb. [Law Walls](https://lawwalls.com/florida-window-tint-laws)
- Medical exemptions for darker tint are available under Florida Statute §316.2954 with a physician's prescription. [Florida Tint Exemption](https://www.floridatintexemption.com/florida-window-tint-laws)

### Sun Exposure
- The Cape Coral–Fort Myers metro area (including Pine Island) averages 264–271 sunny days per year, well above the US average of 205. [Current Results](https://www.currentresults.com/Weather/Florida/annual-days-of-sunshine.php)
- The UV index averages between 7 and 11+ from April through October, classified as Very High to Extreme by the EPA. [UV Blocker](https://uv-blocker.com/blogs/sun-protection/florida-sun-protection)
- Maximum UV index reaches 12 (Extreme category) during April, May, and August. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)
- Pine Island's open water surroundings amplify UV exposure through water surface reflection, increasing effective UV radiation compared to mainland locations. [Vitally Doc](https://vitallydoc.com/2026/07/27/vitallydoc-com-blog-sun-damage-skin-aging-fort-myers/)
- Annual average UV index in the area is 7.6–7.7, among the highest in the continental United States. [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/florida-usa/cape-coral-climate)

### Vehicle Types
- SUVs and trucks dominate the Florida market, accounting for nearly 75% of new vehicle sales; sedans make up about 22%. [AutoLand Jax](https://autolandjax.com/blog/most-popular-autos-in-florida)
- Lee County had 607,021 autos and pickups registered plus 70,587 heavy trucks as of January 2024. [FL HSMV](https://www.flhsmv.gov/pdf/vehicle-vesselreports/cvr_01_2024_ada.pdf)
- Top-selling vehicles in Florida include the Toyota RAV4 (3.3% market share), Honda CR-V, Toyota Camry, Ford F-150, and Chevrolet Silverado. [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/best-selling-cars-in-florida)
- Pickup trucks are especially common on Pine Island due to the island's rural character, boat-towing needs, and limited public transit. [Florida Farm Bureau](https://floridafarmbureau.com/2023/07/the-most-popular-vehicles-in-florida/)
- Boats and boat trailers are ubiquitous on Pine Island; tint shops may also find demand for marine-grade UV-protective films and boat windshield tinting. [Visit Florida](https://www.visitflorida.com/places-to-go/southwest/pine-island/)
