## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Brickell: Major financial district south of downtown with high-rise condos and a walkable urban core; population 31,759. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Downtown_Miami)
- Little Havana: Cultural hub west of downtown with a population of 76,163, known for Cuban heritage and Calle Ocho. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighborhoods_of_Miami)
- Wynwood: Arts district north of downtown known for its murals, galleries, and creative businesses. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_%26_Entertainment_District)
- Coconut Grove: Historic waterfront neighborhood with a bohemian character and population of 20,076. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighborhoods_of_Miami)
- Downtown Miami / Central Business District: Urban core including Edgewater, Midtown, Park West, and Overtown subdistricts. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Orlando)

### Climate
- Tropical monsoon climate with hot, humid summers and short, warm winters; annual mean temperature of 78 °F. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Miami)
- Average humidity of 73%, ranging from 84% in early morning to 61% in afternoon. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Miami)
- Annual average high of 91 °F; August is the hottest month (average high 89 °F, low 78 °F); January is coolest (high 76 °F, low 63 °F). [Time and Date](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/usa/miami/climate)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- Most residential interior floor coating projects do not require a building permit in Miami-Dade County for single-family homes. [National Concrete Polishing](https://nationalconcretepolishing.net/epoxy-flooring-permit-requirements-miami/)
- All commercial properties, including condominium units, are required to obtain a permit for flooring, with submissions including a Flooring Permit Affidavit, floor plans, and soundproofing manufacturer's literature. [Miami Beach Building Dept.](https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Flooring-Permits.pdf)
- Every industrial floor installation in Miami-Dade requires ASTM F2170 in-situ relative humidity testing before a coating system is applied. [Urbanac Surfaces](https://urbanacsurfaces.com/industrial-flooring-miami-complete-guide/)

### Local Regulations
- Commercial concrete floor coatings must meet ADA compliance, fire code adherence, and industry-specific regulations for healthcare, food service, and educational facilities. [Urbanac Surfaces](https://urbanacsurfaces.com/industrial-flooring-miami-complete-guide/)
- Coved bases are required in food service environments to eliminate 90-degree corners where bacteria and dirt collect, per DBPR requirements. [Kongcrete Coatings](https://kongcretecoatingsco.com/health-code-compliant-epoxy-flooring-in-orlando-inside-sanford-airports-commercial-kitchen/)

### Concrete Condition
- Miami sits on porous oolitic limestone with a water table often just 4–6 feet below grade; in coastal areas it can be as shallow as 12 inches during rainy season. [Foundation Waterproofing 101](https://foundationwaterproofing101.com/our-services/florida-water-table/)
- Slab moisture vapor transmission averages 4–8 lbs per 1,000 sq ft per 24 hours — higher than inland Florida slabs due to the shallow water table. [Self-Leveling Florida](https://www.selflevelingflorida.com/blog/concrete-moisture-problems)
- Many slabs were poured on grade with thin or aged vapor barriers, allowing ground moisture to wick upward through capillary action. [Pure Spec Environmental](https://www.purespecenvironmental.com/resources/florida-slab-on-grade-moisture)

### Humidity Impact
- Average humidity of 73% year-round creates persistent challenges for epoxy adhesion and curing. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Miami)
- Hydrostatic pressure pushes moisture through concrete when the water table rises to within inches of the slab. [Foundation Waterproofing 101](https://foundationwaterproofing101.com/our-services/florida-water-table/)
- Moisture-mitigating primer on the slab is recommended even for floating-floor installs; mandatory ASTM F2170 testing applies to all industrial coatings. [Urbanac Surfaces](https://urbanacsurfaces.com/industrial-flooring-miami-complete-guide/)
