## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Bayside Lakes: Master-planned community in southeast Palm Bay with resort-style amenities and lakefront homes. [Niche](https://www.niche.com/places-to-live/palm-bay-brevard-fl/)
- Palm Bay Colony: Established residential area in the central part of the city with mature landscaping. [Homes.com](https://www.homes.com/local-guide/palm-bay-fl/)
- Port Malabar: Northern Palm Bay neighborhood near the Indian River Lagoon and Turkey Creek Sanctuary. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_Bay,_Florida)

### Climate
- Subtropical climate with very mild winters and hot, humid summers with frequent thunderstorms. [Climates to Travel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/united-states/palm-bay)
- July is the hottest month with average highs of 86.7 °F; January is the coldest with average lows around 57 °F. [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/palm-bay-1629/)
- Mean annual temperature of 73.4 °F (23.0 °C). [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/palm-bay-1629/)

## Niche: bathroom-remodel

### Plumbing Codes
- The City of Palm Bay Building Department enforces the Florida Building Code (8th Edition), which is more stringent than the national IRC in hurricane-resistant design and flood-zone construction. [DoINeedAPermit.org](https://www.doineedapermit.org/permits/cities/palm-bay-fl)
- Any bathroom remodel involving plumbing, electrical, or mechanical work requires a building permit; subpermits for plumbing, electrical, and HVAC must be filed by licensed contractors. [DoINeedAPermit.org](https://www.doineedapermit.org/permits/cities/palm-bay-fl)
- Moving or adding plumbing fixtures (toilet, shower, tub relocation), adding electrical outlets, and removing or adding walls all require permits. [FL Permit Help](https://flpermithelp.com/blog/bathroom-remodel-permits-florida)
- Replacing tile on existing surfaces does not require a permit in Florida; work valued under $7,500 on an owner-occupied single-family home may qualify for exemption. [FL Permit Help](https://flpermithelp.com/blog/bathroom-remodel-permits-florida)

### Typical Home Age
- Median construction year for Palm Bay homes is approximately 1993; the median home age is roughly 29 years. [Point2 Homes](https://www.point2homes.com/US/Neighborhood/FL/Palm-Bay-Demographics.html)
- Housing stock breakdown: 39.3% built 2000 or newer, 56.2% from 1970–1999, 4.1% from 1940–1969. [Community Scale](https://app.communityscale.io/dashboard/place/1254000/Palm-Bay-FL)
- Many homes from the 1970s–1990s era have original bathrooms that are approaching or past their expected remodel lifecycle. [Neighborhood Scout](https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/fl/palm-bay/real-estate)

### Water Quality
- Palm Bay has relatively hard water with Total Dissolved Solids at 380 PPM and average hardness of 120 PPM, containing elevated calcium and magnesium. [Soft Pro Water Systems](https://softprowatersystems.com/pages/palm-bay-water-quality-report-contaminants-tap-water)
- Water is treated by reverse osmosis and lime softening at the City's two water treatment plants. [Soft Pro Water Systems](https://softprowatersystems.com/pages/palm-bay-water-quality-report-contaminants-tap-water)
- Residents commonly experience spotty dishes, film on shower glass, dry skin, shortened appliance lifespans, and chlorine taste/smell from hard water. [Brita Pro FL](https://britaprofl.com/services-area/water-softener-filtration-in-palm-bay-fl/)
- Water source comes primarily from the Floridan aquifer and surface water resources; tested for lead, chlorine, and microbial organisms per the Safe Drinking Water Act. [Tap Water Data](https://www.tapwaterdata.com/fl/palm-bay)
