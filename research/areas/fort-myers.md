# Local Facts: Fort Myers, Florida

**Researched:** 2026-11-09
**Niches:** epoxy-flooring
**County:** Lee County
**Population:** 95,051 (2024 ACS) — [Census Bureau](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fortmyerscityflorida)

## City Overview

### Neighborhoods and Districts
- Downtown Fort Myers: Historic River District with dining, galleries, and the waterfront along the Caloosahatchee River. [City of Fort Myers](https://www.cityftmyers.com)
- North Fort Myers: Unincorporated CDP in Lee County with a population of 42,719, located across the river. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Fort_Myers,_Florida)
- Fort Myers Shores: Eastern residential community with a population of 5,774. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Myers_Shores,_Florida)
- Gateway: Planned community bordering Fort Myers to the east with newer subdivisions and commercial centers. [World Atlas](https://www.worldatlas.com/cities/fort-myers-florida.html)

### Climate
- Humid subtropical climate with a mean annual temperature of 74.2 °F (23.4 °C). [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/fort-myers-1620/)
- Average annual rainfall of approximately 54 inches, with June as the wettest month (7.3 inches). [Climate-Data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/united-states-of-america/florida/fort-myers-1620/)
- Summer highs range from 89–92 °F; winters are mild with temperatures of 45–75 °F. [Weather and Climate](https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Rainfall-Temperature-Sunshine,fort-myers-florida-us,United-States-of-America)

## Niche: epoxy-flooring

### Permit Requirements
- Most residential epoxy projects over existing concrete do not require a building permit in Fort Myers or Lee County. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/florida-painting-permit-questions-for-fort-myers-and-naples)
- Commercial epoxy installations may require permits, especially when involving floor drains, structural modifications, or changes affecting fire ratings. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/florida-painting-permit-questions-for-fort-myers-and-naples)
- City addresses use the City of Fort Myers Building, Permitting & Inspections Division (1825 Hendry St, Fort Myers, FL 33901; 239-321-7925); unincorporated addresses go through Lee County Community Development. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/florida-painting-permit-questions-for-fort-myers-and-naples)

### Local Regulations
- Florida does not require a specific state certification for epoxy flooring contractors, though individual municipalities may impose local licensing requirements. [Flooring Clarity](https://www.flooringclarity.com/do-you-need-a-license-to-do-epoxy-flooring/)
- Unpermitted commercial work can result in stop-work orders, fines, complications with property sales, and insurance claim denials. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/florida-painting-permit-questions-for-fort-myers-and-naples)

### Concrete Condition
- Most Fort Myers homes sit on concrete slab-on-grade foundations; salt air and high humidity year-round affect concrete condition. [Pro Work Flooring](https://www.proworkflooring.com/locations/fort-myers-fl/concrete-polishing/)
- Fort Myers' sandy, mucky soils and high water table cause concrete to settle gradually over time. [CAT 3 Recovery](https://cat3recovery.com/slab-leaks-structural-damage-southwest-florida/)
- Concrete slabs naturally wick ground moisture upward; in summer, slab moisture levels can be surprisingly high even in conditioned spaces. [Self-Leveling Florida](https://www.selflevelingflorida.com/blog/concrete-moisture-problems)

### Humidity Impact
- Average humidity levels hover between 65–80% throughout most of the year, creating conditions where moisture penetrates unsealed concrete capillaries. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/how-to-test-concrete-slab-moisture-before-floor-coating-in-florida)
- Slabs dry slower in Fort Myers than in drier climates because ambient air is often saturated with moisture for half the year. [Self-Leveling Florida](https://www.selflevelingflorida.com/blog/concrete-moisture-problems)
- The RH probe test (ASTM F2170) is required to confirm slab moisture is under 75–80% internal RH before applying epoxy. [EFC Contractors](https://www.efccontractors.com/how-to-test-concrete-slab-moisture-before-floor-coating-in-florida)

### Garage Usage
- Many Fort Myers homeowners do not park vehicles inside because garages become de facto storage units for accumulated belongings. [Suncoast Haulers](https://suncoasthaulers.com/garage-cleanout-fort-myers/)
- Common uses include home workshops, sports gear storage, and housing kayaks and paddleboards with overhead racks. [Modern Garage FL](https://moderngaragefl.com/blog/garage-storage-solutions-in-fort-myers-florida)
- Seasonal residents (snowbirds) use garages for overflow storage during months away. [Modern Garage FL](https://moderngaragefl.com/blog/garage-storage-solutions-in-fort-myers-florida)
- Fort Myers heat and humidity stress garage interiors, causing moisture settling on cabinets, shelves, and hardware. [Hamilton & Carter](https://hamiltonandcarter.com/fort-myers-garage-cabinets/)
