# US Cities — 80k–300k Population

Reference list of mid-size US cities for rank-and-rent region selection.
Source: US Census Bureau Vintage 2024 estimates (July 1, 2024).

## How to use this list

Mid-size cities are the sweet spot for rank-and-rent: enough search volume to generate leads, but not so competitive that SEO takes years. Look for cities with:
- Blue-collar or trade-heavy economies (oil/gas, military, manufacturing)
- Growing populations (new construction = more home services demand)
- Limited existing digital competition

## Notable clusters for rank-and-rent

### Oil & gas regions
- **Permian Basin, TX**: Midland (143k), Odessa (120k) — boom/bust but high demand for services during up cycles
- **Gulf Coast, TX/LA**: Beaumont (113k), Lake Charles (81k), Baytown (86k), Pasadena TX (150k) — petrochemical refining
- **Bakken region**: Billings MT (121k), Fargo ND (136k) — oil and agriculture
