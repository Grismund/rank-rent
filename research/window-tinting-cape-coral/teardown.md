- tintsandbeyond.com: only 95 referring domains, domain rank 93

Two small local competitors with under 100 referring domains each, plus a franchise city page. The franchise's city page won't have deep local content. A dedicated Cape Coral window tinting site with 60-90 quality referring domains and strong local content should reach page 1.
