# Niche Viability Investigation — Air Duct Cleaning, Junk Removal, Dryer Vent Cleaning

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Cost:** ~$0.04 (4 SERP API calls)

## Purpose

Three shortlisted niches raised questions about whether their unit economics actually support rank-and-rent. This report documents live SERP verification and final verdicts.

## 1. Air Duct Cleaning Houston — PROCEED WITH CAUTION

### Unit economics

- Average residential job: $390–$500 nationally, $550–$1,050 for NADCA-standard work
- With upsells (dryer vent, antimicrobial, sanitizer): $450–$700/job
- A 2-person crew does 3–4 jobs/day = $18,000–$30,000/mo gross
- Net margins: 40–55% (low materials cost)
- $1.1B US industry, 18,597 businesses

### CPC analysis

The $82.27 Houston CPC is inflated by bait-and-switch operators. Live SERP confirms this: a paid ad from novacleanpro.com advertises **"$85 Air Duct Cleaning"** — the classic loss-leader that upsells to $1,500+ once inside. At $82/click and 10% conversion, a lead costs ~$820, which is absurd for a legitimate $400 job but makes sense for scam operators running bait-and-switch.

In other markets CPC is $19–$30 — the math works there: ~$250/lead on a $400–700 job with 40%+ margins.

**Key observation:** Multiple exact-match domains ranking well (houstonairductcleaning.com, houstontxairductcleaning.com, houston--airductcleaning.com, houstonairductcleaningservice.com, reliablehoustonairductcleaning.com). This is a positive signal — EMDs clearly work in this niche. However, it also means the space is somewhat crowded with rank-and-rent style sites already.

### Rent estimate reality check

- $4,628/mo estimate is based on inflated $82 CPC. A more realistic CPC for legitimate operators is $25–$35, which would put the rent estimate at $1,400–$1,950/mo.
- Even at the lower estimate, this is viable if the site ranks well given Houston's massive search volume (5,530 total).

### Verdict: KEEP — but adjust rent expectations downward

The market is real, the volume is real, and EMDs work. The $82 CPC and $4,628 rent estimate are inflated by scam operators. Realistic rent is $1,400–$2,000/mo. Still worth pursuing.

## 2. Junk Removal — STRONG BUY (all three markets)

### Unit economics

- Average job: $300–$500 (half to full truck load)
- Minimum charge: $100 (single item)
- Gross margins: 60–70% per job
- Net margins: 18–38% overall
- Solo operator revenue: $90K–$150K/year
- $10B+ US industry with 5%+ annual growth

### Why Facebook free pickups don't kill this market

Free Marketplace pickups only work for desirable items (working furniture, appliances). Nobody picks up a broken hot tub, construction debris, a hoarder's garage, or a foreclosure cleanout for free. The bread-and-butter jobs ($500–$2,000 estate/commercial cleanouts) are untouched by free alternatives.

## 3. Dryer Vent Cleaning — DROP (all markets)

### Unit economics

- Average job: $145 nationally ($104–$188 range)
- Jobs take 30–60 minutes per vent
- Often bundled with air duct cleaning ($340–$800 combo)

### The problem

At $145/job, lead values are too low. Even Houston's $33 CPC produces ~$330/lead at 10% conversion — more than double the job value. The CPC is propped up by companies using dryer vent as a loss-leader to upsell duct cleaning and HVAC maintenance.

Houston shows 4,030 total volume (real demand) but at $145/job: 15 leads/month × $145 = $2,175 gross to operator × 40% margin = $870 profit. Paying $1,090/mo rent for $870 profit doesn't work.

All non-Houston markets produce rent estimates under $150/mo — too low to justify building a site.

### Verdict: DROP as standalone niche

Only revisit if building combined "duct + dryer vent cleaning" sites where dryer vent pages serve as additional organic entry points.
