# Niche Brief: Paver Sealing

**Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Cities:** Cape Coral

## Service Overview

Paver sealing is a protective maintenance service that applies sealant to brick and concrete paver surfaces — driveways, patios, pool decks, and walkways. The customer is typically a Florida homeowner with an existing paver installation that needs protection from sun, rain, weed growth, and staining. Job triggers include fading/discoloration, weed growth between joints, paver settling, or a new construction that needs initial sealing.

## Sub-Service Editorial Notes

### Paver Sealing
- Core service; protective sealant application after cleaning
- Customer needs to know about sealer types (wet-look enhances color, matte preserves natural look, penetrating for invisible protection)
- Cross-link to cleaning and to cost guide

### Paver Cleaning
- Pressure washing and stain removal as a standalone service
- Often the entry point — customer searches for cleaning, then upsells to sealing
- Differentiate from general pressure washing: paver-specific techniques to avoid joint sand displacement
- Cross-link to paver sealing and cleaning-and-sealing combo

### Paver Cleaning and Sealing
- Combined service in one visit — the most popular package
- Customer gets better value vs. booking separately
- This is the primary conversion page; most searchers want both done together
- Cross-link to individual cleaning and sealing pages for detail

### Paver Repair
- Releveling, replacing, and re-sanding damaged pavers
- Different customer intent: something is broken, not just maintenance
- Mention common causes (tree roots, settling soil, heavy loads)
- Cross-link to sealing (seal after repair to protect the fix)
