# Niche Brief: Pressure Washing

**Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Cities:** Conroe

## Service Overview

Pressure washing cleans exterior surfaces — house siding, driveways, decks, patios, and roofs — using high-pressure water and (for soft washing) chemical solutions. The customer is a homeowner dealing with dirt, mold, mildew, algae, or stains on their property's exterior surfaces. Job triggers include annual maintenance, home sale prep, mold/mildew buildup after rain seasons, and curb appeal improvement. Fast jobs with low overhead drive decent margins.

## Sub-Service Editorial Notes

### House Washing
- Exterior siding, brick, stucco cleaning — the broadest residential service
- Customer cares about mold, mildew, and curb appeal
- Important to explain soft wash vs pressure wash for different surfaces
- Cross-link to cost guide and FAQ

### Driveway Pressure Washing
- Concrete and paver driveway cleaning — most visual before/after impact
- High-pressure safe on concrete; customer wants stain, oil, and tire mark removal
- Cross-link to deck cleaning and house washing

### Deck and Patio Cleaning
- Wood deck, composite, and patio surface washing
- Critical to note: wood requires lower pressure to avoid damage
- Seasonal demand around spring entertaining season
- Cross-link to house washing for full-property packages

### Roof Soft Washing
- Low-pressure chemical wash for shingles and tile — NOT pressure washing
- Essential distinction: high pressure damages shingles
- Appeals to homeowners with dark streaks (Gloeocapsa magma algae)
- Cross-link to house washing
