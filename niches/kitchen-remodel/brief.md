# Niche Brief: Kitchen Remodel

**Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Cities:** Goodyear

## Service Overview

Kitchen remodeling encompasses full renovations (layout changes, new cabinets, countertops, appliances), cabinet refacing, countertop replacement, and kitchen island installation. The customer is a homeowner with an outdated or dysfunctional kitchen — triggered by aging home, lifestyle changes, home resale, or worn-out fixtures. These are the highest-ticket projects in the portfolio ($12K–$35K) with the longest sales cycles in home services.

## Sub-Service Editorial Notes

### Full Kitchen Remodel
- Complete renovation including potential layout changes
- The primary search term and highest-value sub-service
- Customer needs to understand the long timeline (4–8 weeks typically) and kitchen-out-of-service period
- Cross-link to cost guide and FAQ

### Cabinet Refacing
- Update cabinet doors and hardware without ripping out the boxes
- Lower cost alternative ($4K–$8K) to full cabinet replacement
- Appeal to homeowners who like their layout but want a fresh look
- Cross-link to full remodel for when refacing isn't enough

### Countertop Replacement
- Swap old countertops for granite, quartz, butcher block, or solid surface
- One of the most impactful upgrades for the price
- Customer cares about material durability, maintenance, and cost per square foot
- Cross-link to full remodel and cabinet refacing (often done together)

### Kitchen Island Installation
- Add or rebuild a kitchen island for workspace, storage, and seating
- Requires enough floor space; may need plumbing/electrical if adding sink or outlets
- Popular in open floor plans common in newer Goodyear homes
- Cross-link to full remodel and countertop replacement
