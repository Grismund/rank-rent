# Niche Brief: Epoxy Flooring

**Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Cities:** Fort Myers, New Braunfels, San Antonio, Orlando, Miami

## Service Overview

Epoxy flooring is a durable coating applied to concrete surfaces — primarily garage floors, but also commercial warehouses, showrooms, and interior spaces. The customer is either a homeowner upgrading their garage or a business owner protecting a commercial floor. Job triggers include new home purchase, garage conversion, concrete damage, or aesthetic upgrade. Very low material costs ($0.30–$1/sq ft) drive strong margins.

## Sub-Service Editorial Notes

### Garage Floor Epoxy
- The primary search term and most common residential application
- Customer wants durability, chemical resistance, and a clean look for their garage
- Explain prep process (diamond grinding, moisture testing) — this is what separates pro work from DIY
- Cross-link to cost guide and FAQ

### Commercial Epoxy Flooring
- Warehouses, shops, showrooms, restaurants
- Different customer intent: durability under heavy traffic, safety (non-slip), regulatory compliance
- Higher square footage = higher ticket
- Cross-link to epoxy floor coating for general info

### Epoxy Floor Coating
- General-purpose page covering all epoxy applications
- Broadest keyword — captures searches not specific to garage or commercial
- Good hub page for internal linking to more specific sub-services
- Cross-link to all other sub-services

### Metallic Epoxy Flooring
- Decorative, high-end finish with swirl/marble effects
- Premium pricing ($5–$12/sq ft vs $3–$7 for standard)
- Appeals to design-conscious homeowners and upscale commercial spaces
- Cross-link to garage floor epoxy and commercial for context
