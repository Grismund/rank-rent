# Niche Brief: Junk Removal

**Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Cities:** Port St. Lucie, Georgetown

## Service Overview

Junk removal is a hauling service that picks up and disposes of unwanted household items, furniture, appliances, and construction debris. The typical customer is a homeowner dealing with a cleanout (moving, estate, post-renovation) or simply accumulated clutter. Job triggers include moving, downsizing, home renovation, estate liquidation, and storm cleanup.

## Job Economics

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Avg job value | $300–$500 | Half to full truck load; $100 minimum single item |
| Gross margin | 60–70% | Low COGS — labor + disposal fees |
| Net revenue per job | ~$260 | Mid-range job at 65% margin |

## Content Positioning

- **Frame as:** Lead-generation service connecting homeowners with independent local haulers
- **Angle for service pages:** Process-focused — what gets hauled, how scheduling works, responsible disposal
- **Cost angle:** Include price ranges ($300–$500 typical job, $100 minimum) with "costs vary" disclaimer
- **Trust signals:** Responsible disposal emphasis (recycling, donation), same-day/next-day availability, no hidden fees
- **Banned claims reminder:** No invented years in business, no fake licenses/certifications, no fabricated reviews, no "licensed and insured" claims, no guarantees unless renter provides them

## Sub-Service Editorial Notes

### Residential Junk Removal
- The broadest sub-service; covers whole-home cleanouts
- Customer needs to know what items are accepted vs. hazardous waste exclusions
- Cross-link to cost guide and FAQ

### Furniture Removal
- Specific to couches, mattresses, dressers
- Differentiate from general junk removal by focusing on the logistics of large/heavy items
- Mention donation options for usable furniture
- Cross-link to residential junk removal

### Appliance Hauling
- Refrigerators, washers, dryers, water heaters
- Customer cares about safe disconnect and disposal (Freon recovery for fridges)
- Emphasize responsible recycling
- Cross-link to construction debris for renovation contexts

### Construction Debris Removal
- Post-renovation waste: drywall, lumber, tile, flooring
- Different from household junk — heavier, may require different disposal
- Mention permit requirements for dumpster placement if applicable
- Cross-link to residential junk removal and town pages
