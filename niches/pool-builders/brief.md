# Niche Brief: Pool Builders

**Date:** 2026-11-09 | **Cities:** Port St. Lucie

## Service Overview

Pool building covers new inground pool construction (concrete/gunite, fiberglass, vinyl liner), pool remodeling/resurfacing, screen enclosure installation, and pool deck resurfacing. The customer is a homeowner making a major property investment — typically $25K–$65K for new construction. Job triggers include new home purchase, home value investment, lifestyle upgrade, or aging pool needing remodel. The highest-ticket niche in the portfolio with the longest sales cycles.

## Content Positioning

- **Frame as:** Lead-generation service connecting homeowners with local pool construction contractors
- **Angle for service pages:** Decision-focused — pool types (concrete vs fiberglass vs vinyl), permit process, design options, timeline expectations. Pool buyers do extensive research before committing to a $25K+ purchase

## Sub-Service Editorial Notes

### Inground Pool Construction
- The primary service and highest-value keyword
- Customer needs to understand: concrete vs fiberglass vs vinyl liner pros/cons, permit timeline, total cost including decking and enclosure
- Florida-specific: screen enclosures are nearly universal, high water table affects construction
- Cross-link to cost guide and FAQ

### Pool Remodeling
- Resurface, retile, or redesign an existing pool
- Different customer: already has a pool, wants to update it
- Lower ticket than new construction ($5K–$20K) but higher margin
- Cross-link to pool deck resurfacing (often done together)

### Pool Screen Enclosure
- Florida-specific service — screened lanai/cage around pool area
- Nearly required in Florida for bug and debris protection
- Customer cares about screen type, frame material, and building code compliance
- Cross-link to inground pool construction (new builds include enclosure)

### Pool Deck Resurfacing
- Resurface or overlay existing concrete pool decks
- Cross-sells with pool remodeling — customers often update both
- Cool-deck and spray-deck options popular in Florida heat
- Cross-link to pool remodeling
